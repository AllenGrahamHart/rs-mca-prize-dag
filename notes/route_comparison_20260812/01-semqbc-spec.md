## 3. Constraints any proof must survive

- **Carrier fold (#1158 §3)**: refuted exactly — the direct-coordinate identification: τ(T)=1−T does not preserve D (`τ(D) ≠ D`, boxed), even with all 36 fibers split on the carrier and (CM) holding. Freedom remaining (Conjugacy disclosure): `gτg⁻¹(T) = −T` with g(T)=T−½ *does* preserve D (n even, −1∈D); "The packet therefore does not prove that no conjugated fold can preserve the carrier" — but using it requires "a new theorem prov[ing] that it also transports the actual received line, support, explaining data, owner, chronology, and all record guards."
- **Dimension shift (#1159 Thm 4.1)**: an actual deployed record has `d1^(k) = d1^(k+1) = 67473`, which is the *boundary* profile at K=k (w=67472) but *first-interior* at K=k+1 (w=67471); K↦K+1 creates one extra interior coefficient (deg B ≤ 0 vs ≤ 1, m'=m vs m+1). So SEM-QBC condition (4) cannot be discharged by silent substitution; the numerical profile alone assigns no owner (Q-theorem covers only its prefix family, `grande_finale.tex:4997-5013`).
- **Route cut B terminal shape (#1157)**: "Success condition B" — the maximal source-bound attack terminates *before* the eleven-route eliminations, at the missing selector; two routes (`433-1a→O0b`, `433-1b→O0a`) are raw-zero with `null` slope payment; the `433-1b→O0b` transport fails within the declared natural map class (R_BC = −1 vs +1; incidence signatures Ξ differ), leaving only newly-specified orientation-changing transports or native O0b algebra.

## 4. Theorem or definition+theorem package? (MY-INFERENCE, grounded in quotes)

SEM-QBC is unambiguously a **definition+theorem package**, not a theorem awaiting proof: no candidate `P_Q`/`P_BC` exists to prove correct — the frozen predicates are opaque strings, so the package must (a) *define* executable witness-level predicates and the `Z_BC^(2,4)` membership predicate (currently undefined anywhere, #1158 §2 item 2), then (b) prove the equivalence to the frozen first-match cells (#1159: "prove their slope projections agree with the frozen first-match cells"). The #1159 §5 audit shows why this matters decisionally: a definition-only move (our compiler node) is exactly what was already tried and rejected as substitution-not-equivalence. The load-bearing content is the equivalence direction "coverage" against the independently frozen cell plus the K-adapter — those are theorems; but they are unposeable until the definitions are fixed, and the anti-tautology clause in (COV) means the definitions themselves carry proof obligations. `Rec_2_4` is likewise a package: ValidBC (definition+SEM-QBC) ∧ End_{2,4} (definition) + selector/(FIB)/fence theorems.
