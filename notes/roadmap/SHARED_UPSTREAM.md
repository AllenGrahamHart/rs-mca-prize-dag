## Shared terminology

- `(Q)`: quotient/prefix flatness.
- `(A)` and split-pencil census: the base-field-normalized residual census.
- SPI: primitive shift-pair control.

Use those names in outbound PRs.  Local node IDs do not change.

## Transport rule

An upstream result is first recorded with immutable commit, file, statement,
scope, normalization, and chronology.  It enters the local DAG as evidence
unless a proved transport identifies field, row, object, quantifier,
projection, unit, endpoint, and ownership conventions exactly.

The correspondence source of truth is
`notes/correspondence/JOINT_CROSSWALK.json`.  Mathematical truth
`status_ours` and upstream bankability `status_his` remain independent.

## Packet rule

Consolidate related results into broad, bankable packets.  A packet should
lead with the exact theorem and consumer, include proof/certificate replay,
state nonclaims, and use current upstream terminology.  Numerical evidence
and no-go fences are supporting material, not promoted theorems.
