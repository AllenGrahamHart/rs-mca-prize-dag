=== D3b: DIMENSION LEDGER for the (SAT3) configuration ===
The object is the affine curve F(Z,x)=0 of bidegree (e,rho)=(m,4m-1)
(F = the apolar generator Q_Z read as a polynomial in the domain
variable x).  Free data: the curve ((m+1)*4m - 1 projective coeffs),
the slope set G (|G| = T), the domain D (|D| = N = 16m).
Conditions: (SAT4) forces sum_x d_x = T*rho - O with d_x <= m and
|D| = 16m, i.e. all but 1+O domain points must have ALL m roots of
the degree-m polynomial F(.,x) inside G -- i.e. F(.,x) | H_G(Z).
Each such point is m conditions on a P^m, minus 1 for the free x.
