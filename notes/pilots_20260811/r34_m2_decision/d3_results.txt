--- ORBIT ARITHMETIC: which symmetry order k can host the design ---
Setup: a cyclic symmetry of order k acting on the x-line with the
net invariant.  Its induced action tau on the slope line is a
Mobius map of the same order, so the 9 supported slopes split into
tau-orbits of size k plus at most 2 tau-FIXED slopes.  A fixed
slope's member f satisfies f(zeta x) = nu f(x), hence
f = x^j h(x^k): its NONZERO roots come in mu_k-orbits, so
u_gamma == 0 (mod k) for every fixed slope.

 rho = 7, T = 9, admissible u-profiles: 7^9 (O=0) and 7^8,6 (O=1)
 k | 9 = A*k + F, F <= 2 | fixed-slope u must be divisible by k | verdict
  k= 2: A=4, 1 fixed slope(s) with u in [6]
  k= 3: A=3, no fixed slope
  k= 4: IMPOSSIBLE (no admissible split)
  k= 5: IMPOSSIBLE (no admissible split)
  k= 6: IMPOSSIBLE (no admissible split)
  k= 7: A=1, 2 fixed slope(s) with u in [7]
  k= 8: IMPOSSIBLE (no admissible split)
  k= 9: A=1, no fixed slope
  k=16: IMPOSSIBLE (no admissible split)
  k=32: IMPOSSIBLE (no admissible split)

  => the ONLY symmetry order that can host the full 9-slope design
     is k=2 (four orbits of 2 plus one fixed slope with u=6).
     k=8 gives 8 split members from ONE splitting event, i.e.
     exactly T = rho+1 = 8 -- the strict target, ONE SLOPE SHORT of
     the failure size rho+2 = 9, and it cannot be extended: a 9th
     slope would drag its whole 8-orbit along (8*6 = 48 extra
     incidences against a budget of 64-56 = 8).
