## 1. Scope audit

At the tuple-incidence boundary put

```text
ell=floor((h-4)/7),  r=h-d=2ell+1,
sigma=d-ell-1-2r.
```

The coordinator shorthand for `(FR)` says that every selected block is a
union of complete fibers of `phi=[P:Q]`, plus at most one further point.
The shorthand is not yet a DAG proposition and does not print whether it is
intended:

1. for every active-defect target at this boundary;
2. only for the first unpaid affine dimensions `s=11,11,10`; or
3. only after removing every profile already paid by the tuple-incidence
   envelope.

This distinction is load-bearing.  A counterexample to (1) is a route cut,
but it does not falsify an official-row statement scoped as (2) or (3).

## 2. Exact block equation

For a primitive family

```text
(f_tau,g_tau)=(f_*+Q tau,g_*-P tau)
```

and a slope `lambda=[alpha:beta]`, write

```text
L_lambda=alpha Q-beta P.
```

On the active defect `D`, where `rho=P E_tau+Q E'_tau` is nonzero, a point
in the selected block necessarily has `L_lambda(x) != 0` and satisfies

```text
tau(x)=(alpha E_*(x)+beta E'_*(x))/L_lambda(x).       (BE)
```

Thus a block is a level/interpolation set for `tau`; fiber rigidity is not
formal from `L_lambda` divisibility.  The pilot first verifies `(BE)`
independently and then tests whether maximality, the tangent gate, and exact
window deficiency restore the claimed all-or-nothing fiber behavior.

## 3. Pre-registered fixture

Use the exact smooth row

```text
q=193, n=64, H=mu_64, k=4, d=13,
h=18, A=k+h=22, ell=2, r=5, sigma=0.
```

Choose

```text
P=X^2+1,  Q=X,  phi(x)=x+x^(-1).
```

Choose `D` as five inverse pairs in `H`, avoiding `+-1` and the roots of
`P`.  Hence `|D|=10=2r`, and its five complete `phi` fibers all have size
two.  Partition `D` into disjoint five-point blocks `B_+`, `B_-` so that
each has profile

```text
(2,1,1,1).
```

In particular, each block contains three points from split two-point
fibers and is not a union of complete fibers plus one point.

Construct a received pair with:

- a seventeen-point joint core (`k+d=17`);
- errors on `D` lying on two fixed projective rays, one on each block;
- `P E+Q E'=0` on `H\D`;
- deterministic pseudorandom nonzero scalar errors away from the core.

The seed is advanced only until the exact tangent and first-match checks
pass; every rejected seed and reason is counted.

## 4. Predictions

**P1 (primitive deficiency).** The stacked depth-13 window matrix has a
one-dimensional left kernel generated by

```text
(Z_D P,Z_D Q),
```

so its primitive pair is exactly `(P,Q)`, its common forced-root set is
exactly `D`, and `ell=2`.

**P2 (exact target).** The reconstructed zero codeword pair has full joint
core exactly 17, tangent ceiling exactly `A=22`, and exactly two selected
first-match exact-`A` slopes.  Their off-core blocks are `B_+` and `B_-`.

**P3 (active-defect locality).** The invariant residual has support exactly
`D`; both selected blocks are contained in `D`, are disjoint, and cover it.

**P4 (fiber-rigidity route cut).** Both selected blocks have exact
`phi`-profile `(2,1,1,1)`.  Therefore the field-independent reading (1) of
`(FR)` is false even after imposing exact maximality, the tangent gate,
first-match selection, primitive deficiency, and `sigma=0`.

**P5 (honest scope).** Even if P1-P4 pass, the fixture is not an official
prize row and has affine target dimension zero.  It does not refute an `(FR)`
statement explicitly restricted to the official unpaid dimensions or to the
post-envelope residual.  Its valid conclusion is that any useful `(FR)`
must consume an official-subgroup or high-affine/profile hypothesis absent
from the proposed THEOREM-R-style local forcing route.

## 5. Falsifiers and outcomes

- **F1:** the displayed syzygy is absent, the kernel has dimension other
  than one, or its primitive pair/common roots differ from `(P,Q,D)`.
- **F2:** a nonzero codeword or another pencil member has agreement above
  22 for every bounded seed attempted.
- **F3:** either planted exact-22 support loses the first-match selector.
- **F4:** a block point does not satisfy `(BE)`, a selected block leaks
  outside `D`, or its fiber profile is not `(2,1,1,1)`.
- **F5:** the fixture passes but already lies in a previously proved paid
  profile.  Then it is only a semantic route fence, and no critical status
  changes.

P1-P4 with F5 false would falsify the broad `(FR)` reading and require a
corrected conjecture.  P1-P4 with F5 true still kills any claim that fiber
rigidity follows from the primitive equations alone.  Failure to find a seed
is evidence only.

## 6. Computation discipline

All construction and checking is exact.  The primary program will emit a
canonical JSON certificate containing the field/domain, received words,
kernel generator, core, blocks, slopes, fiber partition, agreement maxima,
and coverage counts.  An independent checker will reconstruct every claim
from the certificate and include hostile mutations.

The algebraic construction and quick checks run under `tools/ramguard tiny`.
The all-codeword/all-slope tangent replay is the only potentially non-small
step.  It will be measured on one seed; if it is not comfortably local, a
single pre-registered Modal job with a hard five-minute and sub-dollar cap
will be logged before launch.  No broad search or unbounded enumeration is
authorized.
