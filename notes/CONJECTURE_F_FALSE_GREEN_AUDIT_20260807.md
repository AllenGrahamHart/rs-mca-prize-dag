   absolute `B_F`.

   the mixed-petal received-word/Pade family escapes both coordinate and

gaps. They therefore promoted `f_many_sparse_structure` and
`f_dim_induction`, then promoted `f_primitive_case` and `conj_f` by modus
ponens over false-green inputs.
