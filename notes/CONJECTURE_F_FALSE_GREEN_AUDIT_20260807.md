## Finding

The green chain contained three open mathematical assertions in its own source
text:

1. `f_sparse_rank_split` sends growing support-minimal weight-at-least-three
   sparse words to Face-4 configuration machinery. The conditional proof of
   `f_many_sparse_structure` treated that routing as if Face 4 had paid the
   resulting actual section points. No owner-aware aggregate payment is
   proved.
2. `f_dim_induction` records a remaining global packing step. The spread leaf
   theorem gives `binom(n,r)/binom(j,r)`, and polynomially many memoized states
   do not turn its dimension-dependent `n^r` numerator into `n^B_F` for an
   absolute `B_F`.
3. The attempted historical scope repair excluded the full-space example but
   never proved that every prize caller lies in the smaller quantitative
   dimension regime. The old two-family compiler is already REFUTED because
   the mixed-petal received-word/Pade family escapes both coordinate and
   Hankel classes.

The auto-discharge packets checked dependency labels, not these semantic
gaps. They therefore promoted `f_many_sparse_structure` and
`f_dim_induction`, then promoted `f_primitive_case` and `conj_f` by modus
ponens over false-green inputs.
