## 1. Executive summary

The sweep found **no external solution to either grand challenge**, and no external work that resolves any of the 23 open targets. What it did find is a dense July-2026 cluster of adjacent results that changes our *citation obligations*, *credit position*, and *route selection* far more than our mathematics. Eight pre-registered direct-hit hypotheses were tested adversarially; **zero survived**. Two survived as corrected-attribution findings, and the adversarial passes generated ten new proposals of their own.

The six most consequential items, in order:

1. **One genuinely actionable import exists, and it is not where anyone expected.** Mattarei's refinement of Garcia–Voloch, quoted verbatim as Lemma 5.4 of Cochrane (arXiv:2602.04111), gives `r(c) <= 3·2^(-2/3) t^(2/3) = 1.88988 t^(2/3)` for the shift-intersection count of a multiplicative subgroup, with hypotheses (`k>=4`, `t <= k^3/4`) verified to hold across the **entire** f3 official window `n^2 <= p <= 6^(n/4)`, `13 <= s <= 41`. Substituted for our in-house `51/16` on the order-`n` quotient-line factor alone, the DSP8 nodal class-blind coefficient falls from 2382.1 to 1412.4 against the live allowance 1914.975 — a **26% margin**, clearing the PROVED NSB4 barrier on the `p ≡ 1 mod 3` rows. Two named blockers: the two-coset (twisted Fermat) extension for `t ∉ H`, and a mandatory consistency check, since Mattarei's 1.88988 sits **below** our proved NSB2 floor `2^(5/3) = 3.1748`, which is only compatible if his auxiliary construction lies outside the NSB1 one-auxiliary-polynomial class. Locator: arXiv:2602.04111 Lemma 5.4 citing S. Mattarei, *Finite Fields Appl.* 13 (2007) 773–777 (accessed=yes / accessed=**no**).

2. **Headliner D is dominated by an already-merged upstream artifact.** DannyExperiments' agreement-weighted transverse-secant theorem (`rs-mca:experimental/notes/thresholds/agreement_weighted_transverse_secant.md`, merged upstream `ea4eb078`, 2026-07-12) subsumes our open PR #1106 on **both** axes: its per-witness transversality is implied by our global CF1 column-far hypothesis, and its constant beats ours by the exact symbolic ratio `(nu+h)/(h(nu+1)) ~ 1/(nu+1)`.
