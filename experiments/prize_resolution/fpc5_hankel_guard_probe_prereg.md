# FPC5 guarded Hankel probe preregistration

- **status:** route-decision experiment; not theorem evidence
- **target:** `l1_fpc5_large_source_payment`
- **launcher:** `fpc5_hankel_guard_probe_modal.py`
- **compute envelope:** 12 independent Modal workers, one CPU and 512 MB each,
  60-second hard timeout, partial records returned before an internal
  54-second deadline

## Question

After fixing the required background set, does the rational moment sequence
generated by `chi_R/P_R` create substantially more primitive core-split
locators than a random full-row-rank Hankel block with the same
`(q,N,d,ell-1)` dimensions?

## Cells

The six exact small analogues have `t in {4,5}`, `ell in {2,3,4}` and
`u>=0`. They satisfy the large-source fixed-cell arithmetic

```text
d=(t-1)ell+u,
r=2d-t ell>=0,
u<=b,
2d<=N+(t-2)ell+b,
d^2<=Nr,
J_bg=b d^2+N u^2-Nbr<=0.
```

Their fixed-background Hankel vector dimensions range from 7 to 15.

## Functionals

For every random source partition and label assignment, and for every fixed
required background set `R`, record:

```text
Z_split(R)       core divisors in the rational Hankel kernel,
Z_primitive(R)   those passing every punctured moment,
Z_guarded(R)     those also avoiding the untouched-petal label,
Z_random(R)      primitive core divisors in an independently sampled
                 random full-rank Hankel kernel,
mean_ambient  binom(N,d)/q^(ell-1).
```

The union of the three rational counts over `R` is retained as an aggregation
diagnostic, but it is not compared to one random chart and does not drive a
decision.

The primary normalized functionals are

```text
E_actual(R)=Z_primitive(R)/(1+mean_ambient),
E_guard(R)=Z_guarded(R)/(1+mean_ambient),
E_random(R)=Z_random(R)/(1+mean_ambient).
```

## Decisions

- **Rational-excess alarm:** the fixed-chart `max E_actual` exceeds
  `16*max E_random` in at least two cells, or the ratio grows monotonically
  by a factor above four across `ell=2,3,4` at fixed `t`.
- **Guard-suppression signal:** the median fixed-chart
  `Z_guarded(R)/Z_primitive(R)` is below `1/4` in at least two cells.
- **No-separation result:** neither event occurs. This would support
  attacking the generic guarded Hankel incidence but would prove no
  flatness theorem.

Every completed configuration is retained. A timeout returns the completed
prefix and is reported as partial, never as a zero count.
