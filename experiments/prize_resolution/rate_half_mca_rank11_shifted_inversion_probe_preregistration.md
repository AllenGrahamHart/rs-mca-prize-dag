# Shifted-inversion probe preregistration

## Question

Attempt to falsify the candidate inequality

```text
I(tau,kappa)<8740
```

for nonzero shifted-inversion parameters on the official subgroup. This is
an evidence probe only; survival cannot prove the inequality.

## Fixed design

- field `p=2130706433`;
- subgroup `H=mu_(2^21)`, generated by `3^1016`;
- `96` shift classes `tau=3^j`, `0<=j<96`;
- `64` distinct `kappa` values per shift;
- first 32 `kappa` values from pinned SplitMix64 streams;
- final 32 obtained as `(x+tau)(y+tau)` for pinned distinct `x,y in H`,
  so the tested graph is conditioned to contain a planted pair;
- exact bitset membership in production;
- exponent-membership recount of the maximizing parameter in every shard;
- fixed points removed before comparison with `8740`.

The primary endpoint is the maximum nonfixed graph-point count over all
`6144` planned parameters. A count at least `8740` falsifies the candidate
cap immediately. Counts below it are heuristic evidence only.

## Resource and failure contract

One Modal container handles one shift class with one CPU, 768 MiB RAM, and a
hard 60-second timeout. At most 96 containers run. The dispatcher records all
completed rows and every failed or timed-out shard, and writes an INCOMPLETE
result rather than discarding partial output. No local census is permitted.

## Nonclaims

- no exhaustive search over either parameter;
- no statistical confidence interval;
- no payment of antipodal, constant-product, nonquadratic, or high-complexity
  branches;
- no proof of rank eleven or MCA.
