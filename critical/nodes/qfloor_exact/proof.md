# proof: qfloor_exact

This is `prop:qfloor` in the pinned upstream source
`tex/slackMCA_v4.tex`. For two distinct characteristic-zero quotient classes,
the difference of their `e_1` values is a nonzero cyclotomic integer. Every
archimedean conjugate has modulus at most `2 ell'`, so its rational norm is a
nonzero integer of absolute value at most `(2 ell')^(N'/2) < p`. It therefore
cannot vanish modulo a degree-one prime above `p`. Thus reduction preserves
the exact characteristic-zero value-set size `Acl(N',ell')`. The locator
identity turns each value into a bad slope at the displayed canonical-line
radius.

The source scope is load-bearing: the proof uses `p = 1 mod n`, a prime field,
the displayed quotient order, and the strict norm threshold.
