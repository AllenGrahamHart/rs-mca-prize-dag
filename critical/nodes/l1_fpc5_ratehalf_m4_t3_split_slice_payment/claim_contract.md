# Claim contract

- **Input:** one fixed exact Johnson-nonpositive rate-half `M=4,t=3` LS6 atom.
- **Output:** a polynomial/profile payment uniform over the official source,
  triple, defect, and determined-cross-ratio parameters.
- **Outer composition:** at most four anchors plus fewer than `4n` fixed
  atoms, already proved by first-layout atom collapse.
- **Must preserve:** the fixed source, triple, `a`, determined `lambda`, split
  divisor, and multiplicity. The exactness guard may be forgotten only via a
  proved injective enlargement, not by replacing split points with the whole
  ambient linear slice.
- **Proved enlargement:** each nonempty atom injects into one unpunctured,
  gcd-trivial, sub-balance full-domain split flat with `2r<j`.
- **Proved exclusions:** `deg Etilde<a` and all common-pencil source data,
  aligned or misaligned, give empty atoms.
- **Proved low-degree adapter:** `a<=deg Etilde<=ell-a` is an exact prefix
  ladder with effective average depth `ell+a-1`.
- **Proved high-degree adapter:** `deg Etilde>ell-a` is an exact guarded Pade
  quotient cell with `D=quo_E(MQ)`, `V=-rem_E(MQ)`, `gcd(D,Q)=1`, and
  `deg(E^(-1) mod M)>=ell+a`.
- **Proved source gate:** every nonempty atom has
  `deg rem_(L_3)(L_1L_2^(-1))>=a`; the complementary short-syzygy stratum is
  empty.
- **Nonclaim:** each fiber has size at most two; linear dimension implies a
  split-divisor count; an unguarded prefix-flatness theorem applies; `lambda`
  remains free after fixing the source triple; current Conjecture-F owners
  automatically preserve FPC5 first ownership.
