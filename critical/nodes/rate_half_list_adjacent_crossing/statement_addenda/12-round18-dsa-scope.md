# Round-18 DSA scope addendum

THEOREM DSA (`crossing_dsa_refutation`, witness verified at `n=2^41`)
proves that non-structural window members exist at admissible tower rows when
`p^delta_a < 2^(L-2)`; this occurs for 10 of 19 admissible pairs at
`w=2^34`. Consequently, the `(ES)` route to the count bound is dead at those
rows. The gamma-shell population of the accidents, and hence the budget
question there, is reopened rather than decided.

At `e=1` prime rows DSA cannot apply: `B*>=3` forces
`log2(p)>=129.585>126`. The ternary repricing there has a heuristic 53--61
bit margin. Whether tower rows belong to the official family remains a
maintainer scope question. `es_ternary_suppression_instruments` gives
unconditional positive coverage at `w>2^37.3131` for 256-bit `p`.
