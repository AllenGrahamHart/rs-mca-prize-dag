# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every actual prize consumer of Conjecture F has one typed,
  multiplicity-preserving flat descriptor accepted by the packing/payment
  ledger.
- **Strict callers:** `imgfib` and `spi_point_counting`.
- **Required families:** every LIST family actually charged to `conj_f`, and
  the Hankel/split-pencil slope fibers.
- **Required data:** domain, `j`, `r`, root threshold, punctures, and first
  owner.
- **Nonclaim:** the full Pade section is linear; every proper flat is
  packable; a support-level chart is an actual section-point transport; the
  current two-caller inventory remains exhaustive after a new strict edge.
