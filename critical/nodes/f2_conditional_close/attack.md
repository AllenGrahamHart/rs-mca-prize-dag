# Attack surface

## Generating rows

When `k=ord_n(p)=e`, the proved direct-sum theorem reduces each open moving
window to one explicit prime-field `[S,S-R,R+1]_p` GRS kernel and
`Z(L)=Z_1^C`, `C<=4`. The exact target is the deployed ternary-mass bound
`Z_1<=2^o(S)` with the finite tolerance printed by the consumer. A dimension
bound alone is not sufficient.

## Non-generating rows

When `k<e`, `(O1)` is false by `2^Theta(n)`. Do not attempt to repair it by
assuming generation. Prove the final extras budget by a different count, or
rigorously route these rows through the existing extension-field descent and
show that its hypotheses and constants cover every such row.

## Seams

- Freeze the PP5.0 average-to-sum normalization. `log2|K1|=n/2` exactly;
  it cannot be hidden in `o(n)`.
- Any use of the Frobenius antipodal-descent identity must handle a domain
  coset representative outside the intermediate subfield. The K1 kernel
  itself is coset-invariant, so avoid this seam when possible.
- Growing-order Myerson remains evidence on matching sectors, not an
  all-row premise.

The preferred next attack is the explicit GRS ternary mass on generating
rows, because it is a classical single-code statement with no false
generation premise. In parallel, seek a direct formulation of the
non-generating extras count before proposing any conditional assembly.
