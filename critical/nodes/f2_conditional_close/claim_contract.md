# Claim contract

- **claim:** the final F2 extras-plus-trades budget on all official
  prize-max admissible rows
- **status:** direct TARGET; no conditional proof is currently accepted
- **proved suppliers:** empty and edge bands, full-ladder dictionary,
  contraction, near tails, structural census, and the admissible direct-sum
  reduction
- **open surfaces:** generating-row GRS ternary mass, non-generating direct
  payment, PP5.0 normalization, and coset-sensitive descent
- **nonclaim:** `(O1)` is not assumed on non-generating rows; growing-order
  Myerson is not claimed sufficient
- **falsifier:** an official admissible row with more than `n^3` extras and
  trades
