# Review protocol

1. Pin the campaign and upstream commits and active row manifest.
2. Replay every terminal verifier and independent audit in the composed
   K3 sub-DAG.
3. Check the active bad-slope-to-component bridge and its exact fibers before
   reviewing any component workboard as a slope payment.
4. Check route, source-line, source-cover, trivial-stabilizer, and orientation
   exhaustiveness directly from the defining objects.
5. Check every transport preserves received-line owner, witness support,
   affine slope, and bridge multiplicity.
6. Review any nonzero pencil/ray payment for genuine-pencil hypotheses and
   exact line multiplicities.
7. Check that `U_K3_allocation` is not the jointly owned unpaid reserve unless
   the sibling `U_Q` and `U_new` cells are proved zero.
8. Record objections and their resolution. Promote only with no unresolved
   load-bearing objection.
