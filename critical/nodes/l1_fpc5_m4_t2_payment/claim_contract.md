# Claim contract

- **Input:** first-owned `M=4,t=2` full-petal FPC5 contributors at rates
  `1/2` and `1/4`.
- **Petal envelope:** primitive degree-`<=s` cofactor pairs in the
  `2s+2`-dimensional petal-equation locator slice.
- **Exact-cell guards:** background roots of `W`, exact nonagreements, and
  first ownership remain binding.
- **Official codimension:** at least `2` at rate `1/2` and `(k+4)/5` at
  rate `1/4`.
- **Sharp boundary reduction:** the apparent rate-half codimension-two cell
  has a background-guarded locator slice of dimension and codimension
  `ell-1`.
- **Output:** an aggregate polynomial/profile payment of the guarded split
  locators.
- **Nonclaim:** positive codimension alone gives an absolute-exponent split
  count; a per-source count composes without source-multiplicity control.
