- **status:** TARGET
- **closure:** open

support-wise first-match priority map. In each primitive residual full-prefix
fiber choose a base support `S0`. Prove that every other support `S` maps,
with multiplicity at most one, from its canonical order-`t` star record

```text
(S\S0,S0\S)
```

to exactly one record counted by `u1_x4_direct_column_budget`, without
changing any quotient, dihedral, moment-trade, U2-boundary, or DLI/skew owner.
Then

```text
|F_z^prim|-1 <= R_post.
```

the star-PTE lemma produces general order-`t` records with `t<h`. The theorem
must justify this minimalization step or broaden the `u1` ledger and re-prove
its bound.
