# Claim contract

- **Input:** two coprime degree-`ell` petal locators, two distinct labels, and
  `d=ell+s<2ell`.
- **Output:** an exact linear bijection between solutions of the two petal
  equations and two degree-`<=s` cofactors; every relevant contributor maps
  injectively into this envelope.
- **Exact dimensions:** `2s+2` for the pair/locator slice and
  `ell-s-1` locator codimension.
- **Primitive guard:** under petal disjointness,
  `gcd(F,W)=1 iff gcd(A_1,A_2)=1`.
- **Retained PMA guard:** a fixed cell also requires `W=0` on its background
  agreement set, exact nonagreement conditions, and first ownership.
- **Nonclaim:** a polynomial split-locator count, a complete fixed-cell
  parametrization, first-owner aggregation, or payment of the `M=4,t=2`
  branch.
