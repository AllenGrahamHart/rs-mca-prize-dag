# Claim contract

## Claim

For exact `(k+t)`-supports, the joint top-coefficient map at exchange distance
`d` has rank `t+min(d,t)`. Consequently the fixed-slope support indicators
have the exact pair probability and restricted-family factorial moment in
`statement.md`.

## Dependencies

- distinct evaluation points;
- `1<=k<k+t<=n`;
- uniform independent words over the ambient slope field.

## Nonclaims

- no worst-case exchange-rigidity or inverse theorem;
- no cross-slope independence;
- no paid-family ownership or official-row instantiation;
- no generated-field transfer.

## Falsifier

An admissible field/support pair whose combined interpolation map has rank
different from `t+min(d,t)`, or whose exact fixed-slope pair count differs
from `alpha_d(1-2q^(-t)+alpha_d)`.
