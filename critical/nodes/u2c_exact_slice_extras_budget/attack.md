# Attack surface

The target is a fixed-weight maximum-prefix statement for one structured
column, not the final primitive residue and not a raw null-fiber theorem.

1. Preserve the exact `x4` first-match order and define the actual supports
   assigned to the moment/null pullback owner.
2. Enumerate each admitted generator family and its allowed combinations.
   Count expanded supports, not just blocks or maps.
3. Prove parameter-image coalescing and charge any multiplicity explicitly.
4. Prove strip-aware maximum-prefix transport. It may send a heavy structured
   prefix to a null/p-free census, but it must preserve weight and first owner.
5. Treat the full-ladder dictionary, complement involution, empty edge bands,
   and near-tail widths `15,14,13,12` as generator-route inputs only until the
   expansion and transport steps are proved.
6. Preserve the fixed Hamming weight. The proved
   `x4_fixed_slice_pfree_fullcube_route_cut` shows that the unweighted Boolean
   syndrome map necessarily has a fiber of size at least `2^129>N^3`.
7. If the count exceeds `N^3`, exhibit the official structured family and
   re-run the list-side summed budget rather than weakening the falsifier.

The guarded `(C)`-depth F2 mass problem and historical all-`t`-null target
remain useful background mathematics, but neither counts `max_z |M_z|` by
itself. Primitive local shift-pair control belongs to
`x4_primitive_star_u1_coverage` and `u1_x4_direct_column_budget`.
