# ImgFib Conjecture-F consumer descriptor

- **status:** TARGET
- **consumer:** `f_prize_consumer_flat_scope`

Replace the prose-level `conj_f` charge in the `imgfib` assembly by an
exhaustive map from every LIST branch that actually invokes Conjecture F to
an exact linear projective split-locator section. For each branch print

```text
(received-word branch, domain after canonical punctures,
 locator degree j, projective dimension r, exact root threshold,
 first paid owner, section-point/codeword multiplicity).
```

The inventory must distinguish the independently open mixed-petal payment
from the Conjecture-F calls inside possible routes to it. In particular, the
full-locator Pade section is generally polynomial, not linear. The proved
`l1_rootfree_rational_q_projective_packing` interface may be used only after
its canonical planted-root descent: it emits the exact punctured projective
cell

```text
H'=H\roots(D),  j=m-deg(D),  r=k-deg(D),
V=span(G,W_1 F[X]_<r),
```

with its first owner and no split point at infinity. The proved coordinate
MDS branch may likewise be reused. Prove that these and any additional named
families exhaust the actual `imgfib` Conjecture-F charge without support
overcount or owner duplication.

## Falsifier

An `imgfib` branch charged to `conj_f` with no exact linear descriptor; a
full Pade polynomial section treated as a flat; a puncture pooled across
different canonical owners; or a flat-section count that is not in bijection
with the codewords charged by the branch.
