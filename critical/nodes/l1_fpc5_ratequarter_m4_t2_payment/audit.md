# Audit

1. The strict inequality is load-bearing: `2ell>k-1`, not `>=k-1`.
2. The uniqueness count is over unordered touched pairs, giving six.
3. No defect, background-support, or cofactor multiplicity survives once the
   two full petals are fixed; all such data belong to the same polynomial.
4. The extra four in the global bound is deliberately conservative. A first
   layout anchor normally has the planted one-petal profile, but the general
   composition theorem is consumed exactly as stated.
5. The result does not use random splitting, generated-field size, or a
   quotient-profile assumption.
