# Claim contract

- **Scope:** official rate `1/4`, maximal source size `M=4`, exactly two
  touched full petals.
- **Fixed-layout payment:** at most one non-planted contributor per unordered
  touched pair, hence at most six.
- **Global composition:** first-layout domination adds at most four anchors;
  no source-layout multiplier remains.
- **Output:** absolute class bound ten, which is a polynomial payment.
- **Nonclaim:** any rate-half payment or any internal rechart count there.
