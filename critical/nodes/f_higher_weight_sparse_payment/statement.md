# Higher-weight sparse Face-4 payment

- **status:** TARGET
- **consumer:** `f_many_sparse_structure`

Let `P` be a gcd-trivial locator flat in the proper-flat regime of Conjecture
F. Apply `f_sparse_rank_split` and remove the proved bounded-rank and abundant
weight-two branches. The remaining sparse-dual words have support-minimal
weight at least three and growing span. The proved syzygy-flat dictionary
routes this branch to the Face-4 configuration object.

Prove that every such routed branch has one canonical outcome:

1. a tangent/common-divisor, multiplicative-pullback, or
   dihedral/Chebyshev first owner; or
2. a strict sparse descent whose complete first-owner descendants have total
   unpaid contribution at most `n^B_hw` for one absolute exponent `B_hw`.

The bound must count actual section points after ownership. Merely naming a
Face-4 chart, a syzygy, or a local circuit is not a payment.

## Falsifier

A family of proper gcd-trivial flats with growing higher-weight sparse span
whose routed Face-4 objects avoid all paid owners and produce more than every
fixed power of `n` distinct unpaid section points or first-owner descendants.
