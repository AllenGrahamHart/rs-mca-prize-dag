# Biquadratic source-cover payment

Compile the non-lifting diagonal branch for both exact passports:

```text
genus 0: eta, eta', mu
genus 1: eta, eta, eta', eta'
```

and for every live source-mixing row. Each terminal must be proved empty,
transported by a same-owner bijection to a paid active image, or paid in
distinct affine slopes with exact bridge multiplicity. Print the disjoint
total `U_source_cover`.

## Falsifier

An uncompiled passport/row, a transport that loses source or slope identity,
or an unpaid surviving component.
