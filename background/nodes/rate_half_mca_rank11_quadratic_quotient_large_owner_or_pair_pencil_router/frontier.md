# Frontier

The quotient-only compatibility problem has become two payment problems:

1. prove upstream `prob:large-owner` for a KoalaBear owner of size at least
   1,183,521 carrying the complete residual mass;
2. classify and charge at least 520 pair types in one rank-two `F(X)` pencil.

The first should reuse upstream owner-image/fiber notation. The second should
be expressed as the base-field-normalized split-pencil census, with degree and
denominator data extracted from the pair vectors before any numerical search.
