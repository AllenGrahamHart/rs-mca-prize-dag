# Claim contract

- **Proposition:** the strict endpoint kernel biform has exactly one small
  `F_q(X))-irreducible factor, of parameter degree at least
  `ceil((3m+1)/4)`.
- **Quantifier:** every `m>=1` saturation-failure profile satisfying
  `(KFD1)` and the two proved kernel nonvanishing statements.
- **Dependencies:** endpoint saturation rigidity and the rational-normal
  kernel curve.
- **Falsifier:** a valid endpoint biform whose irreducible factor degrees
  violate `(KFD4)`, have zero or at least two small factors, or have
  maximal parameter degree below `ceil((3m+1)/4)`.
- **Nonclaim:** no incompatibility theorem for the surviving dominant factor.
