# Proof

The multiplicative group of `F_211` is cyclic of order `210`, generated by
`2`. The map `z -> z^7` has a kernel of size seven, and every
`2^(35i)` is a seventh power because its exponent is divisible by seven.
The four chosen values therefore have four disjoint seven-element
preimages, giving `|X|=28`.

Similarly, `z -> z^5` has a kernel of size five, and every chosen value is
a fifth power because `35i` is divisible by five. Its four preimages have
twenty elements. Adjoining zero gives `|Gamma|=21`. This proves `(DLF2)`.

Fix `x in X` and put `c=x^7 in C`. The roots in `Gamma` of

```text
G(t,x)=t^5-c
```

are exactly the five fifth roots of `c`; zero is not one of them. Thus every
row has the required exact degree and five distinct roots.

Now fix a nonzero `delta in Gamma` and put `c=delta^5 in C`. Its fiber is

```text
G(delta,X)=c-X^7=-(X^7-c).
```

The seven roots of `X^7-c` are precisely the corresponding part of `X`.
At `delta=0`, the fiber is `-X^7`, which has no root in the nonzero set
`X`. Therefore the twenty nonzero columns have incidence degree seven and
the zero column has incidence degree zero. With fiber degree `n=7`, this is
exactly `(DLF4)`, and `(DLF5)--(DLF6)` follow.

Every residual block in `(DLF5)` is nonzero. The all-excess parameter-MDS
equivalence now puts its coefficient vector in `ker K_all`. The primary
replay also constructs the `120 x 28` matrix directly and obtains rank
`27`; hence this kernel spans the full nullspace. QED.
