# Audit

- `P_*` records distinct supported roots of one row. Its divisibility into
  `D` uses positive residual rank loss at each root, not squarefreeness of
  the full adjugate factor.
- The Forney divisibility uses a squarefree row radical, so distinct zero
  evaluations suffice.
- Contact zeros at heavy incidences are counted only once. Any higher local
  multiplicity is retained in the residual effective divisor `E_u`.
- `Z_c` includes repeated supported roots and unsupported vertical roots
  with their residual multiplicities; no separability assumption is made.
- The divisor in `(SBN5)` is signed in five packets. It is not silently
  replaced by an effective divisor.
- Nonvanishing of the row-specialized Forney numerator uses the contact
  degree on every mixed component, as in the two-point normal form.
