# Audit

1. The field is generated by projective coordinate ratios, so basis scaling
   does not change the stratum.
2. The tuple includes `P`, `Q`, and `[g]`; stratifying only the factor point
   would miss a non-rational pencil.
3. The possible degrees are divisors of the relative extension degree six,
   not arbitrary integers from zero through six.
4. The mass partition is by first-owned factors. No slope is counted in two
   field strata.
5. The fixed-factor cap is spent once per projective factor, giving 11 rather
   than incorrectly dividing by the original 41-factor floor.
6. The symbolic packet refutes only an algebraic inference. It is not called
   an MCA counterexample and supplies no received word or ownership ledger.
7. The countermodel uses Frobenius stability, not the presence of a
   non-base coefficient in one chosen basis, as the descent obstruction.
8. The base-field normalization guard remains active: no extension stratum
   is priced with the ambient field without a transfer theorem.
