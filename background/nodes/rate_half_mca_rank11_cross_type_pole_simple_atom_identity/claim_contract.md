# Claim contract

- **Shared deck:** `r>=16` distinct exact supports after one common
  shortening.
- **Type multiplicity:** at least three supports from each of two distinct
  saturated pair types.
- **Certificate hypothesis:** both scalar-locator certificates are
  pole-simple on the shared supports.
- **Conclusion:** the certificates are projectively identical.
- **Exact threshold:** the independent-scalar incidence margin is negative
  at `r=15` and positive at `r=16`.
- **Not claimed:** deck existence, a canonical atom across a whole family,
  owner payment, or MCA closure.
