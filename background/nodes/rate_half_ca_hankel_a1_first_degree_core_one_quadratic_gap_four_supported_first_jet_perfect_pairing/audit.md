# Audit

- The exact `D_1` factorization is essential. The older lower bound
  `c_gamma<=ord_gamma(D_1)` would not force unit Smith exponents.
- The square symmetric pencil has left and right specialized kernels of
  dimension `c+1`; this is not the rectangular cubic calculation, whose
  left kernel has dimension `c`.
- A root shared by a supported factor and `S_B` or `S_1S_2` is deliberately
  excluded. Shared roots are not silently counted as transverse.
- The exception bounds are projective and count distinct slopes; divisor
  multiplicity can only lower the number of distinct exceptions.
- The theorem identifies a local form and radical. It neither proves that
  the correction divisor is unsupported nor excludes a packet.
