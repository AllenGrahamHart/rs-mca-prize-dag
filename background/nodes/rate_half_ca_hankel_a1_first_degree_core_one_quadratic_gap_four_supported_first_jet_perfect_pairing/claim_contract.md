- **Claim:** away from the exact correction divisor, every supported
  residual rank-loss slope has only unit positive Smith exponents, and its
  symmetric Hankel derivative form has radical exactly the actual excess
  locator.
- **Currency:** local Smith valuation, specialized Vandermonde-source rank,
  and the exact correction-factor identity for `D_1`.
- **Dependencies used:** the quadratic root normal forms, the contracted
  supported-slope kernel description, and the Pade regular-factor identity.
- **Status movement:** none; this adds a proved local constraint to the live
  quadratic packet.
- **Falsifier:** a correction-free supported slope with
  `ord(D_1)>c_gamma`, a nonunit positive Smith exponent, or a derivative
  radical larger than `span{R_gamma}`.
- **Nonclaims:** no assertion at correction-supported overlaps and no root-
  arm or packet exclusion.
