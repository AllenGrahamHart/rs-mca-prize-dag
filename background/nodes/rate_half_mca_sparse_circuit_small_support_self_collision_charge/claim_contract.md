# Claim contract

- Exact supports: `c=2,3,4,5`.
- Exact defects: `0<=s<=q`.
- Empty convention: `M_c=0` iff the circuit stratum is empty.
- Positive intersection: `12-2c`.
- Outside budget: `s+c-1`.
- Outside circuit stratum: divide by the exact number `j` of external
  deletion exposures.
- Incidence multiplier: `C(m-c,11-c)`.
