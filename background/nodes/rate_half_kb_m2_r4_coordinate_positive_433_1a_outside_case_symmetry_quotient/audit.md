# Audit

1. The count is per fixed common row and fixed cycle sign.  No common-cell,
   duplicate-role, root-sign, or cycle-sign quotient is silently composed.
2. Aligned cases require `eta=xi` as a labeled record.  Near-aligned cases
   require distinct records, giving `75+450=525`, not `5*7*15` with invalid
   placement semantics.
3. The order-four vertex-sign stabilizer has a global-sign kernel; the
   faithful record action has order two.
4. Perfect matching pairs and the three deck-pair slots are already
   unordered.  No extra factor of `2^3*3!` is present.
5. The 267 representatives are formal necessary cases.  The computation
   does not test common equations, resultants, sums, guards, or
   realizability.
6. Templates A and B are one record-gauge orbit only after allowing a
   permutation of the three residual source deck pairs.  Their displayed
   single-row sum cuts remain different orientations of that orbit.
7. Template coverage counts all permitted `eta` locations because the
   target-elimination identities themselves do not assume an `eta`
   location.
