# Audit

- Equality `O=sum c_gamma` is derived from `O=Delta` and the upper regular
  determinant budget. It is not assumed fibrewise.
- Formula `o_gamma=c_gamma-t_gamma` counts a new distinct domain root once,
  regardless of its excess multiplicity; repeated minimal roots produce
  omission with full excess multiplicity.
- The local congruence uses intersection lengths, not transversality.
- Unsupported vertical roots contribute multiples of three by the same cube
  identity with zero horizontal multiplicity.
- The single unallocated determinant order is treated in both locations:
  on the deficit-one row it overshoots, and elsewhere it leaves the modular
  contradiction unchanged.
