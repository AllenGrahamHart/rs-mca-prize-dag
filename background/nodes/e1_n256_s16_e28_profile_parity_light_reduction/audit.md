# Audit

The primary packet recomputes the slack dynamic program, all 14 energy
profiles, the exact rational cubic boundary, every normalized light-support
signature, the six zero-odd affine representatives, and both diameter ledgers.
It pins the already-proved two-, four-, and six-odd atlases and uses only the
four-odd one in the final router.

The deterministic verifier independently rederives the profiles and cubic
forms, expands all six antipodal-pair orbits, checks the pinned 148-orbit
four-odd atlas, and confirms that only those 154 templates are relevant.
The audit uses a recursive profile enumerator, a separately solved Hermite
basis, and direct matching enumeration.

All replay is verifier-scale; no large census is rerun locally.
