# Audit

1. The scalar product formula applies only when `d_x=m`. Deficient points
   carry quotient polynomials of degree at most `Delta_x`.
2. The exact unknown count is `|W|+Delta_W`, not always `|W|`.
3. In the important `O=0` stratum the correction is at most one column, but
   it is load-bearing for an exact rank argument.
4. The row count `(m+2)(4m+1)` alone proves nothing about rank. The rows may
   have structured dependencies.
5. A nonzero kernel is only a necessary relaxation. A genuine pencil gives
   the stronger blockwise-nonzero kernel vector printed in the statement.
6. Projective fibre slopes need no special case: `L_x(Y)` is retained as a
   nonzero linear polynomial or constant, including the slope at infinity.
7. Both verifiers reconstruct the coefficient matrix from polynomial
   multiplication and compare it with direct apolar moment coefficients.
