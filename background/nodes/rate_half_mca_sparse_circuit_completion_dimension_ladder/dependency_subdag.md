  + rank-ten eleven-set

  -> dim H_A=11-c
  -> generalized MDS gives at most q=K-10 completions              [PROVED]

  -> private coordinates span the q-dimensional quotient
  -> every sparse circuit lies in one carrier of size <=q+4        [PROVED]

no A has q completions
  -> at most q-1 completions per deletion
  -> full-rank exclusion plus exact deletion count                 [PROVED]

maximum of both branches
  -> support-stratified sparse-circuit cap                          [PROVED]
