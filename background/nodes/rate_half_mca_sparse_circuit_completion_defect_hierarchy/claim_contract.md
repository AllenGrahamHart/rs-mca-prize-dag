# Claim contract

- **Claim:** completion defect `s` forces the carrier `(DC)` whenever the
  exact Vandermonde budget `(DH)` holds.
- **Inputs:** only the parent completion labels, their private coordinates,
  and independence of at most `K` evaluations.
- **Incidence cap:** all carrier branches are retained, together with the
  exact maximum over the final deletion-count branch.
- **Boundary:** support five receives no improvement.
- **No geometry import:** no secant-variety classification is used.
