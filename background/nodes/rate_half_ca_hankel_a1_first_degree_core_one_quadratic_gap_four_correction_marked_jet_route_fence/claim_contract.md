# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the correction factors force marked orders eight and seven,
  and those orders are realizable by abstract symmetric affine pencils with
  the same generic singular-block profile.
- **Inputs:** the Pade regular-factor identity and marked determinant gate.
- **Outputs:** `(MJF1)--(MJF6)` and the route fence.
- **Falsifier:** a wrong exponent in `(MJF1)--(MJF2)`, failure of
  `L_eta q=0`, or a marked determinant different from `(MJF5)--(MJF6)`.
- **Nonclaim:** the displayed countermodels are Hankel, split-locator, or
  received-word realizations.
