# Audit

1. The new exponents are `2+2*3=8` and `1+2*3=7`.
2. The countermodel is symmetric and affine in `z`; no quadratic matrix
   entry is hidden in the order-two regular factor.
3. The singular block has one primitive polynomial kernel vector and
   generic corank one.
4. The marking is constant; its pairing with the polynomial kernel is the
   coordinate `z^3`.
5. The countermodel deliberately does not claim the Hankel, source,
   splitting, or column-far conditions.
