# Audit

1. Inversion invariance is imposed on the squared-root set, not inferred
   from membership in `mu_1024`.
2. Anti-reciprocity uses both degree nine and constant term `-1`; the sign is
   negative.
3. Divisions by `2` and `8` are charged to the exceptional-characteristic
   ledger.
4. The resultant is a necessary coverage router. Every factor is handled;
   the repeated linear factors yield two `z` branches each.
5. On cubic factors, solving the linear equation requires the coefficient
   resultant. Its prime support is included before the divisibility
   obstruction is used.
6. Quotient powering is exact and reduces after every multiplication. A
   gcd of branch resultants, not a sampled remainder coefficient, supplies
   the characteristic obstruction.
7. The primary checker reconstructs the SymPy calculation. The audit
   checker independently uses rational coefficient arrays, binary powering,
   Sylvester determinants, and Bareiss elimination.
8. Gcd one does not classify characteristics where a parameter denominator
   vanishes; those are exactly the printed four small primes.
9. This theorem removes only the inversion-invariant component. Promoting
   `dli_wcl_slot_4_9_emptiness` from it would be invalid.
