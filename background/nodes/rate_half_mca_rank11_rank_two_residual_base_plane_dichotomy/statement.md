# Rank-eleven rank-two residual base/plane dichotomy

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** the shortened `2 x 3` rank-two-triple bucket

After deleting the rank-two coordinate triple, let `H` be the resulting
three-dimensional residual base, on an anchor-good universe of size at most

```text
U=1116045.
```

Every first-owned residual class has dimension two or three and at least
`b=37733` common-zero coordinates. The complete bucket has mass at least

```text
M=388650911452.
```

Let `Z_0` be the common-zero set of all of `H` and put `q=|Z_0|`. Then one
of the following holds.

1. **Active common residual base.** `q>=10001`. The complete mass `M`
   lies in corrections vanishing on `Z_0` and reversibly shortens again by
   its squarefree locator.
2. **Heavy rank-four residual plane.** `q<=10000`, every residual class is
   a two-plane, there are at most

   ```text
   max_(0<=q<=10000) floor((U-q)/(b-q))=39             (BP1)
   ```

   distinct classes, and one class has first-owned mass at least

   ```text
   ceil(M/39)=9965407986.                              (BP2)
   ```

   Its correction span has dimension exactly four and its common-zero set
   contains at least `27733` coordinates outside `Z_0` in one projective
   clone class of the evaluation code of `H`.

## Nonclaim

Neither output is paid. The heavy plane is not identified with an upstream
split pencil, and separate rank-two triples are not summed.
