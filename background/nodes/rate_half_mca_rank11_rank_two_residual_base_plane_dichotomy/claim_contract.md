- A `10001`-coordinate common base of the residual three-space shortens the
  complete rank-two bucket and preserves all retained mass.
- Without that base, all residual classes are planes and there are at most
  39 of them.
- One plane has mass at least `9965407986`, correction rank exactly four,
  and at least 27,733 nonbase projectively proportional evaluation columns.

- Residual classes retain the parent first-match partition and the selected
  37,733-root sets after triple cancellation.
- `Z_0` is taken inside the shortened anchor-good universe.
- The rank-three cap is applied to one complete plane class, not separately
  to its slopes.
