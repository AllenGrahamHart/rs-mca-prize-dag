# Audit

The polynomial `F` is necessary only in the quadratic chamber with exactly
two antipodal repeated colors. It must not be imposed on collision-free or
one-repeat packets.

The denominator `D` is proved nonzero from the collision equation before it
is divided out. The inherited saturations also remove `d=0` and `d=-1`.

The gcds must be computed over a field containing all eighth roots, not by
treating the non-real colors as formal integers in `F_p`. A common root is a
candidate only; the colored assignment, full cyclotomic divisibility, and
inner lift remain necessary.

No local or current-account Modal execution is authorized while the Modal
workspace spend limit remains active.
