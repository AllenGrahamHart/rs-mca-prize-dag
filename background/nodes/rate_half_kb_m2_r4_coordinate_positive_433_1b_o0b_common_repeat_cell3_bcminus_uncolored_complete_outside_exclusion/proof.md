# Proof

The three record-owned leaves exclude all 120 representatives with missing
`DE+`, all 120 with missing `DF+`, and all 120 with missing `EF`. Their label
sets are disjoint. The outside-label involution bijects `DE+` with `DE-` and
`DF+` with `DF-`, while fixing `EF`. Therefore these leaves cover

```text
120+120 + 120+120 + 120 = 600
```

formal systems. Generic and exceptional fibers are exhaustive in each leaf,
so no uncolored system remains. QED.
