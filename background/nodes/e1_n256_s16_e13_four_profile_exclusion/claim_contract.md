# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the complete E13 four-profile/111-template router;
- the proved proper-conductor collision exclusion;
- the collision norm criterion and pair-feasible prime threshold;
- the proved pair-feasible prime-field reduction.

## Output

No profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate at `V=26` collides on the pair-feasible branch.

## Nonclaims

No lower variance, profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform pair allowance,
row, or prize terminal is closed here. Exactly 112 whole norms and four odd
parts reach `2^250`; exact compositeness of the two distinct odd exceptions is
the replacement certificate.

## Falsifier

A missed vector, engine disagreement, conductor leak, nonpositive resultant,
a fifth odd part at least `2^250`, an exceptional odd part at least `2^251`, a
prime exceptional odd part, or a field/normalization mismatch refutes the
result.
