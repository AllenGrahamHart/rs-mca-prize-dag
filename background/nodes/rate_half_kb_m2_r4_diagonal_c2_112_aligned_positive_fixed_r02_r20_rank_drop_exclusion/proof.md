# Proof

The quadratic compression theorem gives `R=U^2-VZ`. A common root on `V=0`
must lie in the original four-row q-slice system and on the zero locus of
`V`. Exact factorization over `QQ`, before reduction to the deployed prime,
separates factors already inverted by the original named open from exactly
two retained factors.

For each of the sixteen literal factor branches, the verifier compiles the
four original rows and selected factor over `F_2130706433`, computes a
`singular:slimgb` basis, and reduces the square-free product of the original
named factors. The degree-2 branches have bases of size 8 or 10. The large
branches have sizes 254/255 on `R02` and 287/289 on `R20`. In every case the
second localizer power reduces to zero. The localized ideal is therefore the
unit ideal.

The two retained factors cover `V=0` on the named open. Since localized
emptiness is geometric over the base-field closure, it remains empty over
the challenge extension. QED.
