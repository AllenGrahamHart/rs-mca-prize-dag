# Attack surface

Rank drop is closed for all eight remaining fixed cells. Further work should
touch only the generic `V!=0` charts. In those charts the resultant has three
nonnamed factors of degrees 3, 3, and 12; one cubic branch closes directly,
one requires full quotient identities, and the degree-12 basis route needs a
finer reduction.

A falsifier is an omitted nonnamed factor, use of a transported generic unit
on `V=0`, a nonzero second localizer power, or a literal cell absent from the
sixteen-case ledger.
