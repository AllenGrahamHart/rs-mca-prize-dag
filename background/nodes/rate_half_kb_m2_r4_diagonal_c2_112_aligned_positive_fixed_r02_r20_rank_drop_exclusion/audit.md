# Audit

1. All sixteen branches are compiled literally; no fingerprint transport is
   consumed.
2. The localizer is built from original named units. `V` and factors created
   only after `w=-U/V` are not inverted.
3. Factorization occurs before specialization; exact identities are then
   checked over the deployed prime.
4. Every branch reports `PASS`, dimension 2 before localization, and
   nilpotence index 2.
5. The final Modal ledger used official SageMath 10.9 and is pinned by raw
   SHA-256 in `verify.py`.
