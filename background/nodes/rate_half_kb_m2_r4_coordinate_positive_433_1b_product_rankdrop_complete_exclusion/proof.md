# Proof

By the rational classifier, every deployed product-rank-drop common packet
must specialize to one of sixteen explicit points in cells 9 and 10.  Each
point has full common rank seven and nonzero `A_2` at its five common labels
and at `xi`, so its product map `(KBP1BRX-1)` is well defined there.

The signed atlas exhausts the target data by the four lanes and seven
records in `(KBP1BRX-2)`.  The source facet consists of two complete deck
pairs and one singleton on the five common labels.  Adjoining the singleton
mate `xi` leaves three complete deck pairs among the other six source
labels.  Therefore choosing the record at `xi` and a perfect matching of
the residual six gives all necessary product placements.  The resultant in
`(KBP1BRX-4)` is symmetric in `y,z`, so the fifteen unordered perfect
matchings require no hidden orientation multiplier.

The complete positive Vieta equations give both equations in
`(KBP1BRX-3)`.  The three residual pairs give the three resultants.  Hence
every actual packet determines a zero of one of the 105 target ideals for
its common point and sign lane.  The ledger deliberately allows all seven
records at `xi` and does not impose the additional condition that the
distinguished `eta` record be internal; it is therefore a superset of the
actual placement ledger.

The generated certificate covers every Cartesian-product case in
`(KBP1BRX-6)`.  Singular returns dimension `-1`, basis size one, and the
unit basis for every localized ideal.  Thus none of the necessary target
assignments exists, even in the deliberately enlarged ledger.  No actual
packet can lie on this branch. QED.
