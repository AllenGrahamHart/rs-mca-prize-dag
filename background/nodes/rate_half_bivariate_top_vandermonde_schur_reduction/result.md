# Result

The universal top-coefficient part of the bivariate rank problem is closed.
Exactly `4m+1` columns are paid by a scaled Vandermonde block. On the clean
open frontier the unresolved Schur matrix has at most `3m-1` columns, rather
than the original `|W|` or `|W|+1` columns and `(m+2)(4m+1)` rows.

The `m=1` failure is now localized to one zero Schur column. The `m=2` search
found full residual rank in every sampled bad pattern. The mathematical
frontier is to express `S_W` in incidence terms and prove that bad overlap
forces its columns independent.
