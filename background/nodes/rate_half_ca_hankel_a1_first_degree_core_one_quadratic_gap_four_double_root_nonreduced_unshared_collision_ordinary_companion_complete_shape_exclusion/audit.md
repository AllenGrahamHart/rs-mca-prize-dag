# Audit

1. A full six-row fiber has thirty ordered off-diagonal pairs. The known
   fixed-point-free involution graph contains exactly six, leaving twenty-four.
2. Division by four is necessary: two row quartics may share four parameter
   roots. No generic multiplicity-one assumption is used.
3. Removing one graph from the degree-six cap of five components leaves at
   most four, not five.
4. Both terms of the positive-characteristic Corvaja--Zannier maximum are
   compared against `P_res/4` with exact integers.
5. The forced second toral component is chosen to carry a counted `H^2`
   point; this is what proves its constant lies in `H`.
6. Equality of the two projection subdegrees follows from their common
   degree-six parameter function before the toral character comparison.
7. The second graph cannot secretly be the first graph: the first component
   was deleted before the residual point count.
8. The group contradiction uses `|Deck(C/P^1)| | 6`; it does not assume the
   cover is Galois.
9. The theorem removes companions only. Shape A remains a genuine large
   irreducible-factor problem.
