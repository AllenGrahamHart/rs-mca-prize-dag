# Audit

- `C` is the outer component normalization; `Gamma` is the source
  normalization. Their genera are not conflated.
- The elliptic involution classification is used only in tame odd
  characteristic.
- The two degree-two projection involutions are distinct because the image
  has bidegree `(2,2)`, not merely because their formulas look different.
- Finiteness of the generated dihedral group comes from invariance of the
  nonconstant degree-60 common function.
- The dihedral factor is geometric; no challenge-field normal form is
  asserted.
- The pole count includes the exceptional `n=5` index-five point and does
  not incorrectly require `n|6` in that row.
- The four surviving factor degrees are necessary cases, not realizations.
- No ledger quantity moves.
