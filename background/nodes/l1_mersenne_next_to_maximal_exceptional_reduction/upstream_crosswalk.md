# Upstream crosswalk

This is an exact finite split-pencil reduction. In the upstream second-moment
ledger it isolates the only remaining next-to-maximal passport on every
`m=8,16` Mersenne row: zero valuation, constant Euler eliminant, and simple
complement-disjoint tangent fibers, with `deg T=h-2` and
`deg(XR')=p-m`. Its remaining outer quotient is quadratic and contains
`R(0)`; the two quotient fibers saturate the complement and ramification
degrees exactly. Equivalently, the residue is one domain-supported polynomial
Belyi normal form with critical values `0,1`. The proof uses a general Euler tangent
reduction and a printed base-field/quadratic-extension packet; it does not
claim that passport is empty, any lower value degree, or the primitive
width-above-`p` ledger.
