# Result

The next-to-maximal split-value strata `m=8,h=7` and `m=16,h=15` reduce on
all five official rows to one constant-Euler passport. Every generic tangent
branch and both binomial outer forms are empty. The remaining passport has
`nu=0`, constant `H`, simple nonzero tangent values, and squarefree tangent
fibers disjoint from the complement. Its tangent polynomial has exact degree
`h-2`, while `XR'` has exact degree `p-m`. After removing the tangent and
possible zero factors, `G-(m alpha/q)Y` has a quadratic quotient containing
`R(0)`. Its two quotient fibers saturate both the complement and derivative
degrees exactly. After normalizing those values to `0,1`, the entire residue
is a degree-`p` Belyi polynomial whose two fibers lie on the official domain.
