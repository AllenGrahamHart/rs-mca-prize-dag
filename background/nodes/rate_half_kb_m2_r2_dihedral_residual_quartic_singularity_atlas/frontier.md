# Frontier

Do not spend further effort factoring arbitrary residual quartics: the full
allowed family is irreducible and rational. Parameterize `Q_(a,b)` from its
three-node model (or node-tacnode model at `b=a`) and pull back the six
order-five pole fibers. The next useful equations are the exact degree-24
source locator, the twelve quartic row divisors, and their challenge-field
descent. A contradiction must occur there, or the residual profile is a
genuine geometric producer requiring owner analysis.
