# Audit

- Irreducibility is proved over the geometric closure by square classes,
  not inferred from sample factorizations.
- The `b!=a` and `b=a` cases are separated because the coefficient of
  `S^4` vanishes in the latter affine chart.
- Both Hessian determinants retain every parameter factor; the standing
  fences `a in {-1,1}` and `b notin {-2,2}` make them nonzero.
- The point at infinity in the `b=a` case is audited as a tacnode, not
  counted as two distinct nodes.
- Arithmetic genus three and total delta three prove both rationality and
  completeness of the singularity list.
- No source realization, profile deletion, owner, or payment is inferred.
