# Claim contract

- **Input:** one normalized atom on all canonical pairwise welds and at least
  two large types.
- **Move:** replace one of 18 fixed anchor records while retaining the same
  recovery types.
- **Overlap:** 31 supports, including 17 from the anchor type and at least
  five from a distinct counterpart.
- **Output:** high complexity or the same atom certifies every large-type
  record.
- **Not claimed:** a cap or owner payment for the global atom, rank-eleven
  closure, or MCA closure.
