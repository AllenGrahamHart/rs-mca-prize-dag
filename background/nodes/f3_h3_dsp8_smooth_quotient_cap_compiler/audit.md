# Audit

- `U_sm^c` forgets only the quotient pair. Dropping richness,
  signed-disjointness, class, or smoothness would define a different and much
  larger object.
- The quotient relation is ordered, so its multiplicity is exactly `R(t)`;
  no factor two or four is introduced.
- The retained exclusions `t!=0,1` make both affine slopes nonzero and the
  two forms nonproportional.
- The strict Mattarei bound is safely consumed by a non-strict sufficient
  unweighted target.
- `C_M<189/100`, not equality, is used. The integer multipliers
  `144344=4*36086` and `3213=189*17` preserve the exact live constant.
- Shkredov multi-shift intersection bounds do not prove `(SQC4)`: a rich
  product fiber supplies repeated witnesses for the same target, not distinct
  additive shifts of the quotient variable. A marginal shifted-energy bound
  also loses the required `n^(4/3)` scale.
- The generic smooth trace curve has genus one, so the nodal rational
  parameter and its multi-shift reductions cannot be reused on this branch.
