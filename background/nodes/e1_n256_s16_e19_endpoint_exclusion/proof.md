# Proof

The E19 router proves that every `V=38` candidate lies in one of four profiles
on eight templates. The four-profile exclusion proves that every proper-
conductor vector is removed by the conductor theorem and every full-conductor
norm is positive and below `2^250`. These branches exhaust the router, so the
entire `V=38` chamber is empty. Variance is even, hence the next live positive
endpoint is at most 36.
