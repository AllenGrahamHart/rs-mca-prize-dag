# Proof

Let `n=rho+1`. The top square block `H_0(t)` has generic rank `rho` and
radical generated by `q(t)`. Hence

```text
adj H_0(t)=E(t)q(t)q(t)^T.                            (1)
```

Use the rank-`rho` compound of the full `(n+1) x n` rectangular pencil.
Its scalar factor is the regular determinant `Delta`, its right factor is
`q`, and its left factor is the wedge of the two shifted kernel vectors.
Selecting the top `n` rows contracts that wedge against the omitted last
row. The resulting scalar is the last coefficient `a=q_rho`, and the
remaining left factor is `q`. Thus

```text
E=c_0 a Delta,                                        (2)
```

which proves `(FRF4)`.

Every coefficient of `q^vee` has parameter degree at most `m`, while every
`y_i` is linear in `t`. Formula `(FRF2)` therefore proves `(FRF3)`.

Work now over the algebraic closure of `F(t)`. The parent theorem supplies
squarefree degree-`rho` supported specializations of `q`, so its discriminant
and leading coefficient are not identically zero. Thus generically `q` is
separable, `a!=0`, and the rank is `rho`. Write

```text
q=a product_(x in S)(X-x)=a Lambda.                   (3)
```

The first `rho` moments determine unique weights `theta_x`, and the kernel
recurrence extends their representation through all moments used by `H_0`:

```text
y_i=sum_(x in S)theta_x x^i.                          (4)
```

No `theta_x` vanishes, since otherwise the Hankel rank would be below
`rho`. The reciprocal generating identity is

```text
q^vee(Z) sum_(i>=0)y_iZ^i
 =a sum_(x in S)theta_x product_(u in S\{x})(1-uZ).
                                                                    (5)
```

The right side has degree at most `rho-1`, so the truncation in `(FRF2)` is
exact. Reversing `(5)` gives

```text
P(x)=a theta_x Lambda'(x)=theta_x q'(x),              (6)
```

proving `(FRF6)`.

The corner cofactor of `(1)` deletes the last monomial row and column. Its
Vandermonde factorization and `(2)` give

```text
c_0 Delta a^3=Vandermonde(S)^2 product_x theta_x,      (7)
```

up to the fixed ordering sign. This proves `(FRF7)` wherever the displayed
quantities specialize nontrivially.

Finally put `P_0=P/a`. Since `Lambda` is monic, `(6)` gives

```text
Res_X(Lambda,P_0)
 =product_x theta_x Lambda'(x)
 =constant*Vandermonde(S)^2 product_x theta_x.         (8)
```

The formal resultant is homogeneous of degree `rho-1` in the coefficients
of its first argument and degree `rho` in those of its second. Substituting
`q=aLambda` and `P=aP_0` into `(8)`, then using `(7)`, yields

```text
Res_X^(rho,rho-1)(q,P)=c_1 a^(2rho+2)Delta.            (9)
```

Both sides are polynomials in `t`, so the generic identity extends through
all specializations, including `a=0` and degree drops. This proves `(FRF5)`.
The count of `2m+3` good supported slopes is supplied by the parent theorem.
QED.
