# Result

The layer-A rank programme is repriced: (LA-EQ) shows any rank theorem
strong enough to help already implies the endpoint exclusion, and the two
natural hypothesis rungs below it are constructively false — saturated
configurations with positive count excess and nullity exactly 1 exist in
closed form, and the `Z^m - X^{2m}` family fences the bare count at every
`m >= 2` with nullity exactly `2m`.

What survives: layer A as a per-witness instrument, and the surviving
question — rank over (SAT2)-completable configurations — which IS the
realizability question. A positive on either lane settles the other's
negative; the H1+H2 closed form transports to the realizability search as
a starting variety.
