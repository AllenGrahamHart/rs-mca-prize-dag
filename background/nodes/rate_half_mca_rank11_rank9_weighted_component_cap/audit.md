# Audit

The cap counts component extensions, not records. Rank ten on `T` is used
twice: it selects one owner point and ensures at least one added coordinate
is outside the rank-nine kernel zero set. The other coordinate is allowed to
lie in the common core. The product in (2) deliberately overcounts pairs
with two petal coordinates.

The `981105` bound uses actual support-wise pair noncontainment; without it a
core of size at least `m'` would invalidate the fixed-owner ratio.
