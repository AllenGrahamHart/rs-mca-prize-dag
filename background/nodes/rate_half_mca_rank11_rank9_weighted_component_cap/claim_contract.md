# Claim contract

## Input

One fixed rank-nine nine-cell in the typed affine-owner lane, with actual
records, exact supports, pair noncontainment, owner lines, and disjoint
owner-plane petals.

## Output

The incidence-weight cap `(WP1)` in exactly the weighted concentrator's
unit.

## Nonclaim

No kernel-lane cap, cross-chart count, chronology owner, or prize closure is
asserted.
