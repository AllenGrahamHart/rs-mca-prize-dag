# Claim contract

- **Input:** one surviving arbitrary-`t` full-petal cell, one exact anchor,
  and one fixed exact joint defect/background owner.
- **Output:** an exact finite packing bound and a polynomial payment for every
  bounded co-deficiency fixed-owner chamber.
- **Preserved:** field, source partition, exact defect and background roots,
  primitive contributors, owner identity, and contributor count.
- **Nonclaim:** owner-count control, owner aggregation, first-match
  chronology, complete-cell payment, or deployed row.
