# Audit

The packing uses the full exact background zero sets, not independently
chosen `u`-subsets. Their sizes may vary, but the lower bound `v=max(0,u)`
is enough for the denominator in `(JP3)`. Pairwise intersections use the
joint determinant budget before any owner sum.

The polynomial consequence is per fixed owner. The theorem deliberately
does not multiply by the number of divisors of the anchor polynomial.
