# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the official energy-at-most-four exclusion;
- exact four-singleton parity masks;
- the cyclotomic resultant norm identity;
- the energy-adaptive product threshold at `V=12`.

## Output

No official collision has `E=5`; every survivor has `E>=6`, `V>=12`,
`m<=853574`, and lies in an exact `608`-cofactor frontier.

## Certificate boundary

The energy-five exclusion is a complete exact computation, independently
replayed. Source hashes, run identifiers, counts, and empty outputs are
pinned.

## Nonclaims

- no exclusion of energy six or above;
- no collision-vector or orbit count;
- no profile payment or critical status promotion.
