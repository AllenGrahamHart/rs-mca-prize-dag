# Audit

- The fixed automorphism is `sigma_63(zeta)=-zeta^(-1)`.
- The fixed field has degree 32, not 16 or 64.
- The pinned polynomial comes from the exact squared resultant
  `Res_Z(Z^64+1,Z^2-YZ-1)`; its 32 roots modulo 257 are distinct.
- Exact residues are `9^63=57`, `9^(-1)=200`, and `57^(-1)=248`
  modulo 257, giving the common fixed-field residue 66.
- The proof targets one lower prime and does not claim a complete class-group
  presentation.
- The Stickelberger relation is paired with a nontrivial residue character;
  no annihilator-only inference is made.
- Nonprincipality descends from `J_63` to `p_66` by contraposition of ideal
  extension; the stronger odd-class-number injectivity is not needed here.
