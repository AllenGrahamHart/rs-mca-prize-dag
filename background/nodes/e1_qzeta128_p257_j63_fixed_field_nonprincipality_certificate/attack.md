# Attack

Closed by a Kummer-style route:

1. construct an explicit `2*21121` power generator for
   `J_63/bar(J_63)` from 32 Jacobi sums;
2. prove that generator is not a unit times a `21121`st power using one
   auxiliary split prime and the complete conductor-128 unit basis;
3. extend a hypothetical generator of `p_66` to contradict the resulting
   nonprincipality of `J_63`.

No BNF or heavy computation is used.
