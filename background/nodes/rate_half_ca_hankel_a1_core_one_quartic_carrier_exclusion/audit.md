# Audit

- The degree-four pole-clearing form is allowed to contain a contact-active
  component; containment is the input to the carrier descent, not an omitted
  genericity assumption.
- All active components must divide the same clearing form. Their squarefree
  product, not an individually classified component, is the low-degree
  recurrence carrier.
- Reducedness is needed when active components are multiplied into `Q_A`
  and all contact-inactive components into `Q_0`.
- The cancellation of `Q_0` in the recurrence uses the absence of a
  domain-only factor, in particular `gcd(Q_0,u)=1`.
- The contradiction is generic nullity one, not a numerical rank sample;
  it remains valid after extending the coefficient field to split the
  components.
- The official endpoint is a sufficient exact prefix. Equality occurs in
  the arithmetic test at `e=floor(16m/13)`, so that degree is not claimed.
