# Audit

1. `q_kappa=0` is equivalent to an antipodal edge only because common-`K`
   source lifts and the negative leading coefficient are nonzero.
2. The three loop roots occur at distinct quotient labels.
3. A nonloop edge between distinct signed label pairs has nonzero sum in
   odd characteristic.
4. Parent injectivity prevents reuse of one of the three loop products.
5. The gate removes two multiplicity skeletons, not all signed assignments
   or complete packet graphs in those profiles.
