# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | A negative coordinate packet has at most two antipodal common-`K` edge orbits. |
| exact reduction | The seven injective common-`K` multiplicity skeletons reduce to the five rows in `(KBNL-2)`. |
| factor pin | One or two loop fibers force the corresponding linear factors of `A_1`. |
| assumptions | The complete negative Vieta equations, leading support, distinct labels, and the parent injectivity cut. |
| nonclaim | Exclusion of the five survivors, positive parity, orientation/type deletion, owner/payment movement, a row, or either Prize theorem. |
