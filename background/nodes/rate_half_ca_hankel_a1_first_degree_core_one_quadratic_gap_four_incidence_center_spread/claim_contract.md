# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the `u=4` quadratic double-root packet has the exact block
  degrees `(ICS2)--(ICS5)`, every received slope has a unique radius-`rho`
  codeword center, the deficient slopes have exact error weight `rho-1`,
  each affine center line obeys `h<=rho+1-r`, and a locator pair has at
  least three, four, or five triple-union expanders according as it contains
  zero, one, or two deficient slopes.
- **Currency:** the zero-omission normal form, exact heavy incidence, RS
  minimum distance, and column-farness.
- **Dependencies:** the quadratic gap-four normal forms and the split-pencil
  equivalence.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** the resulting nonuniform block design is not excluded.
- **Falsifier:** a supported locator with fewer than `rho` distinct roots, a
  row degree differing from `(ICS5)`, an incorrect exact error weight in
  `(ICS8)`, or a center line violating `(ICS10)`.
- **Next gate:** combine the exact degree sequence with `(ICS8)`, or sharpen
  the line cap using rank-loss/error-weight deficits.
