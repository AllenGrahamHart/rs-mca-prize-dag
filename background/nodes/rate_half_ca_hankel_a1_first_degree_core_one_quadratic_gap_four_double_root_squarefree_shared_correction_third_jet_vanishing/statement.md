# `A=1` quadratic squarefree shared-correction third-jet vanishing

- **status:** PROVED
- **closure:** unconditional extension of the cubic quotient through squarefree shared roots
- **consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`

Retain the squarefree shared-root setup of the third-jet gate. Then its local
obstruction always vanishes:

```text
kappa_tau=0.                                        (HSV1)
```

Consequently, at every simple common root `tau` of `g_*` and `S_B`,

```text
D_1 divides F_i       for every 0<=i<=d,            (HSV2)
```

the degree-at-most-three quotient vector and recurrence extend across
`tau`, and the regular local Smith type is

```text
Smith_tau(D_1)=[3].                                 (HSV3)
```

Thus, for squarefree `S_B`, supported/correction sharing does not obstruct
the cubic heavy-quotient reduction.

## Scope

This theorem does not treat a nonreduced correction root. It extends the
cubic quotient and local Smith classification; it does not by itself prove
the global barycentric remainder gate fails or exclude the double-root arm.
