2. Reducedness follows from a primitive squarefree characteristic
   polynomial, not from the previously proved length 24 alone.

   nonzero at `t=2`.
4. The checker replays `U*L=ell*U` in all 64 rows at that fiber.
5. The cyclic vector is registered in advance as the first coordinate
   vector; no search-dependent probability enters the proof.

   and changed target coordinate.

   row, or Prize status is claimed.
