# Claim contract

- **Input:** one labelled `t`-petal pair slice with `d<h<=2d+1` and one
  saturated degree-`d` anchor.
- **Output:** exact pair-slice and locator-image dimension `e+1`, plus a
  nonempty monic affine locator chart of dimension `e=2d+1-h`.
- **Preserved:** field, petal locators and labels, locator and numerator,
  monicity, splitness filters, primitivity, and exact contributor identity.
- **Nonclaim:** a split-point count, owner aggregation, or a large-source
  profile payment.
