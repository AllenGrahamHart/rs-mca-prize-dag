# Claim contract

## Inputs

- The PROVED fixed rank-eleven anchor deck with eighteen dense-pair records
  and ten deviation-basis records.
- The PROVED coefficientwise containment `D_H in V'[Z]` with slope degree
  at most 31.
- The PROVED conclusion that every unsafe component absorbs all high
  coefficients.

## Output

The coefficients in slope degrees `18..31` span `V'`. Hence every absorbing
correction survivor has the unique correction space `W=V'` and dimension
exactly ten.

## Nonclaim

The theorem does not count components, identify one affine owner, or sum
componentwise payments. Aggregate chronology remains open.
