# Rank-eleven dense-root high-span saturation

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** every high-core-absorbing survivor of the fixed rank-eleven
  relative `(H_C)` core

Let

```text
D_H(X,Z)=H(X,Z)-a_0'(X)-Zb_0'(X)
```

be the deviation interpolant of the fixed 32-record anchor-star tuple. Its
values vanish at the eighteen dense-pair anchor slopes, while ten other
anchor values span the full ten-dimensional deviation space `V'`. Then

```text
span{H_j:j>=2}=span{(D_H)_j:j>=2}=V'.
```

Indeed, after division by the monic locator of the eighteen dense slopes,
the coefficients of `D_H` in degrees `18..31` triangularly recover every
coefficient of the quotient.

Consequently every correction space `W<=V'` which absorbs all high
coefficients satisfies

```text
W=V',       dim W=10.
```

Thus the absorbing dimensions `2..9` in the earlier ten-flat route are
empty. Every surviving clone or rank-flat component belongs to the same
fixed ten-dimensional correction space. This theorem does not aggregate
the components or affine owners.

## Falsifier

Fewer than eighteen zero deviation values; failure of the ten selected
anchor deviations to span `V'`; slope degree above 31; a nonmonic or
incorrect dense-root locator; a quotient coefficient outside the span of
the degree-`18..31` coefficients; or an absorbing proper subspace of `V'`.
