  joint charge by a majorization-maximal fill; the resulting recursion pays
  exactly `e=130220,130221` beyond the parent interval.
- **dependencies:** the parent joint-core sum bound, line-size cap, exact
  boundary line bank, and distinct-line inside-core intersection bound.
- **quantifiers:** the two printed official Mersenne full-lift supports, over
  every field and received-word instance covered by the parent route.
- **not claimed:** safety or unsafety at `e>=130222`; optimality of the
  printed cutoff; or closure of the global MCA target.
- **falsifier:** a feasible vector satisfying `(LA1)` whose line-charge sum
  exceeds `(LA4)`, an arithmetic replay mismatch, or an official instance
  violating the parent line-bank hypotheses.
