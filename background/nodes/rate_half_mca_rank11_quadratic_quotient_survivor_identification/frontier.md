# Frontier

The quadratic route has two disjoint tasks.

1. **Quotient ownership:** show that cyclic antipodal and dihedral
   constant-product records are already charged by a retained factor,
   denominator, recursive quotient, or chronology ledger.
2. **Primitive pointwise cap:** prove fewer than 8,740 nonfixed graph points
   for shifted inversion with `lambda!=0,1`.

A local fiber-count theorem cannot perform task 1: these pencils have at
least 1,048,575 official fibers.
