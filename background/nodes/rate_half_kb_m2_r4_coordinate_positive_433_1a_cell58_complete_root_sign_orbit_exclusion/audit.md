   not an assumption that every evaluation point is fixed by inversion.

   support and its facet incidences bijectively.

   are transformed.

   antipodal loop and `q=0`; this shortcut is not asserted for a nonloop
   anchor.

   transport is still needed to cover both `epsilon_1` rows.

   follows from the form and Vieta identities, not from elimination output.
