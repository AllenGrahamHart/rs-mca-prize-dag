# Proof

Specialize the 48 globally valid admissible ratio-graph generators at each
exceptional root, then add the original polynomial `q5,q7,q6` equations.
This gives a unit ideal at five roots and exact bases at the other nine.

For each survivor introduce `e` with `e^2=E`. Saturate successively by all
sixteen original route guards, by `e,s,x,a0m,a2m`, and by the six-generator
rank-cofactor ideal. Finally add the original `q4` quadratic resultant.
Every final basis is `[1]`. The first unit stages are route guards at seven
roots and `q4` at roots `47655010` and `1893783428`. Thus no exceptional
fiber contains an admissible `FFF` point. QED.
