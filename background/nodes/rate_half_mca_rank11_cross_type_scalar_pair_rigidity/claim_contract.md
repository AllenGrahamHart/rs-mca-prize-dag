# Claim contract

- **Input:** two distinct pole-simple scalar-locator certificates sharing a
  28-support deck split 14+14 between two distinct saturated pair types.
- **Case removed:** linearly independent scalar coefficient pairs.
- **Exact margin:** the strengthened common-core floor exceeds distinct-pair
  uniqueness by 31,136 coordinates, independently of shortening.
- **Survivor:** proportional scalar pairs; in degree two, the two quotient
  maps have the same projective value at infinity.
- **Not claimed:** existence of cross-type decks, equality of quotient maps,
  a bound on proportional-scalar types, aggregate owner payment, or MCA
  closure.
