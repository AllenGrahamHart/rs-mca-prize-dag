# Claim contract

- **Proposition:** weld nullity one is equivalent to multiplicative cycle
  consistency; extremal consistency is certified by four-cycles alone, and
  strict consistency by four-cycles plus fiber-transition triangles.
- **Quantifier:** both proved pair-boundary profiles and every guaranteed
  selected zero-excess fiber set.
- **Dependency:** the connected scalar-weld rank dichotomy.
- **Falsifier:** a passing printed local certificate with full weld rank, or
  a failed printed local identity with a nonzero weld kernel.
- **Nonclaim:** no assertion that all allowed profiles fail the certificate.
