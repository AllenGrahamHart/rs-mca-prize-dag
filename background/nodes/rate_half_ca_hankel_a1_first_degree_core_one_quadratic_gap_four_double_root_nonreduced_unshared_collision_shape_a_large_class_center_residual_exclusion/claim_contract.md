# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the large-class interpolation map has exact rank `r-1`, and
  all three coefficient defects are the corresponding center fibers.
- **Inputs:** the center-fiber/large-class dichotomy and the exact
  regular-quartic pushforward of the residual four-core.
- **Output:** exclusion of the domain multiplication chain
  `B_0,(X-x_*)B_0`.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaims:** no incompatibility among the three remaining center
  fibers is proved.
- **Falsifier:** a valid rank-`r-2` large-class map whose extra center
  common point is absent from the residual cycle or whose correction
  quartic is supported at that assigned center.
