# Proof

The center-fiber dichotomy gives

```text
rank T_(gamma_0) in {r-1,r-2}.                    (1)
```

Suppose the second value occurs. Then that theorem produces a nonzero
`B_0 in W_X` with

```text
G(gamma_0,X)=(X-x_*)B_0(X).                      (2)
```

The center locator has the exact factorization

```text
Qbar(gamma_0,X)
 =chi_0(X-x_*)L_rest,0(X).                       (3)
```

Equations `(2)--(3)` show that the finite point

```text
(gamma_0,x_*)                                    (4)
```

is a common point of the two coprime curves `Qbar=0` and `G=0`.

This point is not among the mandatory intersections removed in the exact
four-core. Those intersections are the roots of `A_delta R_delta` over
the `3e` off-line supported slopes. By contrast, `gamma_0` is an assigned
center and `x_*` lies outside `U_0`. Hence the positive local intersection
multiplicity at `(4)` survives in the effective residual cycle `Z_4`.
Its parameter pushforward therefore contains `gamma_0` in its support.

The exact resultant identification gives

```text
pi_*Z_4=div(E_4)=2 div(S_B).                     (5)
```

In the retained nonreduced collision

```text
S_B=c_S ell_tau^2,                               (6)
```

so the right side of `(5)` is supported only at the collision parameter
`tau`. The unshared collision theorem places `tau` off the three assigned
centers. Thus

```text
tau!=gamma_0,                                    (7)
```

contradicting the support forced by `(4)`.

Therefore `rank T_(gamma_0)=r-1`, proving `(LRE1)`. The center-fiber
theorem already proves `(LRE2)--(LRE3)` for the two small classes and
places the large image inside `V_(gamma_0)`, which has dimension `r-1`.
Equality of ranks proves the remaining instances. Formula `(LRE4)` is the
large-center value calculation from the same theorem. Finally `(LRE5)`
would recreate the excluded rank-`r-2` defect, so no such form exists.
QED.
