# Claim contract

- **input:** at least 520 distinct scalar polynomials in an `F`-space of
  dimension at most four, in the fixed coprime-direction normal form;
- **output:** affine-line occupancy at most 15, scalar dimension in
  `{2,3,4}`, and at least 38 projective secant directions;
- **root output:** each represented direction has at least 134940 distinct
  official-domain roots;
- **dimension-two specialization:** one base-field polynomial pencil has at
  least 38 root-rich members;
- **not claimed:** full splitting of the direction polynomials, payment of
  the pencil, or a census in dimensions three and four.
