# Audit

1. The common intersection is attached to one affine scalar line, not to all
   520 types.
2. Pairwise intersections on that line are equal because all pair codewords
   coincide on `Z_D(T)` and are distinct off it.
3. The line-cap denominator is positive: `1116046-1048575=67471`.
4. A fixed projective direction may occur on many parallel affine lines;
   the count uses the full `34*15+10` partition rather than only one line.
5. Root-rich means at least 134940 domain roots, not full splitting.
