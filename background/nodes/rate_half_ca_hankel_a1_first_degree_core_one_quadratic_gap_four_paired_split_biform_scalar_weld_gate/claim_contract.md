# Claim contract

- **Proposition:** joint row/fiber realizability is equivalent to one
  full-support kernel of the row coefficient-MDS matrix augmented by the
  sparse scalar-weld matrix.
- **Quantifier:** every field, row/fiber root system, and selected zero-excess
  fiber set satisfying the two proved pair-boundary factorizations.
- **Dependencies:** the fixed-domain coefficient-MDS gate and paired padded-
  fiber factorization.
- **Falsifier:** a common biform violating the weld equations, or a
  full-support augmented kernel from which the stated common biform cannot be
  reconstructed.
- **Nonclaim:** no augmented-rank theorem or pair-boundary exclusion.
