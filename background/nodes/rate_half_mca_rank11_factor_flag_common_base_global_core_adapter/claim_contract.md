# Claim contract

## Inputs

- one actual anchor-good coordinate `x in G_0`;
- the complete post-gauge correction space `C'`;
- an exact equality `C'=span(PB)`;
- `x` is a common zero of `P` or of the residual factor space `B`.

## Output

`x` belongs to the maximal agreement support of every selected post-near
record, so the complete line-global core is nonempty and enters route `(C)`.

## Falsifier

A product in `PB` nonzero at the common pencil zero; a selected pair whose
component difference from the anchor is outside `C'`; an anchor-good
coordinate outside the anchor pair core; or a record missing agreement after
both pair components agree.
