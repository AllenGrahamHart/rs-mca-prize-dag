# Proof

Fix `x in G_0` with `g(x)=0` for every `g in P`. Every generator of `PB` has
the form `gb`, and

```text
(gb)(x)=g(x)b(x)=0.
```

Evaluation at `x` is linear, so it vanishes on `span(PB)=C'`.

In the anchored row-space gauge, a selected minimizing pair
`e=(a_e,b_e)` differs from the anchor pair `e_0=(a_0,b_0)` by two elements
of `C'`. Hence

```text
a_e(x)=a_0(x),  b_e(x)=b_0(x).
```

The definition `G_0 subset H_{e_0}` says
`r_0(x)=a_0(x)` and `r_1(x)=b_0(x)`. For every selected slope `gamma`,

```text
r_0(x)+gamma r_1(x)=a_e(x)+gamma b_e(x).
```

Thus `x` lies in the maximal agreement support of every selected post-near
record. Their complete intersection `C_*` is nonempty. The proved 31-anchor
globalization router sends exactly this branch to the line-global
identity-fiber shortening route `(C)`.
