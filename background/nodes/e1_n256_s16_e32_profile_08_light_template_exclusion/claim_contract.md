# Claim contract

## Claim

No folded coefficient vector in the `N=256`, `(3,4,0)`, `V=64` chamber has
autocorrelation magnitude profile `(0,8)`.

## Dependency

- `e1_n256_s16_e32_profile_parity_diameter_reduction` for the exact profile,
  parity, and diameter interface.

## Nonclaims

- no exclusion of profiles `(4,7)` or `(3,5,1)`;
- no exclusion of another positive even variance;
- no claim about the separate first-band profile `(4,2,0)`;
- no promotion of either universal E1/unsafe target.

## Falsifier

A normalized four-light support satisfying the zero-odd parity condition but
outside the six affine-unit templates refutes the router. Any one of the
119,087,616 tested vectors with exactly eight magnitude-two autocorrelation
coefficients and no other nonzero magnitude refutes the exclusion.
