# Attack record

The generic bidegree `(18,1)` Bezout count allowed 198 isolated points for
every eleven-set.  That count ignores evaluation rank.  Rank deficiency
already supplies a positive-dimensional kernel fiber, while full evaluation
rank makes ten equations solve `q(Z)R` as an affine-linear function of `Z`.
The eleventh equation is therefore linear, not degree 198.

The tempting alternative reduction of sparse completion labels by two
quotient dimensions was rejected: pairing a circuit representation with the
received words is support-dependent.  The rank-stratified elimination avoids
that invalid identification.
