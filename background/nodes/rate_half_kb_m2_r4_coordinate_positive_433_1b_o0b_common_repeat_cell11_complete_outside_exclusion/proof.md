# Proof

The symmetric tower covers every original guarded cell-11 source point. Split
the source by whether a registered construction guard vanishes. On the guard
open, the colored consistency theorem excludes missing `BE/CF` and the
uncolored paired-product theorem excludes missing `DE+/DF+/EF`; the selected-
cofactor and reconstruction-denominator nodes prove their exceptional fibers
contain no guarded source point. On the guard boundary, the direct finite
replay excludes the same five missing-record representatives at every one of
the 160 guarded points.

These five representatives contribute `5*15=75` labels for each outside-
cycle sign. The exact `d -> -d` transport exchanges missing `DE+` with `DE-`
and missing `DF+` with `DF-`, including all residual matchings, and fixes the
other records. It supplies the remaining `2*15=30` labels. Hence all 105 raw
labels are empty on each formal row.

There are eight common source towers and two outside-cycle signs, so cell 11
has 16 formal rows. Therefore `16*105=1,680` raw labels are empty. QED.
