# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the proved `V=30` endpoint;
- the proved sparse-L1 recurrence and signed-chord gate;
- the complete proved even-parity light atlas;
- the collision norm criterion.

## Output

Every `V=28` profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate lies in one of four profiles on one
of 1,321 exact affine templates.

## Nonclaims

No surviving vector is excluded here. No cubic cutoff, lower variance,
profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform allowance, row, or prize terminal
is claimed.

## Falsifier

A candidate with `L>10`, a fifth energy profile, a profile with an odd-class
count other than two or six, or a light support outside the pinned even-parity
atlas refutes this router.
