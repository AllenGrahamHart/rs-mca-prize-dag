# Claim contract

- **Claim:** on every budget-three chamber, the harvested rank-flat list
  compiler has integer cap at least four and therefore cannot exclude the
  four-word predecessor obstruction.
- **Scope:** every field, every `RS[F,D,2d]` of length `4d`, and every
  predecessor witness at agreement `3d-1`, for `d>=3`.
- **Dependencies:** the upstream affine-span/rank-flat compiler, the local
  six-type intersection reduction, affine-rank rigidity, and common-mismatch
  zero theorem.
- **Consumer effect:** closes the chamber bridge as a negative route result;
  it does not close `rate_half_list_adjacent_crossing`.
- **Nonclaims:** no chamber existence, chamber exclusion, official threshold,
  MCA bound, or exact values of `d_2,d_3`.
- **Falsifier:** a valid six-type tuple with `d_1=2d+1`, `d_2<=3d+1`,
  `2d+3<=d_3<=4d`, `b=0`, and rank-flat floor at most three.
