# Audit

The proof uses an upper bound on `d_2`, because the goal is a lower bound on
the rank-flat expression. Reversing that inequality would invalidate the
route fence. The exact identity `d_1=2d+1` is load-bearing; generalized
Singleton alone gives only a lower bound and would not permit substituting
the first denominator factor by `d` in this direction.

The selected triple intersection is used only to exhibit one
two-dimensional subcode with support at most `3d+1`. Affine rank three
ensures the two selected differences are independent. No identification of
selected triple intersections with the complete common-zero set is made.

The conclusion is deliberately negative. A rank-flat upper bound whose
right side is at least four is compatible with a four-word witness and
cannot prove predecessor safety. It supplies no evidence that a chamber
exists.
