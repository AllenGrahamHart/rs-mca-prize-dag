# Audit

1. The coefficient order in `(KBPQE-2)--(KBPQE-3)` is independently
   expanded from `A_0-pA_2` and `wB_1^2-s^2A_2^2`.
2. The generic compact expression includes the factor `A^3` relating it to
   the resultant.
3. The linear branch uses the exact root `-C/B`; it never divides by `B`
   without the `B!=0` branch guard.
4. The `A=B=0` branch is deleted by distinct supported common products,
   not by assuming `d_2` or `e_2` nonzero.
5. Common-label roots, denominator roots, repeated roots, and roots failing
   the deck-pair ledger remain forbidden even when the scalar cut vanishes.
6. Small-field complete squared-sum emptiness is heuristic guidance only.
