# Claim contract

- **claim:** the exhaustive five-type residue-signed classification of
  official generating rows and nonemptiness of every type;
- **scope:** maximal rate-half `n=2^41` rows satisfying the existing
  official admissibility hypotheses and `ord_n(p)=e`;
- **dependencies:** the proved plus and minus dyadic order laws;
- **nonclaims:** no classification of non-generating rows, no mass bound,
  no PP5.0 normalization, and no F2/Prize close;
- **falsifier:** a generating official row outside the five types or a
  failed primality/order/field-cap check for one printed witness.
