# Attack

Try to realize the 33/36 flag over a small representable matroid: a rank-two
flat carrier inside rank-three and rank-four carriers, with one shared
residual point parallel to a support-three anchor. Check circuit minimality
for every attaining deletion. For the 37-point branch, search for a
nonnested pair of higher spans. Either construction tests the exact survivor;
it does not refute the proved routing alternatives.
