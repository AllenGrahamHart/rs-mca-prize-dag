# Proof

The upstream compression theorem proves the resultant identity in
`(KBBR-1)`. On `V!=0`, the exact linear combination

```text
DP-AQ=-(Vw+U)
```

forces `w=-U/V`; substitution into the remaining rows and multiplication by
the exact denominator powers gives `R,E2,E3`. Every removed factor divides
either `V` or the numerator obtained from a declared named-open unit after
the same substitution. It is therefore nonzero on this chart.

For all four literal cells, exact computation over `F_2130706433` gives a
151-element basis for the three retained cores. The ideals have dimension
one, but this does not produce admissible points: multiplying the 25 unique
transported unit factors one at a time and reducing after every multiplication
gives zero at factor 16. Equivalently, the square-free localizer lies in the
core ideal. Hence its localization is the unit ideal.

On `V=0`, factorization before localization gives named factors times exactly
two retained factors, of degrees 2 and 10. Any rank-drop point in the named
open must lie on one of them. For each literal cell and each retained factor,
the four original q-slice rows plus that factor were compiled directly over
the deployed prime. The degree-2 branches have 9-element bases. The degree-10
branches have 104 or 106 elements. In every case the product of the original
named unit factors reduces to zero; the recorded nilpotence index is one.
Thus all eight localized factor ideals are empty.

These polynomial identities and ideal-membership reductions hold over the
base field and therefore after extension to `F_(2130706433^6)`. The generic
and rank-drop charts are exhaustive, proving all four exclusions. QED.
