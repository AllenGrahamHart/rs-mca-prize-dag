# Claim contract

- **Quantifier:** every two distinct triple-owner pair types with at least 29
  owned records each.
- **Packet construction:** symmetric 18-anchor packets on one common recovery
  set of at most five represented types.
- **Shared-deck sizes:** 28, 25, 22, or 19 according to recovery-set rank.
- **Output:** high complexity from either packet, or one projective atom
  certificate shared by the two packets.
- **Not claimed:** one canonical atom across all pairwise welds, aggregate
  quotient payment, high-complexity payment, rank-eleven closure, or MCA
  closure.
