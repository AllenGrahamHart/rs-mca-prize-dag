# Frontier

Pairwise deck supply is no longer missing: every two large types can be
welded through 19 or more shared records. The remaining compatibility issue
is one level higher. For three types `p,q,r`, compare the welded certificates
for `(p,q)` and `(p,r)`. Their possible difference is gauge motion along the
`p` explanation line. A triple-cocycle or owner theorem must bound or charge
that motion without summing overlapping local certificates.
