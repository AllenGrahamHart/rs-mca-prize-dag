equation.  The decomposition theorem lists all such deployed points, and

solution lies on the simultaneous section-base scheme.  Its deployed points
are exactly the two points per source-sign row in the decomposition theorem.
The pointwise regularization and all-role base-locus theorem exclude every
target lane and matching at those points, in particular `xi=5` and `xi=6`.
