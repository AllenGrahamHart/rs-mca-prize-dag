# Audit

The equivalence concerns a unit exclusion certificate. A nonunit aggregate
gcd may contain roots from several norm colors or roots that do not lift to
the original HNF packet; it must be split and filtered before interpretation.

Base extension preserves a Bezout unit certificate. In the reverse direction
unique factorization in `K[X]` assigns every irreducible divisor of the
aggregate product to at least one color factor. The statement does not claim
that every root of an individual norm equation lies in `F_(p^2)` or lifts to
a packet.

This compiler changes the replay granularity only. It supplies no gcd result
and does not weaken any inherited saturation, color assignment, Frobenius,
cyclotomic, or inner condition.
