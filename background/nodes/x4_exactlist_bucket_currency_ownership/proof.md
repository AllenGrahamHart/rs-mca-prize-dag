# Proof

Fix `S0` in the residual prefix fiber. For any other `S`, put

```text
C=S intersect S0,  P=S\C,  Q=S0\C.
```

Since both supports have size `A`, `|P|=|Q|=h`. The pair `(P,Q)` determines
`S=C union P` because `S0` is fixed and `C=S0\Q`; hence the map from `S` to
the ordered record `(P,Q)` is injective.

The proved star-PTE normal form gives equality of the first `t` elementary
coefficients of `P` and `Q`, and it gives `h>=t+1`. Trivially `h<=A`; because
`P` and `Q` are disjoint subsets of the complement of their common core,
`h<=n-A` as well. Thus every non-base member is one incident order-`t`
primitive shift-pair record and `|F_z|-1` is at most the size of any universal
ledger into which those records inject.

The other separations are by counted object. The proved moment-trade theorem
constructs list staircases by selecting combinations of moment-null blocks.
Thus a block or family census is not, without its expansion rule, a census of
the resulting list members. The proved QA.22 currency-separation theorem
shows independently that a per-pair count of MCA slopes is not a per-word
list count. These objects can share algebraic records only through an
explicit injection preserving multiplicity and quantifier order. This proves
the stated ownership rules.
