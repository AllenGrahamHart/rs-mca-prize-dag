# Audit

1. The proved injection reaches general order-`t` star-PTE records; it does
   not claim that they are F-4 order-`h-1` minimal records.
2. The base support contributes the additive `1`. Since `u1` is posed with a
   strict `<16n^3` record bound, an eventual multiplicity-one coverage theorem
   would give a non-strict primitive list bound `<=16n^3`.
3. A structured-column proof must count actual assigned list members after
   expansion and coalescing, not only moment-null generators.
4. QA.22 remains valid MCA arithmetic. Only its use as an unproved list-side
   adapter is excluded.
5. No open size bound or priority-map coverage claim is promoted here.
