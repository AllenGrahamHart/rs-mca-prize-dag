# Audit

1. The basis is chosen from `T` and has exactly the evaluation rank of `T`.
2. The quotient solution is common only after two compatible slopes force
   all residual affine-linear conditions to vanish identically.
3. The one-record case is bounded without that identity argument.
4. Translation and locator division preserve the actual MCA object.
5. The common-zero bound is `K'-d`, so subtracting `r=10-d` leaves exactly
   `K'-10`, not `K'-9`.
6. Canonical basis assignment counts every rank-deficient tuple once.
7. The theorem does not infer that capacities for different bases are
   disjoint record sets.
