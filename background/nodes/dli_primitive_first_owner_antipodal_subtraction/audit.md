# Audit

- The proof uses the subgroup lattice of a cyclic `2`-group, so no periodic
  class escapes the order-two test.
- The descent retains all even moments and makes all odd moments automatic.
- Fixed weights are tracked before summation; the central interval therefore
  introduces no boundary approximation.
- The normalized factor is derived after count subtraction and is exact.
- Independent exhaustive checks cover `n=8` and `n=16`, all weights, and
  several values of `t`.
