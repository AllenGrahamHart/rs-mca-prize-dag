# Claim contract

## Inputs

- A cyclic evaluation subgroup of power-of-two order `n` in odd
  characteristic.
- Primitive means trivial stabilizer under rotations of that subgroup.

## Outputs

- An exact fixed-weight primitive-count formula.
- An exact interval version.
- For even `t`, the normalized one-step subtraction formula.

## Nonclaims

- No upper bound on the remaining primitive count is supplied.
- No marginal `100`-bit bound or reduced `21`-bit joint reserve is proved.
- The formula is not asserted for noncyclic domains or even characteristic.
