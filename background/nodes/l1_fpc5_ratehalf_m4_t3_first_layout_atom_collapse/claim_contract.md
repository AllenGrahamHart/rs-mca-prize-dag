# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the canonical first admissible maximal `M=4` source;
- general first-layout domination;
- source cross-ratio normalization and the exact LS6 bijection.

## Output

One fixed source, four touched triples, one cross-ratio per triple, fewer than
`n` defects, and four anchors.

## Nonclaims

- no bound for one guarded LS6 atom;
- no uniform split-flat theorem;
- no free choice of `lambda` inside a fixed source/triple;
- no reintroduction of later source layouts.
