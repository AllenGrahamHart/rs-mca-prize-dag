# Claim contract

- **Claim:** exact row saturation and nonzero parameter fibers force
  separation rank at least three for the official Shape-A bidegree.
- **Dependency:** all-excess common-biform equivalence and its inherited
  Shape-A row/fiber conditions.
- **Falsifier:** a rank-at-most-two biform satisfying both hypotheses, a
  shared pencil root not producing a zero fiber, more than three disjoint
  `m`-root types in `3e` slopes, or a projective row type occurring on more
  than `n` rows without global proportionality.
- **Status effect:** one PROVED background node; the critical target remains
  open.
- **Nonclaims:** no exclusion of tensor rank three or higher and no full-rank
  theorem for `K_all`.
