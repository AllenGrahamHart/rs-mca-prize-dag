# Result

The entire clean `nu=3` cubic case is impossible. Positive valuation now has
only two strata: `(nu,deg H)=(1,2)` and `(2,1)`. In the latter, the tangent
polynomial has exactly three distinct roots, its radical is the printed cubic
`X^2H-kappa`, and its multiplicity factor is the Euler derivative `V`.
The `nu=0` strata and these two positive strata remain open, so L1 remains
open.
