# Audit - L1 m=4, h=3 tangent-radical exclusion

1. The proof is restricted to `nu>0`; only there does the constant identity
   force `b!=0`.
2. `a!=0` comes from the Mason/Wronskian theorem.
3. `g(y_0)!=0` uses distinct outer roots and `y_0!=0`; it is not assumed.
4. Every root of `T` is nonzero because `T(0)=3b`.
5. At a tangent root, `U=y_0/X^nu`; no division by zero occurs.
6. `P=X^nu H-kappa` is nonzero and has exact degree `nu+eta` because its
   constant is nonzero.
7. The derivative identity uses `R'=X^(nu-1)V`, not `X^nu V`.
8. `T'` is nonzero because the Euler theorem proves `V!=0`.
9. A multiplicity divisible by `p` could only be `p`; that would consume the
   full degree and make `T'=0`, so the standard radical-gcd degree identity
   applies.
10. The key comparison is `p-(nu+eta)<=p+eta-4`, yielding
    `nu+2eta>=4`.
11. Equality at `(2,1)` pins both the cubic radical and the degree-`p-3`
    multiplicity factor.
12. No claim is made for `nu=0`, where `b=0` is possible.
