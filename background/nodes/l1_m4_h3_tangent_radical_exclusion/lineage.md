# Lineage

The Euler quotient factorization introduced the degree-`p` tangent factor
`2aR+3b` but did not use its roots. Evaluating at those roots places all of
them on one cubic-or-smaller equation. Comparing that radical cap with the
exact degree of the derivative multiplicity factor produces the inequality
`nu+2deg(H)>=4`, closing the clean valuation and most lower positive strata.
