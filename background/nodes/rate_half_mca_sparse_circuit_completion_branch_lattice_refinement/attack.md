# Attack record

A first completion ladder may stop after finding one near-saturated source,
but the completion maxima at other supports still exist on that terminal
leaf.  Refining only the expensive leaves avoids the combinatorial size of a
full Cartesian product while retaining the same exact information wherever
it affects the maximum.

For the `K'=44` application, two support-five terminal leaves are each
refined by the five support-six alternatives.
