# Proof

The common compiler gives six necessary and sufficient minors on the
rank-six stratum.  Dividing their printed guard factors and adjoining
`zG-1`, where `G` is the product of all source and target guards, preserves
the localized zero set.

The bounded Modal map reconstructs this full ideal independently for cells
`0,3,4,9,11,12,14`, always with signs `(-1,-1)`.  Singular returns
`dim(G)=1`, a nonunit basis, and no stderr in all seven cases.  The exact
basis sizes are `7,23,24,23,26,29,31`.  Therefore each localized full ideal
is proper of Krull dimension one.

The root-sign quotient proves that the selected rows represent all seven
remaining symmetry orbits.  Properness is preserved by those exact
coordinate isomorphisms.  No assertion about base-field rational points is
needed or made. QED.
