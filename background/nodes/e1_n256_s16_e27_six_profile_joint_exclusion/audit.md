# Audit

The production engine uses folded signed chord classes. The independent audit
forms all 49 ordered products of the seven sparse coefficients in the
negacyclic ring and checks anti-reciprocity before reading the profile. Both
engines enumerate all eight light templates, `binom(124,3)` heavy supports,
and 64 relative sign vectors, and agree on every output row after removing
runtime metadata.

The deterministic verifier replays every one of the 2,000 printed exceptional
vectors from its sparse coefficients, recomputes its profile, conductor, and
third moment, and checks the exact aggregate ledger. It also verifies that the
404 norm inputs are exactly the full-conductor subsequence of that ledger.

FLINT and PARI use separate resultant implementations. Their 404 outputs agree
in order; the maximum is independently bounded by the integer inequalities in
the statement. The local audit checks source separation and stored-result
agreement without rerunning either census.
