# Result

For every squarefree double-root survivor,

```text
J=gcd(Lambda,g_*S_B^2)=gcd(Lambda,g_*),
deg J=d_A in {0,1}.
```

Thus `d_A=0` has a nonzero scalar remainder quotient and `d_A=1` has a
nonzero quotient of degree at most one. The overlap degree is no longer an
independent branch variable.
