# Audit

1. Petal labels are nonzero in the sunflower construction; division by
   `c_1` is therefore legitimate.
2. The background condition is not optional: `r=b` forces every background
   point to be an agreement, hence a root of `W`.
3. Pairwise coprimality of `L_0,L_1,L_2` follows from disjoint background and
   petal point sets.
4. Dimension `ell-1` is for the vector slice. Requiring `F` to be monic gives
   an affine section of dimension at most `ell-2`, but no nonemptiness or
   transversality is claimed.
5. Exact touched-petal support and first ownership are exclusions inside the
   guarded slice, not consequences of the linear equations.
6. The replay checks the petal-only and guarded ranks independently and
   includes the exact official source arithmetic.
