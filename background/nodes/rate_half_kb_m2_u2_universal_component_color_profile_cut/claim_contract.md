# Claim contract

- **Claim ID:**
  `rate_half_kb_m2_u2_universal_component_color_profile_cut`
- **Input:** an actual residual degree-two source component, its universal
  five-profile source-facet census, and the exact component edge coloring.
- **Output:** the outside-`K` deficit vector is the left colored-degree
  vector of a four-edge subgraph of the two-regular pole graph; exactly
  three sorted `K`-fiber degree profiles remain.
- **Deletion:** the profiles `(0,4,4,4,4,4)` and `(1,3,4,4,4,4)` are
  impossible for every stabilizer type.
- **Not claimed:** realization or deletion of a surviving profile,
  component/type deletion, kernel failure, owner, payment, row, or Prize
  close.
