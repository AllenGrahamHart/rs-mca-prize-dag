# Claim contract

## New proved content

1. Oriented singleton values biject with `F_257^*`.
2. A 17-term nonzero residual has a unique oriented completion.
3. One explicit 18-term completion has exact local multiplicity one.
4. Galois transport populates the root/local gate at all 128 split ideals.

## Guards

- Completion may fail when the residual is zero or its unique position is
  already occupied; no universal completion count is claimed.
- The explicit family has energy 1478, outside the live norm window.
- A root of `F mod 257` is not by itself a cofactor-514 collision.
- The result is a no-go fence for a proof strategy, not a counterexample to
  the five-ideal occupancy target.
