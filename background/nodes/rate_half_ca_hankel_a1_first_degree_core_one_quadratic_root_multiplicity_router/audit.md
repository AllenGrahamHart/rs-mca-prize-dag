# Audit

- The core-one identity is `u+v=e+2`, not the core-free cubic identity
  `e+1`; this shifts the omission to `O=u-4`.
- The new-root lower bound cancels `C_tot` and its unknown gap exactly. It
  does not assume full regular-rank degree.
- The upper bound `(QRM5)` is used only at simple residual roots. No simple
  estimate is imported into the double-root branch.
- Positive `I_E` is checked before concluding that a residual root is
  heavy.
- The theorem is a uniform router over more than thirty-six billion gap
  values, not an exclusion or a packet enumeration.
