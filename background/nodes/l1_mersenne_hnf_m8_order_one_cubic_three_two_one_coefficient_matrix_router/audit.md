# Audit

The Cramer equations (CMR3) are equivalent only on `Delta!=0`. The
determinant-zero locus is retained and split in (CMR4); it is not discarded
as a generic saturation.

In the `J!=0` singular branch, retain the second equation of (CMR2). The
conditions `Delta=W=0` alone do not impose the required null vector. In the
`J=0` branch, equations (CMR7) do not replace `E_6`, the role equation, the
conic, or arithmetic lift filters.

The `x=0` cubic and the bivariate singular endpoint are exact reductions,
not evidence of emptiness. Any computed unit claim must include a replayable
certificate and official-characteristic denominator audit.
