# Result

The fiberwise omitted-recurrence freedom has been removed. Once the
coefficient-MDS and scalar-weld gates recover the unique projective row
vector `lambda`, every excess residual `H_delta` is its residual RS
interpolant, and its degree drop is the exact initial zero-run of the
explicit parity rows `(RWF9)` applied to that same vector.

The remaining Shape-A task is therefore concrete: prove that one passing
`lambda` cannot satisfy enough rows of the stacked residual-MDS flag to
support the concentrated norm ledger. No independent per-fiber residual
choice remains.
