# Statement

There is an exact principal-system bijection from positive `433-1b` role cell 5 to role cell 8:

```text
B <-> C,
r -> r,
t -> t,
(epsilon_1, epsilon_2) -> (-epsilon_1, epsilon_2),
(D,E,F) -> sigma_c (D,E,F),
BF <-> sigma_c CF.
```

It bijects the four source-sign rows, fixes both target-lane signs, preserves all common and outside rows and guards, and bijects every one of the 105 outside labels. Thus it transports all `4*4*105=1680` signed principal systems. Together with the proved global rank-drop theorem, complete cell-5 exclusion implies complete cell-8 exclusion.
