# Claim contract

## Proved

- an exact `2(n-c)/(m-c)` incidence bound for one subcritical irreducible
  clone component;
- the deployed simplification `N_C<=2c`;
- a simultaneous all-subcritical-class bound `2n=4194304`;
- uniqueness of any remaining class of size at least `m`.

## Not proved

- routing of identically-zero, vertical, or horizontal component branches;
- payment or absorption of the unique large clone component;
- that `2n` may be added to unrelated first-match costs without an allocation;
- complete coordinate-clone or `(E)` routing.
