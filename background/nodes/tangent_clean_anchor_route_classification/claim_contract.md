# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the six clean candidate predecessor agreements pinned by the XR consumption
  replay;
- `rs_tangent_flexible_budget_unsafe_floor`;
- the actual ambient field order `q` or its exact numerator budget.

## Output

- an if-and-only-if numerical applicability test for the tangent supplier;
- exact field-order cutoffs for all six clean predecessor formulas;
- rejection of this theorem's guaranteed `e`-slope payload at the two named
  high-budget envelopes.

## Guards

1. `a` is agreement, while upstream tangent notation counts errors.
2. The conversion is `e=n-a`, not `e=a`.
3. Strict unsafety requires `e>B*`, not `e>=B*`.
4. The cutoff is `e*2^128-1`; equality at `e*2^128` fails.
5. Route failure is never emitted as a safe upper bound.

## Nonclaims

- No named high-budget clean anchor is paid.
- No upper bound on the full bad set of the constructed tangent line is proved.
- No quotient, E1, occupancy, or safe-side statement is proved.
- The six candidate formulas are not asserted to cover every admissible row
  descriptor without the universal row compiler.
