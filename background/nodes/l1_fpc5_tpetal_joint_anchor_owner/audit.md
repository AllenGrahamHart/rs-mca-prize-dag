# Audit

The background identity uses the complete anchor background zero set, not an
arbitrarily selected subset. Core/background/petal disjointness makes both
`F` and `Lambda` units at those points. Squarefreeness of the evaluation
domain turns root equivalence into polynomial gcd equality.

The owner-containment dimension is a coordinate-space statement. It does not
assert independence of the affine equations at background points outside
the anchor zero set and does not count split locators.
