# Claim contract

- **Input:** one general `t`-petal primitive anchor Pade chart and its exact
  background zero set.
- **Output:** one gcd coordinate recovering joint defect/background overlap,
  exact dimensions for owner-containment strata, and affine equations for
  all non-anchor background roots.
- **Preserved:** field, source partition, locator and numerator, anchor,
  background points, exact roots, and contributor identity.
- **Nonclaim:** a split-point bound, owner aggregation, first-owner ledger,
  source payment, or deployed row.
