# Claim contract

- **Input:** one surviving arbitrary-`t` full-petal cell, one exact anchor,
  one distinct exact candidate, and its canonical exact joint owner.
- **Output:** a canonical injective reduction to a primitive determinant
  equation `AV-CU=Lambda K`, and inside every fixed owner chamber the
  two-generator coordinate `K_0(C,V)=K(C_0,V_0)+T(A,U)`, with exact petal
  congruences and `deg K,deg T<=r-deg Q`.
- **Preserved:** field, source partition, touched petals and values, exact
  candidate identity, split locator factors, primitivity, and owner degree.
- **Nonclaim:** same-domain divisor census, converse lifting,
  determinant-representation count, owner aggregation, remaining
  background-root payment, first-match chronology, complete-cell payment,
  or deployed row.
