# Audit

The factor `E` contains only background roots shared with the anchor. A
candidate's additional background roots remain the affine equations from
the joint-owner theorem and are not silently absorbed into the determinant
tail.

The reduced determinant represents the touched-petal locator `Lambda`, while
the varying locators split on the disjoint core. Przemek's active
split-pencil census instead asks the varying locator to divide the same
domain locator represented by the determinant. The two-generator algebra is
shared, but the census is not identical. At bounded co-deficiency the tail
`K` has bounded degree but need not split or be coprime to `Lambda`. No such
property is used.

The fixed-owner identity `(SP8)` involves division by the fixed polynomial
`K_0` when reconstructing `(C,V)`. Divisibility is automatic for actual
candidates but is a nontrivial guard in any converse count.

The reduction is injective in the forward direction. A converse would need
all degree, exact-splitting, primitive, background, and first-owner guards;
none is claimed. In particular, `(SP10)` is not itself a census theorem and
cannot be summed over owner divisors.
