# Claim contract

- **Claim:** neither core-one quadratic `u=4` arm admits two actual error
  supports with union `rho+2`; the uniform pair floor is `rho+3`.
- **Inputs:** exact actual/padded support distinction and total deficit
  `e-6`, the minimum-pair clone classification, light-row degree `e`, the
  fixed core point, and rate-half minimum distance `2rho+1`.
- **Output:** pair floor `(QSX1)`, one-third center-line cap `(QSX2)`, and
  the `2e+3`-scale expanding-third bound `(QSX3)`.
- **Falsifier:** a retained sharp pair for which the common clone-root
  slopes do not lie on the endpoint center pencil, a noncore actual error
  point that is not light, or a line missing-incidence count compatible
  with packet-wide deficit `e-6`.
- **Nonclaims:** no exclusion at pair union `rho+3`, no rank-two row-fibre
  classification, and no complete quadratic-packet or crossing closure.
