# Claim contract

## Proved

- The heavy orientation retains at least 322,417,998 records after singleton
  pair types are removed.
- Its heavy pair-core intersection has size below `K-2`.
- A densest heavy pair owns at least 5,525 actual records.
- At most five heavy pair types recover the complete heavy-core
  intersection.
- Thirty-two actual records can be chosen with at least 24 on one pair line,
  one off-line, and exact support intersection below `K-2`.
- Exact cancellation preserves slopes and support-wise MCA badness and
  forces residual slope degree `24..31`.

## Required semantics

- Pair and record ownership are the deterministic first-owned choices from
  the ruling router.
- Heavy means at least two distinct owned slopes.
- Cancellation is applied to the exact intersection of the selected
  supports, not to an unrelated maximal-support core.

## Not claimed

- A whole-line first owner for all packets or all heavy ruling planes.
- Payment of any partial-relative, S/A/E, or high-complexity branch.
- Closure of the heavy plane, rank eleven, the crossing, or MCA.
