- **Claim:** after every classified-row, exceptional-row, and padded-heavy
  intersection is removed from the extremal resultant, the quotient has
  degree at most four.
- **Inputs:** the coprime-resultant `4+r_bad` ledger and the paired
  all-excess residual-fiber factorization.
- **Outputs:** `(EFC1)--(EFC4)` and the fact that no ordinary supported
  off-line fiber contributes a further common point beyond actual support
  and padding; the residual projective intersection cycle has degree four.
- **Quantifiers:** both extremal `d_A` profiles on the official row.
- **Falsifier:** a common root outside `A_delta R_delta` on an off-line
  supported fiber, a residual projective intersection degree other than
  four, or any mandatory padding factor missing from the resultant.
