# Audit

1. Positive-excess padding is added only after the all-excess factorization
   proves it is a genuine split-biform factor.
2. Padding roots lie outside `U_0`; they do not overlap the classified or
   exceptional row factors.
3. The all-excess theorem separately proves `gcd(B_delta,H_delta)=1`; the
   four-core does not reserve capacity for such roots.
4. Repeated domain coordinates on different slopes are counted with their
   full resultant multiplicity, so the product over slopes is valid.
5. The four is an upper bound. Leading-coefficient degree drops may make
   `W_4` smaller.
6. Actual-support tangency is not charged because the universal first-jet
   theorem proves transversality at every such point.
7. The exact projective count subtracts one copy at each padding point;
   any higher padding multiplicity remains in the effective four-cycle.
   Points at either projective infinity are retained.
