# Audit

1. The `d=1` specialization removes the field factor exactly; no field-size
   asymptotic is used.
2. The six `Lambda` values and their monotonicity are checked by exact
   integer arithmetic over the finite printed list.
3. The first five tiers improve the banked `N_0=256` frontier.  The sixth
   tier equals, rather than strictly improves, that frontier.
4. Unsafe lower bounds do not imply any safe-side upper bound or determine
   the adjacent crossing.
