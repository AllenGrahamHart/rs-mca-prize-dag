# Claim contract

- **Claim:** no quadratic `u=4` pair has actual-support union `rho+3`;
  every pair has union at least `rho+4`.
- **Inputs:** bidirectional heavy-incidence localization, exact global light
  degree `e`, packet deficit `e-6`, minimum distance `2rho+1`, and linearity
  of each residual coordinate on a codeword line.
- **Outputs:** pair floor `(QME1)`, four-per-center line cap `(QME2)`, and the
  strengthened triple-expansion count `(QME3)`.
- **Falsifier:** a residual slope supporting a common-intersection point, a
  positive-deficit slack slope supporting such a point, or a slack census
  satisfying `(7)` or `(12)` on the official row.
- **Nonclaims:** neither quadratic root pattern is excluded and the
  `rho+4` rank-three boundary is not classified here.
