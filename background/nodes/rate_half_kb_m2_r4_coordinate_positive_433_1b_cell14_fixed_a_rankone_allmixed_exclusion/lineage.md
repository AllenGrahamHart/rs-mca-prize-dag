# Lineage

The preceding fixed-`a` theorem isolated nine all-mixed structural shapes,
expanding to 144 sign/lane cases. A complete double resultant produced a
nonzero parameter eliminant in every shape, but eight roots in the first pilot
made all specialized pair resultants vanish. Direct sampling found no original
equation solution.

Exact gcd diagnostics showed that the four projected components shared a
small weighted-homogeneous factor. Treating that factor as a possible solution
component, rather than discarding it as a resultant artifact, supplied the
final proof: its weighted fibers have no field roots in the original equations,
and the factor-removed residual systems have no field roots either. The same
split closes all 144 cases.
