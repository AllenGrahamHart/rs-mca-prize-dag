# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the complete E20 six-profile/154-template router;
- the proved proper-conductor collision exclusion;
- the collision norm criterion and pair-feasible prime threshold.

## Output

No profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate at `V=40` collides on the pair-feasible branch.

## Nonclaims

No lower variance, profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform pair allowance,
row, or prize terminal is closed here.

## Falsifier

A missed vector, engine disagreement, conductor leak, nonpositive resultant,
norm at least `2^250`, or field/normalization mismatch refutes the result.
