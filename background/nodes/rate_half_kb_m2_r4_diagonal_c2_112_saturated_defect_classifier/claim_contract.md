# Claim contract

- **Claim ID:** `rate_half_kb_m2_r4_diagonal_c2_112_saturated_defect_classifier`
- **Input:** a saturated diagonal `(1,1,2)` packet, and optionally the
  source-line branch.
- **Output:** exact square-vertex weights, residual defect one, edge types,
  three `J_0` profiles, universal packet count `1560/123`, and source-line
  count `96/12`.
- **Deletion interface:** reject a saturated packet outside the applicable
  exact list.
- **Non-output:** no realization or universal deletion, no claim about the
  exceptional orbit, branch, orientation, owner, payment, row, or Prize.
