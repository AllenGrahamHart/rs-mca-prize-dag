# Audit

1. The square vertices have weight exactly two, not merely at least two;
   a third occurrence alone exceeds the residual defect budget.
2. A common-`K` quartic with two `J_1` roots could a priori factor as one
   `J_1-J_1` and one `J_0-J_0` edge. The defect budget excludes this before
   the mixed-edge classification.
3. The counts classify edge multisets, not ordered stars and not algebraic
   components. `1,560` is the labeled multiset count.
4. The universal orbit group preserves the printed `tau` matching on
   `J_0`; quotienting by all of `S_4` would incorrectly give 45 orbits.
5. Source-line conditions are not imported into the biquadratic branch.
6. A repeated mixed edge in the source-line branch costs two collisions
   because its transported partner repeats at distinct source slots.
7. The exceptional `(KBDM-10)` orbit is outside the theorem.
