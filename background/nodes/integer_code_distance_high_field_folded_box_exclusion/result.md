# Result

The complete order-128 folded cube is collision-free beyond antipodal
relations whenever `p>253^32`. This replaces an exact-SVP computation on the
high-field branch by a short Parseval proof.

`integer_code_distance_cert` remains `TARGET`: its lower-field and other-order
rows are not covered, its adopted-row registry is not pinned, and a kernel
certificate alone does not establish the required value-set budget.
