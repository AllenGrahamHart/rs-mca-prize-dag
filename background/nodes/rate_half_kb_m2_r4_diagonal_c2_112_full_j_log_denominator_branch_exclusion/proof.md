# Proof

The router gives two degree-7 factors exchanged by `c<->d` and two
degree-11 factors exchanged in the same way. The product in each pair is
symmetric, so exact descent to `s=c+d,p=cd` represents the union of its two
zero loci without selecting a root ordering.

On the existing `V!=0` route, substitute `w=-U/V` and clear the exact power
of `V`. Factoring each result and deleting only factors already inverted by
the transported named chart gives the printed essential equations. Add each
one to the exact ideal generated by the surviving cubic resultant factor,
the essential `E2,E3` rows, and the transformed full-`J` degree-11 factor.

The four resulting Groebner bases have sizes `17,36,22,45`. They are not
unit ideals before localization, but sequential reduction of the complete
square-free transported named product reaches zero in every case. Thus
every algebraic point of each intersection lies on a forbidden named
boundary. The named-open intersections are empty, proving the claim. QED.
