# Claim contract

## Proved

1. The nine terminal degree-10 primitive groups are almost simple with
   socle A5, A6, or A10 and have the printed subdegrees.
2. If the block-kernel projection is trivial, the full action is the A6 or
   S6 degree-60 flag action, and neither action has subdegree four.
3. Otherwise the derived block kernel is subdirect in six simple socles.
4. Scott-strip supports have common size 1, 2, 3, or 6; size one is
   incompatible with the actual transverse size-four suborbit.
5. Every socle automorphism is realized in the degree-10 action and every
   action centralizer is trivial.
6. Scott strips of size t preserve synchronized monodromy blocks of size t.
7. Every m=10 terminal therefore routes strictly to inner degree 2, 3, or
   6; no independent m=10 producer remains.
8. The independent transverse frontier has 18 types in degrees 2,3,4,6.

## Imported proved inputs

- the terminal geometric decomposition and actual transverse size-four
  suborbit from rate_half_kb_source_pencil_rank_transverse_compiler;
- the complete GAP primitive degree-10 catalogue;
- Scott's subdirect-product lemma;
- the monodromy block-system/function-decomposition correspondence and
  Luroth's theorem.

## Not proved

The theorem does not say that the endpoint lacks every degree-10
decomposition; it says such a decomposition is never the terminal route.
No claim is made for closure of degrees 2,3,4,6. No owner, carrier/data
bridge, charge, u=2 close, cap 68, adjacent certificate, or KoalaBear row
close is asserted.
