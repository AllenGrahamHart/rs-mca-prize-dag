# Audit

- The order-120 outer point-stabilizer ceiling does not force a nontrivial
  kernel for A5 or S5. Those two kernel-free exceptions are treated
  separately; they are not silently discarded.
- When the kernel is trivial, the exact global actions are A6 and S6 on
  point/two-subset flags. Their degree-60 subdegree lists are reconstructed,
  not inferred from the degree-10 catalogue.
- Faithfulness on one size-ten block is proved after N=1: the only extra
  order possibility would require a normal subgroup of order two in S5.
  It is not inferred merely from faithfulness on the six outer blocks.
- Scott support size one is excluded using transversality of the actual
  quartic suborbit. Merely knowing its size is not enough.
- The action-twist issue is load-bearing. Aut(A6), including its exceptional
  outer automorphisms, is realized by the printed degree-10 Aut(A6) action.
- Trivial centralizer uses the unique fixed point in every printed socle
  subdegree row; two-transitivity is not assumed for the A5 action.
- The secondary blocks are the synchronized columns inside each Scott
  support. Their block size t, not their count, is the new inner degree.
- The result is strict routing to degrees 2,3,6. It is not geometric
  nonexistence of every degree-10 decomposition and moves no owner ledger.
- The focused replay ledger was written from direct passing executions. The
  incremental replay helper was not used because its pre-existing baseline
  contains an unrelated non-PASS WCL row; that baseline defect was not
  repaired or imported into this proof cycle.
