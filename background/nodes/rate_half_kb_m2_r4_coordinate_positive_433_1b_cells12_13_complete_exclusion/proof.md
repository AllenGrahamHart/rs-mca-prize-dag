# Proof

The cell-12 theorem excludes all 105 labels in every source-sign and target
lane, hence all `105*4*4=1680` principal systems. The exact `B/C` exchange
sends every cell-12 principal system bijectively to a cell-13 system while
preserving all common rows, outside equations, guards, signs, lanes, and the
complete label set. Therefore the principal branch is empty in cell 13.

The global product-rank-drop theorem independently excludes the lower-rank
branch in both cells. The two branches are exhaustive, so cells 12 and 13 are
both empty. QED.
