Every cleared denominator is inherited nonzero: `b!=0` comes from the
fully proportional conic equations, and `D_*!=0` is equivalent to
`3600bD!=0`. The `E_G=0` and `J_*=0` charts are retained rather than
removed during clearing.

The bivariate equations describe the coefficient portion of the endpoint.
They remain coupled to the original structural definitions, selected role
packet, `P_4`, and arithmetic-lift constraints after reconstruction.

The degree bounds are upper bounds before cancellation. They do not assert
that a resultant is nonzero or that any displayed ideal is empty.
