The factor `c+1` is an exact component of the necessary hypergeometric
curve, not a sampled pattern. Its exclusion uses the independent torsion
condition `(c-1)^n=1`, so no reciprocal-resultant implication is reversed.

The rational factorization probe is discovery evidence only. The local
verifier constructs `h!*Phi_h` with exact fractions, divides out all four
linear factors, and checks the official modular-order contradiction.

No irreducibility claim is made for `Psi_h`, either over the rationals or
after reduction to an official characteristic. The residual may and should
be decomposed further before a full order-one saturation.
