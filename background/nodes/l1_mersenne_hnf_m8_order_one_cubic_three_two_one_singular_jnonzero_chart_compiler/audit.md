# Audit

The implication `x=0 iff G_2=0` uses `Delta=0`, `J!=0`, and the inherited
`(q-d)D!=0`. It is false as an unrestricted polynomial identity.

The `x!=0` chart clears powers of `qx` only after the inherited `q!=0`
saturation and the chart condition `x!=0`. Retain `P(q^2x-P)!=0` when
reconstructing `d` and `q-d`; otherwise denominator components can be added.

The official role quadratics are irreducible over `F_p`, but `q,d,x,Y`
live in the ambient quadratic field and are not forced into `F_p` on these
charts. Therefore base-field irreducibility is not an emptiness proof here.

Neither chart is a unit verdict. A computed unit claim must cover every one
of the 21 alternative role packets and include replayable certificates;
retained points still owe all arithmetic lift filters.
