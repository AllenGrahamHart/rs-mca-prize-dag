# Claim contract

- **claim:** the exhaustive 12-type signed `(valuation,k,e)` classification,
  nonemptiness, and the seven-type non-generating subcensus;
- **scope:** official maximal rate-half rows under the existing field cap;
- **dependencies:** the complete generating branch classifier and its two
  branch order laws/certified witnesses;
- **nonclaims:** no payment of a non-generating family, no mass bound, and no
  F2/Prize close;
- **falsifier:** an official row outside the table, a prime `e=6,k=2`
  characteristic under the cap, or an empty displayed type.
