# Audit

- `P_3` is checked coefficientwise against each printed vertical divisor;
  it is not enough that its total degree is below `e`.
- The local direction for multiplicity `p` in a vertical factor of length
  `n` is the full span `s^(n-p),...,s^(n-1)`, not one direction per support
  point.
- The rank-three projection uses a reduced fibre factor outside `P_3`; the
  official bound `deg R_*>=e-6>0` supplies one uniformly.
- `R_0` is disjoint from `P_3` because its domain coordinates are outside
  the unique root of the residual linear form.
- The zero section count is recorded as a classification, not a
  contradiction or packet exclusion.
