- The implication uses only chart equations and printed target guards.
- It applies exactly to masks `FFI` and `FIF` for the canonical
  `xi=2,pairing=0` S0 representative.
- It does not apply to `FFF`, which has no infinity pair.

  `(be-cf)z2=0` and lack a printed nonzero guard for `be-cf`.
