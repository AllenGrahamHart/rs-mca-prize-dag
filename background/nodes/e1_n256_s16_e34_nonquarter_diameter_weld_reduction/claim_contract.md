# Claim contract

## Claim

Every residual nonquarter-diameter vector has the printed 31-case normal
form and satisfies the exact five-position weld disjunction. The chamber has
915,125 light supports per normal form and 1,815,608,000 signed vectors in
total. The light at `64+t` exactly distinguishes `D_64=20` from `D_64=16`.

## Dependencies

- `e1_n256_s16_e34_heavy_chord_template_reduction` for the antipodal
  nonquarter template and both forced singleton-class welds;
- `e1_n256_s16_e34_parity_profile_reduction` for four light positions, the
  light-Sidon property, and the diameter ledger.

## Nonclaims

- no assertion that every counted support realizes the welds with compatible
  signs;
- no `E=34`, profile-`(6,7)`, conductor, moment, or norm conclusion;
- no exclusion of the nonquarter-diameter template;
- no conclusion about the progression or generic templates.

## Falsifier

A residual nonquarter-diameter collision outside the 31 normal forms, failing
the weld disjunction, or with a different diameter ledger refutes the claim.
So does an error in the exact support count.
