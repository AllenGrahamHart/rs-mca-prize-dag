# Audit

The primary verifier enumerates all 341,376 heavy triples, identifies the
7,936 nonquarter-diameter triples, and proves that their symmetry quotient is
exactly the 31 printed representatives with 256 triples per representative.
It reconstructs both heavy-light weld sets, their intersection and
exceptions, the special-position distinctness, the support count, and the
DAG wiring.

The independent audit derives the weld condition from direct circular
distances and recounts it by all 32 occupancy patterns on the five special
positions. Hostile mutations deleting one common weld or allowing either
exception alone fail the two-class requirement.

No large computation is load-bearing. Both verifiers are verifier-scale and
bounded by elementary triples and 32 masks.
