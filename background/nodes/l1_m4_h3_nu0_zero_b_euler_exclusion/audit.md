# Audit

The proof uses all roots of the complete split fiber `R=0`; these roots are
distinct and lie in the nonzero multiplicative domain. The derivative of
`X^n-alpha` contributes `n=4(p+1)=4` in characteristic `p`. The factor `R`
is cancelled as a polynomial identity before evaluation. The final scalar
contradiction is valid because neither remaining characteristic is two,
three, or five.
