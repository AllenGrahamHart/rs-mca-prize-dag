# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the transverse derivative pairing at `alpha` is exactly a
  nonzero weighted evaluation pairing on `S_beta\S_alpha`; this set has at
  least `c_alpha` points, and equality identifies it with the roots of
  `R_alpha`. In the three exact packets every locator pair has union at
  least `rho`.
- **Currency:** unique decoding, dual RS syndrome moments, the first-jet
  perfect pairing, and Vandermonde invertibility.
- **Dependencies:** split-pencil equivalence, the cubic gap-one normal forms,
  and the first-jet perfect-pairing theorem.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted; `beta-alpha` is a unit in the selected
  affine chart.
- **Nonclaim:** no noncancellation beyond the equality case and no packet
  exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a transverse rank-loss slope and another supported slope
  with `|S_beta\S_alpha|<c_alpha`, or equality without the stated roots.
- **Next gate:** insert the known heavy-root location of `R_alpha` into the
  equality branch and use the weighted moments in the strict branch.
