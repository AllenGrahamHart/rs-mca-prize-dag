# Claim contract

- **Claim:** `(gamma_0,x_*)` is the unique simple large-center common point
  of `Qbar` and `P_F`, and the source numerator is nonzero there.
- **Inputs:** center Padé quotient, formal Padé resultant, and the simple
  padded-center root of `D_1`.
- **Output:** exact padded-center transversality and
  `B_src(gamma_0,x_*)!=0`.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaims:** the unique zero of the parameter-linear form
  `B_src(t,x_*)` is not identified.
- **Falsifier:** higher center resultant order, another common root over
  `gamma_0`, or vanishing of the source numerator at the padded center.
