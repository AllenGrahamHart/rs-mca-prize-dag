# Audit

- The zero fiber is unramified because all five outer zeros are simple.
- A fully split rational fiber has identity Frobenius, not merely a trivial
  cycle on one sheet.
- Component descent uses equality of arithmetic and geometric stabilizer
  orbit partitions.
- The `A5 (3),(2,2)` ratio argument checks both square-discriminant descent
  and the possible Galois swap.
- The three same-type/nonsplit twist families are retained explicitly.
- Arithmetic descent is not family deletion or payment.
