# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the exact sharp guarded cofactor congruence;
- pairwise-disjoint background and touched-petal locators;
- distinct nonzero touched labels.

## Output

The entire locator vector flat has maximal common gcd `1`.

## Nonclaims

- no split-point count;
- no assertion that every flat member has `gcd(F,W_F)=1`;
- no payment of untouched-petal or first-owner multiplicity;
- no statement about nonsharp cells.
