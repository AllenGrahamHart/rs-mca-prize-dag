# Claim contract

- **Input:** one general `t`-petal monic anchor chart with primitive anchor.
- **Output:** an explicit remainder inverse for every coordinate `H`, plus
  an exact root-local primitive guard for split squarefree locators.
- **Preserved:** field, petal labels, locator and numerator, monicity,
  contributor identity, splitness filters, and exact guards.
- **Nonclaim:** a split-point maximum, primitive-cell payment, owner sum, or
  source/profile aggregation.
