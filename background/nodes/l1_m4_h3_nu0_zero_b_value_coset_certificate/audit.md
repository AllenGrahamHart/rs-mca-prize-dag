7. Both valid quarter pairs and both roots are retained on the latter two
   characteristics.
8. Changing the choice `s -> -s` swaps the two quarter pairs and preserves
   the final invariant.
9. The finite table is exact and complete, not a sample or no-hit search.
10. The invariant is necessary only and does not construct `R`, `D`, or a
    split pencil.
