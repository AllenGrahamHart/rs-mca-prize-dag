# Claim contract

## Claim

Every `V=64` coefficient vector in either live four-odd autocorrelation
profile has one of 148 exact affine light templates, no light-light diameter,
one repeated light-chord wedge, and the printed diameter ledger.

## Dependency

- `e1_n256_s16_e32_profile_parity_diameter_reduction` for the profile, parity,
  and initial diameter interface.

## Nonclaims

- no exclusion of `(4,7)` or `(3,5,1)`;
- no signed heavy-position or moment bound;
- no result for another variance or coefficient profile;
- no promotion of either universal E1/unsafe target.

## Falsifier

A four-odd normalized light support with two light-light diameters, outside the
148 printed affine orbits, with two repeated disjoint edges, or outside the
printed diameter ledger refutes the claim.
