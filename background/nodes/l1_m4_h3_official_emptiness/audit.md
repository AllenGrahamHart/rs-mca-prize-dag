# Audit

The aggregation introduces no new algebra. Its only obligation is case
coverage. Cartier gives `nu in {0,1,2,3}`. Positive valuation is exhausted by
the tangent-radical reduction and its multiplicity successor. At `nu=0`,
`b=0` and `b!=0` are exhaustive; tangent localization partitions the latter
into degrees zero, one, two, and three, all covered by named suppliers.
