# Audit

1. The product uses 32 global-sign classes, not 64 sign vectors.
2. Distinct squared roots are exactly the reduced/nonantipodal condition.
3. The two lifts of an odd multiplier modulo 512 are consumed by the sign
   aggregation, so the quotient group has order `256*128`, not `512*256`.
4. Product-exponent parity is invariant because the subset size is even.
   The sixth-root normalization is therefore two-sector, not a single
   product-one section.
5. The norm identity squares the `K_0` norm. This changes multiplicities but
   not rational prime support.
6. The exact Burnside result was generated by Modal app
   `ap-lVlwqd9Jq78L9k2fCosqa3` in 1.173965 seconds.
7. The attempted expanded elementary-symmetric formula timed out in app
   `ap-WDu6iFzptBZRCVSDtGD5Wu`. It reached 2,079 terms after eliminating
   through `r_3`; under the preregistered rule this expanded representation
   is retired. The abstract product proof does not depend on that expansion.
8. A smaller orbit count is not automatically a smaller computation: the
   aggregate integers can be much larger and harder to factor.
