# Audit

1. The Picard section proves noninjectivity; it does not prove a numerical
   nullity or uniqueness of the section.
2. Both ambient `H^0` groups vanish because of their negative parameter
   degrees. This is the step making the long-exact-sequence kernel exact.
3. Fibrewise surjectivity is dual to multiplication by a nonzero binary form.
   Absolute irreducibility rules out a projective domain base point.
4. The source of `mu_Q` is smaller than its target for `m>=2`, so injectivity
   is dimensionally possible.
5. Negative average splitting degree is only evidence. The audit explicitly
   retains unbalanced splittings with a degree-zero summand.
6. The four-Hankel frame is extra structure on `Q`; it is not used to assert
   the still-open splitting bound.
7. The child elementary-modification theorem refutes the all-negative
   splitting route. No consumer should cite `(PMI7)` as a plausible open
   theorem after that child is available.
