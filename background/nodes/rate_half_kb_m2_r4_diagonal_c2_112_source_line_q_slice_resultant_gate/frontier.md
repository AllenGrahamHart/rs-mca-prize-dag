# Frontier

Evaluate `(KBQS-1)` on every reconstructed source form, aligned first and
then near-aligned. Factor any nonzero coefficient mismatch before attempting
the two degree-six partial resultants. A universal nonvanishing mismatch
deletes the source-line saturated `(1,1,2)` branch; an exceptional factor
becomes the exact next locus. Survivors still require both colored quotient
identities and all remaining source and endpoint rows.
