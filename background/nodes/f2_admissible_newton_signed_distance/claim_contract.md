  kernel has weight at least `2R+1` when the first `R` odd moments are imposed;
- **dependencies:** the plus-branch admissible direct-sum/half-system
  reduction and the DLI Newton short-window exclusion;

  class meeting the printed initial-run hypothesis;
- **nonclaims:** no ternary-mass bound, no `(O1)` close, no PP5.0 payment,
  no minus-branch statement, and no statement for a translated or gapped
  odd exponent set;

  the printed hypotheses.
