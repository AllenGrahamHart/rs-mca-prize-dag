# Audit

1. Injectivity uses non-invariance of `J_1` under the diagonal involution;
   importing coordinate-branch partition invariance would reverse this fact.
2. The forced and internal reciprocal orbits are distinct, so
   `chi_z(w)!=0` even when the forced orbit is ramified.
3. Edge scalars remain projective but their ratio is fixed by the nonzero
   odd part; the reconstructed `U` uses the same normalization.
4. Positive existence is only for the two-fiber coefficient problem. It
   does not imply that the resulting source form satisfies any other row.
5. The eight-candidate bound is per classified pure multiset and includes
   both signs, modulo exchange of `H` with its deck conjugate; it is not a
   realization count.
