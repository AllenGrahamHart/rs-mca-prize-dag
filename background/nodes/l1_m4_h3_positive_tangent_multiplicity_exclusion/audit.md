# Audit

At a tangent root, `X`, `g(R)`, and `X^nu` are units because `b!=0` makes
the tangent root nonzero. Since at least two tangent roots exist, every local
multiplicity is strictly below the characteristic and `T'` lowers it by one.
The correction order is therefore exactly `2e+d-1`, while the etale tangent
map has order `e`. The proof uses no value-coset census, multiplicity-triple
enumeration, or official-scale computation.
