# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the proved `V=36` endpoint;
- the proved sparse-L1 recurrence and signed-chord gate;
- the complete proved one-diameter light atlas;
- the collision norm criterion.

## Output

Every `V=34` profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate lies in one of five profiles on one
of 111 exact affine templates.

## Nonclaims

No surviving vector is excluded here. No cubic cutoff, lower variance,
profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform allowance, row, or prize terminal
is claimed.

## Falsifier

A candidate with `L>11`, a seventh energy profile, a surviving nine-odd
profile, or a light support outside the pinned one/five-odd atlas refutes this
router.
