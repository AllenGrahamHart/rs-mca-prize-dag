# Audit

1. Transposition acts on endpoint components, not directly on the old
   source equation.
2. The primed source map uses the original first endpoint coordinate as
   its new second projection.
3. All source sets and edge records are primed; no old packet determinant
   is copied unchanged.
4. Exact stabilizers, not merely subgroup containment, are transported by
   conjugation.
5. The diagonal subgroup is fixed by transposition and is not routed to a
   coordinate subgroup.
6. Geometric route equivalence does not transport an owner or payment
   without a separate chronology theorem.
