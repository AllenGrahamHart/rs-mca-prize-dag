# Frontier

The dense-regime shifted-subgroup energy estimate controls

```text
sum_kappa R(tau,kappa)^2,
```

not the maximum of one representation function. At the official density, one
8740-point exceptional fiber is too small to contradict that second moment.
The published `32N^(2/3)` subgroup-curve bound is also much larger than 8740.

The exact parameter is `lambda=kappa/tau^2`. The reciprocal degeneration
`lambda=1` is affine and can be removed immediately. A general proof needs a
pointwise dense-cyclotomy bound, a higher-moment inverse theorem with explicit
constants, or retained factor-owner semantics.
