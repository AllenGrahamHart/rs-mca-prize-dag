# Result

The type-2 spend/list ledger cannot bind anywhere on the open bracket
`[k+2^34, 3n/4)`: its per-slope floor is negative by SIGN at the
adversarial support union, `-1065151889407` at razor shape, and positivity
would need a `98.4%` support overlap nothing forces. The sign flips to
exactly `+1` at the excluded top `a = 3n/4`.

Consequently every instrument derived from `(C2)` — `(C3)`, `(C4)`, the
`X_gamma` bookkeeping, layer-A transports — is out of scope on the
bracket, and any cap on `B_ca^far` there must be built from scratch (the
fibre pigeonhole of the value ledger is exactly that). This is a route
restriction: it discharges no requirement and says nothing about the
bracket's true list size.
