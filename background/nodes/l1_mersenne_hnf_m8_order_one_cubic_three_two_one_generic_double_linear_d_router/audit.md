# Audit

Identity (GDL2) replaces `E_6` only while both `P_4=0` and the conic are
retained. The second linear equation by itself is not a sixth-coefficient
certificate.

On `C_1!=0`, `Omega=0` is required to recover the `M` line after
reconstructing `d`. On `C_1=0,M_1!=0`, both `C_1=C_0=0` remain required.
The doubly singular chart keeps all four coefficient equations
`C_1=M_1=C_0=M_0=0`; none is divided away.

For each rational reconstruction, retain the cleared nonzero numerators of
`d,q-d,Delta,W` and all inherited factors. Multiplication by powers of the
selected linear coefficient is reversible only on that chart.

Equation counts do not prove dimension or emptiness. Every official role
quadratic is an alternative packet and every retained point still owes the
full arithmetic lift.
