# Claim contract

## Input

The ambient all-bases capacity, the per-record support capacity, the actual
residual record count lower bound `R_actual>=N_min`, and the dominant
kernel-lane density.

## Output

The strict exclusion of dominant kernel-lane incidence for every
`10<=K'<=11772` by the per-corank minimum of the two summation orders.

## Nonclaim

The kernel lane remains open from `K'=11773` onward. No rank-eight,
rank-eleven, or prize closure is asserted.
