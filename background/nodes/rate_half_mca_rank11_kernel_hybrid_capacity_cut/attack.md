# Attack

Replay every row and audit the normalization in the unknown actual record
count. The hybrid ratio must be evaluated at its worst case `N_min`, not by
silently setting the actual count equal to its lower bound.

The first wall uses ambient caps for `d=1,2` and record-support caps for all
higher coranks. Both inputs are sharp in their separate abstract models.
The next useful theorem must constrain their simultaneous saturation for
one fixed residual correction space, or couple adjacent corank flats.
