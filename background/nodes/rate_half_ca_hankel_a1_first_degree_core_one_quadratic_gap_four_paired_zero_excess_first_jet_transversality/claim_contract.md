# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every actual-support root of a selected zero-excess fiber is a
  transverse intersection of the contracted locator and split-biform
  curves; common components can run only through padded roots there.
- **Inputs:** the paired padded-fiber factorization, the minimum-word
  circuit, the split-biform interpolation identity, the exact padding
  totals, and the classified row-root dictionary.
- **Outputs:** the exact first-jet mismatch `(PJT4)` and coprimality of the
  full locator and split-biform curves in both paired profiles.
- **Quantifiers:** every selected zero-excess slope in both paired profiles
  on the official row.
- **Guard:** the nonincidence Forney law is not evaluated at an incidence.
  The proof separately derives the incident identity for the minimum-word
  summand and retains the nonzero actual error term.
- **Falsifier:** a selected support root with zero Jacobian or a
  nonconstant gcd in either profile.
