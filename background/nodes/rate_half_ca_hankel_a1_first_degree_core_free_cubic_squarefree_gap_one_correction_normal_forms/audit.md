# Audit

- The proof first removes all excess degree outside the three residual-root
  rows and the possible ordinary incidence; otherwise `sum q_i=3` is false.
- `q_i` is nonnegative only because every residual root is simple in this
  branch. The theorem is not reused for the double-root row.
- An augmented new root lowers the least vertical coefficient by one; an
  augmented overlapping root raises it by two. These are separate cases.
- The corrected row contributes exactly one additional contact degree even
  when its vertical correction is `3P`.
- The Picard class has degree `e+1`, not bounded degree. It supplies no
  immediate section-vanishing contradiction.
- In the previously classified double-root branch, the canonical
  degree-one section is the reduced two-root divisor divided by `s_F`; it
  is not an independent ambient section and gives no uniqueness
  contradiction.
