# Audit

- Direct enumeration of all valid class-vector pairs at several small
  `(h,ell)` values agrees with `M_ell(a,b)` for every realized difference.
- The factor `2^r` is necessary because each common singleton coordinate has
  two signs. The independent audit includes a mutation that removes it.
- The final factor `1/2` removes only orientation. It does not quotient
  shifts, Galois actions, odd units, or stabilizers.
- Official profile maxima are computed only after applying the proved norm
  floors, including the distinct all-even thresholds and the stronger
  prize-field floor. RowC and prize rows are not conflated.
- All arithmetic is exact integer arithmetic. No random or remote computation
  is used.
- The new cap is not claimed to hold. The open task is to prove it, or prove
  the weaker weighted budget directly, for every assigned row.
