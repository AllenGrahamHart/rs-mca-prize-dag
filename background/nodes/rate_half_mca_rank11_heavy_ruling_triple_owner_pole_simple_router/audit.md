# Audit

1. Low multiplicity means one or two owners, so its total cost is
   `2Q_4`, not `Q_4`.
2. The large-core contradiction restores the complete low-multiplicity
   cost after applying the dimension-two cap to triple-owner types.
3. Every selected nonanchor pair has three actual records. The selection
   never assumes that the original merely heavy types have a third owner.
4. At most four nonanchor types are needed because component differences,
   not pair types, span a four-space.
5. Core saturation occurs after ownership. It changes neither slope nor
   explanation and is permitted by pair noncontainment.
6. The anchor count is `32-3t>=20`, so the packet remains inside the
   imported degree-`18..31` range.
7. In the rational branch the affine locator scalar has at most one zero.
   Three selected slopes per pair leave two forced supports even in that
   exceptional pair.
8. Absence of common poles implies pole roots are support-singleton, but it
   does not make `Q` root-free on the whole domain or pay the rational atom.
