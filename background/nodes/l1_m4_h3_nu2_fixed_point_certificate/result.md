# Result

Every surviving positive `nu=2` record has normalized domain exactly `cK`,
with one of `+c,-c` in the fixed split fiber and the other in the complement.
The free domain scalar is gone. Each multiplicity triple must pass one of two
explicit prime-field scalar equations and a scalar-free divisibility into
`W^n-1`, giving a substantially cheaper and more auditable next
classification target. The equations are not sufficient, the divisibility
is not classified, and L1 remains open.
