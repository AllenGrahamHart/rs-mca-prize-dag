# Audit

1. The classified grid has exactly `Rm_j` distinct factor incidences; the
   heavy row is disjoint from that row set.
2. Fiber capacity and `s_j` both count distinct roots.
3. The degree-`e-6` form `g_*` contains exactly `d_A` center factors, which
   are not in the off-line set `Gamma` and are removed in `g_off`.
4. The valid lower bound is `sum_j s_j>=e-6-d_A`; this gives a budget of
   four for `d_A=0` and five for `d_A=1`.
5. Projective bihomogeneity prevents hidden degree drop at infinity.
6. Multiplicities or roots shared by factors cannot weaken the lower bound.
7. The `d_A=1` official integer thresholds happen to be unchanged after
   parity rounding, but their symbolic numerators are reduced by two.
8. Profile exclusions use the exact trichotomy and total parameter degree,
   not numerical experiments.
