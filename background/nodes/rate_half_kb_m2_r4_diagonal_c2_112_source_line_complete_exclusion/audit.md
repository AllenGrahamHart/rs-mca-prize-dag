# Audit

1. The saturated defect classifier exhausts the source-line branch by 96
   labeled packets in 12 matching-preserving orbits; it is not sampled.
2. The colored quotient compiler partitions every orbit into the aligned or
   near-aligned quotient, with no third target type.
3. The aligned negative theorem is universal. The aligned positive branch is
   partitioned by ramification and, when unramified, by two internal templates
   and three UFD allocations. All `1+6` cells are explicit dependencies.
4. The near negative theorem includes `w=0`. The near positive branch is
   partitioned into 18 affine charts and seven homogeneous boundary shards.
   The dependency list contains every chart theorem and the boundary theorem.
5. Full-quotient exclusions are used where the q-slice itself has survivors;
   the composition never upgrades them to false q-slice-emptiness claims.
6. No conclusion is drawn about coordinate/source-cover orientations or
   packet/source-row assembly outside the source-line branch.
