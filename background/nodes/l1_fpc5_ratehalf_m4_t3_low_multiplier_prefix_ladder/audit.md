# Audit

1. `Q` denotes the Euclidean quotient polynomial; `Q_0` denotes the field
   order.
2. Monicity fixes the leading coefficient of `Q`, leaving exactly `e-a`
   free coefficients.
3. Prefix depth counts nonleading coefficients; this is why it is
   `ell+e-1`, not `ell+e`.
4. The low-degree tail `R` is unique because `deg R<=s-e`.
5. `(PL5)` is average-scale arithmetic, not a maximum-fiber conclusion.
6. The split-core and gcd guards are not used to create the prefix cells.
