# Source Evidence

The proof sources are the required theorem nodes in `dag.json`.  The primary
verifier reconstructs their exact `(xi,matching,source-sign,target-lane)`
scopes and proves a disjoint 1,680-element union.  The independent audit
checks the printed supplier scopes and rank-drop cell list directly.

No new computational output is load-bearing beyond the hash-pinned exact
certificates already owned by the supplier nodes.
