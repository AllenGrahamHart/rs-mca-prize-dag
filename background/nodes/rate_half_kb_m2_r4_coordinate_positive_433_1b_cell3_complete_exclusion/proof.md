# Proof

Let `P` be the five-row common product block.  It has at most rank five, so
the disjoint alternatives are `rank(P)<=4` and `rank(P)=5`.

For the first alternative, the product-rank-drop common exception classifier
computes the exact guarded ideal generated by all six maximal product
cofactors and all 45 maximal full-common minors.  Its cell list of unit ideals
contains cell 3, for all four source-root sign rows.  Thus no cell-3 packet
lies on the rank-drop branch before an outside assignment is needed.

On `rank(P)=5`, the common quadratic quotient and signed atlas enumerate the
outside data by

```text
y=(de,de,-de,df,sigma_o ef,bf,sigma_c cf).       (1)
```

Choosing one missing row `xi` and a perfect matching of the other six is an
exhaustive necessary placement ledger.  There are seven missing choices and
fifteen matchings.  Four source signs and four target lanes give 1,680 raw
systems.

For `xi in {0,1,2}`, the first-pair aggregate proves matchings `0,1,2` empty
and the twelve pairing-specific theorems prove matchings `3,...,14` empty.
These scopes are disjoint and pay `3*15*16=720` systems.

For `xi=3`, the six matching blocks have scopes

```text
{0}, {1,2}, {3,6}, {11,14}, {7,8,10,13}, {4,5,9,12}.
```

They are disjoint and their union is `{0,...,14}`, paying 240 systems.  The
outside-role transport theorem maps `xi=4` bijectively to this full `xi=3`
slice while fixing the common cell, target lane, guards, and matching index,
so it pays another 240 systems.  The finite-source theorem excludes all 240
`xi=5` systems, and the endpoint compatibility theorem excludes all 240
`xi=6` systems before matching specialization.

Thus the principal suppliers form a disjoint cover of

```text
{0,...,6} * {0,...,14} * {source signs} * {target lanes},
```

and every covered system is empty.  Both rank alternatives are empty, so the
complete deployed cell-3 route is empty. QED.
