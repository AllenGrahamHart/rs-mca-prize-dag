# Audit

1. The 55 shadows are the 55 omitted coordinate pairs of one eleven-set.
2. A pair is rank eight exactly when it is disjoint from the unique circuit.
3. The resulting low-support multiplicities are `19,27,34,40`; the high
   minimum is 45 at support six.
4. Every incidence contributes its own marked shadows to the global chart
   ledger.  No injectivity stronger than the existing marked capacity is
   assumed.
5. The algebra adds only `45-q_c`, not another full unit cost 45.
6. Alternative sparse branches are maximized recordwise before multiplying
   by the number of records.
7. The floor is taken only after all mark and premium capacities are added.
8. Kernel incidences are outside this ledger and must be paid separately.
