# Attack notes

The K'=14 direct generalization failed only after adding a complete
recordwise sparse cap to `G/45`.  That sum forgets that every sparse
incidence also creates between 19 and 40 rank-nine shadows already counted
inside `G`.

The repair is not a sharper geometric estimate.  It is the exact linear
combination (3), which gives sparse class `c` only its missing premium
`45-q_c`.  Keeping all four sparse support strata separate is essential;
replacing them by one total would lose their different premiums.

The ledger is reusable for any future sparse theorem that supplies a
recordwise vector rather than just one aggregate cap.
