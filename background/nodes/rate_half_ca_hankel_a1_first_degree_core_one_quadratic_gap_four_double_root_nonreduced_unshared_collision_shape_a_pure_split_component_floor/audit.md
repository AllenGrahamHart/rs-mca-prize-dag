# Audit

1. `a_delta=0` gives at least `2e` slopes; subtracting at most `e-7`
   positive-padding slopes gives `e+7`, not merely `e`.
2. Zero excess forces `H_delta` constant and `q_delta=0`; zero padding then
   makes the complete degree-`n` fiber equal to its classified-row locator.
3. Absolute irreducibility is proved from all `Rm` classified grid points;
   irreducibility over `F(X)` alone is not silently upgraded.
4. The diagonal multiplicity is `m`, the parameter degree of `G`, so the
   resultant bidegree drops from `mn` to `m(n-1)`.
5. Pair occurrences are divided by `m`, since two exact-degree row
   polynomials can share up to `m` slopes.
6. The cover has degree `n` over the parameter line, hence at most `n-1`
   off-diagonal components. The symbols `m` and `n` are not interchanged.
7. The point floor is not fed into the low-degree torus theorem: the
   component bidegree remains uncontrolled and macroscopic.
