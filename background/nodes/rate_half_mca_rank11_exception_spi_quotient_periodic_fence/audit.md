# Audit

1. Only powers of two `e<=11` are used, so `e` divides the official
   power-of-two domain order.
2. The roots of `X^e-y` are counted inside the evaluation group, not merely
   over an algebraic closure.
3. Squarefreeness uses that the field characteristic is coprime to `N`.
4. Distinct fibers are disjoint by the homomorphism, so no support overlap is
   hidden.
5. The examples satisfy the abstract exception-SPI equations but do not
   construct the received line, rational certificate, factor ownership, or
   chronology of an MCA counterexample.
6. The result fences a naive fiber-count proof; it does not say that every
   surviving pencil is quotient-periodic.
