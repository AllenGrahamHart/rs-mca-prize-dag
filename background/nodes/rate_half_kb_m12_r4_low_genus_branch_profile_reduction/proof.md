# Proof

## 1. Genus bound from the actual component

The retained equality-wall geometry supplies an actual irreducible curve
`H0` of bidegree `(u,2u)=(2,4)`. The quadratic base-change argument maps
`H0` birationally to the bidegree-`(4,4)` self-correspondence component
`Gamma`. Hence, on normalizations,

```text
g(Gamma)=g(H0)<=(2-1)(4-1)=3.                       (1)
```

In the live `r=4` inner-degree-12 type, the transverse compiler gives a
finite map

```text
Gamma -> C,            degree delta=12.              (2)
```

If the normalization of `C` had genus at least two, Riemann-Hurwitz would
give

```text
2g(Gamma)-2 = 12(2g(C)-2)+R >= 24,                  (3)
```

whereas `(1)` makes the left side at most four. Thus `g(C)<=1`.

## 2. Tame polynomial branch ledger

The outer map `F` has degree five and one pole of order five. The pole is
rational, but only geometric information is needed here: after a domain
normalization, `F` is a degree-five polynomial. Since the deployed
characteristic is greater than five, the cover is tame. Its branch cycle at
infinity is a 5-cycle `c` and contributes index four. Riemann-Hurwitz for
`F:P^1->P^1` gives total index eight, so the nonidentity finite branch
cycles `sigma_1,...,sigma_s` satisfy

```text
sum index(sigma_i)=4,
sigma_1 ... sigma_s = c^(-1).                        (4)
```

The `r=4` component is the entire geometric off-diagonal component. Thus
the geometric monodromy is two-transitive on five letters. The normalization
of `C`, viewed over the output line of `F`, is the transitive cover on

```text
Omega={(i,j): 1<=i,j<=5, i!=j},      |Omega|=20.     (5)
```

For a branch permutation `sigma`, let `ind_Omega(sigma)` be 20 minus the
number of cycles induced on `Omega`. The exact values by cycle type are

```text
cycle type       (2)  (2,2)  (3)  (3,2)  (4)  (5)
ind_Omega          7      10   12      15   15   16. (6)
```

Riemann-Hurwitz on the 20-sheet ordered-pair cover is

```text
g(C)=1-20+(16+sum ind_Omega(sigma_i))/2.             (7)
```

## 3. Complete degree-five enumeration

Fix one 5-cycle `c`. Enumerate every ordered tuple of nonidentity
permutations in `S_5` whose positive indices form a composition of four,
retain exactly the tuples satisfying `(4)`, and compute the subgroup
generated by `c,sigma_1,...,sigma_s`. Requiring its point stabilizer to be
transitive on the other four letters leaves 310 ordered tuples. They have
exactly seven profile/group/genus rows:

```text
group  finite profile              genus   ordered tuples
A5     (3),(2,2)                       0       10
A5     (3),(3)                         1        5
S5     (2),(3,2)                       0       10
S5     (2),(4)                         0       10
S5     (2),(2),(2,2)                   1       75
S5     (2),(2),(3)                     2       75
S5     (2),(2),(2),(2)                 3      125
```

The counts total 310. There is no generated group of order 20, so tame
`AGL(1,5)` polynomial monodromy is absent. The last two rows contradict
`g(C)<=1`; the first five are exactly the statement's surviving profiles.
QED.
