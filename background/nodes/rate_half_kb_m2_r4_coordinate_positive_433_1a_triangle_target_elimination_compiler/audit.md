# Audit

1. Templates A and B are hypotheses, not a claimed deployed-field matching
   classification.
2. The identities divide only during their derivation; all divisions are by
   nonzero target products or supported values of `A_2`, and the final cuts
   clear those denominators.
3. The theorem uses one residual squared-sum row.  It does not infer
   unsquared `q` signs from a squared row.
4. The `F_29` trace motivated the two templates but is not proof input.
5. A raw seven-variable standard-basis timeout is an algorithm boundary,
   not a survivor and not an emptiness certificate.
