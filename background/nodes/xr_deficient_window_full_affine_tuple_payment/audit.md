# Audit

- The `2^s` cap counts irreducible components of the common zero set, not
  merely isolated Bezout intersections. Positive-dimensional components are
  admissible only because the distinct-pole argument makes their selected
  ray constant and the interaction strip leaves at most one target on each.
- There are `s+1` point functions plus the constant function, hence `s+2`
  affine functions in an `(s+1)`-dimensional function space. This is the
  required linear dependence.
- Pairwise distinct `phi` values are essential: they give distinct poles.
- The factor two on the left of `(4)` uses the two disjoint selected blocks.
  It is not a count of arbitrary live slopes.
- `ell=1` makes `phi` injective on the evaluation domain, but it does not make
  the target family one-dimensional.
- The first failing integer is a limitation of `(FAT3)`, not a falsifier of
  the XR target.
