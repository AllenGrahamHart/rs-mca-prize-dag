# Claim contract

| field | value |
|---|---|
| claim | The full-affine distinct-fiber tuple cap `(FAT1)--(FAT4)` and its exact official `ell=1` payment. |
| inputs | The active-defect affine family, at least two disjoint selected blocks, the `phi` fiber cap, and the same-ray interaction strip used by the affine-plane component payment. |
| output | A `2^s` owner cap for a fixed `(s+1)`-tuple and the stated adjacent-row cutoffs. |
| load-bearing hypotheses | Pairwise distinct `phi` values, invariant nonzero residual on `D`, affine dimension exactly `s`, and first-match same-ray ownership. |
| falsifier | More than `2^s` target owners for one fixed tuple, or failure of either exact cutoff comparison. |
| nonclaim | No payment beyond the printed cap failure and no assertion that the next tuple is unsafe. |
