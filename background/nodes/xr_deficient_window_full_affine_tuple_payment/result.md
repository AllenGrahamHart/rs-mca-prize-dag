# Result

Using the entire next-dimensional affine hull instead of cutting it to a
plane pays most of the previously open `ell=1` upper tail. The rate-`1/4`
and `1/8` cutoff moves upward by `1,612,954,075`; the rate-`1/16` cutoff
moves upward by `727,342,236`.

The XR target remains open above the new cutoffs and on the split-pencil and
higher-`ell` endpoints.
