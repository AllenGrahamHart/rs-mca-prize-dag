  generic degree.
- The V4 action is on the two endpoint coordinates, not the unrelated
  source deck alone.

  two projective rows are reconstructed independently.
- An outer decomposition is not counted as progress unless its endpoint
  destination is routed. Every proper factor of 30 is included.

  recurrence, not contradiction.

  not by an unsupported assertion that every lift uses the source deck.
- The divisor refinement removes both `z_i` and `z_bar(i)`; it does not
  assert a global carrier pairing.
- The weight-three deletion applies only in coordinate-stabilized rows.
