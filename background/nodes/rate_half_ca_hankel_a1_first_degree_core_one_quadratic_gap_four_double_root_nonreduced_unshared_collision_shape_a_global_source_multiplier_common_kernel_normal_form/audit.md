# Audit

1. The affine coordinate is chosen only to keep the three centers finite;
   the final ratio `varphi` and intersection are invariant under a
   projective change of parameter.
2. `J` is a unit only in the split algebra on `U_0`; no nonvanishing at
   `x_*` or away from the classified domain is asserted.
3. The sum in `E_3` is proved direct by classwise polynomial injectivity,
   not by a generic-position assumption.
4. The fixed gcd `H` is retained in the multiplier `J`; it cancels only in
   the class map `varphi`.
5. The large lower-boundary intersection is a necessary condition, not a
   contradiction from ambient dimensions.
