# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the common kernel is exactly
  `S_n intersect J(W_X+varphi W_X+varphi^2W_X)^perp`, and the three-multiplier
  space has dimension `3r`.
- **Inputs:** the parameter-linear source numerator, the exact three-class
  source partition, and the center residue-pairing router.
- **Output:** one intrinsic Padé intersection carrying the complete
  common-kernel deficit.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaim:** no upper bound on the intersection and no Shape-A exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a classified point at which `J=0`, a nontrivial relation in
  `W_X+varphi W_X+varphi^2W_X`, or a mismatch between `(GSM7)` and the three
  class maps.
