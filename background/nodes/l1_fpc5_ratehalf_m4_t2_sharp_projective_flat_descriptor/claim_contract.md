# Claim contract

## Inputs

- one official rate-half sharp `M=4,t=2` source and touched pair;
- the proved guarded pair slice with `5ell=k+4` and `d=2ell-3`;
- the exact split-core, primitive, untouched-petal, and first-owner guards.

## Output

- one affine/projective split-locator descriptor with
  `(N,j,r,s)=(5ell-5,2ell-3,ell-2,ell-1)`;
- an injective exact-contributor map with unique numerator reconstruction;
- exact maximal common-GCD division, including the punctured root domain and
  reduced codimension.

## Nonclaims

- no polynomial bound for the growing-dimensional split intersection;
- no generic Conjecture-F theorem and no new strict `conj_f` caller;
- no assertion that a common locator gcd is a tangent/common-numerator owner;
- no payment of nonsharp defect/background cells.
