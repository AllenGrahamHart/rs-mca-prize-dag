# Audit

1. `V_F` is the image of the full guarded `(F,W)` slice, not the unguarded
   two-petal envelope.
2. The monic affine chart exists only when the leading-coefficient functional
   is nonzero; otherwise the split intersection is empty.
3. Projective normalization is unique because `D_j(C)` contains monic
   degree-exact locators.
4. The numerator is reconstructed uniquely before primitive and exact
   nonagreement filters are applied.
5. Common-GCD division punctures the root domain and changes affine
   codimension from `ell-1` to `ell-1-w`.
6. A common divisor of every `F` is not asserted to divide the reconstructed
   `W`; no tangent payment is inferred.
7. The projective dimension `ell-2` grows with the row, so fixed-dimensional
   Conjecture-F bounds are not presented as a prize payment.
