# Audit

1. Splitting is taken after algebraic closure; all dimensions and splitting
   degrees used in the conclusion are invariant under that base change.
2. Basepoint-freeness is required for the quotient in `(CSB2)` to be the
   line bundle `O(m)` with locally free kernel.
3. The three pair-products form a basis of binary quadratics; mere spanning
   by three possibly dependent quadratics would not suffice.
4. Surjectivity of quadratic multiplication is exactly the vanishing of
   `H^1(E(2))`, and on `P^1` this excludes every `mu_i>=4`.
5. `dim J=c_1` uses minimal linear syzygies, not arbitrary quadratic
   Koszul relations.
6. The two minimum-rank profiles are possibilities, not existence claims.
7. No critical status changes.
