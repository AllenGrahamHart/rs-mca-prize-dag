# Attack

## Regression falsification

At small fields, enumerate five-dimensional polynomial subspaces after
factoring a fixed common locator. Record only squarefree sections whose roots
lie in the evaluation domain, reconstruct the corresponding rank-three and
rank-four flats, and deduplicate minimal circuits before applying weights.
Scale the observed saturation mass against the exact bound, not against the
number of coefficient vectors.

## Closed route

The weighted-coupling route closes the cell.  Every rich rank-three flat
that can support four-circuits forces a proportional loss of five-circuit
completions on triple-plus-outside deletions.  This avoids classifying
degree-34 split cores individually.

Future attacks should mutate the rank-three or rank-four root ceilings,
remove circuit minimality, or attempt to make one independent four-set carry
more than four rich-triple witnesses.
