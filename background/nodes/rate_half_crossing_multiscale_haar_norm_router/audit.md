# Audit

1. The node assumes `p=1 mod n`.  It does not replace the rowwise
   decomposition-group count needed for extension-field characteristics.
2. `beta_j=0` means algebraic zero before reduction.  A modular zero with
   nonzero norm is active and carries the printed `p`-divisibility.
3. Moment indices are partitioned uniquely as `2^j t` with `t` odd; no
   equation or prime ideal is counted twice.
4. Zero scales are not discarded.  Their exact dyadic cyclotomic factors
   contribute `T_J` powers of two to every active norm.
5. The shared energy is `r-r^2/n`, not `r`; the terminal constant fold is
   load-bearing.
6. Failure of the all-active inequality at `w=2^37` proves only that this
   certificate cannot exclude that pattern.  It is not a construction of a
   pattern or a claim that the crossing node is false.
7. The `w=2^38` reversal does not improve the critical frontier because the
   existing single-scale theorem already excludes that checkpoint in the
   same near-256-bit regime.
