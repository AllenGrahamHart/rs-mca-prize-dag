# Result

All dyadic moment levels admit one exact Haar-energy norm product.  It can
exclude active/zero-scale patterns more sharply than treating each level
independently.  Its all-active specialization, however, cannot fire at
`w=2^37` for any official `p<2^256`; the next power checkpoint is already
covered by the older ideal/Galois theorem.  The crossing safe-side frontier
therefore still requires a constant-weight BCH population bound, a sharper
relation instrument, or genuinely non-norm information.
