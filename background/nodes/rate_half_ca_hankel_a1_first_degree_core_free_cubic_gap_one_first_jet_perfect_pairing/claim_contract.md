# Claim contract

- **Claim:** away from the one optional residual line, every supported
  rank-loss slope has only unit Smith exponents; the derivative moment form
  gives the perfect pairing `(FJP4)--(FJP6)`, with the explicit scalar test
  `(FJP7)` at rank loss one.
- **Currency:** exact regular-determinant valuation, local Smith form, and
  the specialized apolar complete-intersection kernel spaces.
- **Dependencies:** the core-free cubic regular-factor pin, the four-packet
  normal form, and the half-distance minimal-index/apolar theorem.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** no transversality at the possible `E_1` slope, no assertion
  that all losses are one, and no packet exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a supported slope away from `E_w` whose derivative pairing
  has rank below `c_gamma`, or whose right radical is larger than the moving
  recurrence line.
- **Next gate:** combine `(FJP7)` and its higher-corank analogue with the
  heavy-row cube/contact identities and the actual endpoint source moments.
