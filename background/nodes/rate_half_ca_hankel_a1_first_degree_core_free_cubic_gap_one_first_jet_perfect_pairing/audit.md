# Audit

- Equality `ord_gamma(D_0)=c_gamma`, not mere divisibility, is what forces
  every positive Smith exponent to equal one.
- The generic kernel line remains in the right radical after differentiation;
  the Smith rank proves it is the complete radical.
- The two apolar kernel dimensions are `c+1` and `c`; the second
  complete-intersection generator begins only in degree `rho+c+1`.
- Rescaling the local parameter rescales the pairing but preserves rank and
  its radical.
- In the `w=1` packet, the exceptional line can repeat a supported factor;
  that slope is deliberately excluded from the conclusion.
