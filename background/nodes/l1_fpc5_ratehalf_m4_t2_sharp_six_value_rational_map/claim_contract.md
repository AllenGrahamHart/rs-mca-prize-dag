# Claim contract

- **Input:** one exact official sharp rate-half `M=4,t=2` contributor in a
  fixed source and touched pair.
- **Output:** an injective degree-`2ell-3` rational-map encoding with one
  complete core fiber, fixed zero/pole petal blocks, a fixed background
  block in a fourth fiber, and two exact forbidden-fiber petal blocks.
- **Preserved:** monic normalization, candidate-wise primitivity, all source
  blocks, source labels, and untouched-petal nonagreements.
- **Nonclaim:** a count of marked maps, a classification of their remaining
  zero/pole divisors, or a quotient/dihedral payment.
