# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the explicit balanced seven-mark cyclic construction realizes
  the exact abstract block sizes and point degrees of the quadratic `u=4`
  packet for every `e>=7`.
- **Currency:** one mechanical binary word, cyclic intervals, and double
  incidence counting.
- **Dependencies:** the exact quadratic gap-four incidence-center spread
  theorem, which identifies the degree sequence being modeled.
- **Characteristic:** not applicable; this is a finite set-system theorem.
- **Nonclaim:** no field labels, RS words, center line, Hankel pencil,
  minimal-index condition, or cube identity is realized.
- **Falsifier:** a length-`e` window with a mark count outside `{2,3}`, a
  number of deficient blocks different from `e-6`, or a wrong block size.
- **Route consequence:** support-degree arguments alone cannot exclude the
  retained packet.
