# Audit

- The mechanical word is extended periodically before telescoping across a
  cyclic boundary.
- The inequality `2<7e/(3e+3)<3` uses `e>=7`.
- Light points are distinct even when they share a start; each has degree
  exactly `e`.
- `x_*` is added only to the `e-6` low-light blocks, making every residual
  block the same size without changing light degrees.
- Inactive points are needed to recover the full official domain size.
- The construction is deliberately support-only and is not an RS or Hankel
  counterexample.
