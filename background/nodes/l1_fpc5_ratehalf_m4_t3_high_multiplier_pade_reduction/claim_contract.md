# Claim contract

## Input

One guarded LS6 atom with multiplier degree `e>ell-a`.

## Output

An exact bijection with quotient polynomials `Q` satisfying the remainder,
split-core, and primitive-coprimality conditions `(HP5)`--`(HP7)`, plus the
inverse-multiplier gate `deg(E^(-1) mod M)>=ell+a`.

## Preserved

- the fixed source, triple, defect, and determined cross-ratio;
- monicity and exact degree of `D`;
- the split-core divisor guard;
- the LS6 exactness guard, transported exactly to `gcd(D,Q)=1`.

## Nonclaims

- the affine `Q`-constraint has generic rank;
- the number of split quotients is polynomial;
- primitive, quotient, reciprocal, or dihedral strata are paid;
- a high-multiplier cell is an ordinary locator-prefix cell.
