# Claim contract

- **Input:** exact `(PF6)` cells at `n=8192`, their proved GRS
  constant-weight caps, and canonical first-layout domination.
- **Output:** simultaneous global caps for the complete rate-`1/8`
  `M=29..32` and rate-`1/16` `M=57..67` prefixes.
- **Preserved:** official rate, row size, field gate, source scale, touched
  count, defect, background incidence, and first-layout ownership.
- **Nonclaim:** payment of a blocked scale, a lower field slice, a larger
  official row, or the complete large-source target.
