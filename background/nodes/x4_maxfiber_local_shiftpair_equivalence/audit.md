# Audit

1. The first-match deletion must be support-wise, so each support has one
   owner before the residual graph is formed.
2. The graph identity is local and exact; no averaging or asymptotic loss is
   introduced.
3. Prefix rigidity is characteristic-free and uses full elementary
   coefficients, not Newton inversion.
4. The theorem identifies the local supplier but does not bound its degree.
5. The exact second-moment ledger remains useful evidence; without a local
   regularity or tail theorem it cannot certify the maximum.
