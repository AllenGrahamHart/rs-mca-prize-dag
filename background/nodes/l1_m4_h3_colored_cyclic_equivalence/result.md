# Result

four-color word with three colors of size `p`, zero complement of size
`p+4`, and simultaneous membership of the word and its coefficientwise
square in the explicit Mersenne cyclic code. The next theorem may attack this
two-Schur section directly; no split-pencil coefficient or value enumeration
