# Audit - L1 m=4, h=3, nu=2 prime-field Belyi normal form

1. The tangent polynomial has exactly three distinct roots by the preceding
   equality case, not by an assumption.
2. Multiplicities are positive integers summing to the exact degree `p`.
3. `P=X^2H-kappa` has no linear coefficient, so its nonzero roots have
   reciprocal sum zero.
4. The weighted reciprocal relation comes from `R'(0)=0` and `y_0!=0`.
5. Equality of all multiplicities in `F_p` would force one nonzero integer
   below `p` to vanish; the two relation rows are independent.
6. Every cross-product coordinate must be nonzero, proving pairwise
   distinctness as well as the root-ratio formula.
7. The differences in `(PBN3)` are interpreted in `F_p` before inversion.
8. Scaling contributes `lambda^p` because the multiplicities sum to `p`.
9. The subtraction of `S_e(0)` enforces `R(0)=0` and fixes the tangent value
   sign.
10. The derivative passport includes the simple critical point at zero and
    no others.
11. Prime-field normalization does not by itself make an arbitrary
    multiplicative coset Frobenius-stable.
12. The node is a discrete normal form, not an exclusion.
