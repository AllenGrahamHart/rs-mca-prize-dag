# Result

The `(nu,deg H)=(2,1)` branch is reduced to a discrete Belyi family. An
unordered pairwise-distinct positive triple `(e_1,e_2,e_3)` summing to `p`
determines all three tangent-root ratios and a prime-field polynomial
`S_e-S_e(0)`; the original inner polynomial is one scalar conjugate of it.
The remaining task is to exclude or price three complete regular fibers of
these normal forms on the official multiplicative coset. L1 remains open.
