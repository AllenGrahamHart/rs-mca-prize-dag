# Audit

The verifier pins the launcher, result, and compact-kernel input; checks the
four-sign census and exact printed torus cores; checks all embedded polynomial
digests; and requires both forced-relation remainders, the gcd identity,
nonunit dimension-one parameter ideals, and unit guard-saturated residual
ideals. Hostile mutations remove a row and alter the gcd identity, torus
core, forced relation, residual-unit flag, and parameter dimension.
