# Proof

The `SDE` outside packet contains two records with exactly the same product
`de` and squared sum `(d+e)^2`. Exchanging those copies is an exact incidence
automorphism. Likewise, `SDF` contains two identical `df,(d+f)^2` records.
Neither exchange changes common rows, source roots, coordinates, or guards.
The deterministic seven-record router shows that a single duplicate
transposition has fifteen fixed labels and 45 doubletons among the 105
missing-record/perfect-matching labels.

The B/C--E/F action exchanges `SDE` with `SDF` and transports the two `DE`
positions to the two `DF` positions. Therefore it conjugates the `SDE`
duplicate swap to the `SDF` duplicate swap. The lane-dependent duplicate
action and lane exchange commute on all formal rows and generate a
Klein-four action.

There are `4*15*4=240` lane/cell/source-sign states and 25,200 formal rows.
The identity fixes all rows. The duplicate element fixes `240*15=3,600`
rows. Lane exchange and its product with the duplicate action fix none,
because both exchange `SDE` and `SDF`. Burnside gives

```text
(25,200+3,600)/4 = 7,200.
```

The exact orbit router equivalently finds 1,800 doubletons and 5,400
four-element orbits. Adding the proved 3,420 `S0` representatives gives
10,620 representatives for the complete split-principal block. QED.
