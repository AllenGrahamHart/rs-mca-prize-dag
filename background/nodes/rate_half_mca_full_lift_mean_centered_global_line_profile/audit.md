# Audit

The theorem uses the entire proved suffix-minimum prefix profile. A coarser
two-threshold substitution is valid but stops Mersenne at `e=98220`; it is
not used for the claimed wall. The exact scan is constant-memory and was run
under RAMguard, with no Modal spend.

The full-lift use is a checked extension of the earlier sparse-range MCA
corollary, not an unannounced scope import: the ordinary incidence proof only
needs `A_h>K-1`, and `A_H>K-1` holds at every scanned support.

The two adjacent failures are deliberately different and separately
checked. KoalaBear loses theorem legality because `T_H<0`. Mersenne remains
theorem-legal and merely has an upper bound above budget. Neither implication
can be reversed into an unsafe statement.
