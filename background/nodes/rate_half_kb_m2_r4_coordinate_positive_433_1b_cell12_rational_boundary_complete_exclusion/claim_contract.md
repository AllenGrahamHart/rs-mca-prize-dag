# Claim Contract

## In scope

- The eight deployed rational points in the cell-12 leading boundary.
- All four target sign lanes and all 105 outside labels at each point.
- Exact missing-product, missing-sum, residual-pair, and target-guard tests.

## Out of scope

- The generic elliptic leading-open common curve.
- Nonrational points in the eight nonsplit `c`-leading fibers.
- Product-rank drop, already handled by a separate parent theorem.
- Cell `13`, duplicate-role transport, complete cell/orbit closure, or a
  Prize conclusion.

## Promotion rule

`PROVED` means exactly that none of the eight deployed rational boundary
points extends to any of the 105 outside labels in any target lane.
