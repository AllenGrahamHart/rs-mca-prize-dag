1. The full component density is legitimate because every eleven-set has
   rank ten at `K'=11`; no half-lane pigeonhole is used.
2. The rank-nine chart cap takes the maximum over `j=9,10` before summing, so
   no incidence loss is charged for unknown core size.
3. Circuit-shadow marks remain in `(record,T,B)` units; they are never
   deduplicated to records.
4. Sparse coalescence concerns representations of the same global functional
   `lambda`, not merely isomorphic local circuits.
5. The low-circuit per-record cap deliberately allows circuit size one, so
   the contradiction does not rely on the separate maximal-shortening loop
   exclusion.
6. The record-dependent comparison is proved monotone and checked at the
   minimum allowed record count.

   full-support circuit generates 54 rank-nine shadows and is paid globally.
