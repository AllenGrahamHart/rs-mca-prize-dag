# Proof

Put

```text
P_11=F[X]_<11,       dim V'=10,       V'<=P_11.
```

For every eleven-set `T` of distinct residual coordinates, evaluation
`P_11 -> F^T` is an isomorphism.  Hence `ev_T(V')` has rank ten and every
positive-dimensional incidence supplied by the dense-locator theorem is in
the affine-owner lane.  Fix a nonzero global functional `lambda` with

```text
V'=ker(lambda).                                      (1)
```

## Uniform capacity of a rank-nine shadow

Fix a nine-set `B` with `rank(ev_B|V')=9`.  Its kernel is generated by one
nonzero word `u` of degree below eleven.  If `J` is the common core of its
affine owner plane, then

```text
B subset J subset Z_D(u),       j:=|J| in {9,10}.    (2)
```

Off `Z_D(u)`, one owner point can agree with the received pair, so the owner
petals are disjoint.  On each distinct retained record line, its selected
support outside `J` gives masses

```text
sum_p x_(L,p)=P=m'-j,       x_(L,p)<=s_p<=P-1,
sum_p s_p<=S=n'-j.                                  (3)
```

A marked extension has at least one petal coordinate.  Its other coordinate
is either in the same petal or in `J minus B`.  Thus the line contributes at
most

```text
sum_p C(x_(L,p),2)+(j-9)P.                          (4)
```

Apply the weighted split-pencil core-offset theorem with `r=j-9`.  The two
exact capacities are

```text
j=9:  P=67474, S=1048578, r=0, cap=9274924665987729;
j=10: P=67473, S=1048577, r=1, cap=9275866238180030.
```

Therefore every fixed rank-nine shadow has marked load at most

```text
C_*=9275866238180030.                               (5)
```

Summing (5) over all nine-sets gives global rank-nine marked capacity

```text
G=C(1048587,9)C_*
 =39175699244380414710274828706724897216747022733515052264273968850.
                                                               (6)
```

## Circuit split

For one component incidence `(gamma,T)`, the rank-ten hyperplane
`ev_T(V')<=F^T` has one circuit.  Equivalently, (1) has a unique expression

```text
lambda=sum_(a in C_T) mu_a ev_a,       mu_a!=0.     (7)
```

Write `c_T=|C_T|`.  A nine-shadow of `T` is rank eight exactly when its
omitted pair is disjoint from `C_T`; otherwise it is rank nine.  Hence the
number of rank-nine shadows is

```text
r_9(c_T)=55-C(11-c_T,2).                            (8)
```

In particular `r_9(c_T)>=45` when `c_T>=6`.  If `H` is the number of such
high-circuit incidences, (6)--(8) give

```text
H<=floor(G/45)
 =870571094319564771339440637927219938149933838522556716983865974.
                                                               (9)
```

Now consider incidences with `c_T<=5`.  Given two of them, subtract their
representations (7).  The resulting relation involves evaluation
functionals at at most ten distinct points.  Those functionals are
Vandermonde-independent on `P_11`, so every coefficient vanishes.  Thus all
low-circuit incidences have one common support `C_*` and the same
coefficients.  Let `c=|C_*|`, `1<=c<=5`.

Each low-circuit `T` contains `C_*`.  A selected support of size `m'=67483`
contains at most

```text
C(m'-c,11-c)<=C(m'-1,10)                            (10)
```

such eleven-sets.  For `R` actual residual records, their total count `L`
therefore satisfies

```text
L<=R*C(67482,10).                                   (11)
```

## Exact contradiction

The dense-locator theorem supplies at least

```text
ceil((990810934/10^9) R C(67483,11))                (12)
```

component incidences.  By (9) and (11), all component incidences together
are at most

```text
floor(G/45)+R*C(67482,10).                          (13)
```

The coefficient of `R` in (12) minus the variable term in (13) has positive
cross numerator

```text
3277538744750188045701745970410737654908675596614324570.
```

Thus the difference is smallest at the proved record floor
`R=N_min=274980728111260126`.  There (12) equals

```text
901408286315387898338134887980054663001598216883356906995509296,
```

while (13) equals

```text
870719390190680409022824387604193486699840723094988553120053384.
```

The former exceeds the latter by

```text
30688896124707489315310500375861176301757493788368353875455912,
```

a contradiction.  Hence the component target is empty at `K'=11`.
