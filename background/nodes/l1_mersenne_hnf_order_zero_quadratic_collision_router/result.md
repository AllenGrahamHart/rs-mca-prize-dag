# Result

The quadratic order-zero colored system now has a collision router. Two
repeated colors force a zero collision center; the nonzero-center alternative
would make Frobenius affine, and its only nontrivial reflection fails the
cyclotomic remainder. On all four `m=8,h=7` rows, two antipodal pairs are
also impossible, so a quadratic candidate uses at least six of the eight
colors. The `m=16` even multi-collision branch remains open.
