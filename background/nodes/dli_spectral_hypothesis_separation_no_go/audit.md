# Audit

- The first construction is genuinely one-sided: all frequencies are below
  `n/2`. Its failure is not conjugate-band aliasing.
- The first construction is not valuation-pure; this is an explicit scope
  exclusion, not hidden in the verifier.
- In the tensor construction, each event uses one exact valuation class:
  odd, valuation one, or valuation two. The complete residue bands cross the
  global one-sided boundary.
- Primitive deletion is applied only to the joint numerator. Every marginal
  remains unconditional, matching C2''.
- The finite local counts are checked by two independent exact enumerators.
  Tensorization and field existence are proved symbolically.
- The theorem does not license inference about the conjunction of the two
  hypotheses or about initial-prefix nesting.
