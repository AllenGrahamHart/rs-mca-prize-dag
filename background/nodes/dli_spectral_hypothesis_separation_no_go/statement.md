# DLI spectral-hypothesis separation no-go

- **status:** PROVED
- **closure:** exact finite certificates and tensorization
- **consumer:** `dli_c2pp_joint_reserve`

Two exact constructions separate the spectral hypotheses needed by a DLI
correlation theorem.

## One-sidedness alone is insufficient

Over `F_17`, on the order-16 domain generated by `zeta=3`, let

```text
E_A: frequencies {2,3} vanish,
E_B: frequencies {4,6} vanish.
```

Both sets lie strictly in the one-sided range `1,...,7`, and they are
disjoint. Their exact counts are

```text
|E_A|=160,       |E_B|=388,
|E_A intersect E_B|=20,
|Owner intersect E_A intersect E_B|=4.
```

Hence the primitive joint-to-product ratio is

```text
16*2^16/(160*388)=8192/485>sqrt(32).                     (SH1)
```

The defect is that each event mixes distinct 2-adic valuation classes.

## Valuation purity alone is insufficient

For a power of two `r`, put `n=16r` and work in a characteristic-17 field
containing an order-`n` root whose `r`th power is `3`. Define three events by

```text
G_0={1+16l:0<=l<r},
G_1={2+16l,6+16l:0<=l<r},
G_2={4+16l:0<=l<r}.
```

Every `G_j` lies in one exact 2-adic valuation class. Their local marginal
counts are `3856`, `1296`, and `5124`; the local joint count is `20` and its
antipodal owner count is `4`. Tensorization gives primitive correlation ratio

```text
C^r(1-5^(-r)),       C=83886080/25006401.                (SH2)
```

At `r=2`, `n=32`, this is

```text
2251799813685248/208440030324267 > sqrt(64).
```

These bands are valuation-pure but not one-sided globally.

Therefore neither one-sidedness nor valuation purity alone supports the DLI
square-root scale. A viable theorem must retain their conjunction and the
actual nested initial-prefix allocation, together with primitive ownership.
Neither construction falsifies the official Haar-prefix candidate.
