# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the proved `V=44` endpoint;
- the proved sparse-L1 recurrence and signed-chord gate;
- the complete proved one-diameter light atlas;
- the collision norm criterion for the ambient E1 route.

## Output

Every `V=42` profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate lies in one of the seven profiles and
111 affine templates printed in `statement.md`.

## Nonclaims

No surviving vector is excluded here. No cubic cutoff, lower variance,
profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform allowance, row, or prize terminal
is claimed.

## Falsifier

A candidate with `L>13`, a ninth energy profile, a surviving nine-odd profile,
or a light support outside the pinned one/five-odd atlas refutes this router.
