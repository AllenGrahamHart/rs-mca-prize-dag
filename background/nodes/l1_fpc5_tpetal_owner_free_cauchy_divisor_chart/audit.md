# Audit

The moments in `(CC6)` are weighted by the actual petal labels and by
`Lambda'(z)^(-1)`. They are not ordinary top-coefficient constraints on
`G`, and no change of variables to upstream quotient/prefix flatness is
asserted.

The reciprocal system `(CC12)` retains the full split predicate because
`A` is required to divide the source-core locator. Dropping that divisor
condition returns a much larger rational variety and is not a valid
relaxation at the required scale.

The primitive tests in `(CC9)` and background equations in `(CC11)` are
part of the simultaneous census. The ambient owner MDS theorem shows why
discarding them before summing owner strata has no coalescing power.

The theorem counts no first-owner atlas and proves no upper bound. Its gain
is an exact owner-free target, not a status change for the large-source
payment.
