# Claim contract

- **Input:** one squarefree split full-petal locator, one disjoint source
  core and background, one label per petal, and the degree range
  `d<h<=2d+1`.
- **Output:** an anchor-free CRT reconstruction, exact weighted moment
  equations for the low-degree numerator condition, exact punctured-moment
  primitive guards, and exact Cauchy equations for background zeros.
- **Preserved:** field, petal labels, source core, background, locator
  degree, split divisor identity, numerator, primitivity, and every
  background root test.
- **Nonclaim:** a divisor-census bound, quotient/prefix flatness,
  base-field-normalized split-pencil control, first ownership, source
  aggregation, or a deployed-row payment.
