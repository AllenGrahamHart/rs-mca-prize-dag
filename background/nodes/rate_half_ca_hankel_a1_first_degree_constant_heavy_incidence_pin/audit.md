# Audit

- Cancelling `B` changes the pole divisor of `G/H`; no claim equates the new
  pole divisor with the original one.
- Regularity after cancellation is used only to force `N_F=0` at a heavy
  supported incidence.
- A zero Forney value places the incidence in the excess recurrence factor,
  not necessarily at a repeated domain root. An extra simple domain root is
  also charged correctly.
- `I_H` counts distinct grid incidences, matching the residual incidence
  ledger. It is bounded by excess-factor degree fibre by fibre.
- The exact gap equations use only `I_H<=Delta` and `O<=Delta`; no ordering
  between `I_H` and `O` is assumed.
