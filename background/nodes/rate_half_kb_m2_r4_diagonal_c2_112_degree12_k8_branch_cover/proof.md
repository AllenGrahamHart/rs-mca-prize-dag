The parent pseudo-remainder identity is therefore reversible. Exact
factorization leaves degree-37 cores with `4124` and `3813` terms. The
printed Groebner basis for their intersection with `R12=K8=0` has size 62.
Sequential reduction of `s`, `L6`, `K10`, and every transported named-open
factor reaches zero at named factor 14, so no point survives this chart.

three-equation ideal `(R12,K8,K10)`. Its size-27 basis reduces the product of
`s`, `L6`, and all transported named-open factors to zero at named factor 14.
Thus even this larger residual variety has no admissible point.

The cases `K10!=0` and `K10=0` exhaust `K8=0`. QED.
