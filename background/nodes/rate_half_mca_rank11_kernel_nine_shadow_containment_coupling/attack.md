# Attack

Audit the rank partition carefully. A rank-nine nine-subset contained in a
kernel eleven-set forces that eleven-set to have rank nine, so the small
closure cap applies. Lower-rank nine-subsets receive only the unrestricted
support-pair cap; no hidden closure claim is made for them.

The next useful strengthening must subdivide the lower-rank class or
couple the nearly saturated corank-one and corank-two flat families. A
ten-shadow or eight-shadow alone is nonbinding at the certified wall.
