# Audit

## Semantic checks

1. The complete common set is taken after maximalizing the selected
   explanations. Its size is below `K` by non-affinity, not by the weaker
   exact-support bound from the previous cycle.
2. The maximal-core adapter supplies actual noncontained supports through the
   core; support-wise MCA-badness is not inferred from polynomial division.
3. The extraction unknown count is recomputed with `K'=K-c` and `m'=m-c`.
   No deployed-row constant is silently reused on the punctured domain.
4. Global affinity is excluded by the eighteen dense-pair records plus the
   selected off-line record.
5. Denominator roots are retained as a named branch. No division by `Q` is
   used in the router.
6. Certificate lifting multiplies the shortened identity by the common-core
   locator and checks both degree contributions separately.
7. Two-cover complexity adds exactly two per common coordinate, restoring
   the official threshold algebraically.

## Executable controls

The primary verifier checks the complete shortening staircase, unknown
counts, complexity lift, and an exact GF(17) polynomial certificate lift.
The independent audit recomputes endpoint and sampled-core values and checks
the same toy identity with a separate polynomial implementation.
