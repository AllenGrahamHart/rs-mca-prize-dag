# Rank-eleven shortened partial-relative router

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** the degree-18 order-32 seed emitted by the dense-pair compiler
- **source vocabulary:** Grande Finale v4 scalar-locator and two-cover
  complexity interfaces

Maximalize the `32` selected agreement supports and let `C` be their complete
common agreement set, `c=|C|`. The selected explanations are not globally
affine, so `c<K`. The maximal-common-core cancellation adapter supplies
actual support-wise MCA witnesses in

```text
RS[F,D\C,K-c]
```

at agreement `m-c`, with empty maximal common agreement and slope degree in
`18..31`.

For exact residual supports `S_i'`, define

```text
chi'=sum_(x in D\C) min(2, |{i:x in S_i'}|).
```

Exactly one of the following interfaces is available.

1. The residual explanations admit a pure-locator certificate.
2. They admit a nontrivial scalar-locator rational certificate, possibly
   with denominator roots, of denominator degree at most
   `m-K=67472`.
3. Their two-cover complexity satisfies

   ```text
   chi' >= 3(m-c)-(K-c)+3.
   ```

Every certificate in the first two branches lifts to the original exact
supports with the same denominator and affine locator scalars. In the third
branch, adjoining `C` to every support adds exactly `2c`, so the original
supports satisfy

```text
chi >= 3m-K+3 = 2299571.
```

This is an exact local router. It does not pay a scalar-locator branch,
prove deployed spread abundance, assign a whole-line chronology owner, or
close the KoalaBear row.

## Falsifier

A non-affine selected tuple with at least `K` maximal common coordinates; a
low-complexity residual with no nonzero interpolation certificate; failure
of scalar-locator classification; a lifted certificate exceeding degree
`m` or changing its monic support locators; or a complexity lift other than
exactly `2c`.
