# Result

Every surviving Mersenne row contains an explicit `n/2`-pair checkpoint
family obtained by embedding the exact order-`2(p+1)` antipodal construction
in its `m/2` subgroup cosets. It lives only at depths `p,p+1`. Hence future
work must classify and pay this family rather than seek total row emptiness;
the genuinely exclusion-oriented branch begins with `h>=3` or nonembedded
`h=2` records.
