# Audit - embedded two-fiber family

1. The subgroup has order `2(p+1)`, not `p+1`; its index is `m/2`.
2. Each subgroup coset contributes `p+1=N` antipodal complements, so the
   total is `(m/2)N=n/2`.
3. The construction is valid in an ambient coset because complete splitting
   in a subset implies complete splitting in the full domain.
4. Distinct ambient subgroup cosets cannot duplicate a pair: the union of
   its two supports identifies its coset.
5. The exact perturbation degree `p-2` gives depths `p,p+1`, not every
   first-checkpoint depth.
6. No exhaustivity claim is inferred from the small analogue.
