- **Claim:** every live Shape-A coefficient syzygy bundle has profile
  `(2r-e,e-r-1,0)`, and each small source class has the unique defect form
  `(LQD8)`.
- **Inputs:** cubic syzygy classification and locator-interpolation rank
  amplification.
- **Output:** cubic syzygies are eliminated and the remaining source defect
  is represented by two explicit forms in the common domain coefficient
  space.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaims:** no incompatibility between the two defect forms is yet
  proved.
