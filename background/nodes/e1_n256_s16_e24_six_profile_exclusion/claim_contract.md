# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the complete E24 six-profile/154-template router;
- the proved proper-conductor collision exclusion;
- the exact collision norm criterion and pair-feasible prime threshold.

## Output

No `N=256`, square-mass-16, profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate at `V=48` can collide
at a pair-feasible row prime.

## Nonclaims

- no lower positive variance is classified;
- no claim is made about folded profile `(4,2,0)`, later swap bands, or
  `N!=256`;
- this node alone does not prove the route-uniform collision allowance or
  either prize terminal.

## Falsifier

A missed profile/template/vector, engine disagreement, a proper-conductor
vector outside the conductor theorem, a nonpositive resultant, a norm at
least `2^250`, or a normalization mismatch refutes the exclusion.
