# Audit

1. The lane floor is an incidence count, not a record count.
2. Canonical basis assignment prevents one tuple from entering multiple
   rank-basis capacities.
3. The rank-zero stratum is empty by the predecessor's empty global common
   support.
4. Every `M_d` is evaluated after cancelling exactly `10-d` coordinates.
5. The rank-nine cap excludes the already disjoint near charge.
6. All comparisons are exact integer or rational comparisons.
7. `K'=4599` is checked and retained as a failure of this method.
