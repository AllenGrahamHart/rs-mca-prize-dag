# Claim contract

## Input

The dominant kernel-lane incidence floor and the canonical-basis capacity
for all nine nonzero evaluation ranks.

## Output

The kernel lane is impossible for every residual shortening
`10<=K'<=4598`.

## Nonclaim

The cut does not exclude the kernel lane for `K'>=4599`, does not pay the
rank-eight affine-owner branch, and does not close rank eleven.
