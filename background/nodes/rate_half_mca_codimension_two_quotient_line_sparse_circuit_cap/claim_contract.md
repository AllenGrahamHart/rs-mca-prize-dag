# Claim contract

This is a characteristic-free linear-algebra and polynomial-root theorem.
It assumes only distinct field coordinates, `dim V=10` inside
`F[X]_<12`, rank ten on the counted eleven-set, and circuit support at most
five.

The count is recordwise: `S` is one selected record support.  It counts
full-rank eleven-sets, not quotient labels in isolation.  The constant-kernel
case uses full rank to prevent two labels from being charged to the same
eleven-set.

No component-incidence density, rank-nine chart capacity, chronology rule,
or prize-row closure is included in this theorem.
