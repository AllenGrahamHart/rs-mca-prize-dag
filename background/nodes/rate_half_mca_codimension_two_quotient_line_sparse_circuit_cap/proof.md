# Proof

Write `E_A=span{ev_x:x in A}`.  Evaluation functionals at any at most twelve
distinct field points are independent on `P_12`, by the Vandermonde
determinant.

For an eleven-set `T`, evaluation `P_12 -> F^T` is onto and `dim E_T=11`.
Annihilator duality gives

```text
rank(ev_T|V)=11-dim(Lambda intersect E_T).          (1)
```

Thus a rank-ten `T` selects exactly one projective point `[lambda_T]` of
`P(Lambda)`.  Its expression in the basis `{ev_x:x in T}` is unique.  Its
nonzero coefficient set is `C_T`.

## 1. Support one

A support-one quotient label is proportional to one `ev_x`.  Three distinct
such labels would place three distinct evaluation functionals in the
two-dimensional vector space `Lambda`, contradicting their Vandermonde
independence.  Hence there are at most two labels, and each contributes at
most `C(m-1,10)` eleven-sets in one selected support.  This is the `c=1`
term of `(QL2)`.

## 2. The catalecticant pencil

Fix `2<=c<=5`.  For `lambda in Lambda`, form the Hankel catalecticant

```text
H_c(lambda)=(lambda(X^(i+j)))_(0<=i<=11-c, 0<=j<=c),
```

of size `(12-c) x (c+1)`.  If

```text
lambda=sum_(a in C) mu_a ev_a,
|C|=c,       mu_a!=0,
```

then the two Vandermonde factors in this expression show
`rank H_c(lambda)=c`.  Its one-dimensional right kernel is generated by the
locator

```text
L_C(X)=product_(a in C)(X-a).                       (2)
```

Parameterize `P(Lambda)` by `(U:V)`.  Every `(c+1)`-minor of
`H_c(U,V)` is homogeneous of degree `c+1`.  If one is nonzero, every
support-`c` label is one of its at most `c+1` projective roots.  This gives
the first entry in `(QL1)`.

It remains to consider the case in which every `(c+1)`-minor vanishes
identically.  If no support-`c` label exists there is nothing to count.
Otherwise choose one such label and choose `c` rows of `H_c` which are
independent there.  Let `A(U,V)` be the resulting `c x (c+1)` pencil.  One
of its `c`-minors is a nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree `c`, so
there are at most `c` bad parameters where `rank A<c`.

The signed maximal cofactors of `A` give a kernel polynomial

```text
Q(U,V;X)=sum_(j=0)^c Q_j(U,V)X^j,                  (3)
```

whose coefficient forms have degree `c`.  Cancel their common homogeneous
parameter factor.  The resulting primitive coefficient map has a common
degree `e<=c`.  At every good support-`c` parameter, the rows of `A` span
the rank-`c` row space of `H_c`; hence `(2)` and uniqueness imply that
`Q(U,V;X)` is proportional to the circuit locator.

If `e=0`, all good labels have one fixed support `C`.  An eleven-set
containing `C` cannot carry two distinct such labels: then
`Lambda<=E_T`, and `(1)` would give rank at most nine.  Thus the good labels
together contribute at most `C(m-c,11-c)` full-rank eleven-sets.  Including
the at most `c` bad labels gives the `c+1` alternative in `(QL1)`.

Assume `e>=1`.  Let `G` be the set of domain points `x` for which
`Q(U,V;x)` vanishes identically, and put `g=|G|`.  These are fixed roots of
every locator.  Since the locator has `X`-degree `c`, `g<=c`; equality
would make the primitive coefficient map constant, contrary to `e>=1`.
Thus `g<c`.

Count pairs `(t,x)` where `t` is a good support-`c` label whose circuit lies
in `S`, and `x` is one of its roots outside `G`.  Every such label
contributes `c-g` pairs.  For fixed `x notin G`, the nonzero homogeneous
polynomial `Q(U,V;x)` has degree `e`, hence at most `e` projective roots.
Therefore the number of good labels visible in `S` is at most

```text
floor(e(m-g)/(c-g)).                                (4)
```

Each label contributes at most `C(m-c,11-c)` eleven-sets.  Adding the at
most `c` bad labels proves the second alternative in `(QL1)`.

The five support-size classes are disjoint, so summing their independently
valid upper bounds proves `(QL2)`.  Direct integer evaluation at `m=67484`
gives `(QL3)`.  QED.
