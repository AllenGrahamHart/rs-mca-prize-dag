# Attack

The theorem is designed for the `K'=12` quotient line.  Its immediate
consumer should combine the recordwise low-circuit cap with:

1. the exact absolute capacity of rank-deficient eleven-sets;
2. the rank-nine-shadow charge for circuits of size at least six; and
3. the full dense-locator component-incidence demand.

For `K'=13`, the quotient is a projective plane.  A new argument will be
needed: determinant restrictions become plane curves rather than bounded
root sets, so this line theorem must not be extrapolated.
