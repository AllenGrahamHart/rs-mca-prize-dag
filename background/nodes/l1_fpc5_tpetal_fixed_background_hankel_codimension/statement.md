# Fixed-background FPC5 Hankel codimension

- **status:** PROVED
- **consumer:** `l1_fpc5_large_source_payment`

Use one arbitrary-`t` full-petal FPC5 cell with petal size `ell` and

```text
u=d-(t-1)ell>=0.                                     (FB1)
```

Fix a set `R` of `u` background points at which the numerator must vanish.
Let `L_R` be its locator and put

```text
P_R=Lambda L_R,       h_R=deg P_R=t ell+u.           (FB2)
```

Let `chi_R` be the unique polynomial of degree below `h_R` satisfying

```text
chi_R==c_i (mod L_i),       chi_R==0 (mod L_R).       (FB3)
```

For `G` of degree at most `d`, define

```text
B_(G,R)=rem_(P_R)(chi_R G).                           (FB4)
```

Then `(G,B)` satisfies the touched-petal congruences, `B|_R=0`, and
`deg B<=d` if and only if `B=B_(G,R)` and the coefficient vector of `G`
lies in the weighted Hankel kernel with

```text
h_R-d-1=ell-1                                        (FB5)
```

rows generated by `chi_R/P_R` at infinity.

If this fixed-`R` cell is nonempty and contains a primitive monic
degree-`d` pair, then its Hankel block has full row rank `ell-1`. Its
locator vector space and monic chart have dimensions

```text
dim V_(F,R)=d-ell+2,
dim M_(F,R)=d-ell+1,                                 (FB6)
```

so the monic chart has affine codimension exactly `ell-1` inside all monic
degree-`d` polynomials. These quantities do not depend on `t` or `u`.

Let `F` be any family of exact contributors in the fixed source/touched
cell, and let `R_G` be the complete background zero set of member `G`.
For

```text
F_R={G in F:R subset R_G},
```

one has the exact incidence identity

```text
sum_(R subset Bkg, |R|=u) |F_R|
 =sum_(G in F) binom(|R_G|,u).                        (FB7)
```

When `u=0`, both sides equal `|F|`. For `u>0`, assigning `G` to the
lexicographically first `u`-subset of `R_G` gives a disjoint partition of
`F` by fixed-`R` Hankel subcells.

## Scope

This theorem does not bound one fixed-`R` split-divisor intersection, sum
the possible `R`, or prove that lexicographic subcells retain linear
structure. The `u<0` branch has no required background set and remains in
the incoming unaugmented Hankel chart.
