# Claim contract

- **Input:** one arbitrary-`t` FPC5 cell with `u>=0`, one required
  `u`-subset of background zeros, and one primitive monic member when the
  dimension/rank conclusion is invoked.
- **Output:** one augmented zero-label CRT chart, its exact `ell-1` row
  full-rank Hankel block, exact locator dimensions, and the background-set
  incidence/first-subset identities.
- **Preserved:** petal labels, fixed background set, locator, numerator,
  split and primitive filters, complete background zero set, and source
  chronology labels.
- **Nonclaim:** a fixed-cell split-divisor bound, a sum over background
  subsets, linearity of the first-subset partition, or a deployed-row
  payment.
