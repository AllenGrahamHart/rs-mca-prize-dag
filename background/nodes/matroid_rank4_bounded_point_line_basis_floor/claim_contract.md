# Claim contract

- **Claim:** under looplessness, rank four, parallel-class ceiling `a`, and
  rank-two-flat ceiling `a+1`, the recursive floor `(BPL)` bounds the number
  of unordered bases.
- **Range:** `a>=1`, `r=m-a>=3`.
- **Coloop branch:** ordered triple counting in the rank-three deletion.
- **Non-coloop branch:** deletion-contraction at a smallest parallel class,
  followed by the proved rank-three bounded-parallel basis floor.
- **Smallest-class ceiling:**
  `c<=min(floor((a+1)/2),floor((a+r)/4))`.
- **Evaluator:** one-sign reset search plus exact residue-class sums.
- **Status:** PROVED.
- **Nonclaim:** the theorem does not prove that an incident normal matroid
  has these ceilings and does not itself bound a Reed-Solomon record count.
