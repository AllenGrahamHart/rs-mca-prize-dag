# Claim contract

- **Claim ID:**
  `rate_half_kb_m2_u2_colored_source_resultant_split_compiler`
- **Input:** an actual residual degree-two source equation, the exact
  component color class, and complete-source saturation.
- **Output:** one squarefree colored quartic `C_H` dividing the `L^c`
  pullback, the two exact partial-resultant identities `(KBCR-1)--(KBCR-2)`,
  and recovery of every outside-`K` deficit from `C_H`.
- **Deletion interface:** a proposed source form or colored divisor failing
  either resultant identity cannot be actual.
- **Not claimed:** existence or universal failure of an admissible colored
  quartic, exact source degree/irreducibility after interpolation,
  component/type deletion, owner, payment, row, or Prize close.
