# Result

PROVED. Every residual degree-two source component determines one
squarefree colored quartic `C_H` for which the `J` partial resultant is
`D_K^2 C_H` and the `I` partial resultant times `C_H` is `D_R^2`, up to
nonzero scales. The three surviving degree profiles are the left gcd-degree
profiles of `C_H`. Universal incompatibility is not proved.
