# Audit

1. `P_I` and `P_J` each have six distinct source roots, so their resultants
   are exactly the corresponding six row products up to scale.
2. The `J` product has multiplicity two over `K`, multiplicity one at each
   of the four colored roots, and no other zeros.
3. Colored roots lie in free `L^c` fibers, so `C_H` is squarefree and has
   degree four.
4. The `I` identity follows by dividing the complete-source square only
   after writing the divisor equality; no illegal polynomial denominator
   is introduced.
5. `E_j=bZ_j` contains exactly the two edge roots incident to left vertex
   `j`, proving the gcd-degree formula.
6. No stabilizer symmetry, zero-migration condition, or profile
   realizability is assumed.
