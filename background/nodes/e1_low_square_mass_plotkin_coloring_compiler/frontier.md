# Frontier

The preferred E1 attack is now the exact graph-coloring target rather than
profile-by-profile injectivity.

For the binding prize rate-`1/8` row, prove `chi(G_p(33))<=3`. Sufficient
stronger statements include:

- maximum low-mass collision degree at most two;
- an explicit three-color invariant whose value changes across every
  low-mass collision edge;
- a decomposition into three collision-free class families.

A counterexample should be equally exact: one admissible row and a certified
four-chromatic subgraph, with all vertices valid classes, every graph edge a
same-E1 collision of square mass at most 66, and the row field and quotient
root pinned. A `K_4` is the cheapest falsifier but is not the only one.

The other five row bounds have substantially more color slack. They should be
attacked only after the three-color row is understood, unless a uniform
degree or coloring theorem pays them simultaneously.
