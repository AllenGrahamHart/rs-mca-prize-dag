# Result

PROVED. The direct E1 image problem has an exact finite max-fiber route. It is
enough to color the graph of same-value pairs with square mass at most
`2ell`; a `c`-coloring caps every fiber by `c(ell+1)`.

The tight envelope is prize rate `1/8`, where three colors give fiber cap 102
and an image strictly above `B*`, while four colors are not certified by this
compiler. The coloring itself remains open in
`e1_official_low_square_mass_collision_coloring`.
