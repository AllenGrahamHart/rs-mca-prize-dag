# Statement

## Claim `(KBP1B4-TOWER-1)`

In deployed positive `433-1b -> O0a` role cell `4`, the singleton is `AB`
and the paired roles are `(LA,BC+)` and `(AC,BC-)`.  On the
product-rank-five branch and under the printed source/target guards:

1. the common Vieta condition is equivalently generated by three compact
   `7 x 7` compatibility determinants using the `AB` sum row as pivot;
2. for all four source-sign pairs and all six product-cofactor charts, the
   pivot boundary is empty and the saturated common locus is a
   one-dimensional curve with compact basis size `16` and lex basis size `9`;
3. within each source-sign row all six charts have byte-identical lex bases,
   and the localized ideal is generated exactly by three relations:

   ```text
   F_epsilon(t,r)=0,       degree_t=2, degree_r=3, 10 terms;
   G_epsilon(b;t,r)=0,     degree_b=2, palindromic, 18 terms;
   H_epsilon(c,b,t,r)=0,   degree_c=1, 10 terms;
   ```

4. writing `eta=epsilon_1 epsilon_2`, the leading and constant coefficients
   of `G_epsilon` coincide, while the leading coefficients needed to recover
   `b` and `c` are

   ```text
   eta=+1: L_b=r(r-1)(r+1)^2,  L_c=(t+1)(r-1);
   eta=-1: L_b=r(r+1)(r-1)^2,  L_c=(t-1)(r+1),
   ```

   so every division is by an existing route guard;
5. the discriminant of `F_epsilon` in `t` is square-free of degree six, so
   the normalized base double cover has genus two; and
6. one eight-coordinate polynomial coefficient kernel, independent of the
   source signs, annihilates seven common Vieta rows identically and the
   remaining three modulo the compact common ideal in all four sign rows.

Equivalently, every guarded common survivor has an exact four-basis
quadratic-over-quadratic quotient-algebra presentation, with linear recovery
of `c`.  Irreducibility of the second quadratic is not claimed.

This theorem does not decide an outside signed-edge case, close cell `4` or
its duplicate-role partner `7`, close `433-1b -> O0a`, or prove K3, LIST,
MCA, or either Prize problem.
