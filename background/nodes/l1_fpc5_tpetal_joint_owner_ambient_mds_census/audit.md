# Audit

The count is over the complete monic pair chart, not over exact FPC5
contributors. The anchor Pade chart reconstructs a degree-bounded pair for
every `H`, but its locator need not split and its numerator need not pass
the primitive or background guards.

The zero coordinate is the anchor and is excluded from `(MC3)--(MC6)`.
For exact owner `Q`, the residual polynomial `K` need only avoid roots of
`P_0/Q`; it may vanish at roots of `Q` because `P_0` is squarefree and this
does not enlarge `gcd(QK,P_0)`.

The theorem is a no-go for owner-only coalescence, not evidence that guarded
owner strata are large. Any upper theorem must retain the simultaneous
split and guard predicates that this ambient census deliberately omits.
