# Claim contract

- **Input:** one complete arbitrary-`t` monic pair chart, one exact
  squarefree anchor, and its joint marked locator `P_0`.
- **Output:** the exact number of unguarded chart points in every exact
  joint-owner stratum, including the exact aggregate top-owner count.
- **Preserved:** field size, anchor marked set, determinant-coordinate
  degree, exact owner identity, and pair-chart multiplicity.
- **Nonclaim:** split reconstructed locator, primitive candidate, exact
  background/list guard, first ownership, guarded contributor count,
  complete-cell payment, or deployed row.
