# Audit

- Products `P_1P_2` retain multiplicity at a slope shared by the two rows.
  The regular determinant is charged by excess-root degree at that fibre.
- The ordinary incidence contributes two additional determinant orders even
  if its slope is already a root of `P_1P_2`.
- Individual Forney row specializations may vanish identically. Only their
  simultaneous vanishing is excluded by the total contact degree.
- The residual contact point `E_1` may coincide with another marked point;
  it is an effective divisor with multiplicity, not assumed disjoint.
- The degree-one class in `(CFN5)` is signed and is not declared effective.
