  affine-plane cap 218, affine-line cap 15, and plane-218 endpoint bank;
- **plane output:** every endpoint row needs 339 to 358 distinct 218-point
  owner planes;

  intersection size exactly 15;
- **line output:** at least 217 distinct saturated 15-point affine lines;
- **recurrence output:** every saturated line has at least `K'-2609`, hence
  2,351, residual common-core coordinates;
- **not claimed:** endpoint exclusion, record payment, dimension-four
  progress, rank-eleven closure, or MCA closure.
