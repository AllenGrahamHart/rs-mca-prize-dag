# Audit

- The role coefficients belong to one fixed alternative packet. The 21
  packets must never be intersected as simultaneous equations.
- `L_Phi=W_Phi=0` is promoted to an equivalence only through the reversible
  `U_1=U_0=0` calculation with `c_0q!=0`.
- The `d` reconstruction uses a root of the selected irreducible role
  quadratic in the ambient quadratic field. Both conjugate roots are kept.
- `P_4=0` follows from the exact remainder identity (DQR7), not from a
  square-class heuristic.
- The reconstructed `d` still has to pass `d*(q-d)!=0`, every other inherited
  saturation, and all arithmetic lifts.
- The verifier sources are complete but intentionally unexecuted locally;
  only syntax, manifest, DAG, and hash checks are authorized without Modal.
