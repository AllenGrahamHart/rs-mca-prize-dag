primitive shift-pair ledger for the `h=7`, cubic `3+2+1`, fully proportional
J-zero residue. For each of upstream's 21 alternative irreducible quadratic
role packets, it adds two bounded b-only filters and an explicit ambient
root reconstruction of `d`; the quotient identity pays `P_4` exactly.

It belongs in the existing draft h=7 profile-reductions PR. Saturation and
