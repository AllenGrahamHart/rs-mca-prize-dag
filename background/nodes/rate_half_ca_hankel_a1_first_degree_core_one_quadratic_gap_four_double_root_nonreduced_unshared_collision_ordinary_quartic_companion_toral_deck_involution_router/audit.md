# Audit

1. Absolute irreducibility is proved from rational grid points; irreducible
   over `F_P(X)` alone would not suffice.
2. The quartic resultant has diagonal multiplicity four, not two. Removing
   it changes the bidegree cap from `(24,24)` to `(20,20)`.
3. The defect fourteen comes from `6(3e)-(18e-14)` and does not assume all
   slope fibers split.
4. Thirty ordered pairs per full six-row fiber are divided by four because
   two row quartics can share at most four slopes.
5. At most five components is the degree-six point-stabilizer orbit bound;
   no two-transitivity assumption is made.
6. The Corvaja--Zannier comparison includes the factor five and both terms
   of the published maximum.
7. The map degree `q` and subdegree `h` are retained in `(6)`. The toral
   component is not assumed to be a graph before degree comparison.
8. Distinct generic row values force `h=1`; this is the step that promotes a
   toral relation to a deck automorphism.
9. In the scaling arm, the order divides both six and `2^41`, so the only
   nonidentity possibility is `-1`.
10. The quotient forms are reductions, not contradictions. Shape C remains
    open.
