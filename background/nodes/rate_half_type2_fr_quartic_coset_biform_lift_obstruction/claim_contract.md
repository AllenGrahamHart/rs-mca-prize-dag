# Claim contract

- **claim:** no coset-preserving embedding of the explicit quartic
  incidence countermodel can be the root-incidence table of a biform with
  parameter degree `m` and locator degree at most `4m-1`, even after
  arbitrary nonzero row scaling;
- **scope:** the four-copy quartic construction, with three complete copies
  and point identification `(i,x) -> tau_i x` into `mu_(16m)`;
- **load-bearing input:** the leading two row coefficients and the exact
  degree threshold `rho=4m-1`; no Hankel equation is used;
- **consumer use:** reject the natural algebraic lift of the incidence-only
  `(FR)` countermodel and keep the general algebraic `(FR)` question open;
- **not claimed:** `(FR)`, arbitrary-permutation nonrealizability, a
  classification of endpoint biforms, a slope bound, a crossing movement,
  or a critical status change;
- **falsifier:** nonzero degree-at-most-`4m-1` polynomials `A,B` satisfying
  `B(tau_i x)=xA(tau_i x)` on three distinct full `mu_(4m)` cosets, or an
  error in the pinned `m=64` field/domain arithmetic.
