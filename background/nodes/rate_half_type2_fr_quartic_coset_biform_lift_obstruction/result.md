# Result

The quartic incidence witness does not survive its most natural algebraic
lift.  On any coset-preserving embedding into the smooth cyclic domain, its
leading two parameter coefficients already contradict locator degree
`rho=4m-1`.  For `m=64`, no degree-`255` endpoint biform realizes the
construction under such an embedding.

The general strict-`A=3` type-2 residual remains open.  A future falsifier
must use a different incidence geometry or a non-coset-preserving placement;
a positive proof must control arbitrary realizable apolar curves, not only
this construction.
