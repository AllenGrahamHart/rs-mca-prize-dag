# Proof

Let `G` be the common ideal supplied by `(KBP1B3-CURVE-1)` after sequential
saturation by the selected product cofactor and all printed route guards.  In
block-lex order `(c,b,t,r)`, its reduced basis `K` has ten elements in every
one of the `4*6=24` sign/chart cases.  The first, third, and seventh elements
of `K` have the respective forms

```text
E_epsilon(t,r),
A_epsilon(t,r)b^2+B_epsilon(t,r)b+A_epsilon(t,r),
D_epsilon(t,r)c+C_epsilon(t,r,b).
```

Exact coefficient extraction gives

```text
A_epsilon=r^2(t+epsilon_2 iota r),
D_epsilon=t-epsilon_1 epsilon_2 r^2.
```

The first coefficient is a route unit: `r` is inverted, and

```text
(t+epsilon_2 iota r)(t-epsilon_2 iota r)=t^2+r^2
```

because `iota^2=-1` in the deployed field, while `t^2+r^2` is a printed route
guard.  For the second coefficient, the compiler forms `G+(D_epsilon)` and
then re-saturates by the selected cofactor and every route guard.  The result
is the unit ideal in all 24 cases.  Hence `D_epsilon` is also a unit in every
guarded coordinate ring.  The same boundary computation for `A_epsilon`
returns the unit ideal in all 24 cases.

Let `Q_0` be the ideal generated by the three displayed basis elements, and
let `Q` be its sequential saturation by the selected cofactor, all route
guards, `D_epsilon`, and `A_epsilon`.  Since `Q_0` is contained in `G`, and
`G` is saturated by every route/cofactor guard while the two recovery
coefficients are units modulo `G`, saturation cannot move `Q_0` outside `G`.
Thus `Q` is contained in `G`.  Conversely, Singular reduces every one of the
ten generators of `K` to zero modulo `Q`, in each of the 24 cases.  Therefore
`G` is contained in `Q`, and the localized ideals are equal.

Every completed row has dimension one and saturated basis size ten.  For a
fixed sign pair, the full basis and the three-relation interface are identical
in all six cofactor charts.  This proves the global presentation and all
enumerated shape claims. QED.
