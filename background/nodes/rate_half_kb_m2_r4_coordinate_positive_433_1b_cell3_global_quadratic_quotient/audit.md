  curve: they have extra components.  Equality is asserted only after the
  exact localization printed in the statement.

  and every saturation element is already a unit there.
- The `b` leading coefficient is a route unit algebraically; the `c`
  denominator is discharged by a separate 24-chart unit-ideal computation.
- Chart uniformity is checked within each source-sign pair.  No equality
  between different sign rows is asserted.
- A quadratic extension is not a birational parametrization.  No genus,
  rationality, or base-field point claim is inferred.

  signed edge, matching, lane, or missing-record case.
