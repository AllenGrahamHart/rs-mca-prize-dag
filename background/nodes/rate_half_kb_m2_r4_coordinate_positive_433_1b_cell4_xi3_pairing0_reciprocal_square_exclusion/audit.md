# Audit

The proof is computer-assisted but finite and exact over the deployed prime
field. It does not infer emptiness from random search or from a generic
resultant alone. Every root capable of invalidating division, tower
recovery, or the resultant implication is included in the replay union.

The independent verifier does not trust the stored terminal labels. It
recomputes field roots from the printed norm and guard polynomials, rebuilds
the base-field tower lifts from the pinned structure file, evaluates the
pinned compact kernel, and reruns every target equation and guard.

The three `q` branches overlap when more than one `L_i(q)` vanishes; this is
harmless for exclusion and is why the 24 internal rows pay only 16 raw
target-lane cases. No completeness claim multiplies by the branch count.
