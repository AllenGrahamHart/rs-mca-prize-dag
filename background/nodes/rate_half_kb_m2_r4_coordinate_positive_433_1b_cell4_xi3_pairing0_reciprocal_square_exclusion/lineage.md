# Lineage

This packet adapts the cell-3 reciprocal-square method to the final open
cell-4 role pair. It depends on the cell-4 four-basis tower introduced in
Wave 63 and the signed-edge atlas shared by the positive 433-1b campaign.

The result is intentionally narrower than the universal `xi4 -> xi3`
transport theorem. Keeping direct exclusion and transport application in
separate nodes makes each future matching payment independently auditable.
