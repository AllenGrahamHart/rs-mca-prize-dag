# Proof

The parent owner partition is the disjoint union of blocks of sizes
`37,800`, `1,680`, and `3,360`, with the final block owned by repeated cells
11/14. The complete cells-11/14 theorem excludes every label in that final
block. Removing it from the disjoint partition leaves the first two blocks,
with `360+16=376` common rows and `37,800+1,680=39,480` raw labels. QED.
