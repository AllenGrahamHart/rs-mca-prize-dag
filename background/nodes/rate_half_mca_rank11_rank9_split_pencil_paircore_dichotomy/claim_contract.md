# Claim contract

## Input

One lifted rank-nine affine owner plane from the PROVED split-pencil cell
ledger, with its exact record-line block design, common pair core `J`, and
pairwise disjoint owner petals.

## Output

If `|J|<134944`, ordered record pairs consume disjoint petal coordinates and
the plane contains at most 1434405 records. Every larger plane has a pair
core of size at least 134944 shared by all of its owners and records.

## Nonclaim

The theorem does not bound the number of owner planes, identify their common
cores, install a first-match owner, aggregate the low-core caps, or pay the
large shared-core route.
