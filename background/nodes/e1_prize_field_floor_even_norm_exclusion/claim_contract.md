# Claim contract

## Claim

On prize-envelope pair-feasible prime rows, no nonzero E1 class difference
with `S<=16` at `N=256` or `S<=4` at `N=512` vanishes modulo the row prime.

## Guards

1. The field floor is the prize interval `I_P`, not the lower RowC interval.
2. `S` is folded square mass, not raw representative distance.
3. Evenness is proved for differences of two classes of the same `ell`; it is
   not asserted for an arbitrary cyclotomic integer.
4. The norm is nonzero because the folded polynomial has degree below
   `deg(Phi_N)`.
5. This sharpens only the prize-row profile floors and closes no complete E1
   pair budget.

## Falsifier

A pair-feasible prize row and a nonzero same-`ell` class difference in the
displayed mass range whose reduction vanishes, or failure of any exact
integer comparison in the proof.
