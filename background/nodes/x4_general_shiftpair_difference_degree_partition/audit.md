# Audit

1. The degree estimate uses elementary locator coefficients and is valid in
   every characteristic; Newton division is absent.
2. `H` cannot be zero because equality of monic split locators determines the
   root set.
3. The orientation `L_P-L_Q` changes sign if the pair is reversed but does
   not change `d`.
4. Constant difference is equivalent to the complete F-4 minimal condition,
   not merely to the order-`t` prefix condition.
5. The top side width `e=t+1` lies in `d=0`, but `d=0` may also occur at
   larger `e`; the converse is not asserted.
6. The theorem partitions a local degree.  It supplies no estimate from the
   global second moment.
