# Claim contract

- **Proved object:** one exact partition of the local same-prefix degree by
  `d=deg(L_P-L_Q)`.
- **Scope:** every field, evaluation set, locator size, prefix depth, and
  support-wise residual subfamily.
- **Minimal branch:** `d=0`, equivalently equality of all residual elementary
  coefficients through `e-1`.
- **Nonminimal branch:** `d>=1`, necessarily at side width `e>=t+d+1`.
- **Transport:** quotient descent and first-owner deletion preserve the
  partition because the theorem is applied after they define the residual
  family.
- **Nonclaims:** no bound on any `D_d`; no claim that the minimal branch
  exhausts a full prefix fiber; no official-row budget; no LIST/MCA currency
  conversion.
