# Audit

1. The common divisor is defined fiber by fiber from coordinates that are
   actually fixed. It is not assumed to be globally constant.
2. Division punctures the doubled domain. The supplier flatness theorem must
   be uniform under exactly these punctures; this node does not hide that
   requirement.
3. Aperiodicity survives division because every residual support remains
   antipodal-free. It is not inferred from the aperiodicity of the original
   selector alone.
4. When fewer than `2R` locator coefficients remain, the residual monic
   polynomial is unique; no codimension larger than the coefficient space is
   used.
5. The normalization uses the locator average, not the F2 average. Its
   exponent is `2 Delta-c`, which is why saturation leaves only a rounding
   factor.
6. Polynomial or subexponential mass control is not by itself the exact
   `n^3` consumer certificate. PP5.0 and finite constants remain open.
