# Claim contract

- **Input:** the proved antipodal-selector transport and a nonempty binary
  odd-syndrome fiber on a power-of-two half-system.
- **Output:** an exact fixed-coordinate factorization into a punctured,
  gcd-trivial, aperiodic prefix fiber, plus the normalized bound `(FACE-3)`
  conditional only on the explicitly printed primitive flatness estimate.
- **Closure:** proof; the face reduction and normalization arithmetic have
  no hidden assumptions.
- **Nonclaims:** no master flatness theorem, no deployed first-match owner
  identification, no PP5.0 composition rule, and no finite F2 budget close.
- **Falsifier:** a syndrome fiber whose fixed roots do not divide every
  selector locator; unequal residual prefixes; a common residual root; a
  periodic residual selector; or failure of `(FACE-3)` assuming `(FACE-2)`.
