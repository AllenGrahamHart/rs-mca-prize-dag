# Proof

Work in one exact finite source algebra `A/F_p(x)` from the symmetric tower.
The common-kernel theorem reconstructs the missing endpoint product `q` and
squared sum `s^2` on its registered rational open.

For missing `BE`, nonvanishing of `b` forces `e=q/b`; therefore

```text
C_B = (b+q/b)^2-s^2
```

must vanish.  Similarly, missing `CF` forces

```text
C_C = (c+q/c)^2-s^2 = 0.
```

For either element `C`, the certificate forms the multiplication matrix
`m_C:A->A` and computes its exact determinant in `F_p(x)`.  This determinant
is the source-algebra norm of `C`.  It is nonzero in all `8*2=16` cases, with
the common witness `x=2`.

Exact base-field root extraction gives the same determinant in all four
epsilon rows of a fixed BC sign and for both colored records:

```text
BC-: numerator degree 33; roots 0^5 and 1^12
BC+: numerator degree 36; roots 0^4 and (-1)^8.
```

The remaining degree is carried by factors with no root in the deployed
field.  Every displayed linear root makes a registered construction guard
zero.  At every deployed off-guard `x`, the determinant is consequently
defined and nonzero, so `C` is a unit in the specialized source algebra and
cannot vanish at a source point.  Thus missing `BE` and `CF` are impossible
there, before any residual matching is imposed.  QED.
