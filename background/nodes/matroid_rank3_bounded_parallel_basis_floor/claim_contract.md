# Claim contract

- **Claim:** a loopless rank-three matroid on `m` elements with parallel
  classes of size at most `a` satisfies
  `2b(M)>=(m-1)(m-1-a)`.
- **Range:** `m>=3`, `a>=1`.
- **Proof mechanism:** deletion-contraction at an element in a smallest
  parallel class.
- **Contraction hinge:** every nonloop class of `M/e` is a union of original
  classes and therefore has size at least the chosen minimum `c`.
- **Sharpness:** one coloop over a rank-two matroid with equal classes of
  size `a`, whenever `a` divides `m-1` and at least two classes occur.
- **Status:** PROVED.
- **Nonclaim:** this abstract theorem supplies neither the Reed-Solomon
  parallel-class bound nor a capacity comparison.
