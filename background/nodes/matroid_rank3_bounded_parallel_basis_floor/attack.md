# Attack

The critical step is the contraction.  Parallel classes in `M/e` can be
much larger than classes in `M`, but each is a union of whole original
classes.  Choosing `e` from a smallest class is what leaves every nonloop
contraction class with size at least `c`.

A counterexample must break that union statement, leave only one nonloop
contraction class despite rank two, violate `m>=3c`, or defeat the
deletion-contraction recurrence.  Dropping looplessness or rank three makes
the theorem inapplicable.
