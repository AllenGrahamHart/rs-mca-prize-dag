# Audit

The second curve in the Bézout argument uses the pointwise equations
`E(x)=x^(p+1)` and `p=-1 mod m`. The weaker color-membership congruence is
not enough.

Repeated colors do not reduce the intersection count: distinct reduced
roots give distinct affine points `(x,E(x))`. The no-common-component check
is included explicitly, including the exceptional linear degree.

The barrier excludes only degrees with `d(d+1)<H`. Equality at
`(H,d)=(6,2)` is the saturated quadratic resultant chamber, not an
emptiness conclusion.
