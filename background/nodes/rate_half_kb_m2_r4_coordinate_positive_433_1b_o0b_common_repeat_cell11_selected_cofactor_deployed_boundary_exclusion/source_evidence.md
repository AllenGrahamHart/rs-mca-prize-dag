tower packet, and finite-algebra core.  For each of the eight tower rows it
substitutes the selected product-rank cofactor, computes the determinant of
its multiplication matrix, factors the exact norm numerator, extracts all
base-field roots, and evaluates every pre-cofactor tower guard there.
