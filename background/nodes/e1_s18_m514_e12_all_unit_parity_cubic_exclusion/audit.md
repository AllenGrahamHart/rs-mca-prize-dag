- The second Hasse derivative is checked in the folded modulo-two ring; the
  exact multiplicity-two conclusion is not inferred from positivity.
- Relation parity leaves only `EEE` and `EOO`; all six odd values of `o` are
  evaluated explicitly.
- Opposite ordered pairs are removed because their determined third entry is
  zero, outside the autocorrelation support.
- The cubic Hermite remainder is nonpositive on all `y>0`.
- The leading coefficient is certified positive before `M_3<=510` is used.
- The verifier pins exact rational logarithm margins and DAG wiring.
