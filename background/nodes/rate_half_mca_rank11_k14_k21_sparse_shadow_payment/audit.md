# Audit

1. Every nonzero kernel corank is included; `d=9` uses its separately
   proved margin/interleaving cap.
2. Extension factors are `C(K'-10,d+1)`, not a fixed low-row value.
3. Every core size from 9 through `K'-1` is scanned before choosing `C_*`.
4. Structured and unstructured completion vectors are both computed.
5. Support strata remain separate until their exact premiums
   `26,18,11,5` are applied.
6. Sparse shadows consume the same global rank-nine marks as high circuits;
   no mark capacity is spent twice.
7. Kernel capacity is added only after the full-rank joint ledger.
8. The unfloored record cross is positive independently on all eight rows.
9. Primary and independent arithmetic use different endpoint and quadratic
   maximization implementations.
10. The K'=22 negative gap is checked and retained as a nonclaim boundary.
