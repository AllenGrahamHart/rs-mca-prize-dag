# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the proved `V=42` endpoint;
- the proved sparse-L1 recurrence and signed-chord gate;
- the complete proved even-parity light atlases;
- the collision norm criterion for the ambient E1 route.

## Output

Every `V=40` profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate lies in one of the six profiles and
154 affine templates printed in `statement.md`.

## Nonclaims

No surviving vector is excluded here. No cubic cutoff, lower variance,
profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform allowance, row, or prize terminal
is claimed.

## Falsifier

A candidate with `L>12`, an eighth energy profile, a surviving eight-odd
profile, or a light support outside the pinned zero/four-odd atlases refutes
this router.
