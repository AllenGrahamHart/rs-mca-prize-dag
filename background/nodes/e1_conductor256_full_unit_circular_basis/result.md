# Result

`PROVED`. Every algebraic unit of `Z[zeta_256]`, modulo its 256 roots of
unity, has one unique integer exponent vector in the 63 real circular units

```text
eta_a=zeta_256^((1-a)/2)(1-zeta_256^a)/(1-zeta_256),
a=3,5,...,127.
```

Consequently the full rank-63 E1 unit log lattice is explicit, with matrix
entries `(CUB4)`. No unproved class-number, Hasse-index, or circular-unit
index assumption remains. The lattice-point and sparse-associate counts are
still open.
