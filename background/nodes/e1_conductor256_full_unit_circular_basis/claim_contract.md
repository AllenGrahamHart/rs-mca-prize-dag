# Claim contract

## External theorem imports

- Miller's unconditional Theorem 2.1: the maximal real subfield of the
  conductor-256 cyclotomic field has class number one.
- The Kummer-Sinnott prime-power unit-index theorem:
  `[R^x:C]=h(K+)`, with `C` generated by roots of unity and the standard
  cyclotomic quotients `(1-zeta^a)/(1-zeta)`.

## New specialization

The 63 normalized real units indexed by odd `a` in `[3,127]` form a basis of
the complete conductor-256 unit group modulo `mu_256`, and their exact log
embedding is `(CUB4)`.

## Guards

1. The theorem is specific to conductor 256.
2. The imported class number is that of the maximal real subfield. The full
   conductor-256 cyclotomic field has a large class number; it is not claimed
   to be a PID.
3. The theorem identifies the full unit lattice but supplies no regulator
   lower bound, shortest-vector bound, lattice-point count, or sparse-product
   count.
4. Transcendental matrix entries are used only to describe the log lattice.
   Any finite count needs certified interval arithmetic plus exact algebraic
   replay.
5. The 63 generators are a basis modulo roots of unity, not a power-basis of
   the ring of integers.
