# Frontier

Write every E1 associate unit uniquely as

```text
u=zeta^j product_a eta_a^x_a.
```

The next count is now over `x in Z^63`, not over an unknown finite-index
subgroup. For each residual cofactor `mu`, retain both filters:

1. the exact log-body inequality from the common-prime associate router;
2. the requirement that multiplication by `u` carries one profile-`(3,6)`
   collision vector to another such sparse vector.

A bare count of all circular-unit lattice points in the log body can be much
larger than the sparse-associate count and is not itself the target. Any
enumeration must use certified interval bounds for `(CUB4)` and exact replay
of the sparse product in `Z[X]/(X^128+1)`. The aggregate target remains 367
torsion orbits across `mu=1,2,3,4`, followed by the lower-profile weighted
ledger.

No compute is authorized by this theorem. A proposed lattice enumeration
must first print a conservative cost and partial-result protocol under the
repository compute policy.
