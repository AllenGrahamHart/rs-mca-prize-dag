# Conductor-256 full-unit circular basis

- **status:** PROVED
- **closure:** Kummer-Sinnott unit-index theorem plus the unconditional
  conductor-256 real class-number theorem
- **consumer:** `e1_pure_cofactor_common_prime_associate_router`

Let

```text
K=Q(zeta),        zeta=zeta_256,
K+=Q(zeta+zeta^(-1)),
R=Z[zeta].
```

For every odd `a` with `3<=a<=127`, put

```text
eta_a=zeta^((1-a)/2) (1-zeta^a)/(1-zeta).            (CUB1)
```

Then `eta_a` is a real algebraic unit and

```text
R^x = mu_256 x <eta_3,eta_5,...,eta_127>,            (CUB2)
```

where the product is internal after quotienting by `mu_256`. Equivalently,
every algebraic unit has a unique representation

```text
u=zeta^j product_(a=3,5,...,127) eta_a^x_a,
j mod 256,       x_a in Z.                           (CUB3)
```

Choose the 64 conjugate-pair representatives `b=1,3,...,127` and define

```text
lambda_b(u)=log |u(zeta^b)|^2.
```

The full algebraic-unit log lattice is therefore the integer column span of
the explicit `64 by 63` matrix

```text
L_(b,a)=2 log |sin(pi*a*b/256)/sin(pi*b/256)|,        (CUB4)
```

and every log-lattice point has one unique exponent vector
`(x_3,x_5,...,x_127)`.

This theorem uses John C. Miller's unconditional theorem
`h(K+)=1` and the prime-power Kummer-Sinnott formula equating `h(K+)` with
the index of circular units in the full unit group. It makes the E1 lattice
explicit; it does not count points in any body or prove the E1 orbit cap.

## Falsifier

A conductor-256 unit outside the subgroup generated by `mu_256` and the 63
displayed units, or a nontrivial integral relation among their classes modulo
`mu_256`.
