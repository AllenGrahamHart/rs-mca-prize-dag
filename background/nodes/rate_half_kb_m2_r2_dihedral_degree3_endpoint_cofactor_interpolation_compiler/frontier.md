# Frontier

Classify the exact `s=6` locator ownerships up to the two `K_(2,2,2)` star
automorphisms and the source-deck involution. For each orbit, impose the
four-edge component-color condition and prove that the stacked cofactor
matrix has no full-support kernel over the deployed field. A universal
full-rank theorem is sufficient but stronger than necessary: sparse kernels
are already excluded by nonzero source weights.

If a full-support kernel survives, reconstruct its bidegree-`(9,18)`
cofactor and test the fixed active divisor and block fibers. If that also
survives, route the resulting actual record to a chronology-valid owner.
