# Frontier

Packet dependence is no longer an obstruction on the dense anchor. Any branch
classification of one base rational pencil automatically propagates to all
5524 anchor locators unless a one-swap packet emits the existing
high-complexity terminal.

The next tasks are therefore class-level:

1. compose exact fixed-pencil caps with the 5524 synchronized locators;
2. route `c=0` and other quotient-periodic pencils to their owner-safe budget;
3. classify or bound the remaining nonaffine/primitive pencils; and
4. pay the alternative `chi>=2299571` output.

No independent per-packet charge should be introduced.
