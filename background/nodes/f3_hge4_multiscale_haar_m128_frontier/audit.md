# Audit

1. `F(1)=0` is an integer identity from equal positive and negative support
   sizes; it is not inferred from a congruence modulo the row prime.
2. Only nonzero evaluations occur in the norm product. Haar-zero scales are
   represented by structural cyclotomic factors instead.
3. `Norm(1-zeta_O)=2` holds for every dyadic `O>=2`; all orders used at
   `m=128`, `h>=4` meet this condition.
4. `X-1` and the structural dyadic cyclotomic factors are coprime, so their
   two-adic contributions are additive rather than alternative explanations
   of the same factor.
5. The gate uses `p>n^2`, not `p>=n^2`; equality in the integer comparison
   still yields a strict contradiction.
6. The finite check is over Haar-pattern masks only, not over support pairs.
   It verifies a closed-form sufficient inequality and is therefore a proof
   of the printed scopes, not empirical sampling.
7. Widths `h=32,...,64` are paid by pre-existing theorems. No endpoint is
   lost at `h=42/43`.
8. The surviving masks in `(MX4)` are necessary only relative to this gate;
   no existence is claimed.
