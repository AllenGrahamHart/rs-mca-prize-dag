# Upstream crosswalk

This is an exact finite-track primitive shift-pair result. In the terminology
of Paper D v13 and `agents.md`, it belongs to primitive shift-pair control and
the exact second-moment ledger. The portable statement is the strengthened
norm-product divisor `(MX2)` plus the complete `m=128` width table.

It does not prove upstream quotient/prefix flatness, the split-pencil census,
or a uniform shift-pair bound. It is suitable as a constants-first lemma that
removes the second exact ratio level above the already closed `m=64` level.
