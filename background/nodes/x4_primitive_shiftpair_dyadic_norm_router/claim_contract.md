# Claim contract

- **Proved input conversion:** equal locator prefixes imply all power-sum
  vanishings used by the dyadic folds, without a characteristic restriction
  beyond the existence of the dyadic domain.
- **Proved arithmetic gate:** each nonzero fold pays one residue-degree
  factor `p^f` for every distinct Frobenius orbit reached by the prefix, and
  therefore at least `p^M` for `M` distinct available odd frequencies.
- **Proved energy control:** `E_j<=2^(j+2)e`, followed by the exact DLI norm
  ceiling `|Norm|<=E_j^(n_j/4)`.
- **Proved primitive router:** coefficient primitivity forces the root fold
  `beta_0` to be nonzero.
- **Quantifier:** every dyadic multiplicative coset, every finite field
  containing it, and every disjoint equal-size locator-prefix pair.
- **Nonclaims:** the gate is sufficient; the gate has few solutions; every
  higher fold is nonzero; first ownership is operational; the X4 allowance is
  paid; or a prize terminal closes.
