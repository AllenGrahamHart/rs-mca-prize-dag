# Audit

1. The lower norm divisor is indexed by Frobenius orbits, not by individual
   odd frequencies.  This prevents a false split-prime extrapolation.
2. The norm is the absolute cyclotomic norm of the integer fold vector;
   vanishing is only its reduction at the selected prime ideal.
3. The energy estimate allows coefficients larger than one after folding.
   Ternariness is used only before Cauchy-Schwarz through
   `sum c_a^2=2e`.
4. Only `beta_0` has a proved primitive nonvanishing statement.  Higher zero
   folds are retained as explicit branches.
5. The theorem supplies a necessary arithmetic filter, not a population
   estimate.
