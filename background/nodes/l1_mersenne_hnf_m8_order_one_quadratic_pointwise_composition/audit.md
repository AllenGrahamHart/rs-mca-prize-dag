# Audit

The polynomial `J` is formed from the pointwise identity
`E(x)=x^(p+1)`, not from the weaker color-membership congruence. Thus the
root/color assignment is retained.

Degree equality is special to six reduced roots and a quadratic interpolant.
The identity is not extrapolated to `h=15` or to higher color degree.

The coefficient ratio divides by `T`, but `T/A=l_1=6/d` proves it nonzero on
every official row. No color-collision assumption enters the argument.
