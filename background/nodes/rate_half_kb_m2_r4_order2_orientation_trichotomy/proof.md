# Proof

The V4 outer-recurrence theorem proves that the degree of the actual
component over its outer image equals the order of its setwise stabilizer in

```text
V4=<a,c>,  a=tau x 1,  c=1 x tau.
```

For `(m,r,delta)=(2,4,2)`, this stabilizer therefore has order two. The
three and only three order-two subgroups of `V4` are

```text
<a>, <c>, <ac>.
```

Endpoint transposition exchanges `a` and `c`, fixes `ac`, and preserves the
actual self-correspondence. The coordinate-transpose theorem proves that an
actual component stabilized by `<c>` becomes one stabilized by `<a>` and
that the source reduction must be rerun on the transposed second projection.
Thus `<a>` and `<c>` are one existence/deletion route without identifying
their source presentations.

For `<ac>`, the diagonal source-subfield theorem applies to the quadratic
source intermediate field. Its image under the diagonal action is either
the same field or a distinct quadratic intermediate field. Equality gives
the reciprocal source-line lift. Inequality makes the full degree-four
source extension biquadratic; tame Riemann-Hurwitz gives exactly the proved
genus-zero and genus-one passports. The alternatives are exhaustive.

These arguments classify the three order-two orientations and assert no
active-slope projection or branch deletion. QED.
