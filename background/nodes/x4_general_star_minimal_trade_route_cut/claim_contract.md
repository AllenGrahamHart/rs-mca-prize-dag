# Claim contract

- **Claim:** on `F_17^*`, the printed witness is a nonminimal general
  order-1 record with no minimal width-2 subrecord, and the complete raw
  general population is larger than the complete raw minimal population.
- **Quantifier:** all unordered disjoint records on this one cyclic domain,
  with widths bounded by `floor(16/2)=8`.
- **Consequence:** a universal, strip-free, multiplicity-one
  general-to-minimal bridge cannot prove the critical coverage target.
- **Nonclaim:** an official prize-row counterexample; survival after the
  official first-owner strips; failure of every strip-aware bridge; failure
  of the `16n^3` bound; or failure of either prize statement.
- **Required next route:** use official row/strip hypotheses to prove a
  smaller residual family is minimal-coverable, or broaden `u1` to the
  general order-`t` currency and re-prove its budget.
