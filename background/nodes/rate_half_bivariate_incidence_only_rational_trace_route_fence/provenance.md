# Provenance

The construction was designed after the pivot-free trace criterion isolated
`nu_x=Q(x)/P(x)` at `m=2`. Six inverse pairs realize the degree-two rational
map, and a nearly complete seven-vertex graph pays the remaining incidence
degrees while preserving minimum pair union 12.

Modal was used only to execute the deterministic construction and print the
JSON packet. Both verifiers reconstruct the evidence locally.
