# Claim contract

## Claim

The six row-uniform unordered collision-pair bounds in `statement.md`.

## Guards

1. `E_low` counts characteristic-zero classes after reduction, not raw subset
   pairs, coefficient vectors, templates, or Galois orbits.
2. Every edge has `0<S<=2ell` and equal reduced E1 value in the actual row
   prime field.
3. The field, quotient root, clean predecessor, and row envelope are pinned.
4. A finite selected-field count does not prove a row-family entry.
5. High-square-mass collisions are not counted; the Plotkin second-moment
   inequality prices them automatically.

## Falsifier

The exact row and excess edge ledger described in `statement.md`, or any
missing class-pair multiplicity in a purported upper bound.
