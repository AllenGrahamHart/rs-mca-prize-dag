# Attack

Start with prize rate `1/8`, where `N=256`, `ell=33`, and `S<=66`.

1. Prove maximum low-mass collision degree at most three. This immediately
   gives `E_low<=3K/2`, below the exact budget `1.648K`.
2. If degree four occurs, count the degree distribution rather than abandoning
   the route; the target is an aggregate edge budget, not a max-degree claim.
3. Express neighbors of one class as feasible folded kernel vectors and retain
   the exact number of class-pair realizations of every vector.
4. Use common-prime ideal/resultant constraints to bound how many low-mass
   vectors can be incident to one class at one row prime.
5. Falsify with a pinned row and enough exact edges to exceed the table, not a
   single collision vector.

No broad local or Modal census is authorized. Large exact relation searches
must be recorded as external compute requests with partial-result output.
