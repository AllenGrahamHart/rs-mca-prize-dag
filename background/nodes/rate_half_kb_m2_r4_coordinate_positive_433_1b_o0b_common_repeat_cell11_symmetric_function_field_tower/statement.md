- **status:** PROVED
- **field:** `F_2130706433`
- **scope:** all eight guarded cell-11 common rows

```text

```

finite algebra over `F_p(x)` has the displayed bases

```text

```

algebra and then `c=y-b`.  All four symmetric tower generators reduce to zero
modulo the two displayed relations.  Conversely, the two relations are drawn
from that tower, so the localized ideals agree.

record, pay a formal label, close role cell 11 or 14, or close K3.
