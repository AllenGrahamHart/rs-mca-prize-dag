# Claim contract

## Input

The ambient/record individual caps, the rank-preserving nine-shadow
resource, the full-containment nine-shadow resource, and the exact dominant
kernel demand.

## Output

Exact exclusion of the dominant kernel lane for every
`10<=K'<=15670`, with the first method wall at `K'=15671`.

## Nonclaim

No kernel exclusion above `15670`, rank-eight payment, dense-owner
chronology payment, rank-eleven payment, or prize closure is asserted.
