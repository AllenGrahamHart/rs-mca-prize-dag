six light-light differences and supplies the one-orbit-per-mask atlas.  For a
fixed mask, choose the class promoted from magnitude one to three and four of
the remaining 57 classes to have magnitude two.  This gives
`6*binom(57,4)` assignments.

The production engine evaluates the signed zero-sum cubic kernel from
normalized vertex triples.  The audit independently enumerates positive
circular gaps and reconstructs the kernel from pair-sum tables.  Their 64
rows agree on every count, histogram, maximum, witness, and exceptional
record.  The complete summaries are `2,924,654,040` assignments, `1,456`
exceptions, `321` exceptional masks, and maximum `1278`.  The node verifier
checks source and result hashes and replays the rowwise equality.  QED.
