# Audit

The primary packet independently recomputes the slack dynamic program, all
12 energy profiles, the exact rational cubic boundary, all normalized light
supports, their affine canonical forms, and every diameter ledger.

The separate checker rederives the profile list and cubic forms without
importing the Modal program. It uses a recursive profile enumerator, Gaussian
elimination for the Hermite basis, and direct affine-orbit expansion. The
eight used representatives expand disjointly to all 960 three-odd supports.
The unused one-odd and five-odd orbits are checked as well, so the complete
one-diameter atlas totals 119 orbits and 15,624 supports.

All replay is verifier-scale; no census is rerun locally.
