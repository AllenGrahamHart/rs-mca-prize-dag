# Frontier

The common locus is not empty.  Its smallest projection is the irreducible
degree-six, 14-term `(t,r)` relation.  The full two-variable reciprocal
projections alone did not expose a useful recovery map, but the proved child
`(KBP1B3-QUOT-1)` now gives a guarded quadratic-in-`b`, linear-in-`c`
presentation of the complete common ideal.  A direct six-variable outside
formulation using the full polynomial kernel timed out during SymPy expansion
before Gröbner work.  Do not scale that formulation.

The next attack should use the child theorem's quadratic pair algebra,
eliminate the missing record before forming pair equations, and reduce
coefficient pairs against the `(t,r)` base relation at every stage.  Only after
one representative yields a low-degree guarded factorization should any
sign/lane/matching census be launched.
