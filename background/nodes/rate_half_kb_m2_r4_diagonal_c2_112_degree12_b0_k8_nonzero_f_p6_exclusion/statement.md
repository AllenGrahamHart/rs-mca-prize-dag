# Statement

Let `p0=2130706433` and let

```text
I = (R12, C_A, C_B) subset F_p0[x,s,pvar]
```

be the `F04-R02`, `B0` degree-34 core ideal. Let `K8` be the nonnamed
degree-8 factor of the `B0` leading coefficient and put

```text
B = V K8 (s^2-4pvar).
```

Then `I` has no `F_(p0^6)` point on the complete named-open chart with
`K8!=0`.

More precisely, the Rabinowitsch ideal

```text
I + (z B-1)
```

is zero-dimensional, and its elimination to `F_p0[s]` is generated by a
degree-30 polynomial whose irreducible factor degrees are

```text
1,1,1,1,1,4,4,5.
```

The five linear roots are

```text
1691727589, 2130706429, 1804255948, 1065353214, 675900418.
```

At each linear root, the degree-six field equations force the complete open
product to zero.
