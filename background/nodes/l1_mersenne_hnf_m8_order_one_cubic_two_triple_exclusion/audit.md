# Audit

The new relation comes from the `W^2` coefficient of the same exact
two-fiber factorization used by the dependency. It must not be exported to
another cubic multiplicity partition.

No division by `s`, `S`, or `d+2` occurs. The exceptional case `S!=0` is
disposed of by the incompatible simultaneous conditions `s=0,d=-2`.

The final norm argument uses that both candidate `d` values are in the base
field and that every official prime is `7 mod 8` and differs from
`2,5,13`. No external computation or cyclotomic converse is used.
