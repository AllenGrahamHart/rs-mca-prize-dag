# Proof

The existing `F04` route node excludes both `F04-R02` and `F04-R20`.
The literal logarithmic router is exhaustive between its two denominator
unions and guarded numerator. The literal denominator node excludes both
unions in all six `F05`--`F07` routes. The literal guarded-numerator node then
directly excludes `F05-R02`, `F07-R02`, and `F06-R20`.

Apply the proved complete-system involution `b -> b^-1`. It transports
`F04-Rxx` to `F05-Rxx` and `F06-Rxx` to `F07-Rxx`, preserving the full
quotient identities and complete named open. Hence the two `F04` exclusions
give both `F05` exclusions; `F07-R02` gives `F06-R02`; and `F06-R20` gives
`F07-R20`. These direct and transported cases cover all eight routes. QED.
