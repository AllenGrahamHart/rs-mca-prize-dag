# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the complete E16 four-profile/154-template router;
- the proved proper-conductor collision exclusion;
- the collision norm criterion and pair-feasible prime threshold.

## Output

No profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate at `V=32` collides on the pair-feasible branch.

## Nonclaims

No lower variance, profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform pair allowance,
row, or prize terminal is closed here. The whole norms are not claimed to be
below `2^250`; exactly ten are not.

## Falsifier

A missed vector, engine disagreement, conductor leak, nonpositive resultant,
odd part at least `2^250`, or field/normalization mismatch refutes the result.
