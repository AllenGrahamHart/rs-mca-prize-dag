# Audit

- The nested norm is only a necessary condition. This is sufficient for an
  exclusion, and every finite survivor is replayed against the omitted
  colored pair and original target guards.
- The source signs are pinned separately: `de=m, eta=1` for `xi=0`, and
  `de=-m, eta=-1` for `xi=2`.
- The pseudo-remainder is division-free, and the final scalar includes the
  `P_v` leading coefficient, so degree drop is part of the candidate union.
- Every numerator and denominator root of the four rational inversions and
  final norm is directly lifted. Such roots are not declared boundaries
  unless an original route or target guard vanishes.
- The verifier independently reconstructs all finite quadratic root lists,
  missing records, squared sums, `H` values, `f` roots, and colored cuts.
- The `xi=1` transport is checked by ordered residual product and squared-sum
  signatures at matching index 3.
- The theorem counts atlas sign/lane cases, not lifted field points.
