- **Claim ID:** `rate_half_kb_m2_r4_diagonal_facet_mixing_obstruction`
- **Input:** an actual diagonal-order-two component and its proved
  whole-fiber quartic transport with the common-five facet supports.
- **Output:** exclusion of `tau(I)=I`, the five exact crossing-orbit rows
  `(KBDM-3)`, and the transported common-`K` root restrictions `(KBDM-4)`.
- **Deletion interface:** every proposed diagonal packet whose endpoint
  involution preserves `I | J`, or whose surviving mixing row violates
  `(KBDM-3)--(KBDM-4)`, is deleted.
- **Not claimed:** failure of all five mixing rows, source-line or
  biquadratic branch deletion, orientation/type deletion, owner, payment,
  row, or Prize close.
