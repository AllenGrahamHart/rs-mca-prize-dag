# Audit

1. The involution here is the endpoint quadratic deck involution, not the
   unrelated canonical matching also denoted `tau` in Corollary 9.27 of the
   source-facet theorem.
2. Fixed-point-freeness on the twelve labels is inherited from the terminal
   unramified degree-two profile; it is not inferred from a chosen affine
   normal form.
3. The contradiction uses whole-fiber quartics. It therefore does not assume
   individual-star transport or source-line descent.
4. At an `L^c` label, each one-exchange facet has exactly one `J` label, so
   the two-star quartic has at most two `J` roots with multiplicity. This
   uses the source theorem's reduced six-label outgoing fibers; a component
   factor cannot take the unique `J` root twice.
5. `c` counts crossing labels on either side, hence is even. The five rows
   retain both possibilities for whether `xi` is paired into `K`.
6. No claim is made that any of the five mixing signatures is realizable or
   impossible.
