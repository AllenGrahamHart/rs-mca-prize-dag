# Claim contract

- **Proved:** an exact bijection between local depth-`t` records and
  translated complementary divisors `(B,B+H)` of the fixed base/complement
  locator pair.
- **Proved:** `d=1` records are automatically coefficient-primitive on a
  cyclic multiplicative coset.
- **Proved route cut:** fixed base support plus fixed linear `H` does not
  determine the record; explicit multiplicity is at least three.
- **Quantifier:** the divisor dictionary is field- and domain-general; the
  primitivity consequence uses the cyclic-coset quotient sieve.
- **Nonclaims:** a bound on the translated-divisor intersection, a bound on
  the number of possible `H`, or any payment toward `16N^3`.
