# Audit

- The restriction map is an isomorphism, not only a surjection: both
  `H^0(O(-3,j-e))` and `H^1(O(-3,j-e))` vanish.
- Homogeneous parameter degree handles a possible root at infinity. Since
  the supported locator contains only the selected finite slopes, infinity
  belongs to the residual factor when present.
- Repeated supported roots contribute all copies beyond the first to
  `R_x`; this is why `deg R_x=e-d_x` exactly.
- `B_j` is the product of actual heavy evaluation-domain rows, not the full
  gcd of the coefficients. The coefficient gcd may have additional roots.
- Divisibility is rowwise over the base field after scalar extension; the
  split product `B_j` is base-field rational.
