# Claim contract

- **claim:** exact identities `(L2-1)` and `(L2-2)`, their lower bound, and
  the equivalence between ternary mass and weighted odd-prefix L2 collision;
- **dependency:** the admissible direct-sum theorem supplies the exact class
  half-system and moment matrix;
- **scope:** one prime-field class at a time, with the full subset cube;
- **nonclaims:** no L2 upper bound, no fixed-slice theorem, no full upstream
  `(Q)` identification, no max-fiber bound, and no F2 target close;
- **falsifier:** any finite class for which the two integer sides of
  `sum_v N(v)^2=2^S Z_1` differ.
