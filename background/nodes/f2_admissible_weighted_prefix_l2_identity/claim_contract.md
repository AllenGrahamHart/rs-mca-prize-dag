# Claim contract

- **claim:** exact identities `(L2-1)`, `(L2-2)`, and `(L2-4)`, their lower
  bound, and the equivalence between ternary mass, weighted odd-prefix L2
  collision, and full-agreement two-list GRS recovery;
- **dependency:** the plus-branch admissible direct-sum theorem supplies the
  exact class half-system and moment matrix;
- **scope:** one plus-branch prime-field class at a time, with the full
  subset cube;
- **nonclaims:** no L2 upper bound, no fixed-slice theorem, no full upstream
  `(Q)` identification, no list-recovery bound, no max-fiber bound, and no
  F2 target close, and no minus-branch kernel identity;
- **falsifier:** any finite class for which the two integer sides of
  `sum_v N(v)^2=2^S Z_1` differ.
