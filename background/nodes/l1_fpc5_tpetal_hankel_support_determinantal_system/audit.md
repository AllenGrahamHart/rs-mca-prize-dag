# Audit

The shifted generalized Vandermonde determinant uses that official smooth
evaluation points are nonzero. Distinctness alone is insufficient for
`r>0` if zero is admitted.

The Cramer amplitudes represent only the finite moment segment needed by
the degree-`d-1` punctured polynomials. No infinite exponential-sum
representation is claimed.

The determinantal equations are equivalent to the Hankel recurrences but
do not make their point count small. Quotient-periodic and other structured
components, background Cauchy guards, and chronology must still be handled.
