# Claim contract

- **Input:** one owner-free FPC5 Padé-Hankel kernel over a smooth
  multiplicative domain and one monic core-split locator.
- **Output:** exact generalized-Vandermonde determinant equations and exact
  Cramer-amplitude primitive guards on the unordered support.
- **Preserved:** moment sequence, core support, locator recurrence,
  splitness, squarefreeness, primitivity, background Cauchy guards, and
  first-owner filters.
- **Nonclaim:** a determinantal point count, component classification,
  periodic/aperiodic payment, background coalescence, or a deployed row.
