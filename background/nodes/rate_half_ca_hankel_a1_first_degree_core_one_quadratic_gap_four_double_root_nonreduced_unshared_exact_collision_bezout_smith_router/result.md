# Result

The exact nonreduced locator collision is no longer a four-profile
higher-corank problem. Its contact algebra is quadratic, its regular
corank is exactly two, and one coefficient decides between `[1,3]` and
`[2,2]`. The remaining attack surface is the first parameter coefficient
of the `y`-term in `P_F mod q`, together with the global geometry needed
to exclude the resulting two profiles.
