# Audit

1. The proof uses the complete raw signed-pair resultant, not a selected live
   factor and not sampled sufficiency.
2. Every pseudo-reduction records its step count; only powers of the proved
   nonzero plane-leading scale are introduced.
3. Proportionality is certified by a quotient-ring cross identity, not
   ambient divisibility.
4. The degree-2104 leading norm is factored exactly.  Every base-field root
   is either delegated to the prior exceptional-scale theorem or replayed.
5. The two zero specialized resultants occur only at the original guard
   `b+1=0`; no mathematical conclusion is inferred from a zero polynomial
   without this guard check.
6. Every deployed root in the remaining four lifts is recorded with an
   explicit product, denominator, or source-label guard.
7. Mutation tests alter the projected hash, cross identity, norm roots,
   finite atlas, and input custody; all are rejected.
