# Lineage

The preceding rank-one theorem left 576 cases with a missing `de` record.
Direct Laurent arithmetic over `F_p(r)` required roughly two minutes per
structural case. Rebuilding the equations first in ordinary `(u,v,f)`
polynomials, applying the torus substitution term by term, and moving the
common-`b` projection to FLINT reduced the representative runtime to seconds.
An exact 36-shape survey split the frontier into 27 favorable role/matching
shapes and nine all-mixed shapes. The favorable shapes expand to this complete
432-case theorem; the all-mixed shapes expand to the remaining 144 cases.
