# Audit

1. Divisibility by the cubic `Lambda_A` is termwise: a classified coordinate
   obtains two factors from its locator row and the missing factor from its
   source form.
2. The exceptional coordinate, when present, obtains all three factors from
   its locator row; no assumption on the root of its source form is used.
3. The dual-GRS dimension is `p-2`, so its polynomial representatives have
   degree at most `p-3`, not `p-2`.
4. The Lagrange identity applies through degree `n_0-2`; equation `(6)`
   reaches that endpoint exactly.
5. The clean-fiber count requires both `a_delta=0` and `r_delta=0`. Cycle 128
   proves at least `e+6+d_A` such simultaneous events.
6. At a clean fiber there is no padded factor, and the actual inside and
   outside locators multiply to the full residual locator up to the one
   nonzero specialization scalar `chi_delta`; that scalar is retained in
   `zeta_delta`.
7. Polynomial equality in `(DSB10)` follows from all `|U_0|` evaluations,
   not merely from agreement on `X_delta`.
