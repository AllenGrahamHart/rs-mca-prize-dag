# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the extremal source and locator define the exact biform
  `(DSB4)`, with at least `3p-3+d_A` fully split domain rows and
  `e+6+d_A` fully split parameter fibers.
- **Inputs:** the three-center source partition, the zero-excess
  Forney-barycentric gate, the global Hankel locator equation, and standard
  dual-GRS interpolation on `U_0`.
- **Outputs:** exact bidegree `(e-2,p-3)`, row splitting `(DSB5)`, and clean
  fiber factorization `(DSB10)`.
- **Falsifier:** failure of termwise divisibility by `Lambda_A`, a moment in
  `(DSB3)` that does not vanish, an `X`-degree above `p-3`, or a clean fiber
  not proportional to its inside-support locator.
- **Nonclaims:** the biform is not asserted reducible, rational, or
  impossible; the exceptional row is deliberately left unclassified.
