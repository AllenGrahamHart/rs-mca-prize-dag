# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every quadratic `u=4` pair has actual-support union at least
  `3rho/2-1`; every assigned-center line has at most three supported slopes.
- **Inputs:** the `rho+4` pair floor, fixed light core design, global light
  degree `e`, packet deficit `e-6`, and minimum distance `2rho+1`.
- **Outputs:** macroscopic floor `(MPF2)`, line cap `(MPF4)--(MPF5)`, and
  `rho+1` expanding thirds `(MPF6)`.
- **Falsifier:** a pair with `4<=j<=rho/2-2`, an off-line slope violating
  `(7)`, or a value in `(11)` with `F_e(j)<=0`.
- **Nonclaims:** the quadratic packet, adjacent crossing, and maximal safe
  delta are not closed.
