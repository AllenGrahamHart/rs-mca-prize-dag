# Audit

The primary verifier checks the pinned four-row degree-20 catalogue, all
five incoming `(r,delta)` types, every proper factor degree, and the exact
destination routing.

The independent verifier constructs `PSL(2,19)` and `PGL(2,19)` as Mobius
actions on the 20-point projective line, checks their orders and
two-transitivity, and verifies the natural `A20,S20` subdegree argument.
Classification completeness remains the explicitly pinned imported input.
