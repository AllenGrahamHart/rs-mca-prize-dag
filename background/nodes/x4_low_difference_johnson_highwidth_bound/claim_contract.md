# Claim contract

- **Proved general bound:** fixed-base records of side width `e` and locator
  difference degree at most `d` have changed-set intersections at most `e+d`
  and satisfy `(HJ-2)` when its denominator is positive.
- **Proved X4 specialization:** all official base-row `d=1` widths above
  `N/4` cost at most `N^3/64` in aggregate.
- **Residual:** only `t_XR+2<=e<=N/4` remains in the `d=1` branch.
- **Transport:** arbitrary first-owner deletion preserves the bound.
- **Nonclaims:** the low-width residue, any summed `d>=2` payment, a quotient
  tuple, the complete `16N^3` column, or LIST/MCA closure.
