# Audit

1. The theorem counts records incident to one fixed base support, which is
   the local maximum-degree currency required by X4/SP.
2. The changed set is `P union Q`, not the neighbour support itself.
3. The pairwise intersection bound uses two disjoint root regions and the
   full locator difference; no assumption about fixed `H` is made.
4. The aggregate sums fixed-width bounds over at most `N/4` widths.  It does
   not sum over bases.
5. The numerical cutoff is specific to `N=2^41,d=1`; quotient tuples and
   larger difference degrees need their own inequality.
