# Statement

All eight projective charts of canonical
`(cell=3,S0,sigma_o=-1,epsilon=(-1,-1),xi=2,pairing=0)` are empty.
Consequently the original matching-resultant system is empty, and the exact
Klein-four transport excludes its four raw cases.

After this theorem and the earlier basis-case-0 theorem, the cells-3/6
workboard has `1414` representatives remaining from its original `1416`.
