# Result

The canonical form of `(FR)` is fully proved. At `a*=7m-1`, every type-2
slope spends at least `2m+1` roots outside the minimum pair-union support,
equivalently meeting that support in at most `2m-2` roots.

not close the rate-half ledger: the exact endpoint closing spend is
`9m/4+1`. The remaining problem is additional concentration or algebra beyond
the minimum-pair-union identity, not proof of `(FR)` itself.
