# Audit

- The symmetry exchanges records, not target vertices; no unproved variable
  change is used.
- Identical positive `DE` records have both identical products and identical
  squared sums. Preserving products alone would be insufficient.
- Pair equations are treated as an unordered system; the involution may
  exchange the first two paired equations but preserves their ideal.
- `xi=0 <-> xi=1` is a missing-role transport with matching index fixed.
  For `xi>=2`, the missing role is fixed and matching indices are permuted.
- No target-lane or cell-`4 -> 7` quotient is claimed.
