# Audit

The Round-20 re-pose correctly derives a finite F2 mass target under the
full-subset balance and an exact-value expression after substituting the
exact-slice depth. Its latter calculation is an algebraic diagnostic, not a
guarded F2 consumer obligation: the same positive deficit used in that
calculation makes `|B0|^t<2^N`, even when `B0=F_q`.

The newer ambient-invariance theorem does not contradict the older catch-11
scope rule. It identifies censuses at a fixed `t`; catch 11 decides whether
the consumer invokes F2 at that `t`. Treating ambient invariance as permission
to recalibrate `t` after descent would change the block condition and is not
licensed by the theorem.

The safe route interpretation is therefore:

```text
(C) depth:   F2 guard selects exactly k=e; finite mass target remains open.
(T*) depth:  F2 guard selects no row; a different fixed-slice route is needed.
```

No critical status changes follow until that alternate route is supplied or
the consumer guard is explicitly replaced and re-proved.
