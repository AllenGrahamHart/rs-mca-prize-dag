# Proof

At matching 0 the direct xi3 theorem and its xi4 transport pay `(3,0)` and
`(4,0)`. At matchings 1 and 2 the direct xi3 theorem and its xi4 transport pay
`{3,4} x {1,2}`. The remaining six parents respectively pay

```text
{3,4} x {3,6}, {3,4} x {4,9}, {3,4} x {5,12},
{3,4} x {7,10}, {3,4} x {8,13}, {3,4} x {11,14}.
```

The matching sets `{0}`, `{1,2}`, and the six displayed pairs are pairwise
disjoint and cover `{0,...,14}`. The ten parent scopes therefore form a
disjoint cover of `{3,4} x {0,...,14}`, containing `2*15=30` labels. Every
parent proves emptiness across the same four source-sign pairs and four target
lanes, so the raw census is `30*16=480`. QED.
