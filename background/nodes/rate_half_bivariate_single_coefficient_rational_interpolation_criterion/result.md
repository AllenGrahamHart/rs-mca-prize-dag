# Result

The residual rank problem has a pivot-free scalar formulation. On the clean
frontier, the `h_m` coefficient block fails exactly when

```text
h_m(x)=Q(x)/P(x)
```

on all of `W`, with numerator and denominator degrees below
`r=|W|-(4m+1)`. If the unique deficient point lies in `W`, the same agreement
is required on the punctured support.

This is the next official theorem target. The bounded `m=2` campaign says the
third-root/trace data resist this rational interpolation in every sampled bad
pattern, including the punctured branch. A proof must connect that resistance
to the saturation, minimum-pair, and bad-overlap axioms.
