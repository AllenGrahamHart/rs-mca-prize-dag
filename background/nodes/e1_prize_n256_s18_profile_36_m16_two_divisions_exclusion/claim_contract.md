# Claim contract

## Imported facts

- Cofactor 16 forces exact multiplicity four in the six-singleton binary
  support.
- The bounded product parent leaves `E=2,...,108`, and the energy-adaptive cap
  permits exact specialization at autocorrelation energy `E` and `L1` norm

- Translation, odd Galois multiplication, and global sign preserve the
  collision question.
- The 48-bit fixed-root table has a separate 256-bit Arb audit proving every
  scaled component error strictly below one.

## New theorem

The exact-multiplicity-four support class splits exhaustively into primitive,
once-divided, and twice-divided branches. For the twice-divided branch, an
exact product certificate, a complete 903-orbit atlas, two complete radius
engines, direct replay of every low-energy audit survivor, and certified
fixed-point root intervals exhaust every signed vector. Every norm is strictly
below or above the complete cofactor-16 prize interval.

## Exclusions

1. This node does not exclude all of cofactor 16. The 39,936 primitive and
   9,080 once-divided affine support orbits remain open.
2. The radius syndrome is only a necessary filter. Every hit is followed by
   exact integer autocorrelation replay, and the reverse audit directly
   reconstructs every low-energy survivor.
3. Product-live filtering is conservative in `L`; it can retain extra vectors
   but cannot discard a possible collision.
4. Long-double norm values are diagnostic only. Closure uses integer interval
   products from the Arb-audited fixed-root table.
5. The result supplies a zero contribution from one exact support stratum; it
   does not by itself close a pair budget or a prize row.
