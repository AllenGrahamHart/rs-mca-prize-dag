# Result

No next-to-maximal order-zero quadratic color interpolant is collision-free.
Pairing roots under the quadratic involution produces a degree-`h` invariant
polynomial whose first three coefficients are a scaled copy of the
truncated-binomial coefficients. A punctured-cyclotomic color map then lands
on the already excluded three-coefficient system. Together with the
quadratic router, `m=8` has exactly one repeated color, while `m=16` retains
that branch and an even multi-repeat branch.
