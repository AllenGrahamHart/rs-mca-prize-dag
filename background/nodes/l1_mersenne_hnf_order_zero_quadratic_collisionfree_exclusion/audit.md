# Audit

## Root pairing

The reflected root `S-a_i` is outside `P_s` only under the explicit
collision-free hypothesis. The product in `(2)` is not used for a repeated
fiber. Its invariant `U=W(W-S)` is fixed by `W -> S-W`, and its roots are
distinct because the colors `AU+C` are distinct.

## Coefficient range

Only the first three coefficients are consumed. Their power-sum derivation
uses locator moments through degree six; both `h=7` and `h=15` cover that
range. No pattern observed at `h=7` is extrapolated to `h=15` without this
proof.

The `z=0` branch is kept separate before defining `Y=yz`. The contradiction
there uses the exact nonzero value `(h+1)/(2h)` and does not divide by `z`.

## Nonclaims

The result forces at least one repeated color; it does not classify the
exactly-one-repeat system. The `m=16` even multi-repeat system from the
quadratic router also remains live.
