# Proof

Move `alpha` to affine parameter zero and expand

```text
Qbar(z;T)=sum_(i=0)^e Q_i(T)z^i.                    (1)
```

The two-slope coefficient-rank theorem gives

```text
|X|=r_alpha+2,
rank (Q_i(x))_(x in X,0<=i<=e)<=1.                  (2)
```

No row in `(2)` is zero: otherwise the corresponding residual-domain point
would be a further parameter-independent factor after the exact core-one
factor had been contracted. Thus all rows are nonzero and proportional.
This proves `(QCB2)` for a nonzero parameter form `P_(alpha,beta)`.

Every point of `X` belongs to the actual error support at `beta`. In the two
exact `u=4` ledgers, the only padded roots are the named heavy points and
they are absent from actual errors. Hence every point of `X` is light. A
light row occurs in exactly `e` supported locators, while its degree-`e`
row form divides the squarefree supported-slope form. Therefore the common
form in `(QCB2)` is squarefree and cuts out exactly the common `e` locator
slopes.

Let `eta_x` be the contracted derivative source weight on `X` after
subtracting the affine codeword line through the two assigned centers. The
complete coefficient chain, paired with

```text
Q_min,alpha F[T]_(<=r_alpha),                        (3)
```

gives, for every `deg A<=r_alpha` and every `i`,

```text
sum_(x in X)eta_x A(x)Q_i(x)=0.                      (4)
```

Substitute `Q_i(x)=lambda_x a_i`, where the `a_i` are the coefficients of
the common form. Some `a_i` is nonzero, so

```text
sum_(x in X)eta_x lambda_x A(x)=0
                  for every deg A<=r_alpha.          (5)
```

The Vandermonde matrix in `(5)` has `r_alpha+1` rows and `r_alpha+2`
columns. Its kernel is one-dimensional and is generated by the standard
barycentric vector

```text
(1/L_X'(x))_(x in X).                               (6)
```

Thus `eta_x lambda_x=kappa_0/L_X'(x)` for one nonzero `kappa_0`.

On `X`, the error at `alpha` is zero and the error at `beta` is nonzero.
Core contraction and the affine parameter difference give

```text
eta_x=constant*(x-s_0)v_x(f_beta-c_beta)(x),         (7)
```

with the same nonzero constant for every `x`. Absorb that constant into
`kappa_0` to obtain `(QCB4)`. Interchanging `alpha,beta` proves the reverse
statement. QED.
