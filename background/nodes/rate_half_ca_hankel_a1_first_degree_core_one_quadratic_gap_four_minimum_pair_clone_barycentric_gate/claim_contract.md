  barycentric endpoint-error formula in both quadratic `u=4` arms.
- **Currency:** the Cycle-116 rank-one boundary, exact light/heavy incidence
  ledgers, contracted RS sources, and Vandermonde inversion.
- **Dependencies:** the two-slope rank spread and the split-pencil source
  equivalence.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** existence or exclusion of a sharp pair is not asserted.
- **Falsifier:** nonproportional coefficient rows on a sharp difference, a
  repeated root of their common light-row form, or failure of `(QCB4)`.
- **Next gate:** compare `(QCB4)` with the Forney numerator/contact formula,
  or bound clone fibres globally using the Hankel origin of `Qbar`.
