# Result

The `nu=4` Mason/Wronskian case is impossible. Every hypothetical official
`m=4,h=3` record now has `nu=0,1,2,3` and a nonzero eliminant `H` of degree at
most `3,2,1,0` respectively. Each case also satisfies the exact Cartier
coefficient constraints in `(CRR5)`. Four cases remain, so the branch and L1
remain open.
