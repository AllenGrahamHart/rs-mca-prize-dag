# Audit

1. The coefficient is `x-x_0`; using `x_0-x` only rescales the relation.
2. Column-farness is applied to the joint support of the two source words,
   not separately to either source.
3. Removing `x_0` first gives `|U|>=rho`; Vandermonde invertibility excludes
   `rho+1`, while the rational-normal motion of `Q` excludes `rho`.
4. The generic source combination is chosen after scalar extension; finite
   forbidden projective directions cannot cover `P^1` over the algebraic
   closure.
5. Full support refers to nonzero tensor coefficients `lambda_x`; it does
   not assert that the dependence is a minimal circuit.
6. Saturation is used only after the marked row has been removed.
7. The node does not infer independence from dimension counting.
