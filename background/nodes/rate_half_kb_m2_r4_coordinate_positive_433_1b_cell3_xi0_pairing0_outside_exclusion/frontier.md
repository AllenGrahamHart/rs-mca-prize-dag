# Frontier

The parallel-positive-`DE` transport pays `xi=1,pairing=0`, and the separate
negative-`DE` census pays `xi=2,pairing=0`.  Generalize the same three-stage
route to `xi=3,pairing=0`:

1. form one necessary paired cut in the six-element algebra;
2. compute and independently cross-check its norm;
3. replay only its base-field roots and finish surviving fibers by univariate
   target elimination.

For `xi=3`, the missing record is `df`, its squared-sum row is `(d+f)^2`, and
the first residual pair remains `(de,de)`.  Reuse the proved target-free root
ledger if exact equation comparison permits; otherwise run one source-sign
pilot under the same bounded Modal profile.

Do not build a dense multivariate fraction-field resultant.  SymPy timed out
before completing one such pilot, while Singular cannot use the deployed
prime as a coefficient-field characteristic.  Neither failure bears on the
mathematical truth of the remaining cases.
