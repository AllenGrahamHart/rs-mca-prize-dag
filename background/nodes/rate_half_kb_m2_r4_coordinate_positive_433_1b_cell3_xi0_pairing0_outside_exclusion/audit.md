# Audit

- A norm-zero condition is used only as a necessary condition.  No converse
  is assumed; every norm root is lifted through the original equations.
- Denominator and route-boundary roots are not silently discarded.  The root
  replay records all eleven roots and applies the printed guards directly.
- The target-free cut does not itself exclude the case: four guarded common
  points survive for each source sign.  The separate finite solver pays every
  surviving target lane.
- The solver checks the original five target equations whenever a candidate
  target tuple is reconstructed.  In the completed census no `u` root exists,
  so no tuple reaches that final guard stage.
- Target guards require all six representatives to be nonzero and every pair
  to be neither equal nor negatives.  Zero witnesses and zero boundary
  solutions are recorded separately.
- The `64` point/lane rows are finite branches of only `16` raw outside cases;
  they must not be advertised as 64 independently closed atlas cases.
- The direct SymPy timeout and Singular characteristic failure are route
  no-gos, not evidence for emptiness.
- No symmetry transport to another missing record or matching is claimed.
