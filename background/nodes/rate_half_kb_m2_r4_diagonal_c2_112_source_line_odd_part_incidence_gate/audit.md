# Audit

1. The internal-orbit ramification exclusion uses only the one remaining
   defect unit; it does not exclude ramification of the forced square orbit.
2. The common root is for the two stars over one source fiber, not merely a
   repeated incidence somewhere in the four-star multiset.
3. Formula `(KBOI-2)` uses deck distinction to rule out the zero odd part.
4. The denominator in `(KBOI-3)` is proved nonzero from `J_0` and `J_1`
   disjointness, not assumed generically.
5. The affine formula may be applied after choosing the finite member of
   each reciprocal label pair; its reciprocal partner is automatic.
6. The ramified forced-square, biquadratic, and exceptional branches remain.
