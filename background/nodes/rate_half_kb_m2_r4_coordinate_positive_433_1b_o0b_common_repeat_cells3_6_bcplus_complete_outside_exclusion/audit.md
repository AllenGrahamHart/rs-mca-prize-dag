# Audit

The aggregate verifier pins the finite launcher, compact master, and three
record shards. It rebuilds every one of the 360 compact uncolored case rows
from the detailed shards and checks 7,000 exceptional fibers, 5,840
case/base incidences, 3,520 endpoint roots, zero residual roots, zero
survivors, and zero unresolved rows. Required node edges enforce the colored
record and transport proofs.

Hostile controls delete a compact case, change a fiber count, desynchronize a
shard, and corrupt a shard digest.
