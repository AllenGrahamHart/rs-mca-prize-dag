# Claim contract

The claim is exactly the universal pair deficit and its summed resource
consequence (R8FC). It uses:

- full rank ten on every exact support;
- generalized-MDS closure caps `K'-2` in rank eight and `K'-1` in rank
  nine;
- the proved spanning-shadow multiplicities three and six.

It does not assert that the pair floor is attained by an RS evaluation
matroid, pay the complete kernel lane, or pay error rank eleven.
