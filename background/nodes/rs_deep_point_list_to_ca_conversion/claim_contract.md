# Claim contract

- **Input:** adjacent Reed-Solomon dimensions `K` and `K+1`, `q>N`, an
  integer-radius deep-point gate, and a finite-slope CA numerator bound for
  the dimension-`K` code.
- **Output:** the exact rational-ceiling list bound `(DP5)` for the
  dimension-`K+1` code.
- **Preserved:** field, evaluation set, radius, closed-ball convention, and
  finite-slope normalization.
- **Nonclaim:** generalized-code transport, interleaved transport, MCA
  conversion, or removal of the strict denominator gate.
