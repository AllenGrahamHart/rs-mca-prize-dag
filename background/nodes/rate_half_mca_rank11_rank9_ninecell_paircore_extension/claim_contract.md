# Claim contract

## Input

One original-row rank-nine affine owner plane fixed by a nine-coordinate
evaluation-rank-nine set, together with actual record lines and their exact
size-`m` supports.

## Output

The same low-core cap `1434405` as the ten-cell theorem, or one
`134944`-coordinate pair core shared by the complete plane.

## Nonclaim

The theorem does not produce the nine-cell population, count different
planes, identify cores across planes, or pay the shared-core route.
