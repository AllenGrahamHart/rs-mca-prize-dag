# Proof

Put `P_12=F[X]_<12` and `Lambda=(V')^perp`, so `dim Lambda=2`.
Partition the component incidences supplied by the dense-locator theorem
according to the rank of evaluation on their eleven-set `T`.

## 1. Rank-deficient component capacity

The canonical-basis kernel globalizer gives an absolute, not merely
dominant-lane, bound.  At `K'=12`, the shortening excess is `K'-10=2`.
For corank `d`, the extension factor is

```text
C(2,d+1).
```

It vanishes for every `d>=2`.  For `d=1`, the basis has size nine, the
proved record cap is `M_1(12)=16295594`, and the extension factor is one.
Consequently every rank-deficient component incidence together has capacity

```text
K_cap=C(1048588,9)*16295594
     =68823412552626461731638254358120971630939282959681665560.   (1)
```

## 2. Rank-nine chart capacity

Now let `T` have evaluation rank ten.  Annihilator duality gives

```text
dim(Lambda intersect span{ev_x:x in T})=1.
```

Let `[lambda_T]` be that point and `C_T` the support of its unique
evaluation representation on `T`; write `c_T=|C_T|`.

Fix a nine-subset `B` of rank nine.  Its evaluation kernel in `V'` is one
nonzero polynomial of degree below twelve.  If `J` is the common core of
the affine owner plane, then

```text
B subset J subset Z_D(u),       j=|J| in {9,10,11}.              (2)
```

As in the residual-petal decomposition, put `P=m'-j`, `S=n'-j`, and
`r=j-9`.  The weighted common-core offset theorem gives the exact capacities

```text
j=9:  9275079825564861,
j=10: 9276021414531691,
j=11: 9276963034268184.                                  (3)
```

Thus every rank-nine chart has marked load at most

```text
C_*=9276963034268184.                                    (4)
```

## 3. Circuit shadows

For a nine-subset `B=T minus D`, where `D` is an omitted pair, the
intersection `Lambda intersect E_B` is one-dimensional exactly when
`D` is disjoint from `C_T`.  In that case it is generated by `lambda_T`,
and `rank(ev_B|V')=8`.  If `D` meets `C_T`, the intersection is zero:
another quotient functional in `E_B subset E_T` would contradict the
one-dimensional intersection on `T`.  Hence `B` has rank nine.

It follows that `T` has exactly

```text
r_9(c_T)=55-C(11-c_T,2)                                (5)
```

rank-nine shadows.  For `c_T>=6`, this is at least 45.  Summing the chart
capacity `(4)` over all nine-sets therefore bounds all high-circuit
incidences by

```text
H_cap=floor(C(1048588,9)C_*/45)
     =870681505337379475658181372289433062059140012353857046633355381.
                                                               (6)
```

For `c_T<=5`, apply the codimension-two quotient-line sparse-circuit
theorem to each selected record support of size `m'=67484`.  It gives

```text
L_*=11868577829520852215896202871552159662636920            (7)
```

incidences per record.  Thus `R_actual` records contribute at most
`R_actual L_*` low-circuit incidences.

## 4. Exact contradiction

Combining `(1)`, `(6)`, and `(7)` gives `(K12C)`.  The dense-locator theorem
requires at least

```text
ceil((990810934/10^9) R_actual C(67484,11))             (8)
```

component incidences.  The coefficient of `R_actual` in the unrounded
difference between `(8)` and the low-circuit term is positive, with cross
numerator

```text
3266743881505321144251837279559822170446618230146889560.    (9)
```

The difference is therefore smallest at the proved floor
`R_actual=274980728111260126`.  There, `(8)` equals

```text
901555241262544083284435178226046105523688795046262319915891531,
```

while `(K12C)` equals

```text
873945204333998831582903951502910514268526233054054867526472861.
```

The positive gap is the one printed in the statement.  This contradicts
the existence of the component population and closes `K'=12`.  QED.
