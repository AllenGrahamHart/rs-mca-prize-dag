# Proof

The E14 router proves that every `V=28` candidate lies in one of four profiles
on 1,321 templates. The four-profile exclusion proves that every
proper-conductor vector is removed by the conductor theorem. For the
full-conductor residue, all but six odd norm parts lie below `2^250`; the six
exceptions are composite integers below `2^251` and therefore have no prime
divisor above `2^250`. These branches exhaust the router, so the entire
`V=28` chamber is empty. Variance is even, hence the next live positive
endpoint is at most 26.
