`3+2+1`, fully proportional exceptional coefficient residue. In upstream
terminology it belongs with the exact primitive shift-pair/second-moment
ledger: it turns the last coefficient chart retaining the structural
parameter `G_2` into four explicit univariate filters and prints every unit
guard.

router. It must not be advertised as an exclusion or as closure of the
