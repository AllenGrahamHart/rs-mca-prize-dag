# Proof

For each listed `j`, exact specialization gives a zero-dimensional ideal in
`F_p0[x,pvar]`. Powers modulo its Groebner basis compute the two displayed
field equations without constructing `p0^6`-degree polynomials. Adjoining
their reduced representatives cuts out exactly the `F_(p0^6)`-rational
points of the fiber.

The coordinate field algebra defined by the two field equations is a finite
product of fields, hence reduced. In every listed fiber, sequential reduction
of the complete required open-factor product reaches zero. Therefore every
`F_(p0^6)` point lies on at least one excluded boundary. The exact stopping
factor census is `V` for `j=1,4` and `s^2-4pvar` for the other 61 fibers.
QED.
