# Statement

Let `p0=2130706433`. In the `F04-R02`, `B0`, `V K8!=0` reduced curve, fix
the symmetric coordinate `s=j` for each

```text
j in {1,3,4,...,64} subset F_p0.
```

For every one of these 63 fibers, adjoin the exact field equations

```text
x^(p0^6)-x = 0,    pvar^(p0^6)-pvar = 0.
```

The resulting zero-dimensional ideal has no point on which all required
open factors are nonzero. For `j=1,4`, the open product first vanishes at
`V`; for the other 61 fibers it first vanishes at `s^2-4pvar`.

The `s=2` specialization remains dimension one and is not covered.
