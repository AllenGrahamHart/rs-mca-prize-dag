# Claim contract

## Inputs

- one fixed source, touched pair, and defect parameter `s`;
- exact split core locators and exact background agreement sets;
- candidate-wise primitive cofactor pairs.

## Output

- combined core/background overlap at most `2s` for distinct contributors;
- one aggregate packing bound that does not enumerate background sets.

## Nonclaims

- no fixed polynomial exponent uniform in growing `s`;
- no count across source layouts or touched pairs beyond their existing
  first owners;
- no replacement for master-flatness or stronger shift-pair compression;
- no lower bound showing the packing cap is attained.
