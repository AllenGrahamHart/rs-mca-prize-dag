# Upstream crosswalk

The determinant `A_1A'_2-A'_1A_2` is the exact primitive shift-pair witness
for this FPC5 consumer. It couples missed-core and background-root overlap in
one degree-`2s` polynomial and therefore avoids the invalid independent sum
over background subsets.

The resulting constant-weight packing is the elementary distance layer of
upstream primitive shift-pair control. It remains too coarse in the
growing-`s` regime; an upstream exchange, second-moment, or master-flatness
result would need to improve this aggregate packing rather than re-prove the
per-background flat codimension.
