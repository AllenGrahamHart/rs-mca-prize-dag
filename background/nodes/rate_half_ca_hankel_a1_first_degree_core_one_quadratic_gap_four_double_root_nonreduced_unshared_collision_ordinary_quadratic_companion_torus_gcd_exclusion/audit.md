# Audit

1. The Corvaja--Zannier theorem is applied on the smooth normalization, not
   directly to a possibly singular affine equation.
2. For bidegree `(d_1,d_2)`, the genus and boundary bounds combine to the
   convenient exact inequality `chi<=2d_1d_2`.
3. The coordinate differentials are nonzero because their nonzero degrees
   are at most four and the characteristic exceeds `2^167`.
4. Multiplicative dependence of the two coordinate functions is exactly
   the translated-subtorus alternative; it is not silently discarded.
5. Both terms in the published maximum are checked. The characteristic
   term is not omitted merely because `P` is large.
6. The `S_3` contradiction uses uniqueness of the reduced off-diagonal
   component, which makes it swap-invariant and puts all six generic
   ordered pairs on the same component.
7. In the cyclic case, the map from the original curve to a primitive
   binomial image may have degree two. Equation `(8)` includes this degree;
   it is not assumed birational.
8. A scaling constant belongs to `H` because the cyclic component contains
   actual points of `H^2`. The absence of order-three torsion in `H` is then
   exact.
9. Invariance under `sigma` forces factorization through the degree-three
   quotient, contradicting coordinate degree two.
10. The theorem removes only the quadratic companions. The quartic
    companion and irreducible large-factor shape remain live.
