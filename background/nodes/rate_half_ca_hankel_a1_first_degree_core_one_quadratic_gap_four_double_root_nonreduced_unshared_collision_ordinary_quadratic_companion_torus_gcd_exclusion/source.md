# Published input

P. Corvaja and U. Zannier, *Greatest common divisors of `u-1`, `v-1` in
positive characteristic and rational points on curves over finite fields*,
J. Eur. Math. Soc. 15 (2013), 1927--1942, Theorem 2 and Corollary 2.

For multiplicatively independent rational functions `u,v` with nonzero
differentials on a smooth projective absolutely irreducible curve in
characteristic `P`, with `S` their zeros and poles and
`chi=|S|+2g-2`, Theorem 2 gives

```text
sum_(z outside S) min{ord_z(1-u),ord_z(1-v)}
 <=max{
   3(2 deg(u)deg(v)chi)^(1/3),
   12 deg(u)deg(v)/P
 }.
```

Corollary 2 states the corresponding torsion-point bound for an absolutely
irreducible curve in `G_m^2` that is not a translate of a subtorus.
