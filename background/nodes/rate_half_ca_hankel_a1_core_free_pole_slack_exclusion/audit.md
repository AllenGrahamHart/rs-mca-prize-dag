# Audit

1. The interpolating degree uses the actual pole colength `p`, not its upper
   bound `O`; `(alpha+1)(b+1)>p` for every residue class of `p`.
2. The three cases for `alpha` are exactly the largest domain degrees below
   the minimum possible domain degree of a contact-active component, subject
   to `alpha<=2` in the cohomology step.
3. Three contact copies leave first degree `alpha-3` after subtracting `C`.
4. The strict inequality in `(A1P3)` is necessary: equality gives second
   degree zero and is not excluded by this argument.
5. At `e=m+1`, failure permits exactly `ell=0,1,2`; the pole-deficiency
   ceilings are `3ell+5`.
