- **Claim ID:** `rate_half_kb_m2_r4_diagonal_source_subfield_dichotomy`
- **Input:** an actual diagonal-order-two component and its quadratic
  source intermediate field.
- **Output:** an exhaustive split between a source-line lift with reciprocal
  normal form and a non-lifting biquadratic cover of genus zero or one with
  an exact branch passport.
- **Use:** source-star equivariance is valid in the lifting branch; the
  rational/elliptic V4 passport is mandatory in the non-lifting branch.
- **Not claimed:** universal exclusion of either branch, identification of
  the cover deck group with the ambient component stabilizer, order-two or
  trivial-type deletion, owner, payment, row, or Prize closure.
