# Claim contract

| field | value |
|---|---|
| claim | Exact distinct-`phi` triple minima and the one/two-member split-pencil divisor endpoint `(FSP1)--(FSP7)`. |
| inputs | The active-defect selected blocks and primitive coprime pair `P,Q`; the unconditional affine-plane three-target component cap. |
| output | Every target is routed either to an exact triple-incidence count or to at least two disjoint split-pencil block locators. |
| load-bearing hypotheses | Fiber cap `ell`, square-free evaluation-domain block locators, selected-block size `r`, at least two selected blocks, and first-match ownership for the incidence count. |
| falsifier | A block violating the exact extremum; a low-fiber block locator not dividing the stated pencil product; or a fixed core/triple owning four targets. |
| nonclaim | No census of the split-pencil endpoint and no payment of every high-fiber upper-tail tuple. |
