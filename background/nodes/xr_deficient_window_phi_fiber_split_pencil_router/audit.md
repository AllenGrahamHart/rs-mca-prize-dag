# Audit

- The old lower bound `r(r-ell)(r-2ell)/6` is replaced by the exact packed
  third elementary symmetric function.  They agree for `ell=1` but not in
  general.
- The phrase "the `r<=2ell` two-fiber regime" is only a worst-case label.
  Blocks in that numerical range may have many fibers and are still visible
  to `(FSP6)`.
- Divisibility is by the product of the pencil members determined by the
  values of `phi` on the block.  It is not a claim that the selected slope is
  one of those fiber values.
- Distinct evaluation points make `M_B` square-free.  Without that fact,
  root containment alone would not prove the stated divisibility with
  multiplicity.
- `(FSP6)` chooses one high-fiber block and has no factor two.  The factor two
  in `(FSP7)` is valid only when every selected block is high-fiber, which is
  automatic for `r>2ell`.
- This node does not count choices of one or two pencil members and does not
  close the XR target.
