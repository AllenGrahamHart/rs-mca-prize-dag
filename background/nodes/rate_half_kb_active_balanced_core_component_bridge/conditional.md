Let `z` be a slope in the active first-match cell `Z_BC`. The witness-compiler
premise supplies a canonical source-bound balanced-core certificate for `z`,
on the original received line and with the selected support, affine slope,
and chronology retained. The endpoint-realization premise maps that exact
certificate to an actual `Q=6,s=6,u=2` endpoint record and residual component.
Its printed injection or finite fiber ledger is part of the output, so no
uncontrolled record multiplicity is introduced.

The proved degree-router chain reduces every such actual component to one of
the three independent inner-degree-two types

```text
(m,r,delta)=(2,2,4), (2,4,2), (2,8,1).
```

The proved full-V4 source-facet theorem excludes `(2,2,4)`. Hence exactly the
order-two `(2,4,2)` and trivial-stabilizer `(2,8,1)` types remain. Composition
preserves the labels and fibers supplied by the premises and never identifies
the endpoint parameter line with the evaluation carrier. This proves the
stated bridge conditional on its two red leaves.
