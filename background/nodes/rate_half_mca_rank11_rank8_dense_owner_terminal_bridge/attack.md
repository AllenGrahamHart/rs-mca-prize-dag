# Attack

Use the bridge only as input to the existing dense-owner program. A closing
successor must do one of the following on the identical received line:

1. assign every such owner in the active first-match chronology and meet
   the guarded owner target;
2. couple different `delta<=4` cores strongly enough to pay them jointly;
3. route the explicit twelve-owner fence to an already paid cell.

Do not infer unique ownership from the terminal alone.
