# Audit

- The norm is the norm of a line-bundle section. Its affine representative
  includes `q_d^a`; this is why the leading coefficient cancels only after
  proving `deg P=3a`.
- The core-one domain locator has degree `N-1`, not `N`, after core
  contraction.
- The ordinary cubic packet has a signed divisor. Its negative slope form
  is cubed before cancellation, reducing the `H` exponent there by exactly
  three.
- Parameter pushforwards may be nonsplit over `F`; `S_AB` and `S_B` are
  binary forms over `F`, not necessarily products of displayed linear
  factors.
- Reducibility of `C` is harmless because divisor norm and finite flatness
  are componentwise.
- The exact factorization is necessary and remains compatible with all
  classified local packets.
