- The proof changes only the joint numerator; it does not reduce or
  condition the marginal product after seeing the null event.
- `m` is `log2(t)`, so there are `m` junction factors `B_0,...,B_(m-1)`
  and one terminal level factor `Z_m`.
- Zero primitive numerator is handled before taking logarithms.
- The central-to-all-weight step is one-sided and uses only positivity.
- The 45-row scan is labelled evidence and cannot promote C2''.
