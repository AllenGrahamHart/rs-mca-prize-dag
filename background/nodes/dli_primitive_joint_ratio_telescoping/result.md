# Result

The exact bank contains 45 full-tower rows. Of these, 29 have zero primitive
numerator. All 45 satisfy the 21-bit inequality; the largest primitive ratio
is `14680064/5498847`, or `1.4166572071` bits, at `(n,t,q)=(32,2,5857)`.
This is stress evidence only. The proved payload is `(PRIM-TEL)` and its
reduction of C2'' to `(C2-INT)`.
