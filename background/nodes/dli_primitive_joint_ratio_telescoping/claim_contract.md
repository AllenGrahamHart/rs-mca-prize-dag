# Claim contract

## Inputs

- The exact `U`-weighted dyadic tower with `n` and `t=2^m` powers of two.
- The proved all-weight telescoping lemma.
- The proved antipodal first-owner characterization of nonprimitive
  supports.

## Outputs

- One exact positive primitive joint-ratio formula.
- One exact integer inequality sufficient for C2''.
- A dimensionless statistic in which every explicit power of `q` cancels.

## Nonclaims

- `(C2-INT)` is not proved on any official row.
- The 45-row replay is not a toy-to-official transport theorem.
- No bound on the separate unreduced terms is asserted or used.
