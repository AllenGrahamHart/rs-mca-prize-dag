# Claim contract

## Claim

Every pair-feasible folded-profile `(3,4,0)`, `N=256`, `V=66` collision has
one of four exact autocorrelation magnitude profiles and obeys the printed
parity, diameter, and equal-chord ledgers.

## Dependencies

- the proved `V=68` endpoint, which makes `V=66` the next live endpoint;
- the exact sparse-L1 slack recurrence;
- the signed-chord identity;
- the collision-norm criterion and the rational cubic majorant.

## Nonclaims

- no exclusion of any of the four profiles;
- no exclusion of `V=66` or a lower variance;
- no transport of the `V=68` four-template classification;
- no result for the separate `(4,2,0)` first-band profile;
- no promotion of either universal unsafe target.

## Falsifier

A pair-feasible `V=66` collision outside the four profiles, with zero or two
light-light diameters, outside the printed diameter ledger, or violating the
diameter-Sidon conclusion for a five-odd profile refutes the claim. Failure of
either exact cubic sign also refutes the profile reduction.
