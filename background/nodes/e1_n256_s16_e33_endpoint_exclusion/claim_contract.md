# Claim contract

## Claim

No pair-feasible `N=256`, profile-`(3,4,0)` collision has `V=66`; the live
positive even variance range is at most 64.

## Dependencies

- the exhaustive `V=66` profile/parity/diameter reduction;
- the four proved profile exclusions `(0,6,1)`, `(4,5,1)`, `(1,8)`, `(5,7)`.

## Nonclaims

- no exclusion of a positive even variance at most 64;
- no closure of the complete first-band collision problem;
- no promotion of either universal target.

## Falsifier

A pair-feasible `V=66` collision, a fifth profile omitted by the reduction, or
failure of any one of the four profile exclusions refutes the claim.
