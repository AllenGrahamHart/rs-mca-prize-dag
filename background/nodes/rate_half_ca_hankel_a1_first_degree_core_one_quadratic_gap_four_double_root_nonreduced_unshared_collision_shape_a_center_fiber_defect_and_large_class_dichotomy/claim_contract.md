# Claim contract

- **Claim:** small-class interpolation defects are the center fibers
  `G(gamma,X)`, and the large class has the exact alternative `(CFD9)`.
- **Inputs:** the live linear/quadratic syzygy profile, split-row root
  description, center locator factors, and center-adjusted heavy row.
- **Output:** a common-center-fiber coupling in the rank-`r-1` branch, or
  a multiplication chain and a quadratic residual in the rank-`r-2`
  branch.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaims:** neither large-class branch is excluded, and no
  proportionality between distinct center fibers is asserted.
- **Falsifier:** a coordinate syzygy not vanishing at its center, a small
  defect not proportional to `G(gamma,X)`, or a rank-`r-2` large map
  without the factorization in `(CFD9)`.
