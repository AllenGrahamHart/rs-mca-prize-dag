# Audit

- The degree-eight field is the field generated by the normalized `n`th
  roots, not an assertion that the ambient evaluation field has degree eight.
  A larger ambient field does not change the fact that these normalized HNF
  data already lie in `F_(p^8)`.
- The descent of `b` uses both inputs: `-1/d` is a normalized split value,
  and `g_1` is a coefficient of a monic factor whose roots are a subset of
  the normalized roots of `L`.
- The role root lies in `F_(p^2)`, but that fact alone does not put `b` in
  `F_(p^2)`. The exact field bound for `b` is degree eight.
- An irreducible factor of degree six, for example, has degree at most eight
  but no root in `F_(p^8)`. Eligibility is `degree divides 8`, not
  `degree <= 8`.
- Guard factors may be removed only through their separately proved chart
  exclusions. No unrun factorization is treated as evidence.
