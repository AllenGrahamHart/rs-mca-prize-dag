# Claim contract

## Input

`e1_n256_s16_e30_profile_parity_light_reduction` and its complete six-odd
mask atlas.

## Output

Exactly 33,737 relaxed cubic exceptions on 1,191 affine light orbits.

## Falsifier

A missing mask, an additional affine orbit, any production/audit mismatch,
or a different count or maximum.  This node does not lift the relaxed layers
to actual sparse vectors.
