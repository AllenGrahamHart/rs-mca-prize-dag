# Proof

The six odd classes are the six light-light differences.  Choosing the six
magnitude-two classes among the other 57 classes gives `binom(57,6)` cases per
mask.  The production scanner uses an incremental signed cubic kernel; the
audit derives the coefficients independently and uses a three-plus-three
decomposition.  The verifier compares all 1,234 rows, including every
histogram, maximum, witness, and exceptional count.  Their common totals are
the values in the statement. QED.
