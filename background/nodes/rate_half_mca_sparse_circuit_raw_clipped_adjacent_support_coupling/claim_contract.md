# Claim contract

- **Claim:** `(RCA1)--(RCA6)` bound one fixed adjacent support pair.
- **Quantifiers:** all parent fixed-union hypotheses remain mandatory;
  `2<=d<=g-1`; raw caps are on the same selected-incidence census.
- **Normalization:** divide each raw incidence cap by its exact extension
  factor and floor before treating it as a circuit-count cap.

- **Nonclaim:** no overlapping adjacent pair bounds are added.
