# Audit

1. `tau` is the product of the three cross-edge signs, not the coordinate
   involution.
2. Scaling `A=1` acts on product values by a common nonzero scalar and does
   not normalize the `K` coordinate a second time.
3. The elimination is saturated by actual-packet guards.  Factors
   `b`, `b-1`, and `b+1` are forbidden signed-pair collisions, not extra
   solution rows.
4. Rank at most three requires all five maximal minors.  The verifier checks
   all of them in both directions.
5. Geometric nonemptiness does not assert that every quadratic root passes
   injectivity in the deployed characteristic or descends to the challenge
   field.
6. The other seven complete fibers are absent from this compiler and remain
   mandatory.
