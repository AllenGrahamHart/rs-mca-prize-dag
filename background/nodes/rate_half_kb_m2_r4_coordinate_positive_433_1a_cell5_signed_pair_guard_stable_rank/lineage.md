# Lineage

The target-free interface reduced the outside family to a signed pair plus
one colored edge.  Reciprocal projection and the deployed rational lift
atlas then reduced chart 2 to three equations in `x1,x0,b` over `F_p(t)`.
Generic Singular and SymPy coefficient engines timed out.  Groebner.jl
produced the finite quotient; exact multiplication-matrix factorization and
an independent cleared-denominator NTT replay turn that backend output into
the stable-rank theorem here.
