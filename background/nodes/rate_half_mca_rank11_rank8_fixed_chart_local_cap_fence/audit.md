# Audit

## Claim boundary

This is a route fence, not a counterexample to the component concentrator or
to KoalaBear safety.  The construction reproduces the target's local
rank-eight conclusions and its weighted unit, but deliberately does not
reproduce the unsafe-family size, dense anchors, or normalized full span.

## Mathematical checks

1. The correction space has degree profile `0,...,7,9,10`, dimension ten,
   rank eight on `B`, and kernel `span{u_0,Xu_0}`.
2. Every two-coordinate extension outside `B` has rank ten by the explicit
   nonzero determinant `u_0(x)u_0(y)(y-x)`.
3. The partition is exact: `9+8*67473+508794=1048587`.
4. Greedy slope selection forbids at most `32562896` field values per step,
   below the deployed base prime.
5. Every support is exactly `B union P_e union {x}`; global slope
   distinctness prevents collateral remainder agreements.
6. Pair noncontainment uses the second received column and `67482>10`
   roots, independently of the selected owner.
7. Error differences lie in a two-space, so the rank-three target is met.
8. The marked count and weighted demand are computed in the same
   `(record,T)` unit.
9. The official lift adds one common locator to all objects and preserves
   degrees, ranks, exact supports, and pair noncontainment.

The primary verifier rejects mutations of the row, partition, rank profile,
greedy field budget, record count, component multiplicity, weighted demand,
and error-rank contract.  The independent audit enumerates a small prime-
field analogue, checking exact supports, evaluation ranks, affine error
rank, and inconsistency of every putative containing second polynomial.
