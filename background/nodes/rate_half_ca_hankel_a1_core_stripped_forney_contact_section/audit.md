# Audit

1. Contracting a core of size `s` changes the sequence length to `2rho-s`,
   the generator degree to `d=rho-s`, and leaves exactly `rho` rows.
2. The contact order is therefore `d+rho`, not `2d` or `2rho`.
3. The section's first bidegree is nevertheless `-rho-1`, independent of
   `s`.
4. Its degree is `d-(s+1)e`, exactly the residual regular size.
5. More than `e` clean fibres prove reducedness after the maximal fixed core
   has been stripped.
