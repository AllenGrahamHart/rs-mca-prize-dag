# Audit

- `E` contains only heavy roots of `R_3`; roots outside the evaluation or
  heavy set are allowed until `(CRM8)` forces them out.
- The lower bound `(CRM4)` charges every new root outside `E` against one
  actual excess degree. It does not assume all excess roots occur at heavy
  incidences.
- The simple-row estimate permits extra excess copies to remove the second
  vertical unit at new roots; this is why its right side is
  `c_x+epsilon_x`, not merely `c_x`.
- The squarefree conclusion uses simplicity only after deriving the general
  incidence lower bound `r>=2`.
- The double-root branch is retained. No simple-root congruence is applied
  at its multiplicity-two row.
