# Claim contract

## Input

One live guarded three-petal LS6 atom, with no restriction on `deg E`.

## Output

The necessary source condition

```text
deg rem_(L_3)(L_1L_2^(-1))>=a,
```

equivalently the absence of the short syzygy `(RG6)`.

## Preserved

- the exact LS6 coprimality guard;
- the fixed source locators and normalized distinct labels;
- the official defect range.

## Nonclaims

- every source passing the gate has a candidate;
- source-ratio degree bounds a surviving split fiber;
- all cyclic reorderings have been installed as simultaneous gates;
- quotient or dihedral owners are paid.
