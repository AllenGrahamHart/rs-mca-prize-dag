# Audit

1. The inverse gate does not use the high-multiplier hypothesis.
2. The proof uses `a<=ell/2`; official cells satisfy `a<ell/4`.
3. `lambda^(-1)-1` is nonzero because source labels are distinct.
4. All modular inverses exist because the three petal locators are pairwise
   coprime.
5. The short-syzygy quotient `R` has degree at most `deg U`, hence below `a`.
6. Only one distinguished-label ordering is asserted; simultaneous cyclic
   gates require a separate symmetry adapter.
7. Passing `(RG5)` is not a count or existence theorem.
