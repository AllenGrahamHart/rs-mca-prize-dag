- **claim:** the threshold split `(MI1)` and its KoalaBear rank-ten
  specialization
- **inputs:** a gauged post-near family in `c_0+C'`, exact supports,
  same-support pair noncontainment, and the printed threshold hypotheses
- **dependencies:** support-local transversality, reversible error-rank
  gauge/router, ordinary affine-span list bound, sub-square-root
  interleaving collapse, and the disjoint near-rational charge
- **output:** every direct KoalaBear error-rank-ten family has size at most
  `61871313426765543<B_*`
- **falsifier:** a valid input family violating `(MI1)`, a failure of the
  fixed-pair slope injection, or an exact arithmetic mismatch

The phrase "optimal threshold" refers only to the finite scan of the
printed bound `(MI2)` at `s=9`.  No global optimality is claimed.
