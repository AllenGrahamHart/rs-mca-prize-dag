# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the exact equivalence `(APG1)--(APG9)`, the `4e` column
  count, and the printed official dimensions.
- **Dependencies:** all-excess residual-fiber factorization and the exact
  shape-A excess ledger.
- **Evidence only:** full rank in the two `e=7` fields and their switched
  incidence controls.
- **Nonclaims:** no universal full-rank theorem, no official kernel
  exclusion, no Shape-A closure, and no endpoint movement.
- **Falsifier:** a wrong dual-RS parity range, a missing convolution
  coefficient, a column count other than `4e`, or a compatible fiber family
  rejected by `(APG6)`.
