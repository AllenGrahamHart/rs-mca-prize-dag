# Claim contract

## Input

The fixed typed lane and nine-subset population from the concentrator, plus
the nine-cell rank-nine pair-core extension.

## Output

One fixed kernel chart, one large-shared-core rank-nine owner plane, or one
rank-eight owner flat whose selected errors have affine rank at most three.
Every route carries at least 2578110 actual records.

## Nonclaim

No route is asserted to carry the complete component lane. The theorem does
not pay a fixed kernel chart, a shared pair core, or a rank-three subfamily;
it does not install first-match chronology or close error rank eleven.
