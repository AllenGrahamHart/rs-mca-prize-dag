# Audit

1. Lane typing is inherited before the fixed `B` is selected.
2. A rank-ten evaluation on eleven coordinates restricts to rank eight or
   nine on a contained nine-subset; no rank-seven affine route is hidden.
3. Every affine-lane record supplies an actual owner pair, so its fixed-plane
   owner line or fixed-flat owner fiber is nonempty.
4. The rank-nine route is lifted before applying the original-row support
   intersection floor; deleted coordinates enter the common owner core.
5. The rank-nine population exceeds the proved nine-cell plane cap by
   `1143705` records.
6. In rank eight, owner differences lie in `U^2`, but explanation deviations
   lie in `U`; the resulting error-difference span is `span(U,r_1-B_*)` and
   has dimension at most three. Reversible locator multiplication preserves
   this rank.
7. In the kernel lane, only containment in one fixed ambient kernel is
   claimed; component identity is not inferred.
8. No route is aggregated into a row payment.

No Modal computation is used.
