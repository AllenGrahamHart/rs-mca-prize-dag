## Claim

## Inputs consumed

- the exact ten-profile E26 reduction;
- the complete six-profile two-odd exclusion;
- the complete four-profile six-odd exclusion.

## Output supplied

The live positive even variance frontier in this band advances from `V<=52`
to `V<=50`.

## Scope exclusions

- no exclusion at `V<=50`;
- no closure of folded profile `(4,2,0)`;
- no closure of later `N=256` or `N=512` bands;
- no route-wide pair-incidence theorem.
