# Audit

The twelve packets refine the four rational shape packets; they do not add
new color roles. They form a disjunction of candidate systems. Do not impose
all twelve packet equations simultaneously, and use either exhaustive
representation, never both as if they were independent constraints.

The packet polynomials are Galois-orbit products. Irreducibility is not
needed and is not claimed. Coincident role values across packets, including
special-characteristic collisions, retain their natural multiplicities in
the degree-42 product.

Homogenization in (GRW4) is reversible only on `S=B!=0`. Retain `R!=0`, the
exact gcd-degree conditions, and every inherited HNF saturation. Lower role
degree does not turn a retained p-free point into a Frobenius or inner
split-pencil witness.
