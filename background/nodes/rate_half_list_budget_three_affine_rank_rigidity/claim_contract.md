# Claim contract

- **claim:** every four-codeword rate-half predecessor witness at agreement
  `3d-1`, for `d>=3`, has codeword affine rank exactly three.
- **scope:** arbitrary field, arbitrary distinct `4d`-point RS domain,
  ordinary lists, and four distinct codewords.
- **consumer effect:** removes affine ranks one and two from every one of the
  thirteen official budget-three chambers.
- **nonclaim:** no locator chamber is closed; locator-pencil rank and
  codeword affine rank are different objects until a bridge is proved.
- **falsifier:** four distinct codewords of affine rank at most two satisfying
  `(ARR1)` for some `d>=3`.
