# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the fixed support of the extremal three-center residual line is
  partitioned into three exact coordinate-root classes, with at most one
  exceptional coordinate.
- **Inputs:** the Cycle-127 equality branch, exact error weights, the common
  core point, and affine dependence of the received and codeword lines.
- **Outputs:** the class sizes `(ESP4)`, partition `(ESP5)`, and source-form
  factorization `(ESP7)`.
- **Falsifier:** two line slopes missing the same noncore coordinate, a class
  size other than `p-1+r_gamma`, or more than one unclassified coordinate.
- **Nonclaims:** no relation among the field scalars `eta_x` and no supported
  status for the exceptional source root.
