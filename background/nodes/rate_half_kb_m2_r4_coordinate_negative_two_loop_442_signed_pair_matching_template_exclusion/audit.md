# Audit

1. The residual form `(KB44T-1)` applies only to forced types `cD` and
   `sigma DE`; no claim is made for forced `DF`.
2. Once `a,q` are paired, the three rows `(KB44T-2)` exhaust all perfect
   matchings of the remaining four signed products.
3. The negation contradiction uses original product-injectivity guards,
   not an unstated genericity assumption.
4. The cross-template subtraction uses odd deployed characteristic and
   signed-pair distinctness.
5. Every possible zero of `Alpha` is protected by an exact integer norm;
   its small exceptional characteristics are not silently discarded.
6. The count is a subcase deletion, not a 24-cell deletion.
