# Claim contract

- **claim:** the class decomposition, exact GRS parameters, dimension,
  ternary-mass factorization, trace collapse, and coset invariance in `(ADM)`
  and `(TRACE)`
- **scope:** official `n=2^41` admissible rows and the deployed F2
  exact-order/nested antipodal windows
- **consumer:** structural evidence for the repaired `f2_conditional_close`
- **nonclaims:** no bound on `Z_1`; no proof of `(O1)`; no PP5.0
  average-to-sum choice; no extras-budget close
- **falsifier:** an admissible row with `D>2`, a failed class independence,
  a non-GRS class kernel, or a failure of exact mass factorization
- **replay:** Modal app recorded in `result.md`
