# Attack record

The first carrier argument closed only the codimension-zero case.  Retaining
the quotient dimension as a free one- or two-dimensional space appeared to
leave an uncontrolled family of circuit labels.

The useful invariant is instead the number of carrier-external points in a
whole deletion-completion family.  The joint zero-carrier theorem bounds this
by `delta+3` uniformly.  Deleting an outside point from each circuit converts
that statement into four elementary and disjoint charges, with no quotient
direction enumeration and no field-size factor.
