# Claim contract

This node counts support-four circuit supports after the common-zero carrier
`B` has been closed.  The outside strata are indexed by the exact number of
circuit points outside `B`; each circuit is charged through outside-point
deletions only.

All three-sets of the required shape are counted, including dependent ones,
so `(EC1)` is an upper bound.  Only independent deletions actually contribute.
The eleven-set extension factor also deliberately overcounts.

The node supplies a support-four incidence cap, not a complete weighted
component payment.
