# Audit

The production engine uses folded signed chord classes. The independent audit
forms all 49 ordered products of the seven sparse coefficients in the
negacyclic ring and checks anti-reciprocity before reading the profile. Both
engines enumerate all 111 light templates, `binom(124,3)` heavy supports, and
64 relative sign vectors, and agree on every one of the 111 output rows after
removing runtime metadata.

The deterministic verifier replays every one of the 4,812 printed exceptional
vectors from its sparse coefficients, recomputes its profile, conductor, and
third moment, and checks the exact aggregate ledger. It also verifies that the
820 norm inputs are exactly the full-conductor subsequence of that ledger.

FLINT and PARI use separate resultant implementations. Their 820 outputs agree
in order; the maximum is independently bounded by the integer inequalities in
the statement. The local audit checks source separation and stored-result
agreement without rerunning either large census.

production census: ap-bK8FgTfXIIVwtoGo3F2n5X

exact norms:       ap-Lr43rWTE9sjJ7D87pDRgvj
