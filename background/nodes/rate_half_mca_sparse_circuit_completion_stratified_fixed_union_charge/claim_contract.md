# Claim contract

- `D` and `W` are fixed before the target deletion is selected.
- `M_d` bounds exact completions of every independent target deletion.
- Inside deletion points impose zero conditions on `W`.
- The fixed-union budget is used only when `j<=g`.
- The global completion budget remains active in every stratum.
- Every circuit with `j` outside points is divided by exactly `j`.
- The parallel-class replacement applies only to supports `d>=3`.
- No distributional, generic-position, or field-size hypothesis is used.
