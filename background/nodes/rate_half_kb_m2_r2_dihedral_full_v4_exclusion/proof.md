# Proof

The exhaustive outer-factor reduction applies to every actual
`(m,r,delta)=(2,2,4)` component and gives

```text
n in {2,3,5,6}.
```

The four possibilities are impossible:

1. `n=2` is excluded by the degree-two source-star defect theorem.
2. `n=3` is excluded by the common-five source-facet theorem.
3. `n=5` is excluded by the totally ramified source-star theorem.
4. `n=6` is excluded by the common-pole/source-cover theorem.

The list is exhaustive, so no actual full-V4 component exists. QED.
