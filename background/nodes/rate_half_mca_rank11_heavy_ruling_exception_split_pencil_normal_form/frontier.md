# Frontier

The remaining rational task is an exact bounded-degree SPI census:

```text
1<=e<=11,
u,v coprime,
at least 20 distinct gamma with u+gamma v a split squarefree degree-e
domain divisor,
and all resulting root sets pairwise disjoint.
```

The census may use the smooth evaluation-domain group structure, base-field
normalization, or a low-degree function-field classification. Twenty split
fibers alone do not justify an identically-split pencil claim.
