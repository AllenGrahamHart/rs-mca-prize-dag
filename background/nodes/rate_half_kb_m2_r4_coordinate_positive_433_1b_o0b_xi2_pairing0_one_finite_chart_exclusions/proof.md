# Proof

The projective-chart parent replaces each matching resultant by either two
finite common-root equations in one auxiliary variable or the two
leading-coefficient equations at infinity. The pinned chart compiler applies
that exact replacement to each of `FII`, `IFI`, and `IIF`, while retaining the
proved common basis, `q3`, `q7`, all 40 guards, and the six rank cofactors.

Singular obtains initial dimension-four bases of sizes 57, 74, and 62. In
each row, saturation remains dimension four through guard 4 and becomes unit
at guard 5, `b+1`. Thus every solution in each chart lies on `b=-1`, which is
forbidden by distinctness from normalized coordinate `a=1`. QED.
