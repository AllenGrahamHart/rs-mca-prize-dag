# Audit

The 5.7 MB primary packet is one digest-pinned JSONL shard. The verifier
streams all 24 rows and checks its digest, byte and record counts,
Cartesian cover, terminal status, and orbit transport.

Independent FLINT root custody reconstructs every norm/guard root. A
separate six-container replay reconstructs all 720 missing-quartic roots,
reciprocal lifts, and antipodal/second-pair `q` intersections directly from
the compact kernel. It also joins every paid free row to its exact proved
owner.
