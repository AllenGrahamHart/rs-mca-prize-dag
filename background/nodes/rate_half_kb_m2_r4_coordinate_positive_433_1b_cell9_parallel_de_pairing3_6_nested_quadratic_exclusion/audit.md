# Audit

The 31.5 MB primary packet is split into six digest-pinned JSONL shards. The
verifier checks each shard digest, byte count, record count, and JSON record
before streaming it.

Root ownership is independent: FLINT computes each field-root part by
Frobenius gcd. The direct audit independently reconstructs the quadratic
roots, Cartesian products, squared-sum values, square roots, and colored
cuts. It also joins each paid free row to the exact tower-boundary or
regularized-base certificate that owns it.

Modal printed a client-side asynchronous-generator close warning after all
192 rows, the compact summary, and the complete manifest had been written.
The manifest and all six shards pass independent custody verification; the
warning carries no mathematical evidence and changes no record.
