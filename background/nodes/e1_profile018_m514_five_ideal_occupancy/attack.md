# Attack

1. Falsify first: seek six distinct primitive roots `s mod 257` admitting
   compatible parity-multiplicity-two, fixed-root, and all-singleton
   autocorrelation data. A certified six-root construction kills the stated
   threshold even before exact norm tests.
2. Screen the 15 signed magnitude profiles by the fixed-root trace equation
   `18+sum_d A_d(s^d+s^-d)=0 mod 257` and local multiplicity two.
3. Impose coefficient realization for 18 distinct signed singleton terms;
   do not confuse an autocorrelation target with a realizable polynomial.
4. Canonicalize by inversion and diagonal Galois action while preserving the
   fixed row-prime root.
5. Compute exact norms only for surviving realizable types. Count occupied
   prime ideals, not raw autocorrelation targets or roots of the squared
   magnitude polynomial.

cost cap.
