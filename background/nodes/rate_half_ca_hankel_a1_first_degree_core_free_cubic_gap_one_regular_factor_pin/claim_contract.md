# Claim contract

- **Claim:** in each core-free cubic double-root `u=1` packet, the complete
  supported excess-recurrence divisor divides the regular determinant; the
  quotient has degree `w`, hence is linear in the first packet and constant
  in the other three.
- **Currency:** local Smith valuation, exact packet rank-loss degree, and the
  rectangular Hankel cofactor factorization.
- **Dependencies:** the core-free slope/rank ledger, the four-packet normal
  form, and the marked Hankel determinant gate.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** no location or coprimality for `E_1`, no squarefreeness, and
  no packet exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a supported recurrence loss of order `c_gamma` for which
  `ord_gamma(D_0)<c_gamma`, or a quotient degree different from `w`.
- **Next gate:** compare the three completely supported determinants with
  the source-minor and resultant formulas; locate the first packet's linear
  factor separately.
