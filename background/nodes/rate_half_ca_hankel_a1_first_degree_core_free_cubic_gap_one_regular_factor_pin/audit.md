# Audit

- The exponent of `L_gamma` in `P_C` is the full recurrence/rank loss
  `c_gamma`, not merely the indicator of a bad slope.
- Local Smith form supplies divisibility only. Exact degree accounting then
  makes the quotient constant in three packets.
- In the first packet, `E_1` may repeat a factor already present in `P_C`.
- Projective slopes, including infinity, are represented homogeneously.
- No equality between `E_1` and the degree-one Picard correction is used.
