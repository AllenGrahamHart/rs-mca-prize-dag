# Audit

- `J` removes each center factor only once because `Lambda` is squarefree;
  a center lying on `S_B` can leave one further `S_B` copy in `H`.
- Coprimality of `Lambda_0` and `H` is exact after this cancellation, so
  Euclid's lemma applies to the cubic bracket.
- `T_j` may be zero. The center-disjoint formula is not promoted to a
  nonzero scalar multiple.
- The augmented RS statement concerns coefficient polynomials in `X`, not
  a claim that the external heavy row is itself a classified split row.
- No matrix rank is inferred from its dimensions or from the small number
  of new scalar unknowns.
