# Claim contract

- **Claim:** on the separated double-root locus, cancellation of center
  overlaps leaves the fixed heavy row of `G` equal to
  `(g_*S_B^2/J)T_j` with `deg T_j<=deg J<=3`; this row extends the existing
  coefficient-MDS system with at most four scalar unknowns.
- **Currency:** the cubic heavy-quotient Pade identity, homogeneous gcd
  cancellation, and the degree-`n` coefficient RS code.
- **Dependencies used:** the separated heavy-quotient cubic residual and
  the paired split-biform coefficient-MDS gate.
- **Status movement:** none; this is an exact adapter to the split-biform
  boundary.
- **Falsifier:** a separated profile for which `Lambda/J` does not divide
  the cubic bracket, or an augmented coefficient vector outside the printed
  RS code.
- **Nonclaims:** no full-rank theorem, no nonzero heavy-row scalar, and no
  statement on nonreduced/shared corrections.
