# Audit

- Fourier inversion is over the full formal additive codomain. Rank or image
  collapse can only increase the proved zero-probability lower bound.
- Positivity is not asserted for arbitrary Bernoulli linear forms. It comes
  from an explicit iid-difference representation at every Haar marginal.
- `Prim` is applied only to the joint numerator. Marginals remain
  unconditional, as required by C2''.
- The exponent count is `t-1` odd-band equations plus one terminal equation,
  not `m+1` field equations.
- `kappa_img` uses the realized image `L_w`; `Delta_img=q^t/L_w` is retained
  explicitly and is never assumed subexponential or one.
- Fixed-density scope follows from the Vandermonde extreme-layer exclusion
  only when `t/n` has a positive lower bound, as it does at official aspect
  `1/256`.
- The exact order-16 replay checks the Haar identity, owner subtraction,
  marginal floors, layer dictionary, complement symmetry, and strict bridge.
  The proof itself is symbolic; the replay is not used to infer generality.
