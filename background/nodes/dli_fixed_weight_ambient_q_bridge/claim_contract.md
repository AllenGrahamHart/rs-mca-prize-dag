- Every DLI Haar marginal has nonnegative additive Fourier transform.
- Its zero probability is at least the reciprocal of its formal codomain.
- The marginal codomain exponents sum exactly to `t`.
- `J_prim` is bounded by the binomially weighted ambient-normalized
  fixed-weight primitive zero-fiber inflation in `(AQ3)`.
- Image-normalized Q enters that inflation with the exact multiplicative
  effective-image defect `q^t/L_w`.
- Primitive zero-fiber layers outside `t<w<n-t` are empty.

- Upstream primitive Q does not supply its own Sidon payment.

  `q^t/L_w`.
