# Claim contract

## Claim

Every pair-feasible `N=256`, folded-profile `(3,4,0)`, `V=52` collision has
one of the ten profiles in (1), with light geometry in the exact 1,321-orbit
router. An undifferentiated direct census requires 26,219,123,456 signed
vectors per engine.

## Dependencies

- `e1_n256_s16_e27_endpoint_exclusion` for the preceding variance frontier;
- `e1_n256_s16_sparse_l1_variance_exclusion` for the relaxed slack recurrence;
- `e1_n256_s16_signed_chord_collision_gate` for the signed-chord identity;
- `e1_n256_s16_e30_profile_parity_light_reduction` for the proved two-odd and
  six-odd light atlases;
- `collision_norm_criterion` for the cubic norm exclusion.

## Nonclaims

- no exclusion of any profile in (1);
- no claim about `V<=50`, folded profile `(4,2,0)`, or later swap bands;
- no authorization for a 24.49-billion-vector six-odd census.

## Falsifier

A fourteenth energy-26 profile at `L<=16`, failure of either cubic sign, a
parity-surviving profile outside (1), an omitted two-odd or six-odd support,
or a smaller complete direct-router count refutes the reduction.
