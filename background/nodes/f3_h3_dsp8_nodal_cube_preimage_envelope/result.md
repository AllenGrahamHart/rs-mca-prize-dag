cube-preimage subgroup. The resulting raw nodal DSP8 envelope is

```text
<116n^2  for p=2 (mod 3),
<498n^2  for p=1 (mod 3),
```

after the worst class weight. Mattarei's theorem applies independently to the
order-`gn` parameter fiber and the order-`n` quotient fiber. Both constants
are below the live uniform `G=4K` allowance `48536/25`; in the three-root case
more than `1443n^2` remains. Neither line pays the smooth traces.
