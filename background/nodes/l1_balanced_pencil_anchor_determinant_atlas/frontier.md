# Frontier

The theorem removes three possible ambiguities from the global L1 route:

1. coefficient-pair multiplicity: `Delta_0` is a complete affine coordinate;
2. common-complement ownership: `D=gcd(Delta_0,W_0)` is canonical;
3. fixed-owner split multiplicity: `(DA10)` pays it by root-matroid bases.

The Bezout-dual identity also places every owner in one global Pade family;
the owners are exact gcd strata of `Delta_0` against `W_0`, not unrelated
charts.

The live frontier is now aggregate. A closure theorem must coalesce the
degree-`s-1-j` owners `D`, prove that only a reserve-sized family of them is
realized, or route growing `j` to an earlier quotient/tangent/prefix owner.
Summing `(DA10)` over every divisor of the anchor denominator is not a viable
finite-row closure route.
