# Result

`PROVED` route no-go.

Fixed-weight ambient Q is an exact sufficient interface, but its natural
scale-free square-root payment is too strong. The high-cap counterexample
shows precisely where the lost factor lives: the product of actual Haar
marginals can exceed the reciprocal formal-codomain floor enough to absorb
many primitive orbits.

Future C2 work must retain one of:

```text
- the exact Haar denominator,
- an owner-sensitive joint numerator/denominator cancellation, or
- a finite Q statement normalized to those actual marginals.
```

Image/ambient prefix flatness alone is not the closing theorem suggested by
the previous bridge.
