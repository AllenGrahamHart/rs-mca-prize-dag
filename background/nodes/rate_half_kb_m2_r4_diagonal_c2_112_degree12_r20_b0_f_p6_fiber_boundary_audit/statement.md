# Statement

Let `p0=2130706433`. In each reduced `B0` chart

```text
F04-R20, F05-R20, F06-R20, F07-R20,
```

fix the symmetric coordinate `s=j` for every `j` in `{1,...,16}`. For each
of these 64 literal fibers, adjoin the exact field equations

```text
x^(p0^6)-x = 0,    pvar^(p0^6)-pvar = 0.
```

The resulting ideal is proper and zero-dimensional, but the complete
required open-factor product reduces to zero. In every fiber its first zero
occurs at open-factor step 8, which is the transported named unit
`1+s+pvar`.

Consequently none of the 64 fibers contains an admissible `F_(p0^6)` point.
The statement does not quantify over other values of `s`.
