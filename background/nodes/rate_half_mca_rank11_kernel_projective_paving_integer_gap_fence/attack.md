sharper value and then forgetting the adjacent integer chart.  Any proposed
improvement must control `t=1` explicitly or prove a monotonic comparison
that dominates it.

second turn in the ratio sequence, failure of the corank-one endpoint
comparison, or a record-cap evaluation below one of the three valid
