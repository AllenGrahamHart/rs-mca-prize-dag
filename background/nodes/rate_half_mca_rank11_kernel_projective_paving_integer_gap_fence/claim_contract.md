# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the three-endpoint envelope `(IG)` is the valid combination of
  support-local transversality with the complete projective-paving cap.
- **Range:** `2<=d<=9` and `10<=K'<=1048576`; corank one is handled by its
  separate uniform rank-two argument.
- **Output:** a record cap in one fixed canonical-basis chart.
- **Status:** PROVED.
- **Nonclaim:** `(IG)` is not asserted sharp, and its failure to reproduce
  a complete cap away from `t=0` is not a counterexample to a stronger
  uniform theorem or to the kernel statement.
