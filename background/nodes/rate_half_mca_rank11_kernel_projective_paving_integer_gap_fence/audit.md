# Audit

The primary verifier derives all three formulas, checks the ratio signs and
official corank-one endpoint comparison, and reconstructs the two wall
values.  Hostile controls alter the correction dimension, endpoint, and
printed caps.

The independent audit evaluates the pointwise sequence around its exact
turning index and at both interval ends for every corank, using direct
products rather than the primary helper functions.

A one-container SciPy probe of the resulting capacity system is pinned as
heuristic evidence only.  It observes the old `377673/377674` wall; neither
the floating optimizer nor that observation carries the node's PROVED
status.
