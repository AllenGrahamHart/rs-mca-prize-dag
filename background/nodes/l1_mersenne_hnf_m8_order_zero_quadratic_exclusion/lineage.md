# Lineage

The collision router left at most one repeated color on `m=8`; the
collision-free theorem forced at least one. This node exploits that exact
one-repeat partition rather than launching a generic Frobenius saturation.
Three centered color moments give one quadratic for the HNF parameter, and
the product of locator roots supplies an independent cyclotomic torsion
condition. Their row-exact resultants finish the degree-two classification.
