# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the profile-`(4,4)` local-norm cofactor list;
- the official prize rate-`1/8` prime interval;
- exact negacyclic autocorrelation and cyclotomic resultant identities.

## Output

Every official profile-`(4,4)` collision has `E>=5`, equivalently `V>=10`.

## Certificate boundary

The proof is an exhaustive exact finite computation over all abstract
integer spectra with `E<=4`. The scripts, hashes, run identifiers, counts,
and empty outputs are pinned in `source_contract.json` and
`modal_certificate.json`.

## Nonclaims

- no assertion that every abstract spectrum is coefficient-realizable;
- no count of spectra or vectors above energy four;
- no profile payment or official-row closure.
