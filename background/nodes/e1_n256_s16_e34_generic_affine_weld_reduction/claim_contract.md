# Claim contract

## Claim

Every residual generic E34 vector is affine-unit equivalent to one of 57
representatives and its four light positions meet all three printed weld sets.
The complete necessary-condition representative chamber contains exactly
243,285,056 signed vectors.

## Dependencies

- `e1_n256_s16_e34_heavy_chord_template_reduction` for the generic split and
  all three singleton-class welds;
- `e1_n256_s16_e34_parity_profile_reduction` for four light positions and the
  profile scope.

## Nonclaims

- no assertion that every counted support realizes compatible signed chords;
- no `E=34`, profile, conductor, moment, or norm conclusion;
- no exclusion of the generic template or universal target.

## Falsifier

A missing generic heavy orbit, disagreement in an orbit size, a residual
support failing one of the three welds, a fourth weld shape, or an error in the
exact chamber count refutes the claim.
