# Trivial-stabilizer payment

Close or pay every bridge-produced `(m,r,delta)=(2,8,1)` component. The
output is one exact integer `U_trivial` in distinct affine slopes under the
active received-line owner. Any nonzero payment includes the exact bridge
fiber multiplicity and a genuine locator-ray compiler.

This target is separate from the order-two coordinate/diagonal trichotomy.

## Falsifier

An actual trivial-stabilizer survivor without an exact payment, or an owner,
slope, or multiplicity mismatch.
