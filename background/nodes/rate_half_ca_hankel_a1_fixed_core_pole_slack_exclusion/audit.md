# Audit

1. The residual domain degree is `N-s`; after three contact copies the first
   target degree is exactly `d-1` and the restriction-kernel degree is `-1`.
2. Every fixed-core range has `e<=rho/2-1`, so domain interpolation degree
   two cannot contain a contact-active component.
3. The parameter offset is `3-beta`: two for ordinary fixed-core profiles
   and one at the `s=2,3e=d` boundary.
4. The `s=2` proof separates `Delta>0` and `Delta=0`; no floor division by
   three is applied across the boundary.
5. The first `s=1` exclusion uses the official large `m`, not a claim for
   every toy row.
