# Audit

- The second block system is not assumed from a direct-product presentation;
  it is derived from normality of the full diagonal socle and its trivial
  permutation centralizer.
- The automorphism induced by one monodromy element is common to all five
  blocks because it comes from conjugation on the single normal subgroup
  `D`.
- The exceptional two-action behavior of `M12` was already excluded across
  every block met by the actual `r=4` transversal, which is all four other
  blocks.
- Block size, not number of blocks, is the inner degree. Twelve blocks of
  size five therefore give inner degree five.
- The secondary decomposition need not equal the original degree-12 one.
  The degree-five exclusion quantifies over every geometric decomposition of
  the same endpoint map, so it applies without an identification.
- This deletes all five normal-form families at once but does not pay or
  close any other decomposition degree.
