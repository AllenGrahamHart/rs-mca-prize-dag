# Rank-eleven 31-anchor C/S/A/E globalization router

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** the complete deployed KoalaBear post-near error-rank-eleven
  family on one received line
- **output vocabulary:** Grande Finale v4 `(S)`, `(A)`, and `(E)`

Every unsafe line in this branch admits a fixed set `A_*` of `31` actual
post-near records, with their original fixed explanations and exact support
locators, such that:

1. `A_*` contains eighteen slopes owned by one minimizing pair and one
   explanation outside that pair's affine codeword line;
2. the exact common support of `A_*` has size at most `K-2214`;
3. for every other post-near record `z`, the order-32 tuple
   `A_* union {z}` is non-global-affine, has a cancellable exact common
   support, and reaches the lifted partial-relative interfaces of the
   shortened router.

Consequently the complete post-near family routes, with one received-line
owner and the original slope/support labels, into at least one of:

- **`(C)`**: one anchor-star tuple has a nonempty maximal common agreement
  set. Exact common-core cancellation emits a typed punctured-domain
  residual, but no deployed-domain spread payment is inferred;

- **`(S)`**: one anchor-star tuple contains a first-match primitive spread
  core;
- **`(A)`**: outside named exceptions, all root-free rational certificates
  are projectively identical and form one coherent rational atom, to be paid
  by owner localization and the large-owner input;
- **`(E)`**: a pure-locator, denominator-root, extension, quotient, clone,
  field-drop, nonprimitive collision, or quantified near-sunflower branch is
  present.

The already proved intrinsic near set of size at most `2w=134944` remains a
disjoint charge. This theorem is an exact route reduction. It does not prove
`(C)`, `(S)`, `(A)`, or `(E)`, move their budgets, or close error rank
eleven. In particular, the local common-support branch is not silently
identified with upstream `(E)`.

support at least `K`; a target slope not covered by an anchor-star tuple;
loss of a fixed anchor support locator during cancellation/lift; a
degree-below-18 target tuple; a locally common tuple omitted from `(C)`; two
distinct primitive root-free certificates sharing the 31 fixed records
without a collision/near-sunflower exception; or a residual classification
outside the named alternatives.
