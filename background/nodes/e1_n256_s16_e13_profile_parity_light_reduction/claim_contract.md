# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the proved `V=28` endpoint;
- the proved sparse-L1 recurrence and signed-chord gate;
- the complete proved one-diameter odd-parity light atlas;
- the collision norm criterion.

## Output

Every `V=26` profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate lies in one of four profiles on one
of 111 exact affine templates.

## Nonclaims

No surviving vector is excluded here. No cubic cutoff, lower variance,
profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform allowance, row, or prize terminal
is claimed.

## Falsifier

A candidate with `L>9`, a fifth energy profile, a profile with an odd-class
count other than one or five, or a light support outside the pinned atlas
refutes this router.
