## New theorem

For the once-divided support branch, an exact 9,080-orbit atlas, a square-norm
exclusion for the all-even heavy subbranch, two complete radius engines,
direct integer autocorrelation replay, and certified fixed-point root
intervals exhaust every signed vector. Every possible norm is strictly below
or above the complete cofactor-16 prize interval.

## Exclusions

1. This node does not exclude all of cofactor 16. The 39,936 primitive
   multiplicity-four affine support orbits remain open.
2. The all-even heavy-triple omission is exact: those polynomials lie in the
   index-two cyclotomic subfield and have square norm, unlike `16p`.
3. The radius syndrome is only a necessary filter. Every hit is followed by
   exact integer autocorrelation replay, and the reverse audit directly
   reconstructs every low-energy survivor.
4. Product-live filtering is conservative in `L`; it may retain extra vectors
   but cannot discard a possible collision.
5. The primary early cap proves only strict-below cases. The reverse audit
   recomputes all live vectors with the original full fixed-root interval.
6. The result supplies a zero contribution from one exact support stratum; it
   does not by itself close a pair budget or a prize row.
