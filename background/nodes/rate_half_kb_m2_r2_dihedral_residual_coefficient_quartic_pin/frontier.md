# Frontier

Write the sibling coefficients as `(A,B,C,D,E,F)` and use the exact
quartic `(KBMQ-1)`. The next finite geometric task is to classify when it
is irreducible and rational, hence has total singularity delta three, while
the induced quadratic pullbacks have one of the allowed source-cover branch
passports. The classification should be performed symbolically in these six
coefficients and only then specialized to the `D_3` and `D_6` factor
families. No search over arbitrary plane quartics remains.
