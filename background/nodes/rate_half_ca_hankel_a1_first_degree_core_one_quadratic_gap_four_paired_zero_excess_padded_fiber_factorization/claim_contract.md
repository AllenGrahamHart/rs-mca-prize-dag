# Claim contract

- **Proposition:** every zero-excess fiber of either surviving quadratic
  pair-boundary biform is a nonzero scalar times its actual inside-support
  locator and its complete padded-heavy factor.
- **Quantifier:** every field and profile satisfying the two proved biform
  reductions; every zero-excess off-line slope.
- **Dependencies:** the extremal and strict dual-MDS split-biform reductions.
- **Falsifier:** a zero-excess slope for which the specialized full locator is
  not proportional to `q_delta R_delta`, the minimum-word circuit fails, or
  the specialized biform differs from `A_delta R_delta`.
- **Nonclaim:** no rank exclusion, distinct-fiber theorem, or pair-boundary
  closure.
