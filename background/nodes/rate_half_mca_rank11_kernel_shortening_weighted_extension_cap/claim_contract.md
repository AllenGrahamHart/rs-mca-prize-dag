# Claim contract

- **Input:** the canonical/all-bases kernel chart, complete projective caps,
  the support-local pointwise envelope, and the proved uniform corank-one,
  corank-two, and corank-three caps.
- **Range:** every `1<=d<=9` and `796599<=K'<=1048576`.
- **Output:** a per-basis bound on the joint record/extension count, before
  summing bases and dividing by the proved `d+2` decorations.
- **Status:** PROVED.
- **Nonclaim:** no uniform promotion of `P_d` for `d>=4`, no claim that
  `t=1` maximizes the record cap alone, and no rank-eleven or prize closure
  without the downstream capacity comparison.
