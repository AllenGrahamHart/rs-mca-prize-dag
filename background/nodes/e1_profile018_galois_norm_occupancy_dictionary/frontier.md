# Frontier

Canonicalize actual profile-(0,18) polynomials under diagonal Galois action,
compute their exact norms, and group by the quotient

```text
p=Norm/514.
```

Only groups whose `p` is an official-admissible prime in the prize interval
matter. A group of size at least six falsifies the current occupancy target;
a deterministic proof that every such group has size at most five closes it.
Mod-257 compatibility is only an early filter.
