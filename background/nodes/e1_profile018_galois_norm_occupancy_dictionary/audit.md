- The Galois group and each primitive-root set have order 128.
- Multiplication by odd exponents acts freely and transitively.
- Shift/sign units preserve the principal ideal and do not change its row
  prime.
- A diagonal orbit meets each row-root fiber once, not up to an unprinted
  stabilizer.
- The map from the fixed fiber to `Q_s` uses actual `F(s)=0` ideals.
- Same-ideal injectivity invokes the proved height collapse.

  necessary in any falsifier.
