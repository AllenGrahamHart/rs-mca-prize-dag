# Claim contract

## Consumed content

- The profile-(0,18) router supplies the cofactor-514 ideal factorization.
- The split-prime/common-ideal theorem supplies at most one shift/sign orbit
  per fixed pair `(P_r,Q_s)`.
- Complete splitting supplies regular Galois actions on both root sets.

## New proved content

1. Every diagonal Galois orbit meets a fixed row-root fiber exactly once.
2. Collision orbits in that fiber are in bijection with occupied `Q_s`.
3. Occupancy equals exact norm multiplicity among diagonal Galois orbits.
4. The occupancy count is independent of the selected row root.

## Guards

- The dictionary concerns actual collision polynomials, not squared-magnitude
  roots or autocorrelation candidates.
- Galois canonicalization is diagonal in the row and 257 roots; normalizing
  one root does not erase their relative class.
- Equality of norms alone is not used for injectivity; equal principal ideals
  and the height theorem are essential.
- No bound of five is proved here.
