# Audit

1. The exact resultant `(ERQ3)` is used, not the pre-cancellation identity
   containing `Lambda^d`.
2. Its supported product is explicitly over off-line slopes. Therefore a
   correction root which is a center still has exact resultant order two.
3. Shared roots are allowed by the exact regular-quartic theorem; no hidden
   generic-disjointness assumption is introduced.
4. If the correction root is off-line supported, no false order-two claim is
   made. The proof switches to the complete actual/padding fiber dichotomy.
5. Actual roots are rejected by first-jet transversality; padding roots are
   rejected by `gcd(g_*,S_B)=1`.
6. The proof establishes nonvanishing only. The exact remainder may still be
   a nonzero multiple of `H` and pass the augmented gate.
