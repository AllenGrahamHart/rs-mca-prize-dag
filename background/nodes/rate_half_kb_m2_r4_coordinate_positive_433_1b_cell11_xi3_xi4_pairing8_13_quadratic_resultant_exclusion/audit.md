# Audit

Run `verify.py` and `verify_audit.py`. The primary verifier pins the
adapter, compiler, tower, kernel, independent root packet, and orbit router,
then checks the exact row and terminal census. The independent audit parses
both executable methods, reconstructs every polynomial root set, and replays
the original scalar equations at all 64 lifts and 128 final lanes, including
all 16 cell-11 leading-boundary payments.
