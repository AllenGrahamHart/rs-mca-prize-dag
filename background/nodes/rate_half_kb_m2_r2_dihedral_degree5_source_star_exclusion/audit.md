  exact `n=5` profile uses a simple pole there; it is not inferred merely
  from the existence of a branch point.
- The singleton values `y_0,z_0` may differ in the two reflection quotient
  coordinates; the proof does not identify them.
- Each endpoint source fiber of `h` has two distinct points. The complete
  source pullback identity gives a separate degree-two divisor `D_w` for
  each point; no coordinate-index matching is assumed.
- The two distinct roots of `H(T,x)` follow from local complete-source
  saturation, not merely from set-theoretic containment in a two-point
  endpoint fiber.
- Ramification of the source parameter map does not change the weight sum:
  multiplicities in each `D_w` still total two.
- The contradiction is `w_v>=4` versus the proved `w_v<=3`; no generic
  smoothness assumption is used.
