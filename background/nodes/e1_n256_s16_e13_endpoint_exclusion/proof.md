# Proof

The E13 router proves that every `V=26` candidate lies in one of four profiles
on 111 templates. The four-profile exclusion proves that every proper-conductor
vector is removed by the conductor theorem. For the full-conductor residue,
all but four odd norm parts lie below `2^250`; the four exceptions are two
composite integers below `2^251` and therefore have no prime divisor above
`2^250`. These branches exhaust the router, so the entire `V=26` chamber is
empty. Variance is even, hence the next live positive endpoint is at most 24.
