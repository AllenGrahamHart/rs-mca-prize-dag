# Proof

The pairing-4/9 orbit theorem supplies

```text
(0,4), (1,4), (2,4), (2,9).
```

The exact positive-`DE` pairing-9 theorem supplies the remaining labels

```text
(0,9), (1,9).
```

Their union is exactly `{0,1,2} x {4,9}`, with no overlap between the two
new labels and the four-label parent. Hence all six labels and their
`6*4*4=96` raw sign/lane cases are empty.

Under the proved parallel-`DE` action these six labels are exactly three
orbits:

```text
{(0,4),(1,4)}, {(2,4),(2,9)}, {(0,9),(1,9)}.
```

The prior complete first-pair and pairing-3/6 block paid 15 labels in 9
orbits. Therefore the cumulative ledger is `15+6=21` labels in `9+3=12`
orbits, leaving `105-21=84` labels in `60-12=48` orbits. QED.
