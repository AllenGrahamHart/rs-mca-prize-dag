# Audit

- The matrix pencil is in the common error ray, not in the selected
  annihilator slope. These projective parameters are dual but should not be
  conflated in the rank argument.
- Generic consistency of `(3)` gives a rational affine solution because the
  first homogeneous parameter coordinate has already been fixed to one.
- Specialized rank of `B` cannot exceed its rank over `F(P^1)`. Thus every
  consistent specialization kills the chosen `(t+1)`-minor.
- A nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree `t+1` has at most `t+1`
  projective roots over the base field. Extension-field roots do not increase
  the number of base-field target rays.
- The distinct-pole argument is needed only to exclude generic consistency;
  the finite owner count then follows from elementary matrix-pencil rank.
- The same-ray strip is still load-bearing: the eliminant counts rays, not
  target parameters within one ray.
