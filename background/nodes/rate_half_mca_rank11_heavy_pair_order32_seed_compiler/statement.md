# Rank-eleven heavy-pair order-32 seed compiler

- **status:** PROVED
- **closure:** actual-record cross-pair compiler
- **scope:** deployed KoalaBear MCA row, post-near error rank eleven

## Statement

Suppose a deployed KoalaBear received line has more than

```text
B_*=274980728111395087
```

bad challenge slopes and its selected post-near errors have affine rank
eleven. Fix the reversible gauge, exact supports, support-local margins, and
one actual minimizing pair for every record as in the proved rank-eleven
route cut.

Then there are `32` distinct actual records with `theta<=387` whose exact
selected supports have common intersection of size strictly below

```text
K-4922=1043654.
```

The records retain the original received line, slopes, explanations,
supports, minimizing pairs, and first-match chronology.

## Mechanism

Pair types owning one low-margin record cost at most the exact interleaved
pair cap

```text
Q_10(387)=869784434119.
```

Call a pair type heavy if it owns at least two records. If all heavy pair
cores shared `K-4922` coordinates, shortening plus the proved Johnson
endpoint would cap the complete line at

```text
274791086998147432 < B_*.
```

Thus an unsafe line has smaller heavy-core intersection. All component
differences between heavy pairs lie in the same ten-dimensional explanation
direction code. One anchor and at most ten further heavy pairs span those
components. Two records from each selected pair force their support
intersection into that pair core, yielding at most `22` records whose common
support is already below `K-4922`; arbitrary distinct low-margin records pad
the family to `32`.

## Nonclaims

This compiler does not classify or pay the resulting order-32 seed. It does
not prove the seed is primitive after common-support cancellation, construct
an S/A/E owner, pay rank eleven, or close KoalaBear.

## Falsifier

An unsafe rank-eleven line without such a 32-record seed; failure of the
singleton charge or large-heavy-core payment; or a heavy pair family whose
component differences cannot be spanned by ten selected pair differences.
