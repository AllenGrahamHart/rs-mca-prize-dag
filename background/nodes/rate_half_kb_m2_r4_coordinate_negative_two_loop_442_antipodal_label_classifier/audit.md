# Audit

1. `k_A,k_B` are fiber labels; `A,B` are endpoint-pair representatives.
   Their squares must not be conflated.
2. The shared-endpoint orientation fixes the sign of each source lift and
   edge sum together.  Squaring only occurs after the exact weld equation.
3. Cross-edge sums and every cancelled factor are nonzero because distinct
   signed `J` pairs are used and all five `K` labels are distinct.
4. Scaling by `k_AB` is valid because the coordinate involution has no zero
   label in `I`.
5. The matching table uses the exact fact that `K` is one deletion from
   three antipodal pairs; it is not a generic five-set statement.
6. The root-of-unity loci are retained.  Their existence in the deployed
   field is not an orientation witness.
