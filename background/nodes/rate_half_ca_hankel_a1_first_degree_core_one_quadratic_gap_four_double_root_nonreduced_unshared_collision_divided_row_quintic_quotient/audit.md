   `D_1=g_*S_B^2`.
2. Divisibility of `F_0` follows from the exact Pade syzygy and the proved
   factorizations of both outside rows.
3. The degree bound uses `deg F_0<=e+1` and `deg(g_*S_B)=e-4`, leaving
   exactly the quintic cap.
4. The recurrence forcing term is `S_B^2h_i`, of degree at most five.
5. Cancellation occurs in the polynomial ring after common divisibility
   is proved; no local quotient is globalized by assumption.
6. `C_0(tau)` is nonzero because the quadratic residual is nonzero there.
7. No Smith profile is excluded by the quintic quotient alone.
