# Audit

The complement value `S=B` is not the cubic role polynomial `B(lambda)`.
The notation `B_0` in (TRW3) is the homogeneous numerator of the latter.
Implementations should retain these names or separate them more strongly.

Clearing `S^6` or `S^12` is reversible only on the inherited `B!=0`
saturation. Retain `R!=0` as well; otherwise the forbidden `lambda=1`
diagonal and the `3+3` profile can re-enter.

Each equation in (TRW4) is one rational Galois packet, not one scalar role.
A nonunit welded system remains only a common-quadratic affine-color
candidate and owes all exact-fiber and outer/inner filters.
