# Audit

1. `u` is the sum of the selected quadruple. At an actual relation `u=0`
   also makes the complementary triple sum zero, so the paid weight-three
   single-moment exclusion applies.
2. This does not justify deleting every rational prime dividing `Norm(u)`.
   A rational norm merges 512 split embeddings; `u` may vanish at a
   different embedding from `F` and `G`.
3. Shared `Norm(u)` primes are therefore retained until the polynomial
   common-root test `(QCR6)` is applied. Blind integer saturation is not a
   proof rule for weight seven.
4. The two recurrence equations plus `d^1024=1` force the three normalized
   1024th powers to have locator `(T-1)^3`. Neither equation alone suffices.
5. A surviving common root still owes cubic splitting, distinctness,
   disjointness, and direct replay of both moments. A norm divisor is only a
   candidate.
6. The Burnside action changes the complement-product exponent by `3r`,
   not `4r` or `7r`; the complement always has three roots.
7. The exact count is a route-size theorem, not authorization. The measured
   complete census cost is outside scope.
