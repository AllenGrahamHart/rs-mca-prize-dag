# Attack

1. **Full circuit `c=9`.** Every component eleven-set supplies 54 rank-nine
   shadows. Retain the owner label and the `(record,T,B')` incidence unit;
   do not sum the existing fixed-chart cap over all `C(n',9)` charts. Seek a
   local overlap or chronology charge among the one-coordinate replacement
   charts `B-b+x`.
2. **Proper circuit `c<=8`.** Use
   `L_(C_B)RS_{<11-c}<=V'` and the extra rank-eight shadows. Test whether the
   larger locator ideal forces a common owner core, a further legitimate
   shortening, or a lower-dimensional normalized-deviation route.
3. Preserve exact component-extension weight and pair-noncontainment.
   Converting marked incidences to distinct records without an overlap bound
   is invalid.
4. The eight-petal `c=9` family is a mandatory hostile control. A valid
   global theorem may route it to its eight dense owners; it may not claim
   the fixed chart itself is impossible.
