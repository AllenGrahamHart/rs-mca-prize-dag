# Claim contract

## Input

The `K'=11` residual correction space `V'<=RS_{<11}` has dimension ten,
the complete residual record family has empty global common support, and
one fixed nine-set `B` in the affine-owner lane carries at least `2578110`
distinct slopes with `rank(ev_B|V')=8`.

## Output

The evaluation matroid has one fixed circuit `C_B subset B` of size
`2<=c<=9`. Every eleven-set extending `B` has exactly
`C(11-c,2)` rank-eight nine-shadows, `55-C(11-c,2)` rank-nine
nine-shadows, and `c` rank-ten bases. Moreover
`L_(C_B)RS_{<11-c}<=V'`. Every `c=2,...,9` is realizable at the
correction-space and chart-rank level.

## Nonclaim

No record capacity, cross-chart owner synchronization, chronology owner,
rank-eight payment, aggregate rank-eleven payment, active-v4 movement, or
prize closure follows from the census alone.
