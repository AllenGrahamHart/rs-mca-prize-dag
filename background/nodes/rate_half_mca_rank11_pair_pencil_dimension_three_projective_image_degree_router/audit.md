# Audit

1. The residual common polynomial gcd need not equal the already-shortened
   common received core. Its remaining official roots are zero-owner
   coordinates and are charged explicitly to the 218-fold deficit.
2. The common polynomial gcd is removed before homogenization; otherwise
   `d=ec` would incorrectly count base points. Removing it can delete at
   most 310 distinct official roots from a represented direction.
3. A degree-`d` member prevents a common base point at infinity.
4. Map degree includes inseparable degree, so the number of distinct points
   in a geometric fiber is still at most `e`.
5. Full owner planes with one normal may be distinct parallel affine planes;
   the proof counts their coordinates together and does not identify them.
6. The conic is geometrically integral because it is the image of
   geometrically integral `P^1`. Its base-field point makes the Veronese
   parametrization base-field defined.
7. The raw 2,351-root floor supplies only the primitive 2,041-root floor
   after the gcd charge; it does not say every pullback hyperplane is split
   or that `A/B` is quotient-periodic.
