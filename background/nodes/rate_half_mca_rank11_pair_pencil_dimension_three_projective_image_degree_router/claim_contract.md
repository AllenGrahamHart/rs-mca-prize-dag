# Claim contract

- **input:** a three-dimensional residual scalar polynomial space, the
  `q=3170` endpoint interval, full-coordinate floor and deficit, and
  2,351-root direction bank;
- **gcd budget:** the residual common polynomial gcd has at most 310
  official-domain roots; after division every represented primitive
  direction retains at least 2,041 roots;
- **degree identity:** the polynomial degree factors as `d=ec`, where `e`
  is map degree and `c>=2` is projective image degree;
- **conic output:** `W_hom=span{A^2,AB,B^2}` over the base field with
  `1021<=deg(A/B)<=2490`;
- **higher-image output:** image degree at least three forces at least 597
  distinct full evaluation normals, with exact endpoint rows 597..633;
- **not claimed:** classification of `A/B`, quotient periodicity, endpoint
  exclusion, record payment, rank-eleven closure, or MCA closure.
