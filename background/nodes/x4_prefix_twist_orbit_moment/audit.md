# Audit

1. Prefix scaling uses full elementary locator coefficients and is valid in
   every characteristic.
2. The stabilizer is computed from active coefficient indices, not from the
   support stabilizer of an individual subset.
3. `(TO-1)` is a lower bound on a moment forced by one heavy orbit. It is not
   an upper bound on that moment.
4. Calling an active-index face "quotient-shaped" does not prove that every
   support in it is quotient-periodic; no such classification is claimed.
5. Fixed low moments remain insufficient for the official maximum-prefix
   target unless a sharp upper theorem is supplied.
