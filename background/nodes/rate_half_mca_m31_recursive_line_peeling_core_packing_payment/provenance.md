boundary-line-bank wall with a residual affine-line peeling recursion.  The
key invariant is that distinct peeled affine lines have codeword-pair cores
whose inside-support intersections are bounded by `K-1`.

The interval was located by a constant-memory C scan under RAMguard.  The
scan took seconds and used no Modal credit.
