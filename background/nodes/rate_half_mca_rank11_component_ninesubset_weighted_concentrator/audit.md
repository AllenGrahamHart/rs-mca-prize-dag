# Audit

The lane is selected before `B`, so no lane mixing occurs. The weight is a
count of actual `(record, eleven-subset)` incidences, not owner labels. The
identity with `C(m'-9,2)` is exact, and ceiling is applied only after the
fixed-`B` average.

The primary verifier rejects six mutations. The independent verifier
recomputes the marked and deduplicated endpoints from the two binomial
forms.
