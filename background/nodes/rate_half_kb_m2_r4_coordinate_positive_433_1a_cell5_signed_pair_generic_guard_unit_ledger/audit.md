# Audit

1. A nonzero specialized remainder proves generic nonvanishing; it does not
   prove nonvanishing at every finite `t`.
2. The theorem covers exactly 22 common-chart and eight squared
   outside-incidence factors, not every unsquared source guard.
3. The factors `D(x0)D(x1)` were already inverted in the parent pair
   algebra and are not recounted here.
4. The condition `x_j!=0` does not prove that `x_j` has a square root in
   the deployed field.
5. Squaring loses the distinction between `z0=z1` and `z0=-z1`; no
   source-slot distinctness claim is made.
6. No colored edge, cell, route, K3 row, or Prize status is claimed.
