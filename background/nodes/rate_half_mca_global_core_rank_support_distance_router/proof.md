# Regression proof and surviving gates

The former router took the minimum of three bounds on one gauged selected
family.  Its direction-distance component remains proved.  Its rank and
common-zero components both used the same affine-incidence denominator,
which is refuted by a 31-slope family against claimed bounds 23 and 22.
Taking a minimum with a false upper bound is invalid, so every payment
depending on either component is withdrawn.

For the surviving high-support suffix, the repaired recursive theorem pays
`j<=4337` on KoalaBear at `s=14` and `j<=4334` on Mersenne at `s=6`.
Since `e=R-j` and `R=1048576`, these are

```text
e>=1048576-4337=1044239,
e>=1048576-4334=1044242.
```

The independent sparse-direction punctured-list theorem gives `e<=5` and
`e<=1` at those first dimensions.  No theorem in the repaired dependency
sub-DAG pays the interval between these gates.
