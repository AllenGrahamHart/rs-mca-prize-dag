# Claim contract

## Input

One LS6 atom with `L_i=P-z_i` and source cross-ratio different from the fiber
cross-ratio.

## Output

Every unguarded solution has the forced common factor `Q` in `(MP5)`, of
degree `ell-a>0`; hence the guarded atom is empty.

## Nonclaims

- arbitrary petal locators admit a common-pencil presentation;
- periodicity of one locator forces petal-pencil structure;
- reciprocal/dihedral or primitive split points are bounded.
