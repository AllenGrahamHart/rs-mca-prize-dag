# Upstream crosswalk

This is an exact split-pencil exclusion preceding the BC/master-flatness
census. For three petal locators in one pencil, affine alignment makes the
LS6 degree condition impossible, while misalignment forces a nonconstant
common factor and fails exactness. Thus the full common-pencil source stratum
is empty rather than merely quotient-profile paid.

Any upstream split-pencil or inverse theorem used for the remaining FPC5
atom may assume the touched petal locators are not three fibers of one
degree-`ell` pencil.
