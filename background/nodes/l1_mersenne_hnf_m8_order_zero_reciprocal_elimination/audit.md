# Audit

## Necessity is enough

The reciprocal equations are necessary, not sufficient, for an HNF
survivor. Proving that even three of them have no allowed common solution is
therefore a valid exclusion. No reverse transport from a reciprocal solution
to a locator is asserted.

## Interpolation scope

The proof prints raw determinant degree bounds before interpolation. The
sample counts are one greater than those bounds, and an extra point is checked
outside each interpolation grid. All factorial and interpolation denominators
are nonzero because every official prime exceeds 1792.

The grids start at 200 and end below 2000. They avoid the only roots
`0,-1,...,-6` of `C(s)`, so the numerical polynomial degrees equal the fixed
degrees used by the resultant. The resulting interpolants remain polynomial
identities and may then be evaluated at every algebraic `s`.

## Independent source for Q

The audit's companion matrix has eigenvalues equal to the locator roots, so
its eighth power has eigenvalues `a_i^8`; its characteristic polynomial is
exactly `Q_s`. Newton traces use divisions only by `1,...,7`. This is
independent of the primary symbolic resultant construction.

The audit also compares its recursive polynomial resultants with direct
Sylvester-matrix determinants at three values for both eliminants on every
row (24 exact cross-checks). The final gcd is pinned to the printed monic
factor rather than merely to its degree.

## Nonclaims

The `m=16` order-zero and all order-one systems remain live. The theorem is
an outer endpoint deletion and does not pay the global mixed-petal numerator.
