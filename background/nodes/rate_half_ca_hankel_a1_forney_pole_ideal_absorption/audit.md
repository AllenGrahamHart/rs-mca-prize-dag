# Audit

- The proof uses colon-ideal membership in the full fibre ring, not only
  vanishing at distinct pole points.
- `Q_min` is the contracted split recurrence factor supplied at every
  supported exceptional slope; it need not be the generic generator.
- The Forney numerator factorization is the elementary recurrence identity
  `N_(Q_min R)=R N_(Q_min)` in reversed coordinates.
- Fixed core factors have already been contracted. Accordingly `G` is the
  residual locator on `D\S` and `Q_min` divides that locator.
- The argument works after scalar extension and descends because the colon
  ideal and numerator are defined over the base field.
- Regularity at domain infinity comes from the previously proved contact
  order; `(FPA3)` addresses the finite supported-fibre poles.
