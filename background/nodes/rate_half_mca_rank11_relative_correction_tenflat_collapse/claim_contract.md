# Claim contract

## Inputs

- The PROVED full-rank `(H_C)` deviation-space construction.
- The PROVED relative core-interpolant and one-ray payment.
- The PROVED relative proper-intersection and clone-tolerant router.

## Output

Every over-budget correction residual is a high-core-absorbing
positive-dimensional rank-flat or polynomial clone component with correction
span dimension in `2..10`.

## Nonclaim

The component is not classified, counted, or paid. No rational-owner,
quotient, or spread-terminal promotion is made at this node.
