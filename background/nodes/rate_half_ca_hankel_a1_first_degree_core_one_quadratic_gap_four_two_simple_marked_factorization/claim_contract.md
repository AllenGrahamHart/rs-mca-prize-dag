# Claim contract

- **Claim:** each two-simple heavy-row polynomial is exactly a square of its
  supported factor times a cube of a degree-one or degree-three residual
  form, and its marked Hankel determinant has the factorization `(TSF4)`.
- **Currency:** pushforward of the exact vertical divisors and the general
  core-one rank-one marked determinant identity.
- **Dependencies:** the quadratic gap-four normal forms and the marked
  Hankel determinant gate.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted; `(TSF5)` is the characteristic-three
  specialization.
- **Nonclaim:** no coprimality among `G_i,S_i`, no incidence-set overlap
  bound, and no packet exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a heavy-row form with any valuation outside
  `2div(G_i)+3div(S_i)`, or a marked determinant differing from `(TSF4)`.
- **Next gate:** use the simultaneous two-row factorization, Forney values,
  or a mixed rank-two update to constrain overlap or residual roots.
