# Claim contract

## Proved

- exact Mobius normalization of an irreducible owner-pencil clone;
- slope bidegrees `(1,2)` for its denominator and numerator;
- fixed coherent-owner or globally affine absorption when the denominator
  pencil has projective rank one and `|C|>=m+1`;
- the exact `|C|=m` alternative `0` or `mu Lambda_C` at the sole
  denominator-zero parameter;
- reduction of every unpaid large clone to moving-denominator rank two or
  the nonzero exact locator remainder.

## Not proved

- a count for either residual branch;
- that clone coordinates are selected-support roots;
- complete `(S)`, `(A)`, or `(E)` routing;
- a chronology-correct whole-line selector or a deployed row bound.

## Falsifier

An irreducible common `(1,1)` component violating `(LC1)`, a rank-one
denominator clone with more than `m` coordinates that does not cancel to one
fixed owner, or a boundary remainder not proportional to `Lambda_C`.
