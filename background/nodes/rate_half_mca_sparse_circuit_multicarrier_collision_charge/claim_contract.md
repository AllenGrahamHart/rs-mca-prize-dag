# Claim contract

- Fixed vanishing set: `D`, of size `u`.
- Fixed polynomial space: `W<=V`, of dimension `g`, vanishing on `D`.
- Target deletion space: `H_A`, of dimension `11-d`.
- Positive intersection: `r_d=g+1-d>0`.
- Target outside budget: `R_d=K-r_d-u`.
- Outside target-circuit stratum: divide by the exact number `j` of
  external deletion exposures.
- Negative completion budgets contribute zero.
- Incidence multiplier: `C(m-d,11-d)`.
- The theorem does not manufacture `D` or `W`; a consumer must prove both
  are fixed across every target deletion being charged.
