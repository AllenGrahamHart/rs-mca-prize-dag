# Audit

- The two frequency sets are disjoint but are negatives of one another.
  Treating disjointness as probabilistic independence is exactly the false
  premise exposed here.
- The characteristic bound is used only to lift
  `0<A^2+B^2<=8` away from zero. The exact fixtures use characteristics 17
  and 97.
- Primitive deletion subtracts `4^r`, not zero and not the entire common
  event. This distinguishes the construction from the owner-deleted
  order-four complete-band tensor.
- The official prefix has `t<n/2`, so it cannot contain both `f` and `n-f`.
  The counterexample is a proof-architecture fence, not a target falsifier.
- The ratio uses unchanged unconditional marginals, matching the DLI
  primitive-correlation convention.
