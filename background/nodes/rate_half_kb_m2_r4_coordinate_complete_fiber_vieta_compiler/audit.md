# Audit

1. `Omega` has twelve quotient fibers, not 24: each row represents a deck
   pair of source points and therefore two stars.
2. The `q` record is unchanged only when the source point and edge are deck
   transported together.
3. The product matrices do not use square roots and remain valid before a
   choice of source lift.
4. Leading support is required for the converse Vieta implication.
5. The `K+eta` determinant is a necessary condition only in positive
   parity; the negative product map has degree one and uses rank at most
   three instead.
6. Resultant identities are automatic only after the complete source-facet
   and complete-source hypotheses are retained.
7. The `F_29` calculation is a regression deletion, not a characteristic
   `2130706433` exclusion.
8. Negative injectivity concerns edge products, not oriented edge sums.
   An antipodal edge is fixed by deck transport and contributes one unit of
   defect even when its product occurs at only one quotient fiber.
9. The seven-skeleton census does not claim those skeletons are realizable;
   it is the exact remainder after only degree and injectivity constraints.
10. The 140 separator survivors prove that the six-row separator is not a
    universal abstract deletion.  The zero complete survivors concern one
    fixture and one finite label family only.
