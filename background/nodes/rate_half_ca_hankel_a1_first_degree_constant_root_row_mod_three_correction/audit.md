# Audit

- The residual root may have multiplicity `z>1`; summing the local identity
  retains `z e`, which vanishes modulo three only because the official `e`
  is divisible by three.
- Unsupported vertical points are included. Their equations contribute no
  horizontal multiplicity but do contribute to the complete vertical degree
  `e`.
- `t_x` counts new distinguished roots, while `epsilon_x` counts excess
  multiplicity beyond one at every distinguished incidence. They are not
  interchangeable.
- The global inequality leaves all excess degrees outside the named heavy
  incidences uncharged; it is a lower bound, not an equality.
- No full-overlap assumption is made except in the printed three-entry
  corollary.
