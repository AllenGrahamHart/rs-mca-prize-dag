# Claim contract

- Circuit counts are labelled supports, not deletion records.
- Flat sizes include loops and parallel elements.
- No simplicity, representability, or field-size assumption is used.
- The two flat caps are consecutive: `B` and `B+1`.
- Witness multiplicity is at most `r+1`, once for each `r`-subset of `Q`.
- Circuit minimality excludes completions lying in the lower flat.
- The theorem supplies no row payment by itself.
