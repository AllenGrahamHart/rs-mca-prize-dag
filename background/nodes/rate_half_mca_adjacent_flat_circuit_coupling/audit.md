# Audit

1. Every lower circuit contributes through all `r+1` independent deletions.
2. A higher-circuit completion in the lower flat is excluded by minimality.
3. Each independent `(r+1)`-set has at most `r+1` witness owners.
4. The factor `N-B` uses both `b<=B` and `N>=B`.
5. Replacing the number of independent sets by `C(N,r+1)` is conservative.
6. Uniform rank-`(r+1)` controls attain equality for several ranks.
