# Result

`PROVED`: at `V=60`, exact slack gives `L<=18`; the cubic cutoff is
`M_3=1087`; and parity reduces 18 integer profiles to eight. Five have six odd
classes on at least 1,097 affine light orbits, while three have two odd classes
on a complete 87-orbit router.

This reduction explicitly rules out a blind full light-template census of the
six-odd branch; it does not exclude any of the eight profiles.
