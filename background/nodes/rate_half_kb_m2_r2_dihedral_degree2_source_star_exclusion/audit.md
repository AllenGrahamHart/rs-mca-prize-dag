# Audit

- The `K_(2,2)` incidence is the regular `D_2=V4` coset incidence, not a
  coordinate identification between the `Y` and `Z` values.
- Generic pole fibers are unramified for both reflection quotients and for
  the endpoint map `h`.
- The cross-edge claim uses the isomorphism of normalized source and endpoint
  components. It is not inferred from degree counts alone.
- The two endpoint lifts of each `Z` value and both complete-source units of
  each lift are included: `2*2*2=8`.
- Ramification of `psi` at one of these source values would contradict the
  four distinct normalized points over `W=w`; it is not silently ignored.
- The defect minimum is exact and attained only by `(2,2,2,2)`.
- The remaining `n=3,6` rows have distinct outer incidence pairs, so this
  four-vertex pigeonhole does not apply to them.
- No ledger quantity moves.
