# Proof

The relaxation child proves that every magnitude layer is either below the
cubic cutoff or belongs to the exact 33,737-entry exceptional set on 1,191
light orbits.  The actual-census child proves that these orbits lift to 6,244
actual cubic exceptions, of which 5,012 have proper conductor and 1,232 have
full conductor.

The cubic part of `collision_norm_criterion` excludes every layer with
`M_3<=1087`.  The proper-conductor theorem excludes the 5,012 imprimitive
actual exceptions.  The primitive-norm child proves that all remaining 1,232
exact norms are below `2^250`; the norm criterion excludes those as well.
The branches are disjoint and exhaustive, so profile `(6,6)` cannot collide.
QED.
