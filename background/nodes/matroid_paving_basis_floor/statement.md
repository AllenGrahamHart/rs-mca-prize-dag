# Paving-matroid basis floor

Let `M` be a rank-`r` matroid on `m>=r` elements, where `r>=2`.  Suppose
every subset of at most `r-1` elements is independent.  Then the number of
bases satisfies

```text
b(M) >= C(m-1,r-1).
```

The bound is sharp: take one coloop together with a uniform rank-`r-1`
matroid on the other `m-1` elements.
