# Attack

The dangerous cases are a large hyperplane and multiple competing
hyperplanes.  The one-coloop construction shows that a large hyperplane
actually attains the claimed floor.  In the no-coloop case, deletion and
contraction preserve exactly the smaller-rank independence hypotheses, so
multiple hyperplanes cannot push the basis count below the Pascal sum.

A counterexample must violate deletion-contraction, lose rank on deletion
without a coloop, or create a dependent `(r-2)`-set after contraction while
all `(r-1)`-sets of the original matroid remain independent.
