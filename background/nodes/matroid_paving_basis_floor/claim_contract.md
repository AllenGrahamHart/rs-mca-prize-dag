# Claim contract

- **Claim:** a rank-`r` matroid on `m` elements with every set of size at
  most `r-1` independent has at least `C(m-1,r-1)` bases.
- **Range:** `r>=2` and `m>=r`.
- **Proof mechanism:** coloop split followed by deletion-contraction and
  Pascal's identity.
- **Sharpness:** one coloop over `U_(r-1,m-1)`.
- **Status:** PROVED.
- **Nonclaim:** this node supplies no Reed-Solomon transversality hypothesis
  and performs no prize-row capacity comparison.
