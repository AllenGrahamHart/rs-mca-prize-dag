# Claim contract

- `B_2` is the full nonzero parallel class of an attaining support-two
  deletion and has size `M_2+1`.
- Carrier completions are exact minimal circuits; points creating a proper
  subcircuit are not counted as support-`c` completions.
- Transverse support-`c` position: disjoint carriers and fixed dimension
  at least `10-c`.
- Proper-span position: `H_c<=H_2`; an anchor gives one carrier
  intersection point, while a larger proper span gives none.
- Full-completion position: every point of `B_2` is a completion and
  `M_c>=M_2+1`.
- If `M_3<=M_2`, the full-completion position is excluded and
  `s_2+s_3<q` is impossible.
- If `M_3=M_4=M_2+1`, the six `(u,g)` alternatives in `(PT2)` are
  exhaustive.
- Every numerical cap is delegated to the fixed-union multicarrier node.
