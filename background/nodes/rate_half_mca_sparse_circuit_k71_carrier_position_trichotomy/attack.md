one-step relation `M_3=M_4=M_2+1`.  Do not treat a completion carrier as
the full ground-set intersection with its flat: points producing smaller
circuits are deliberately excluded.  The immediate consumer is the
`K'=71` payment; the same payment first fails at `K'=72` when the active
