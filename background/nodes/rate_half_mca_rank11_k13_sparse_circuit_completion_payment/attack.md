# Attack notes

The quotient-plane route initially appeared to require a classification of
plane sections of rational-normal-curve secant varieties.  That would have
introduced a large and fragile geometric dependency.

The completion dichotomy avoids it.  Generalized MDS allows three
completions of an independent set, but equality itself makes the entire
quotient supported on at most seven coordinates.  Otherwise the completion
ceiling drops to two and exact deletion counting fits the available row
capacity.

The most important audit condition is retention of both rank-deficient
terms.  At `K'=13`, corank two has extension factor one and cannot be
discarded even though it is numerically much smaller than the high-circuit
capacity.
