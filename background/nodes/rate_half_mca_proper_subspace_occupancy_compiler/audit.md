extensions.  This proof separates the two facts:

1. proper subspaces of dimension below `q` are controlled by actual
   Reed-Solomon common-zero bounds;
2. a `q`-dimensional hyperplane has only one unconditional extension, raised
   to `e-(N-m)` only when direction-coset distance supplies it.

The primary verifier recomputes every exact deployed wall and hostile
adjacent value.  The independent verifier scans every allowed zero-normal
count rather than assuming the endpoint reduction and uses rational
arithmetic through the final floor.

It also exhausts all 729 received lines over `GF(3)` for
`RS[GF(3),D,1]` at `(N,m)=(3,2)` and checks all 540 nontrivial rank-one
selected families.

is 471 and therefore contains its 31 slopes.
