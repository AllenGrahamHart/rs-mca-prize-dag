# Audit

1. The Padé exponent is `2d+1`, not the clean-endpoint exponent copied
   without its extra leading-cofactor factor.
2. `P_F` is defined from the actual contracted moments; no arbitrary
   second-kind polynomial is introduced.
3. The proof is first made where `Q` is separable and `a!=0`, then extended
   by polynomial identity.
4. The normalization by `a^(2d+1)` removes the fixed domain-infinity
   contact before identifying `D_1` with `pi_*div(s_F)`.
5. Polynomial cancellation in `(PRI6)--(PRI7)` does not require disjoint
   root sets.
6. The quartic identification is structural and is not promoted to an
   exclusion.
