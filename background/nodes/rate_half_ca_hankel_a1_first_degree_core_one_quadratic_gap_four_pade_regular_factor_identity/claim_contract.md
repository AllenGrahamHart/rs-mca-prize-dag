# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the contracted Pade numerator has normalized resultant `D_1`,
  and the regular quartic is exactly the correction pushforward.
- **Inputs:** the contracted source representation, split-biform
  interpolation, middle-adjugate identity, contact-divisor normal forms,
  and regular-quartic pin.
- **Outputs:** `(PRI1)--(PRI7)`.
- **Quantifiers:** both `u=4` root patterns and both extremal line-deficit
  profiles over the prize base field.
- **Falsifier:** a wrong leading-coefficient exponent in `(PRI4)`, a zero
  of `D_1` outside the contact pushforward, or a quartic quotient not
  proportional to `S_B^2` or `S_1S_2` in its stated arm.
- **Nonclaim:** neither root arm is excluded.
