# Claim contract

- **claim:** one explicit pinned M31 quotient-prefix fiber has rooted degree
  at least `1237` at deficiency `192`, refuting the uniform band cap `1233`.
- **quantifier:** existential target and anchor on the specified support-level
  auxiliary profile.
- **unit:** canonical `479`-supports sharing a depth-`32` locator prefix.
- **dependencies:** finite-field arithmetic and the printed class exchanges;
  no unmerged theorem is assumed.
- **falsifier:** a reconstructed support leaves `Q'`, has the wrong size or
  deficiency, duplicates another support, or has a different locator prefix.
- **nonclaims:** no replacement upper bound, received-word realization,
  first-match survival, row payment, endpoint movement, or Prize-row result.
