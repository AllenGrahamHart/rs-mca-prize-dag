# Audit

The cancellation theorem is only applied after removing the at most one
zero-scalar slope. The pole set is the common zero set of `Q,A,B` on the
evaluation domain, not all roots of `Q`. Exact monic locators are essential:
an arbitrary polynomial merely vanishing on a support need not divide to the
locator of the punctured support.

The imported theorem itself warns that the punctured support may cease to be
MCA-bad. This node retains that warning as an open boundary.
