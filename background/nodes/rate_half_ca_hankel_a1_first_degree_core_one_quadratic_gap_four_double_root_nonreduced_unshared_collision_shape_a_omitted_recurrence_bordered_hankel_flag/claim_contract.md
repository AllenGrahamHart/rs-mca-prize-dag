# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the shape-A omitted recurrence defects are exact replacement
  minors and square roots of bordered source-Hankel determinants; away from
  the known `g_off` regular-factor roots, the nested flag is precisely a
  rank-stagnation locus.
- **Inputs:** the core-one middle adjugate, source moments, dual-MDS biform,
  Pade regular factor, heavy-row factorization, and omitted-recurrence flag.
- **Currency:** polynomial identities over the base field, monic gcds, and
  ranks after specialization at regular off-line slopes.
- **Outputs:** `(BHF3)--(BHF12)`.
- **Nonclaims:** no bound on `deg C_r^pad` or `deg C_r^reg`; no shape-A or
  Lane-T payment.
- **Falsifier:** a replacement minor, bordered determinant, source subset
  sum, or regular-slope rank test differing from the stated recurrence
  defect.
