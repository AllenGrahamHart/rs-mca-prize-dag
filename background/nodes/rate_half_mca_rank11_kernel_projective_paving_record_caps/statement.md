# Complete-chart projective-paving record caps

For each rank-eleven kernel corank `1<=d<=9`, complete shortening gives

```text
(n,K,m,s)=(1048576+d,d,67472+d,d).
```

The incident normal matroid has rank `d+1`, and every set of at most `d`
normals is independent.  Every record therefore owns at least

```text
(d+1) (m-1)_fall_d
```

ordered projective bases.  The resulting record caps for `d=1..9` are

```text
8147918,
84416263,
983902549,
12232092309,
158406193634,
2109949210211,
28689347099870,
396280526311830,
5542092977392141.
```

These caps apply to the displayed completely shortened charts.  They are
not asserted uniformly for charts with fewer global zero normals.
