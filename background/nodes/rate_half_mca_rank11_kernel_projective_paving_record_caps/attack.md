# Attack

The main failure mode is silently applying a completely shortened cap to a
chart with fewer global zero normals.  This node forbids that promotion
explicitly.  Its consumer must retain the integer common-zero deficit and
prove a separate envelope.

Other falsifiers are a proper `r<d` normal span containing more than `r`
incident normals, loss of full incident rank, a failure of the abstract
basis floor, or an independent affine-hyperplane tuple owning two parameter
points.
