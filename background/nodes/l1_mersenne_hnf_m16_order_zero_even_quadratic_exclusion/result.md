# Result

with multiple repeated colors would force the odd and even parts of `P_s` to
have a gcd of degree at least two. The two first-subresultant coefficients
can vanish together only at explicit prime-field values of `s`, contradicting
the HNF non-prime-field condition. Colored Frobenius degree two is now closed
on all five official `m=8,16`, `h=m-1` rows.
