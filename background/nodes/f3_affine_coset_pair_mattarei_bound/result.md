# Result

Mattarei's arbitrary-coefficient Fermat-curve Corollary gives the exact
official-row pointwise bound

```text
#{x:L_1(x),L_2(x) in K}<3*2^(-2/3)|K|^(2/3).
```

It applies to both DSP8 affine factors, including twisted quotient slopes
outside the subgroup. The `2^(5/3)` in-house floor is thereby bypassed, not
contradicted: it belongs to a different auxiliary-polynomial method.
