# Audit

1. The norm identity is projective; the endpoint equation is rescaled once
   before writing `G=U^2-WV^2`.
2. `U` and `V` use the same reciprocal sign. The endpoint sign is positive
   for both source signs.
3. The cubic resolvent is formed only after making the quartic monic over
   `K(W)` and recording the nonzero leading coefficient.
4. Complete resolvent splitting is used with irreducibility and
   separability in the converse direction.
5. A split resolvent is a coefficient gate, not an existence proof for the
   required source facets or outer self-correspondence.
