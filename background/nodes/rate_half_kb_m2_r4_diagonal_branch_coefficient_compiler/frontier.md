# Frontier

For the lifting branch, substitute the exact `K`, `eta`, and one-exchange
fiber factors into `U^2-WV^2` and solve separately in the eight- and seven-
dimensional reciprocal spaces. For the non-lifting branch, combine the
split resolvent with the genus-zero/genus-one ramification passports and
the same twelve fiber quartics. Retain irreducibility, denominator, and
outer-factor checks for every survivor.
