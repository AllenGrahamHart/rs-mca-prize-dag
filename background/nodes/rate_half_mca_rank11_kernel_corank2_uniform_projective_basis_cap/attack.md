# Attack

The proof does not promote the complete paving hypothesis.  At `t>=1` it
permits parallel classes of size `t+1` and pays them through the abstract
bounded-parallel basis floor.

A falsifier must exhibit a zero normal after all global zero coordinates
are deleted, a parallel class larger than `t+1`, loss of full incident rank,
a failure of the rank-three matroid theorem, a second turn in the cap ratio,
or an endpoint quotient above `84416263`.

The complete endpoint is tight for this argument, so no smaller uniform cap
is claimed.
