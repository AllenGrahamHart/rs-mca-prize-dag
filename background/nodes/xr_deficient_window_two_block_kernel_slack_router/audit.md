# Audit

- `G_d` can be larger than `D`; both sets contain the two blocks, and both
  upper bounds come from the primitive degree inequality.
- Exactly two selected slopes follows only when `sigma<r`. The general
  statement retains this hypothesis explicitly.
- The cofactor bound concerns every multiplier after division by the complete
  forced-root locator, not merely a chosen kernel basis.
- `dim K_d=0` is absent because this theorem starts in the deficient branch.
  The full-rank target has no primitive pair or forced-root locator to divide
  out.
- The result strengthens the boundary geometry but supplies no occupancy
  estimate; `PROVED` status does not promote its critical consumer.
