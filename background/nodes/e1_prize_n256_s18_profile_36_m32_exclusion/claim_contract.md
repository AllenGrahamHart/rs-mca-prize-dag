# Claim contract

## Imported facts

- Cofactor 32 forces multiplicity five in the six-singleton parity support.
- The energy-adaptive parent leaves `E=2,...,85`.
- Translation, odd Galois multiplication, and global sign preserve the
  collision question.
- The bounded-product extremum may be specialized at exact autocorrelation
  energy `E` and `L1` norm `L`.

## New theorem

An exact product certificate, a complete primitive multiplicity-five atlas,
two complete radius engines, direct replay of every low-energy audit survivor,
and rigorously audited fixed-point root intervals exhaust every cofactor-32
vector. Every norm is strictly below or above the prize interval, so the
cofactor-32 collision class is empty.

## Exclusions

1. There is no all-one-parity branch: over `F_2`, a polynomial in `x^2` is a
   square and has even multiplicity at one, whereas this branch has
   multiplicity five.
2. The radius syndrome is only a necessary filter. Every hit is followed by
   exact integer autocorrelation replay, and the audit directly reconstructs
   every low-energy survivor.
3. Product-live filtering is conservative in `L`; it can retain extra vectors
   but cannot discard a possible collision.
4. Long-double norm values are diagnostic only. Closure uses integer interval
   products from an Arb-audited root table.
5. This removes one cofactor, not the full profile or aggregate pair budget.
