# Audit - L1 official maximal split-value complement census

1. `u=n-hp` is the complement degree at actual value degree `h`; `s=n-mp`
   is reserved for maximal capacity.
2. The complement compiler applies for every `2<=h<=m`; only its
   maximal-capacity specialization is claimed polynomial on nine rows.
3. `m<p` is supplied by official arithmetic and is needed in the leading
   coefficient and Newton divisions.
4. `Q=0` is excluded before defining `j=p-deg Q`.
5. The first nonleading term of `G(P)` is from `P^h`; the next outer term is
   a full gap `p` below the leader.
6. The normalization `P(0)=0` is load-bearing for uniqueness from `C`.
7. The Vandermonde count is an upper bound on valid split complements, not a
   claim that every selected root set extends to one.
8. The denominator `binom(u,ell_h)` uses squarefreeness of `C` and disjoint
   families of determining root subsets; it is not a probabilistic factor.
9. For lower `h`, the exponent `u-d+p` can grow with `p` and is not silently
   called polynomial.
10. The nine small-remainder rows have `s<=16`; the other seven do not receive
   the `n^16` payment.
11. At the terminal depth, binomial divisibility is checked before using the
   derivative argument.
12. The derivative argument is used only after the atlas has reduced to
    `s=m`; no classification for proper divisors `s|m` is claimed.
13. A critical fiber `P-gamma` has at least `m+1` distinct roots because
    `deg gcd(P-gamma,P')<=deg P'=p-m-1`.
14. The characteristic-`p` integration step is safe because `deg G=m<p`.
15. The symmetry step is over the algebraic closure, where all `m`-th roots
    of unity are distinct since `m<p`.
16. The conclusion is `p=1 mod m`, contradicted by the exact atlas residue
    `p=-1 mod m`; it is not inferred from numerical sampling.
17. Lower split-value degrees and higher tail widths remain explicitly open.
