# Audit

The gate applies only to the order-zero hypergeometric chamber. It uses the
full cyclotomic divisibility from the dependency, not merely its differential
equation.

The reciprocal identity is a multiset identity and remains valid when two
distinct roots of `P_s` have the same `m`th power. The constant `C(s)` is
nonzero on a survivor because every root of `P_s` is nonzero.

The off-diagonal coefficient system is necessary, not sufficient. In
particular, setwise Frobenius-inversion of the `m`th-power roots does not by
itself recover the pointwise equations `x_i^(m(p+1))=1`.

Saturation by `t-s` is a closure test only when its unit-ideal certificate is
exactly verified after reduction modulo the official characteristic. A
nonunit saturation is not a split-pencil witness.
