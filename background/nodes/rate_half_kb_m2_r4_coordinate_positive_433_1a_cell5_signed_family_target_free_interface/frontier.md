# Frontier

Use `(KBSF-4)--(KBSF-7)` as the next cell-5 algebraic interface.  Compute a
common-curve standard basis before expanding the four cuts, reduce each cut
to normal form separately, and only then form a saturated ideal.  Do not
retry raw six-record, scalar-resultant, or expanded squared-sum bases.

The favorable outcome is a guarded unit ideal for either signed family,
which deletes every outside case in this fixed cell/sign row without the
267-case matching ledger.  A nonunit outcome must be classified and then
intersected with the second family or the missing-mate constraint.
