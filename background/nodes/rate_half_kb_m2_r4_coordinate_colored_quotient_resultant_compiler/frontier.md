# Frontier

For each of the two coordinate degree profiles, choose the two right
pole-graph vertices defining `c(W)` and solve `(KBCQ-4)` separately in the
positive and negative coefficient spaces. Preserve nonzero odd part, exact
bidegree, irreducibility, common-five facets, and outer-factor divisibility.
The equations are univariate in `W`; do not return to generic row or
endpoint biform enumeration.
