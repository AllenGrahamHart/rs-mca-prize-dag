# Proof

The active-label theorem excludes exactly

`A = {0,...,4} x {0,...,14}`,

which has 75 labels. The endpoint-role theorem excludes exactly

`E = {5,6} x {0,...,14}`,

which has 30 labels. The sets are disjoint and

`A union E = {0,...,6} x {0,...,14}`.

Both child nodes are `PROVED`, and the executable composition reruns their exact verifiers before checking the set identity. Therefore all 105 cell-11 labels are empty.
