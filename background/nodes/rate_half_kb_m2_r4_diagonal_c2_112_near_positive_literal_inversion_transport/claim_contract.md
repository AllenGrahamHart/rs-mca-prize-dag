| field | contract |
|---|---|
| input | the twelve literal positive source-star assignments in the affine near-aligned chart `w=1/c` |
| exact claim | two restricted involutions preserve reconstructed q-slice residuals, target quadratics, and radical named-open localizers |
| census | `144` oriented cells, `108` semantic cells, `42` transport orbits |

| residual | `30` explicit affine orbit representatives |
| proof engines | exact PR #1140 formula replay and independent generic `5 x 5 solve_right` reconstruction |
| nonclaim | no full quotient transport, projective-boundary coverage, near-negative coverage, or exclusion of a residual representative |

## Falsifier

Any failed residual, target, or radical-localizer identity; an omitted literal
assignment; an orbit-census mismatch; or use of this theorem to delete one of
the 30 residual cells without a separate exclusion certificate.
