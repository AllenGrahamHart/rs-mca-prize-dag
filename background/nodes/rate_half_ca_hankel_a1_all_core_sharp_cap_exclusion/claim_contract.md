# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every official `A=1` failure has positive Euclidean slope
  slack; `(A1C2)` holds for core one.
- **Dependencies:** the core-free, fixed-core, and four-contact pole
  exclusions.
- **Output:** retirement of every `ell=0` `A=1` route, including the old
  maximal-degree corrected-square frontier.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaim:** no positive-slack profile is excluded beyond the parent
  inequalities.
- **Falsifier:** an official integer degree where the relevant pole upper
  bound fails the strict contact inequality at `ell=0`.
- **Replay:** run this directory's verifiers under `tools/ramguard tiny --`.
