# Claim contract

## Inputs

- The active-defect router's exact punctured RS list.
- The proved affine-span Reed--Solomon list compiler.
- The official row parameters and the active-defect inequalities.

## Outputs

- A uniform maximization of the affine cap over all live `d,ell,e`.
- Exact payment through affine dimensions `9,9,8`.
- The necessary counterexample dimensions `10,10,9`.

## Exclusions

- No bound for higher-dimensional parameter families.
- No claim that the surviving target actually realizes the first unpaid cap.
- No use of ordinary Johnson, active-block incidence, or a shift-pair bound.
