# Audit

- The cube identity is used only where both `G_L` and `R_a` are local units.
  Rows where `R_a` vanishes are charged by the older one-copy theorem.
- The local ring need not be regular.  Cartier plus reduced implies the
  one-dimensional Cohen--Macaulay setting needed for
  `length(O/(s^3))=3 length(O/(s))`.
- Horizontal intersection length, not a transverse-point count, is the
  multiplicity of the specialized `X`-root.
- The squarefree minimal factor contributes either one copy of the root or
  none. A positive multiple of three therefore leaves at least two copies
  in the excess factor in either case (and at least three in the latter).
- `R_a` may have roots away from heavy rows.  Only its heavy roots define
  `E`; the smallest packet arithmetic proves that the required roots are in
  fact heavy.
- No changed pole divisor is identified with the original pole divisor.
