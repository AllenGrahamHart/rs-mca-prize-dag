# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the proved `V=38` endpoint;
- the proved sparse-L1 recurrence and signed-chord gate;
- the complete proved even-parity light atlases;
- the collision norm criterion.

## Output

Every `V=36` profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate lies in one of six profiles on one
of 1,321 exact affine templates.

## Nonclaims

No surviving vector is excluded here. No cubic cutoff, lower variance,
profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform allowance, row, or prize terminal
is claimed.

## Falsifier

A candidate with `L>12`, an eighth energy profile, a surviving profile with
more than six odd classes, or a light support outside the pinned two/six-odd
atlases refutes this router.
