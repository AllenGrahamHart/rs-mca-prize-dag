# Audit

- The omission count distinguishes a repeated `Q_min` root from a new
  simple root in the excess factor; it does not assume every supported root
  belongs to `Q_min`.
- Local cube valuations use intersection lengths and remain valid at a
  singular point of the reduced Cartier curve.
- The vertical multiplicity lower bounds already total the full fibre
  degree, so no separability assumption is used.
- `D` is squarefree here because `Delta` distinct supported rank losses
  exhaust its degree, not because the general adjugate theorem asserts
  squarefreeness.
- Nonvanishing of `N_F(U,V;x_*)` uses the global contact degree and the fact
  that the cube identity makes the section nonzero on every mixed component.
- The degree-two line bundle is recorded as a necessary condition, not
  declared impossible.
