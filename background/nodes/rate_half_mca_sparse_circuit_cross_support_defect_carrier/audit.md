# Audit

1. The source deletion is independent and its completion labels have private
   coordinates.
2. Their span has dimension exactly `q-s`, not merely at most that value.
3. At most `s` target labels are added modulo the source span.
4. Each added target label costs at most `d` carrier coordinates.
5. The final target circuit support is added separately in the Vandermonde
   union test.
6. Independence is invoked at union size at most `K`, including equality.
7. The incidence count deliberately overcounts all eleven-set extensions of
   each carrier support.
8. No received-word functional or support-dependent pairing is introduced.
