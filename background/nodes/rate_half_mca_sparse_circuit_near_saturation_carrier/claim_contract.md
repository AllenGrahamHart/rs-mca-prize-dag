# Claim contract

- **Claim:** in the no-`q`-completion branch, support sizes `2,3,4` obey
  the near-carrier/`q-2`-completion dichotomy in `statement.md`.
- **Inputs:** only the dimension-ten correction space, empty common zero
  set, and Vandermonde independence already used by the completion ladder.
- **Units:** rank-ten eleven-sets selecting one minimal circuit.
- **No classification:** no secant-variety or finite-field classification
  is assumed.
- **Boundary:** the proof deliberately stops at `c=4`; for `c=5`, the
  comparison union may have size `K+3`.
