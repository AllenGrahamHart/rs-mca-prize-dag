# Audit

## Claim boundary

This is a theorem under an explicit common-core premise. It does not infer
that an arbitrary rank-eleven family has such a core. The high-margin count
is separated from the low-margin shortened list, and the `2w` near charge is
added exactly once.

## Mathematical checks

1. Dividing differences by the locator of `J` gives an `[n-|J|,K-|J|]`
   generalized RS code; no claim is made about dividing the received word.
2. The residual agreement is `m-387-|J|=d+67085`.
3. The Johnson denominator is derived independently as a linear function of
   `d`; its sign changes between `4922` and `4923`.
4. The ordinary-list endpoint is `94943`, whose square is below the sextic
   field size, so the interleaving collapse applies.
5. The fixed-pair cap is maximized at deficiency one and is not replaced by
   a distinct-neighbor bound.

## Adversarial controls

Johnson endpoint, field degree, pair cap, high charge, total, and dependency
pin. The independent audit recomputes the endpoint and budget without using
