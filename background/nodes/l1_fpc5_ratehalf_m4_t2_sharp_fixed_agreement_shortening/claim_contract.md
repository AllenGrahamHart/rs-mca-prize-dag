# Claim contract

- **Input:** one fixed sharp rate-half `M=4,t=2` source and touched pair.
- **Output:** an injective shortening of exact contributors into
  `RS[C,2ell-1]` at exact agreement `3ell-2` on `|C|=5ell-5` points,
  plus the exact balanced-determinant specialization and fixed-gcd-owner
  bounds `(SH8)--(SH9)`.
- **Preserved:** contributor identity and exact core agreement/defect sets.
- **Forgotten filters:** cofactor congruence, primitivity after shortening,
  untouched-petal nonagreements, and first ownership.
- **Nonclaim:** a converse, a polynomial list-size theorem beyond the
  Johnson radius, or an aggregate bound over all realized gcd owners.
