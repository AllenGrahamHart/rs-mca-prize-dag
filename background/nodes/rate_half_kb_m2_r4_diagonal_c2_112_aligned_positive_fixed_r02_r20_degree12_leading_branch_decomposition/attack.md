# Attack surface

Do not retry raw `F_p(s,p)[x]` remainder/resultant computation or continue
the naive pseudo-remainder sequence. Both increase complexity without
isolating a small terminal.

The next valid routes are:

1. derive a source-level identity that cancels the high essential cores
   before `w=-U/V` substitution; or
2. split the explicit nonnamed leading curves and prove a low-degree
   parametrization or incompatibility on each branch.

The common degree-6 `R12` leading curve and the `s=0` branch are the smallest
first candidates. Every inverted factor must remain an explicit DAG leaf.
