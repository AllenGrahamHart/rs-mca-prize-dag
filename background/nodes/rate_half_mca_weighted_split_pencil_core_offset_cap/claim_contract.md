# Claim contract

The node proves `(SPO)` for integral owner weights and selected masses on a
family of distinct affine lines.  The additive `rP` is a uniform per-line
charge; in the rank-eleven application it prices `r` common-core coordinates
paired with each of the `P` selected petal coordinates.

The proof requires:

1. point weights at most `P-1` and total at most `S`;
2. exact selected petal mass `P` on every line;
3. distinct affine lines, so two owner points determine at most one selected
   line;
4. nonnegative integral `r`.

No sharpness claim is made.  The theorem does not itself supply a component
incidence lower bound or close a prize row.
