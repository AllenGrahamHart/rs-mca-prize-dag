# Audit

1. The regular corank is one less than the full specialized kernel
   dimension because the permanent primitive kernel line has already been
   removed.
2. The compressed minimal recurrence need not split on the original source.
   Collision is therefore the union `P_tau(x_*)L_tau(x_*)=0`; neither
   factor is silently declared nonzero.
3. Positive Smith exponents sum to four and are counted with multiplicity;
   the five rows of `(5)` are the complete integer partitions of four.
4. The first pairing bound is applied to image order two. After
   `kappa_2=0`, the exact vector equation has image order three, so the
   stronger second column may be applied without assuming cancellation in
   individual Smith coordinates.
5. For `[2,2]` and `[1,1,2]`, only the implication
   `kappa_2=0 => kappa_3=0` is proved. The second jet is allowed to be
   nonzero and no relation on `kappa_3` is claimed when it is.
6. The lower bound `r>=2` is a hypothesis of this router, not an output of
   the repaired separated corank-one theorem.
7. Diagonal germs explicitly realize the three surviving abstract
   behaviors. They are route fences, not source-valid counterexamples.
8. Odd residue characteristic follows from the smooth domain containing a
   subgroup of even order; it is also needed for symmetric congruence
   diagonalization.
