# Proof - L1 Mersenne-checkpoint cyclotomic normal form

Because `N` is divisible by `m`, one has `N^2=0 mod mN`. Hence

```text
(N-1)^r=(-1)^r+r(-1)^(r-1)N mod mN.                  (1)
```

For `k=qN+b`, direct multiplication by `N-1` gives `-qN` when `b=0`.
When `b>0`, rewrite

```text
(qN+b)(N-1)=(b-q)N-b=(b-q-1)N+(N-b),                (2)
```

which proves `(MCN2)`. Applying `(2)` twice gives `(q-2b,b)` modulo `m`.

For `b=0`, the Frobenius orbit of `(0,0)` is the singleton itself, proving
the first line of `(MCN3)`. Fix `b>0` and put `g_b=gcd(2b,m)`. Even powers
of Frobenius preserve `b` and move `q` by the subgroup generated by `2b`.
Thus the orbit meets the initial point `(0,b)` exactly when

```text
q=0 mod g_b.                                         (3)
```

The paired initial point has second coordinate `N-b`. Applying one
Frobenius step and then arbitrary even steps shows that its points over `b`
have

```text
q=b-1 mod g_b.                                       (4)
```

For `b=1` or `b=N-1`, only one of the two paired coordinates lies in the
initial interval, but `g_b=2` and the residues in `(3)--(4)` coincide.
Therefore the same formula holds at both endpoints, completing `(MCN3)`.

To count the complement, `b=0` contributes `m-1` frequencies. Every odd
`b` has `g_b=2`, the two displayed residue classes coincide, and exactly
`m/2` values of `q` remain. There are `N/2` odd values of `b`. For
`v=v_2(b)>=1`, put `g=2^min(v+1,A)`. The two residue classes are distinct
and the number of complementary `q` values is

```text
m-2m/g.                                              (5)
```

There are `N/2^(v+1)` values with exact valuation `v`. Equivalently, after
separating `1<=v<=A-2`, all nonzero multiples of `2^(A-1)` contribute the
terminal chamber. Thus

```text
|S^c|=mN/4
      +sum_(v=1)^(A-2) N/2^(v+1) (m-m/2^v)
      +(N/2^(A-1)-1)(m-2)+(m-1).                    (6)
```

Substitution of `m=4,8,16` gives `(MCN4)`.

Taking `q=0` in `(MCN3)` shows that every frequency `b=0,...,N-1` belongs
to `S`. Suppose `B` had `t<=N` nonzero coefficients at distinct exponents
`e_1,...,e_t`, with values `c_1,...,c_t`. Vanishing at frequencies
`0,...,t-1` gives

```text
sum_j c_j (zeta^e_j)^a=0,       0<=a<t.              (7)
```

The coefficient matrix is Vandermonde and the `zeta^e_j` are distinct, so
all `c_j` vanish, a contradiction. This proves `(MCN5)`. A balanced pair of
disjoint `p`-sets has exactly `2p=2N-2` nonzero coefficients, proving
`(MCN6)`.
