# Result

PROVED. In every saturated `c=2` source-line packet, an unramified forced
square fiber imposes four independent linear equations and cuts the
reciprocal source spaces from `8/7` to `4/3`; its three `U,V` coefficient
minors share the reciprocal source-orbit quadratic with exact linear
quotients. The ramified orbit is a genuine weaker branch: it imposes only
two equations and leaves dimensions `6/5`.
