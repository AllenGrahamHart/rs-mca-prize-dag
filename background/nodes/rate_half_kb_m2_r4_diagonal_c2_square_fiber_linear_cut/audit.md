# Audit

1. The square of a whole-fiber quartic does not by itself imply
   `U(T,w)` and `V(T,w)` are proportional. The proof uses the stronger
   parent fact that both reduced component stars equal `P_(J_1)`.
2. The proportionality argument requires an unramified fiber. The orbit
   `{0,infinity}` is retained and has only the rank-two condition on `U`.
3. Fixed points `w=+1,-1` are excluded by the proved fixed-point-free label
   involution, not by a genericity assumption.
4. Dimensions `4/3` and `6/5` are affine linear-space dimensions before
   exact-degree and nonzero-fiber open conditions. No existence is claimed.
5. The common reciprocal sign cancels in the minor identities; `(KBC2-5)`
   holds in both eigenspaces.
6. No part of the proof applies to the biquadratic source-cover branch or
   the exceptional unsaturated `(1,1,2)` orbit.
