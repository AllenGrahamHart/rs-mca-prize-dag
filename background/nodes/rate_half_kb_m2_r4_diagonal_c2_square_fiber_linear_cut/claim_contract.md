# Claim contract

- **Claim ID:** `rate_half_kb_m2_r4_diagonal_c2_square_fiber_linear_cut`
- **Input:** a source-line diagonal component in a `c=2` row carrying the
  forced square fiber of `(KBDM-8)` or a saturated case of `(KBDM-9)`.
- **Output:** the unramified rank-four linear cut, dimensions `4/3`, and
  minor factorization `(KBC2-2)--(KBC2-5)`, or the exact ramified rank-two
  cut and dimensions `6/5` in `(KBC2-6)`.
- **Deletion interface:** reject a proposed source-line packet that fails
  the applicable linear equations or dimension bound.
- **Non-output:** no deletion of the ramified orbit, a `c=2` row, branch,
  orientation, owner, payment, row, or Prize result.
