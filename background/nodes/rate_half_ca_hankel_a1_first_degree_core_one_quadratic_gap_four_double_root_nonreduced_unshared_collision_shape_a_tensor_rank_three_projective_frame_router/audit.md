# Audit

1. Minimal tensor rank makes both coefficient triples independent; otherwise
   the representation has rank at most two.
2. Projective-line preimages are counted with domain-row multiplicity. A
   degree-at-most-`n` nonzero polynomial has at most `n` distinct roots.
3. The fourth-row reserve is `R-3n=7`; no disjointness assumption among the
   three pair-lines is used.
4. General position ensures every circuit coefficient is nonzero and every
   three row polynomials span the complete rank-three parameter space.
5. A common root of three circuit rows forces the fourth root before the
   nonzero-fiber contradiction is applied.
6. Since triple overlaps are absent, the number of double incidences equals
   the sum of the six pair intersections without overcounting.
