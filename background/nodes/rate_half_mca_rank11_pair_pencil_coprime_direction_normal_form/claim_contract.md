# Claim contract

- **input:** at least 520 quotient pair types whose vectors `(1,a_p,b_p)`
  have rank at most two over `F(X)`;
- **ambient input:** their component differences lie in one `F`-space of
  dimension at most four;
- **output:** a fixed coprime direction `(U,V)` and distinct scalar
  polynomials `R_p` satisfying `(NF1)`--`(NF3)`;
- **preserved:** base-field linearity, exact official degree bounds, complete
  pair cores, and the quotient type population;
- **not claimed:** classification, enumeration, or payment of these scalar
  polynomial families.
