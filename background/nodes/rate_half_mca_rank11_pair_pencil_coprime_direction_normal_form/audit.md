# Audit

1. The factorization occurs in `F[X]`, not merely in `F(X)`.
2. Zero primitive components are covered by the coprime/unit convention.
3. Scalar-space dimension is inherited through an injective `F`-linear
   multiplication map; no field-extension normalization is used.
4. Bezout identifies scalar roots with codeword-pair agreement points; the
   received-pair core intersection is only asserted to be a subset.
5. The root floor is only inclusion--exclusion; no unproved census is used.
