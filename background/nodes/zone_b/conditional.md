# zone_b conditional proof

## Predicate node

- `e1_fullness`

## Claim

Every clean-anchor row with `|F_p(Q)|>=b_pair_min(K,B*)` has more than `B*`
distinct ambient E1 values and therefore a direct-value unsafe payload.

## Proof

`e1_fullness` proves exactly this finite image bound after the characteristic-
zero antipodal-rearrangement classes are reduced into the row's ambient slope
field. The canonical quotient locator realizes each distinct reduced value as
a bad slope at the candidate predecessor. The row packet must still pin the
field, quotient embedding, owner, and endpoint.

The current quotient orders are `256` and `512`; the older `{128,256}` label
mixed quotient orders with folded dimensions. Numerical fullness ratios and
`o(1)` collision language are evidence only and are not consumed here.
