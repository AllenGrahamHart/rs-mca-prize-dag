# Audit

The Haar skews are integer differences, not XOR bits. The events are defined
on the same binary vector and are generally dependent. Their marginal
probabilities all have denominator `2^n`; multiplying `m+1` marginals is why
the correlation ratio carries `2^(nm)` rather than `2^(n(m-1))`.

Primitive deletion applies only to the full joint event. Deleting the same
family from each marginal would define a different and invalid ratio.

The exhaustive verifier recomputes direct moments, every recursive band,
all marginal event counts, primitive ownership, and the probability ratio on
three small-field fixtures.
