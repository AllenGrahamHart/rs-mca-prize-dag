# Claim contract

## Input

One `(H_C)` tuple from the PROVED global-core rank-drop/high-complexity
router, on an arbitrary shortened KoalaBear row with full deviation rank ten.

## Output

The fixed core interpolant contributes at most 481 slopes, and one complete
projective correction ray contributes at most 70,227,214,729,216 correction
pairs. Their sum is strictly below the official slope budget.

## Nonclaim

The theorem does not bound a union of many projective correction rays or a
higher-dimensional correction space. It supplies the relative analogues of
the first two deployed spread-residual rungs only.
