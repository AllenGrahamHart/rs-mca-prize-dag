# Upstream crosswalk

No integrated upstream theorem was used. The result is phrased in the
prime-ideal and exact-cofactor language needed by the finite E1 collision
ledger. The circular-unit basis comes from published class-number and
unit-index theorems, not from an upstream branch. It is a candidate future
extension to the upstream split-pencil or primitive shift-pair arithmetic
only after a bounded sparse-associate count is obtained.
