   `ceil(C/(4m-2))` is sufficient but one unit stronger than necessary at
   the official congruence.
2. The type-1 cap is the worst-case value two at `a=7m-1`; using zero or one
   would change only the additive constant, not the leading `9m/4` spend.
3. `(FRC4)` is stated only for clean size-`rho` locators. The spend form
   `(FRC2)` is the counting contract of record.
4. The theorem corrects a route target, not a proved endpoint inequality.
   Critical status therefore remains unchanged.
5. Independent verifiers check the floor transition, closed form, official
   integers, and hostile off-by-one mutation.
