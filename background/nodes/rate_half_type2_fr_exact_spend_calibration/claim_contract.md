- **claim:** at the worst Round-31 support size `a=7m-1`, the exact least
  uniform outside spend that closes the printed type-2 capacity count is
  `floor((9m+1)m/(4m-1))+1`, equal to `9m/4+1` when `4|m`;
- **scope:** calibration of the `(AO1)` outside-incidence route, with the
  type-1 cap two and clean-locator intersection translation stated
  separately;
- **consumer use:** replace the insufficient `2m+2` / `~2m` frontier by the
  exact `9m/4+1` spend target before attempting algebraic `(FR)`;
- **not claimed:** that the calibrated spend holds, that every type-2 slope
  is clean, or that no different use of algebraic structure can close the
  endpoint;
- **falsifier:** failure of `(FRC1)`, a smaller integer spend satisfying the
  floor inequality, or disagreement with the official exact integers.
