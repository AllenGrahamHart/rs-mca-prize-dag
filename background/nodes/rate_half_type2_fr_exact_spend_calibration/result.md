# Result

The Round-31 phrase “spend about `2m`” was under-calibrated after `NEWCAP`
moved the worst support size to `7m-1`. The exact spend required by the
printed capacity ledger is `9m/4+1` on the official row, equivalently a clean
intersection cap `7m/4-2`. The old `2m+2` proposal leaves a `9/8` residual.

The algebraic type-2 problem is therefore harder by an additional `m/4-1`
outside roots per clean slope than the prior frontier stated.
