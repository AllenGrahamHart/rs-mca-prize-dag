# Audit

1. Reducedness gives six distinct residues modulo `256`; it does not imply
   mixed parity. The all-one-parity stratum is retained by exact conductor
   descent.
2. Congruences are modulo `256`, while the roots have order `512`. The
   resulting wrap sign is absorbed into the signed coefficient and is not
   dropped.
3. Maximal descent is taken before the parity involution is called free.
   Without this step, all-even and all-odd supports are fixed strata.
4. A Heron factor is a product of four signed sums, not one independent
   equation. In the mixed even sector its base-field norm contains two full
   signed rational norms.
5. The odd-sector factor `C^2-dD^2` combines two conjugate Heron factors and
   contains four full signed rational norms.
6. Intersecting blocks from different pairings can isolate one conjugacy
   orbit combinatorially, but the integer gcd still contains that orbit's
   complete norm. Combinatorial isolation is not arithmetic compression.
7. The theorem is a route fence. It neither proves the target nor authorizes
   a larger factor census.
