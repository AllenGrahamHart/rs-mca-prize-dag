# Audit

1. The first attempted degree argument incorrectly bounded all preimages of
   a singular image point by the cover degree. The repaired theorem factors
   through normalization and explicitly counts branches.
2. Common finite basepoints are excluded by simple split rows and nonzero
   `Gamma` fibers; infinity is excluded by exact row degree.
3. Two empty actual columns are impossible by the exact total deficit, but
   one is retained through `z in {0,1}`.
4. Squarefreeness of an incident row makes each active normalization fiber
   reduced and complete inside `Gamma`; this is what proves divisibility.
5. The two possible active-set sizes are checked separately through gcds
   with `3e` and `3e-1`.
6. Birationality identifies normalization points over a plane singularity
   with geometric branches. No smooth-point fiber bound is used.
7. General position makes the six frame vertices distinct.
8. The delta floors use only the standard pairwise branch-intersection
   lower bound and a convex integer minimization.
9. The result routes rank three but does not exclude it.
