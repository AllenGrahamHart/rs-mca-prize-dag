# Audit

- The nested cut is only a necessary condition; this is sufficient for an
  exclusion, and every finite survivor is replayed against the omitted
  colored pair and the original target guards.
- The source missing-product signs are pinned separately:
  `de=m, eta=1` for `xi=0`, and `de=-m, eta=-1` for `xi=2`.
- All six rational inversions, the base-cubic leading coefficient, and both
  parts of the final norm are included in the exceptional-root union.
- Exceptional elimination roots are directly solved.  They are not called
  route boundaries unless an original route guard actually vanishes.
- The 32 terminal boundary records all have `f=0` and only the
  `nonzero_5` failure; they are invalid outside targets.
- The `xi=1` transport is checked at matching index 3 by residual product
  and squared-sum signatures.
- The theorem counts 32 computed and 16 transported raw atlas cases.  Lifted
  roots and source points are not recounted as raw cases.
