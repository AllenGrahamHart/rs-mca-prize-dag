# Audit

1. Divisors are compared on the normalization; all multiplicities are
   valuation multiplicities.
2. The common divisor `K` is supported exactly on grid incidences because
   `G` vanishes only over `D` and `H` only over the supported slopes.
3. Formula `(3)` is exact, not an upper bound: pole length removes exactly
   `p` from the total supported-fibre degree.
4. The cases `d=0`, `d=1` with distinct exceptional fibres, and `d=1` with
   coincident fibres are all covered. The normalized pole degree may be zero
   even when `d=1`; both `p=0,1` occur in the last calculation.
5. Complete-fibre multiplicities are checked by total degree before divisor
   support is compared.
6. The contradiction margin is exactly one:
   required excess `2-p` versus available excess `1-p`.
