# Frontier

The exact live question is:

```text
For every binding prize rate-1/8 row, is chi(G_p(33))<=3?
```

The known `(3,4)` variance campaign removes one family of `S=16` edges but
does not control the other `S=16` splits or any even square mass through 66.
The new target permits many edges and many collisions; it asks only for a
global three-coloring of the low-mass collision graph.

Best proof interfaces:

- a color-changing class invariant modulo three;
- maximum-degree two from per-class norm-divisor incidence;
- a structural decomposition of every low-mass kernel relation into three
  bipartite/independent types.

Best falsifier: one exact admissible row and a four-chromatic certified
subgraph. This should be attempted before further variance descent.
