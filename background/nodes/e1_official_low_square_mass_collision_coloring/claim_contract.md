# Claim contract

## Claim

The six row-uniform chromatic bounds printed in `statement.md`.

## Guards

1. Vertices are characteristic-zero antipodal-rearrangement classes reduced
   into the actual prime field of the row.
2. Edges require both equal reduced E1 value and `0<S<=2ell`.
3. The quotient root, field, clean predecessor, and row envelope are pinned.
4. A finite selected-field coloring does not prove a row-family entry.
5. The table is row-specific; only the prize rate-`1/8` entry asks for three
   colors.

## Falsifier

The exact graph certificate described in `statement.md`, or any row omitted
by a purported uniform coloring theorem.
