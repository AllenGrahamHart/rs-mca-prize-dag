# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | In either negative two-loop skeleton, the common-`K` product rows have rank exactly three and, after their kernel is retained, two denominator-free welds `(KBNW-2)` are equivalent to all five common-`K` sum equations. |
| deletion interface | A product-rank failure, leading-support failure, or nonzero weld residual deletes the signed two-loop packet. |
| assumptions | The complete negative Vieta product map, its injectivity and leading support, and the proved two-loop factor pin `A_1=cR`. |
| nonclaim | Either skeleton is empty; complement-fiber completion; positive parity; an owner/payment; a row; or either Prize theorem. |
