1. The product matrix has rank exactly three because both the five labels
   and five products are distinct; rank at most three alone is insufficient.
2. The kernel ordering in `(KBNW-1)` is `(D_0,D_1,N_0,N_1)`.
3. Every division in the proof is protected by distinct labels, injective
   products, and `D(s)!=0`.
4. The two scalar welds are equivalent to the earlier `3 x 3` q determinant
   only after the product-map kernel and leading support are retained.
5. Reversing an edge-orbit orientation can change `q_s`; no sign is
   quotient out by the theorem.
6. The theorem reconstructs only the five common-fiber equations.  The
   other seven fibers and all complete-packet conditions remain live.
