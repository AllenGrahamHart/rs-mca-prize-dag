# Claim contract

- **Claim:** a common factor-product cover with factor dimension at most two
  and residual dimension at most three cannot contain the complete
  nontransverse residue of an unsafe official rank-eleven line.
- **Quantifiers:** every family of all promoted nontransverse containers at
  the exact `tau=1549`, `h=42451` interface.
- **Algebraic premise:** each container is contained in `g_iB` for one fixed
  `B` and some `g_i` in one fixed `P`; no splitness, coprimality, or distinct
  factor is needed for the exclusion.
- **Synchronization addendum:** equality `W_i=g_iB` and projectively distinct
  `g_i` are load-bearing only for the common-locus floor `(PC3)`.
- **Falsifier:** a product span of dimension greater than
  `dim(P)dim(B)`, an unsafe low-span survivor, or a family satisfying the
  synchronization hypotheses with fixed locus below `(PC3)`.
- **Nonclaim:** no general split-pencil census, no assertion that every rich
  family has product form, and no closure of rank eleven.
