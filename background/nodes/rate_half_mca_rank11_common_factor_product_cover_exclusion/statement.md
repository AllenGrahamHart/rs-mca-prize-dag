# Common-factor product-cover exclusion

- **status:** PROVED
- **row:** KoalaBear MCA, post-near affine error rank eleven
- **consumer:** the collective rich-container terminal

Let `U_i` be all nontransverse represented row spaces and `W_i` their
canonically promoted dimension-two or dimension-three rich containers at
`(tau,h)=(1549,42451)`. Suppose there are polynomial subspaces `P,B` with

```text
dim(P)<=2,  dim(B)<=3,
```

and, for every `i`, a nonzero `g_i in P` such that

```text
U_i <= W_i <= g_i B.                                      (PC1)
```

Then

```text
sum_i U_i <= span(PB),   dim span(PB)<=6.                 (PC2)
```

The rank-eleven low-span residual payment proves that every unsafe survivor
has `dim(sum_i U_i)>=7`. Consequently no unsafe survivor can satisfy `(PC1)`.
In particular, a common two-dimensional factor pencil times one fixed
three-dimensional residual space is a paid branch, even when it covers all
`8406` or more promoted containers.

There is also an exact synchronization statement. Suppose `dim(P)=2`,
`W_i=g_iB`, and `g_1,...,g_b` are projectively distinct pencil members. If
each `W_i` vanishes on a selected set `J_i` of at least `H` coordinates inside
an anchor-good universe of size at most `m`, then the fixed locus

```text
C=Z(B) union Z(P)
```

inside that universe satisfies

```text
|C| >= ceil((bH-m)/(b-1)).                                (PC3)
```

At `b=8406`, `H=42452`, and `m=1116048`, this gives `|C|>=42325`.
