# Audit

1. The cover must contain every nontransverse `U_i`; covering only a selected
   subfamily does not trigger the exclusion.
2. Containment `W_i<=g_iB` is sufficient for the dimension-six payment.
   Equality and distinct pencil parameters are not imported into that claim.
3. The synchronization floor uses equality because a proper subspace of
   `g_iB` may have extra zeros unrelated to `g_i` or the fixed `B`.
4. The incidence universe has size at most `m`, not exactly `m`; replacing
   its actual size by `m` weakens the common-locus floor safely.
5. The word "product cover" is deliberately not equated with the upstream
   base-field-normalized split-pencil target.
6. The verifier checks the official integer floor and finite-field product
   span fixtures. The proof of the general dimension bound is analytic.
