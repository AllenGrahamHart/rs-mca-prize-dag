# Audit

The inequality is for the complete contributor family only after summing all
`binom(b,u)` fixed required-background charts. A first-`R` assignment is not
needed for the upper bound, and no chart is counted as if the choices of `R`
were free.

The Johnson denominator uses the fixed-chart cap `d-ell`, not the weaker
unfixed cap `r`. Its strict positivity is load-bearing.

The bounded-polarity corollary quantifies over a fixed absolute `C`. It does
not permit choosing `C` as a growing function of the official row.

Equation `(FP5)` explains why this branch survives the exact prefilter: the
joint denominator may be nonpositive solely because of the background-choice
entropy term `Nu(b-u)`.
