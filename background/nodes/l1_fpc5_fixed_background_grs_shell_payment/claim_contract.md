# Claim contract

- **Input:** one FPC5 cell with `0<=u<=b`, its fixed-required-background
  Hankel charts, and their exact GRS syndrome-shell representation.
- **Output:** the exact binomial-times-singleton or binomial-times-Johnson
  payment and its bounded-background-polarity corollary.
- **Preserved:** source, touched set, degree, required background set,
  primitive locator, all guards, and incidence multiplicity.
- **Nonclaim:** deletion of the binomial factor, middle-polarity payment,
  nonpositive fixed Johnson payment, source aggregation, or chronology.
