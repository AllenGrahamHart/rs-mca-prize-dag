# Claim contract

- **Proposition:** the complement incidence graph is connected, forcing the
  scalar-weld matrix to have nullity zero or one; a nonzero kernel is
  automatically full-support.
- **Quantifier:** both proved pair-boundary profiles and every guaranteed
  selected zero-excess fiber set.
- **Dependency:** the paired split-biform scalar-weld gate.
- **Falsifier:** a disconnected complement graph, a weld kernel of dimension
  at least two, or a nonzero weld-kernel vector with a zero coordinate.
- **Nonclaim:** no proof that the weld matrix has full rank.
