# Claim contract

- **Input:** a set of exact shifted-Johnson FPC5 cells at fixed source scale
  and touched count, their proved fixed-cell caps, and the canonical
  first-layout domination theorem.
- **Output:** the global source-layout and touched-set aggregate `(FL2)`.
- **Preserved:** exact source scale, touched count, touched subset, defect
  degree, background incidence, field, radius, and earlier global owners.
- **Nonclaim:** payment of other scales/profiles, lower field slices, or the
  six rate-`1/16` cells rejected by the exact aggregate budget.
