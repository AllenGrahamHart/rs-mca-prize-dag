# Audit

1. The domain-coordinate change is performed projectively and avoids every
   named finite divisor. It preserves bidegrees, apolarity, incidence, and
   absolute irreducibility; the cyclic locator is used before normalization,
   not silently assumed afterward.
2. The two leading coefficients `a(z)=[X^rho]Q` and
   `q_inf(X)=[z^m]Q` are different objects.
3. The repeated case `S|A_0` is retained. The two local resultants each take
   one copy, giving the correct total double order.
4. Equality in `(3)`, rather than a root-set argument, proves that no
   intersection is lost at domain infinity.
5. `O_C(N,-T)` has degree one as a line bundle. The node does not infer that
   its complete linear system has dimension one or defines a morphism.
