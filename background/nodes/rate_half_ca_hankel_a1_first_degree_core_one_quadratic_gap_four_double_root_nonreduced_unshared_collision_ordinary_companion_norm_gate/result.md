# Result

Every low-degree companion is now controlled by one bounded polynomial:
degree at most six for a `(2,3)` factor and at most twelve for a `(4,6)`
factor. The polynomial is explicitly reconstructed from tangent products
and is nonzero at all classified rows. Whether it remains nonzero at the
heavy row after removing the forced simple or double factor is open.

This does not close shapes B--D. It identifies the next finite obstruction:
derive one additional source or correction value of `E_Q`, or prove that
its tangent-product interpolation is incompatible with the companion's
two-directional splitting.
