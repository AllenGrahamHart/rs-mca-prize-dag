# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | Every injective geometric root of the six `(4,4,2)` product rows has exactly two of eight cross-edge orientation triples satisfying both common-`K` q welds, and either reconstructs all five common-`K` Vieta rows. |
| deletion interface | None at common `K`; the theorem routes every injective product root to source-facet orientation and seven-fiber completion. |
| assumptions | Odd characteristic, the two label identities, an injective product-row root with leading support, and independent deck assignment at this common-fiber interface. |
| nonclaim | The chosen orientations extend to a complete source facet; the other seven fibers; paired-product compatibility; deployed descent; skeleton/orientation deletion; an owner/payment; a row; or either Prize theorem. |
