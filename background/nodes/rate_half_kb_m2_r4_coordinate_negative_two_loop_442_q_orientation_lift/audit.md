# Audit

1. Edge-orbit orientation is not the harmless choice of representative used
   to define `q`; it is the two possible assignments of conjugate edges to
   the two fixed source lifts.
2. The `AB` lift oriented by endpoint `A` and by endpoint `B` can differ by
   a fixed sign determined by the `AB` product type.
3. Squaring is reversed only because the field has odd characteristic and
   orbit reversal realizes the missing sign.
4. The two welds use different loop anchors but both compare the same
   invariant values `q_sB_2(s)/R(s)`.
5. Independent orientation at the common-fiber interface is not a claim
   that either choice extends through all source-facet records.
6. This node proves survival, not realizability of a full component.
