# Audit

1. Heavy weights remain integral and individually bounded by `P-1`.
2. Light mass may be reused across different heavy owners, exactly as in
   the parent theorem; only lines through one fixed owner are disjoint.
3. The line-count floor is relaxed upward to `ell/(P-s)`, never downward.
4. Balanced and two-heavy collision terms are retained unchanged.
5. Every honest core offset `9<=j<=21` is checked.
6. The eight-petal extremizer shows that the new clean cap itself should
   not be lowered without additional ancestry.
