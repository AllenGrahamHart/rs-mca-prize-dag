# Frontier

Retain the fixed support-wise first-owner predicates and bound either the
distinct added-locator projection or the distinct removed-locator projection
of the `d=1` incidence.  A bound of

```text
floor((16N^3-1)/1024)
```

on one projection would pay this stratum by the proved codegree theorem.
This does not address `d>=2`, and counting possible linear `H` remains invalid
because fixed `H` is not injective.
