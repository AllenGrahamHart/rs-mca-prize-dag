# Claim contract

- **Proved:** every fixed-`P` or fixed-`Q` fiber of the `d=1` translated
  complementary-divisor incidence has the Johnson bounds `(PC-2)--(PC-3)`.
- **Proved official specialization:** `t_XR>=2^31`, hence every official
  `d=1` projection codegree is at most `1024`.
- **Object:** actual fixed-base linear-difference records, before or after
  support-wise first-owner deletion.
- **Transport:** deleting records can only decrease projection codegrees.
- **Nonclaims:** a bound on the number of distinct projected `P` or `Q`
  locators, the total `d=1` population, any `d>=2` stratum, a quotient-row
  numerical bound, or X4/SP/LIST closure.
