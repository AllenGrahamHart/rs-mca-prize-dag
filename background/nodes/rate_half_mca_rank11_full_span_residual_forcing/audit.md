   nontransverse pair types, not once per rich container.
2. Common-support interleaving applies after translating both affine
   coordinates by the fixed anchor; the same linear code `V_nt` remains in
   both coordinates.
3. The slope multiplier `n-A` is retained after pair-type counting.
4. Population floors use the least unsafe cardinality `B*+1` and the larger
   dimension-three promoted-container cap.
5. `dim(V_nt)>=10` becomes equality only because the inherited correction
   ambient `C'` has dimension at most ten.
6. The dimension-ten wall is a failure of this formula, not a counterexample
   to the desired row bound.

   reconstructs binomials by direct product quotients.
