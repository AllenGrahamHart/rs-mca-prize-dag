# Audit

The primary verifier checks the source hash, row parameters, Vandermonde
rank consequences, full-density marked demand, all three split-pencil cap
terms, the raw rational sign, exact gap, and hostile contract mutations.

The independent audit recomputes the demand by the original `55*C(m',11)`
marking identity, checks the equivalent `C(m',9)C(m'-9,2)` expression,
replays the split-pencil specialization independently, and scans small
degree/domain pairs for the claimed evaluation-rank rule.

All arithmetic is exact and constant-memory under the `tiny` RAMguard
profile. Modal is unnecessary.
