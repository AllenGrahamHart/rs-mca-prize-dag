# Claim contract

- **Claim:** pair union `rho+3` has exact coefficient-row rank two and the
  weighted affine form `(QR23)`; its split row forms are injectively indexed
  by the difference points.
- **Inputs:** the complete coefficient-chain Vandermonde equations, the
  sharp-pair exclusion, exact light-row saturation, padded-heavy support
  scope, and the one-third center-line cap.
- **Outputs:** explicit forms `A+xB`, no clones, pairwise-disjoint residual
  root sets, common-gcd lower bound `(QR25)`, and center ownership `(QR26)`.
- **Falsifier:** a minimum pair with rank one, two distinct difference points
  giving proportional row forms, a shared residual root outside `gcd(A,B)`,
  or a common-gcd root whose center is off the endpoint pencil.
- **Nonclaims:** no upper bound strong enough to contradict `(QR25)`, no
  identification of the reverse-oriented gcd, and no packet closure.
