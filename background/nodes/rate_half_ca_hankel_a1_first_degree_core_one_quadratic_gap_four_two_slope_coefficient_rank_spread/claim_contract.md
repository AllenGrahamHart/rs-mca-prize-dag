# Claim contract

- **Claim:** symmetry supplies `r_alpha+1` coefficient-chain constraints on
  `S_beta\S_alpha`, forcing pair union at least `rho+2`, coefficient rank at
  most union excess minus one, the line cap `(QRS7)`, and the expander count
  `(QRS8)` in both `u=4` root patterns.
- **Currency:** the symmetric middle Hankel coefficient chain, exact unique
  error supports, contracted nonzero RS sources, column-farness, and minimum
  distance.
- **Dependencies:** the marked source frame and both quadratic center-spread
  theorems.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** no point separation and no packet exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a supported pair with union at most `rho+1`, a coefficient
  rank exceeding `(QRS5)`, or a center line violating `(QRS7)`.
- **Next gate:** test the exact locator degree sequences against the new
  half-size line cap and classify rank-one/rank-two fibres of the residual
  coefficient map.
