`K'=46..53`.  It retains the exact all-core chart, every kernel corank, the
55-shadow ledger, and sharp isolated demand.

bound.  Pareto compression removes only vectors componentwise bounded by a
retained vector and is independently replayed.

The node closes rank-nine component targets only.  It does not close `K'=54`,
rank eight, rank eleven, KoalaBear, or either prize problem.
