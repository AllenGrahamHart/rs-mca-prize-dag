# Audit

1. The source slope is a supported slope outside the row-root union, which
   sharpens `T` to `T-1` in `(OGC1)`.
2. The endpoint-exchange identity is asserted only when both endpoint
   deficits vanish; then the full endpoint locators equal their actual error
   supports.
3. A third center on the endpoint pencil contains all of `X` and `Y` because
   those residual coordinates vanish at exactly one of the two endpoints.
4. The six-set count removes each oriented gcd first. Residual roots within
   one orientation are disjoint by the affine pencil normal form; equation
   `(4)` proves cross-orientation disjointness.
5. `G_X union G_Y` has `g+1`, not `2g`, roots: the `g-1` nonendpoint roots
   are shared and the two endpoint roots differ.
