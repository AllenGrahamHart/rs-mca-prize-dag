# Claim contract

## Input

The proved non-dense record floor and the two-lane component-incidence floor
from `rate_half_mca_rank11_dense_locator_component_incidence_dichotomy`.

## Output

One lane, one fixed nine-coordinate subset, and at least 2578110 distinct
records, each having an eleven-coordinate component incidence in that lane
which extends the fixed subset.

## Nonclaim

The theorem does not identify one irreducible component, affine owner, owner
plane, or chronology owner. It does not assert that the selected records are
a fixed fraction of the complete line and does not pay either lane.
