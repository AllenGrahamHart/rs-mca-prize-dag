# Audit

The quotient relation (DQR2) uses the inherited `q!=0` saturation. The
conic remainder replaces the conic only while `P_4=0` is retained.

The role remainder uses `E_6=0` to identify `(R_D,S_D)=D(R,S)`. On the
doubly singular locus this equation is recovered from `P_4` and the conic
by the dependency; omitting either would make (DQR7) an invalid transport.

Each of the 21 role quadratics is an alternative packet with its own
`(c_2,c_1,c_0)`. They must not be imposed simultaneously. Irreducibility
ensures `c_0!=0`, but does not make either quotient-linear coefficient
nonzero.

The `N_1=U_1=0` chart retains `N_0=U_0=P_4=0`; it is not discarded as a
proportionality accident. Equation counts do not prove emptiness, and every
retained point still owes all arithmetic lift filters.
