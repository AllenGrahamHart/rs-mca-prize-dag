# Claim contract

- **Claim:** exact arithmetic obstruction to serially repeating PR `#1173`'s
  ordered-basis count with independent affine-container charges.
- **Quantifiers:** the official KoalaBear constants, `tau=1547`, dimensions
  `1->2` and `2->3`, and every positive integer gap allocation.
- **Dependencies:** the imported PR `#1173` interface, its PR `#1171`
  rank-one cap, the ordinary affine-span cap, and the proved sub-square
  common-support interleaving collapse.
- **Falsifier:** a shared first-rung threshold above `42452` within the stated
  envelope, or a positive two-rung gap allocation with charge no larger than
  `206105684094104220`.
- **Nonclaim:** no impossibility for coupled, synchronized, Wronskian, or
  chronology-owner routes.
