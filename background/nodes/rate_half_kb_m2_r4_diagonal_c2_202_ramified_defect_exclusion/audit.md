# Audit

1. Both ramified fibers are used only in the source-line branch, where
   `tau(W)=1/W` exchanges `0` and infinity. No such assertion is made in
   the biquadratic source-cover branch.
2. The two doubled square-root vertices are distinct: their label supports
   lie in disjoint sets `J` and `I`.
3. Once the two branch values are occupied, the four `K_0` labels are
   unramified because all twelve source labels are distinct.
4. A reduced `J_0` star is one of six unordered edges. The count is not
   `4^2`, and loops are forbidden by reducedness.
5. The lower bound two for eight units on six vertices is attained, so no
   stronger collision claim is hidden.
6. The proof uses the universal complete-source defect budget, not the
   coordinate subgroup's stronger star-equivariance conclusions.
