# Proof

The projective-chart parent proves that the all-infinity branch is represented
exactly by the two leading-coefficient equations for each matching resultant.
The chart compiler appends those six equations, `q3`, and `q7` to the pinned
21-polynomial common basis. Singular computes an initial dimension-three
basis of size 54. Exact ideal saturation remains dimension three through the
first five guards and becomes unit at guard 5, `b+1`. Since `b=-1` is excluded
by distinctness from the normalized target coordinate `a=1`, the admissible
chart is empty. QED.
