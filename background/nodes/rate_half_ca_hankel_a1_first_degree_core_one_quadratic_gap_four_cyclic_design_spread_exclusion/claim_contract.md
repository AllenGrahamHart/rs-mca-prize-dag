# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the explicit Cycle-107 cyclic design has a pair with exactly
  `e+3` expanding thirds and therefore violates the proved quadratic spread.
- **Currency:** cyclic interval arithmetic, the exact light multiplicities,
  and the Cycle-116 expander lower bound.
- **Dependencies:** the abstract cyclic construction and the two-slope
  coefficient-rank spread.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** no exclusion of arbitrary designs with the same degrees.
- **Falsifier:** every deficient adjacent pair having more than `e+3`
  expanding thirds, or the spread requirement being at most `e+3`.
- **Next gate:** classify arbitrary exact-degree designs satisfying the
  linear spread, or use the coefficient-map fibres directly.
