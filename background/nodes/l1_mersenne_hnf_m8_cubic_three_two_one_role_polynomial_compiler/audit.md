# Audit

The quotient by `(lambda-1)^7` removes exactly the forbidden choices
`gamma=beta`. It does not remove `lambda=0`; that value is absent already
because `C(1)=8` is nonzero in every official characteristic. Retain the
explicit saturation by `lambda*(lambda-1)` in implementations.

The degree-42 polynomial counts ordered normalized triples with
multiplicity. Squarefree reduction may merge equal role ratios and must be
performed after reduction to the working characteristic if exact factor
counts are used.

This compiler replaces role enumeration only. A root of `Lambda_321` is a
valid color ratio, not a common-quadratic HNF solution or an outer/inner
packet witness.
