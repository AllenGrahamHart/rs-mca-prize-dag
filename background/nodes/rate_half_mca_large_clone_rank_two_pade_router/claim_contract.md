# Claim contract

## Proved

- the exact Padé division obstruction `(RP1)`;
- its vanishing on every coordinate of the large clone;
- the degree wall `m+2d`;
- fixed affine-in-slope owner collapse above that wall;
- the exact locator alternative at equality;
- the two deployed integer walls.

## Not proved

- a degree-`d` normalization of the fixed owner;
- payment of denominator degrees `d+1` through `2d`;
- payment of the moving branch `m<=c<=m+2d`;
- complete clone, exception, spread, or row closure.

## Falsifier

A clone coordinate with nonzero `Omega`, a nonzero obstruction having more
roots than its degree, failure of `(RP3)` when `Omega=0`, or an incorrect
deployed wall.
