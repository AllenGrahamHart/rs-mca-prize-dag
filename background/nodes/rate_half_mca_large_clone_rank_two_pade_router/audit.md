# Audit

`Omega` is the remainder numerator for division in the slope variable. It
is not a resultant in `X`, and its zeros are clone owner incidences rather
than selected-support roots.

The theorem uses a strict `m+2d+1` root threshold. At equality a nonzero
multiple of `Lambda_C` is possible and is retained explicitly.

The fixed owner may need denominator degree `2d`; it is not silently fed to
the current degree-`d` large-owner theorem. Root-freeness is asserted only
after reduction and only when two root-free source owner points exist, as in
the order-32 use case.

The least common multiple formula assumes rank two, so `q_0,q_1` are both
nonzero. Rank one and zero-denominator degeneracies belong to the predecessor
router.
