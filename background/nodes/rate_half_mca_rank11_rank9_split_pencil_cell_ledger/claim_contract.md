# Claim contract

## Input

One rank-nine ten-coordinate cell from the PROVED component-star router,
with at least 45153 full-rank component extensions per assigned record.

## Output

The cell has an affine owner plane, record lines of slope-indexed directions,
an exact pairwise-balanced owner-block identity, a common root core plus
disjoint owner petals, and at most 45567658 assigned records.

## Nonclaim

The theorem does not count cells, identify cells across records, choose a
first chronology owner, or meet PR #1169's global owner target.
