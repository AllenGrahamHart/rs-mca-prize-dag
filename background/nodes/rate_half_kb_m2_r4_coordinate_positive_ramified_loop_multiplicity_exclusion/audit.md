1. The argument compares local divisor orders in the complete-source square; it does not replace ramification by two distinct points.
2. The two antipodal target rows each have order one when `B_1` is nonzero at the branch.  They are not manually doubled.
3. Every other target row is a unit because target transport is fixed-point-free and the six signed pairs have distinct squares.
4. Leading support supplies `A_2(w_0)!=0`; the target representative `a` is nonzero.
5. The infinity branch is checked in its reciprocal local coordinate after homogeneous reversal.
6. For two ramified loops, `B_1` may vanish at one branch; the other branch supplies the contradiction.
7. The theorem deletes loop counts two and three, not all positive parity.
8. The final cap is global across all twelve complete fibers, not only the five common fibers.
