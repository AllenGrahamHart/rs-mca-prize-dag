# Audit

1. Supported and correction contacts above one parameter are not assumed to
   be the same normalized point. Their base orders are therefore not added.
2. The correction branch alone proves `z^2|F_0`; the third factor is exposed
   as the explicit coefficient `kappa_tau`.
3. The recurrence error has order four, so reduction after division by
   `z^2` is exact and gives the rank-one leading direction.
4. `Q(t,x_*)/D_1` remains the polynomial scalar multiple of `S_B` even when
   `g_*` and `S_B` share a root.
5. The Smith `[3]` conclusion is asserted only when the third jet vanishes
   and the canonical divided row is divisible by the full determinant.
6. No implication is claimed on the nonvanishing branch beyond failure of
   the unmodified cubic quotient.
