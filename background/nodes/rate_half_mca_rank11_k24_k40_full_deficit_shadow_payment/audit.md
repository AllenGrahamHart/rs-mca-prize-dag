# Audit

1. All kernel coranks one through nine are retained wherever available.
2. Every core offset `9<=j<K'` is evaluated; the last core is observed as
   the maximum, not assumed.
3. Supports two through five use one shared branch maximum.
4. Supports six through nine use the universal cap with the full integer
   completion maximum `0<=b<=q`.
5. Premium weights are the exact deficits `36,28,21,15,10,6,3,1`.
6. Rank-nine marks and circuit deficits are divided once by 55.
7. Demand uses the lower record floor and the printed component density.
8. The cleared record coefficient and floor-record cross are positive on
   every claimed row.
9. The smallest closed gap is the `K'=40` gap printed in the statement.
10. `K'=41` is retained as a nonclaim because its floor-record cross is
    negative.
