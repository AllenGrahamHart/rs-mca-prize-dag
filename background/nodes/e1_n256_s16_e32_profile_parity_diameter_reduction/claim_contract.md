# Claim contract

## Claim

Every pair-feasible folded-profile `(3,4,0)`, `N=256`, `V=64` collision has
one of three exact autocorrelation magnitude profiles and obeys the printed
parity, diameter, and light-chord ledgers.

## Dependencies

- the proved `V=66` endpoint, which makes `V=64` the next live endpoint;
- the exact sparse-L1 slack recurrence;
- the signed-chord identity;
- the collision-norm criterion and the rational cubic majorant.

## Nonclaims

- no exclusion of any of the three profiles;
- no exclusion of `V=64` or a lower variance;
- no transport of either higher-endpoint template classification;
- no result for the separate `(4,2,0)` first-band profile;
- no promotion of either universal unsafe target.

## Falsifier

A pair-feasible `V=64` collision outside the three profiles, with one
light-light diameter, outside the printed diameter ledger, or violating its
profile parity pattern refutes the claim. Failure of either exact cubic sign
also refutes the profile reduction.
