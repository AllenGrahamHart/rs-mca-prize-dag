# Result

The proportional-conic obstruction is removed.  Three pairwise
distinct-`phi` points in one selected block always cap the residual affine
plane at three targets: isolated intersections are controlled by Bezout and
every common component is a fixed selected ray.  The printed next-dimension
slice is now paid unconditionally and, below `4h/5`, uniformly over every
`ell` with at least three `phi` fibers per selected block.
