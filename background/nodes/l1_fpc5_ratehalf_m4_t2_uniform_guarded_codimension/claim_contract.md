# Claim contract

## Inputs

- one fixed rate-half `M=4,t=2` source and touched pair;
- defect parameter `d=ell+s` and exact background root set `R`;
- the proved threshold `s<=|R|` and two-petal cofactor normal form.

## Output

- fixed-cell locator codimension at least `ell-1`;
- exact equality on the `r=s` boundary and a one-step improvement for
  `r>=s+1`;
- one aggregate root-rich split-pair interface without selecting `R`.

## Nonclaims

- no polynomial count for one growing-dimensional flat;
- no sum over background subsets;
- no bound on the number of realized exact background sets;
- no removal of candidate-wise primitive, untouched-petal, or first-owner
  filters.
