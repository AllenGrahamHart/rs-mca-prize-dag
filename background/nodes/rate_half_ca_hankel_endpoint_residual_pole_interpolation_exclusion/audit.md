# Audit

1. `J=(H:G)` is used in the local ring of `C`, not merely on its
   normalization. Thus `FG/H` is genuinely regular and descends across
   singular points.
2. The quotient colength is the pole length. Formula `(2)` remains valid in
   the Artinian fibre ring and needs no transverse-root assumption.
3. Distinct domain roots give a lower bound on common intersection length;
   multiplicity can only reduce the pole length further.
4. The interpolation dimension is strict: `m> d`, since `d<=O<=m-1`.
5. Domain degree one is below every component domain degree (`>=4`), so the
   interpolant cannot erase a whole component.
6. Reducedness is used only to ensure that a section nonzero on one
   component times a section nonzero on every component remains nonzero.
7. The vanishing threshold is `m>=6`; the official `m=2^37` is even and far
   inside scope.
