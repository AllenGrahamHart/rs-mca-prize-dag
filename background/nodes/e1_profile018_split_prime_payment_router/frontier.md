# Frontier

For one fixed prize row prime and quotient root, define

```text
O_514=#{Q_s above 257 occupied by a live profile-(0,18) collision}.
```

The exact payment threshold is

```text
O_514<=5.
```

The live collision must have one of 15 autocorrelation magnitude profiles at
energies five through twelve, parity multiplicity two, and a primitive-root
zero modulo 257. First attempt to falsify the threshold by constructing six
distinct occupied ideals. If that fails, combine the fixed-root equation with
all-singleton realization constraints before any exact norm computation.
