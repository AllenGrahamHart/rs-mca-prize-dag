# Attack

Audit that bases are counted inside the actual exact support, while the
closure bound may safely use the larger ambient common-zero set. Try sharp
rank-`(10-d)` flats with `9-d` coloops and one parallel class: they attain
the `d+2` basis divisor and show that an abstract-matroid improvement needs
additional structure.

A stronger continuation must couple corank strata or rule out simultaneous
saturation of the ambient and record-support profiles for the same residual
correction space.
