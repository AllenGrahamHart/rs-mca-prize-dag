# Audit

The primary verifier rebuilds the producer, checks its hash-bound
certificate, binds the three imported proof contracts, and rejects hostile
mutations of the orbital, passport, genus, degree, and conclusion fields.

The independent verifier uses six-bit masks for two-subsets. It counts
cycles by Burnside averaging fixed points of powers of each branch
permutation, rather than traversing the producer's induced permutation. It
also independently enumerates the two groups, their pair-stabilizer
subdegrees and adjacency orbits, then replays both Riemann--Hurwitz steps.
