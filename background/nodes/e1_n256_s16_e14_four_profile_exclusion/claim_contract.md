# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the complete E14 four-profile/1,321-template router;
- the proved proper-conductor collision exclusion;
- the collision norm criterion and pair-feasible prime threshold;
- the proved pair-feasible prime-field reduction.

## Output

No profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate at `V=28` collides on the pair-feasible branch.

## Nonclaims

No lower variance, profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform pair allowance,
row, or prize terminal is closed here. The whole norms are not claimed below
`2^250`; exactly 152 are not. The odd parts are also not claimed below
`2^250`; exactly six are not. Their exact compositeness is the replacement
certificate.

## Falsifier

A missed vector, engine disagreement, conductor leak, nonpositive resultant,
a seventh odd part at least `2^250`, an exceptional odd part at least
`2^251`, a prime exceptional odd part, or a field/normalization mismatch
refutes the result.
