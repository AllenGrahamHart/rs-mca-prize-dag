# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every unsafe full-span `2 x 5` factor-flag survivor emits a
  common pencil base zero or a deeper residual rich flat of size at least
  `18166`.
- **Quantifiers:** every complete family of promoted nontransverse containers
  at the `tau=1679`, `h=38384` interface satisfying the printed common
  `P,B` factor presentation.
- **Unit:** distinct selected slopes, partitioned first by the canonical
  promoted container and then by the factor-heavy/residual-heavy orientation.
- **Dependencies:** full-span residual forcing, ordinary affine-span list
  caps in dimensions four through six, fixed-pair slope ownership, and
  sub-square common-support interleaving.
- **Falsifier:** a base-free factor flag with every residual `B_i`
  `18165`-transverse and more than the printed total; overlapping zeros of
  distinct pencil members; or a failure of the ordered-basis residual census.
- **Nonclaim:** no existence of the factor presentation, no payment of either
  output horn, and no rank-eleven or row closure.
