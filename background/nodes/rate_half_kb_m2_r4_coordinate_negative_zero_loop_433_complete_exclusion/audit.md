# Audit

1. Finite and rank-deficient Smith systems are separated explicitly.
2. A family is not inferred empty from its 32 sampled exponents.
3. Every collision-free family is converted back to its exact binomial ideal.
4. Cleared squared-sum numerators are necessary because all cleared denominators are protected by the actual packet guards.
5. Unit ideals are computed over `F_p`, hence exclude algebraic-closure points.
6. The verifier is sharded so no local exact run approaches the host RAM limit.
7. The claim stops at one negative profile and does not close the coordinate orientation.
