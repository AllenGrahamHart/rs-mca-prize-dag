- Gauss's lemma is used only to replace the common divisor over
  `Fbar(X)` by its primitive polynomial representative.
- Positive weighted degree is multiplicative, so quotienting cannot hide
  leading-weight cancellation.
- The dimension comparison is over the algebraic closure; scalar extension
  preserves the kernel's lower dimension `938`.
- `M(46)=935` is the rejecting side of the threshold and is load-bearing.
- The higher-degree mass still uses ambient cofactor degree `52-d`; the
  weighted-degree bound does not silently replace that quantity.
- Neither irreducibility nor a component population estimate is asserted.
- Primary and independent verifiers replay all `D=0..264` and
  `d=2..43` endpoint arithmetic.
