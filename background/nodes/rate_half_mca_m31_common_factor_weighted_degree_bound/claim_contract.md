# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the primitive full interpolation gcd has weighted degree at
  most `217`, hence `(Y,Z)`-degree at most `43`.
- **Input:** the weight-`(1,5,5)`, degree-`264` kernel has dimension at
  least `938`; the full line bank and common-factor mass theorem are
  already proved.
- **Exact threshold:** `M(46)=935<938<=M(47)=990`.
- **Higher-degree consequence:** degree `2..43`, at least `5083` captured
  sections, at least `126266` factor points, and at most `3971`
  exceptional inside points.
- **Units:** weighted polynomial degree, distinct polynomial-pair sections,
  and coordinates of the `e=130237` inside support.
- **Nonclaims:** `P` is irreducible or rational; every higher-degree factor
  is a split pencil; the projective-star population is paid; the
  higher-degree full-gcd branch is safe; or the adjacent crossing is located.
- **Falsifier:** a common kernel divisor of weighted degree at least `218`
  in a kernel of dimension at least `938`, or a valid higher-degree branch
  violating one of the printed integer consequences.
