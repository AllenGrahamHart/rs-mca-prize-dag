# Audit

The primary checker scans every legal support size at all eight printed ranks
using exact binomials, verifies monotonicity and adjacent boundaries, and
rejects four mutations.

The independent checker uses gcd-cancelled products, preserves the
`e*floor(...)` order, reconstructs every last-paid support size, and rejects
three controls.

Both checks are tiny arithmetic under RAMguard.  No Modal compute is used.
