# Claim contract

This node changes only the rank-nine full-deficit sparse premium at `K'=45`.
It reuses the exact all-core chart, every kernel corank, the 55-shadow ledger,
and the sharp isolated-incidence demand from the preceding row payments.

The completion product is a Cartesian partition of the eight integer maxima,
not an assertion that every leaf is realizable.  Including unrealizable
leaves is harmless for the upper bound.  The joint support-four cap is used
only when both of its source maxima are terminal and exact.

The verifier streams the leaves in canonical order and records a digest,
sum, counts, and extrema; it does not serialize a 362880-entry table.

The node closes only the rank-nine component target at `K'=45`.  It does not
close `K'=46`, rank eight, rank eleven, or either grand prize problem.
