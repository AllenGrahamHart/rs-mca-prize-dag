# Attack record

Extending only the 35-leaf `K'=44` lattice through supports seven, eight,
and nine left the nested support-five/support-six defect-two leaf unsafe.
Refining every support independently showed why: terminal-terminal leaves
need a shared carrier, while fallback leaves need all eight lower completion
ceilings simultaneously.

The support-four/support-five zero-carrier theorem supplies the first part.
The full Cartesian branch product supplies the second.  Either ingredient
alone leaves a premium above the exact `K'=45` ceiling.  Their combination
moves the active branch to all fallback and closes the row.  At `K'=46` that
fallback branch itself becomes the next wall.
