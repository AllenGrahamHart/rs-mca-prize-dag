   light-support orbit.

   zero-sum kernel. The audit uses positive gaps and pair-sum tables.
3. Both relaxations agree on every one of 64 shards and all 1,456 exceptional
   assignments.
4. The production actual-vector engine folds unordered chords. Its audit
   multiplies by the negacyclic reverse and checks anti-palindromicity.
5. Both actual engines agree row by row over 321 light orbits and
   6,371,187,456 vectors per engine.
6. FLINT and PARI/GP independently compute all 86 full-conductor norms.
7. The verifier replays every retained vector, conductor, `M_3`, packet hash,
   DAG edge, and the exact maximum-norm margin.
