# Proof

The relaxation child proves that every magnitude layer is either below the
cubic cutoff or belongs to an exact 1,456-entry exceptional set on 321 light
orbits.  The actual-census child proves that these orbits lift to exactly 440
actual cubic exceptions, of which 354 have proper conductor and 86 have full
conductor.

The cubic part of `collision_norm_criterion` excludes every layer with
`M_3<=1087`.  The proper-conductor theorem excludes the 354 imprimitive
actual exceptions.  The primitive-norm child proves that all remaining 86
exact norms are below `2^250`; the norm criterion therefore excludes them as
well.  These branches are disjoint and exhaustive, so no profile-(5,4,1)
collision remains. QED.
