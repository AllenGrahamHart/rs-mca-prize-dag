# Claim contract

## Inputs

- The complete primitive Pade-pencil normal form for a nonempty deficient
  high-band family.
- Maximal exact-depth cores and support-wise first-match selected rays.
- The proved high-depth interaction strip.

## Outputs

- A base-independent active defect set `D subset G_d` excluded from every
  joint core.
- Exact scalar factorization and ordinary-list currency on `H\D`.
- Exact selected-block currency on `D`.
- The unconditional outside payment `N_d^out<=n-|D|`.

## Exclusions

- No polynomial upper bound for the punctured ordinary list.
- No claim that arbitrary listed `tau` parameters pass maximality, selection,
  or strip ownership.
- No transport to upstream rational-owner localization or moving-root BC.
- No smoothness assertion for the punctured domain `H\D`.
