# Audit

## Logical reconstruction

- The cancellation of the `tau` terms in `P E_tau+Q E_tau'` is exact, so
  `D` is independent of the chosen base member.
- `D subset G_d` uses the primitive relation only where it was proved;
  nothing is inferred about residual values at forced multiplier roots.
- Coprimality is used pointwise only to ensure `P,Q` do not vanish together.
- `D` is punctured before defining `w`; no division is performed at an
  active defect.
- Exact maximality, not a chosen interpolation subset, gives exactly `k+d`
  agreements in `(AD4)`.
- The projective factorization includes the infinite slope.
- Core interpolation identifies a selected ray with the scalar combination
  used in `(AD5)` before its agreement points are counted.
- A root of `L_lambda` is charged as off-core unless `tau=w`; this is why
  all such roots must lie in the core for a local selected ray.
- Block disjointness is pointwise and does not assume disjoint locator roots.

## Currency audit

The ordinary list lives on `H\D` with length `n-e`, dimension `k-ell`, and
exact agreement `k+d`. The selected off-core blocks live on `D` and have size
`h-d`. Neither currency alone is the original maximal selected locator count;
the router retains both conditions.

Przemek's `thm:owner-localization` in `experimental/grande_finale.tex` puts
whole agreement supports inside an owner set. Importing its punctured MCA
bound here would reverse the roles of core and defect and is invalid without
a new transport theorem.

## Residual risk

The punctured list is below the ordinary Johnson regime at the prize rows.
The active-defect blocks and first-match ownership must therefore remain in
any successful count.
