# Audit

- The identity is stated in the total quotient ring under the affine
  trivializations already used by the cancelled ambient equation. It is not
  presented as an equality of untrivialized line-bundle sections.
- `J^3/R_a` is polynomial only under the printed multiplicity-at-most-three
  scope.
- The ordinary cubic cube root is rational with a pole at `R_0`; it is not
  promoted to a global section.
- The unique sections in the no-ordinary branches are the radical
  quotients themselves, so section uniqueness supplies no contradiction.
- A mixed cube root on `C` does not make the biform equation a separated
  rational pullback. The strict-endpoint separated-pullback theorem is not
  imported here.
- Characteristic three is allowed by the prize field conditions. Any future
  Kummer-cover argument must split it off rather than assume etaleness.
