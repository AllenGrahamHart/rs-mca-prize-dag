# Audit

1. Correction order one means `ord_z f_i(z,0)=1`, so `a_i` is nonzero.
2. Shape B has the large factor and the `(2,3)` companion at the collision;
   shape D has the two `(2,3)` companions there. In both cases there are
   exactly two first-order factors.
3. Every factor with correction order zero is a unit in the completed local
   ring and is absorbed into `u`.
4. Derivatives of `u` multiply `f_1f_2`, which starts in total order two, so
   they cannot affect the constant or parameter-linear coefficient of
   `G_X`.
5. The tangent-sum zero/nonzero condition is invariant under rescaling and
   swapping the two factors.
6. The argument may be made over a splitting field; the resulting statement
   about `G_X` descends because `G` is the original biform.
7. Odd characteristic and unsharedness enter through the Pade/split-jet
   dictionary and are not silently extended here.
8. No claim is made that tangent cancellation is impossible.
