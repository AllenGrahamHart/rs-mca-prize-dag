# Audit

1. The argument is componentwise. A pure unramified fiber contributes
   `nh` points to every component of subdegree `h`, not merely to their
   union.
2. Base-field descent uses uniqueness of the geometric component through a
   smooth etale fiber-product point. Rationality of a singular intersection
   point would not suffice.
3. The two subdegrees are equal because both projections have the same
   composite map to the parameter line.
4. The finite image-map degree `q` is retained. Dividing `(e+7)nh` by `q`,
   rather than by the coarse row degree `m`, is what makes the subgroup
   comparison useful.
5. A toral image has primitive exponents of equal absolute value and hence
   is a scaling or reciprocal graph. Its component subdegree is then one,
   so it is a genuine deck graph.
6. The odd cover degree excludes both a nontrivial dyadic scaling and an
   involution. The identity scaling is the removed diagonal.
7. Corvaja--Zannier is applied only when `D<D_0<P_char`, so the coordinate
   differentials satisfy the theorem's characteristic hypothesis.
8. The theorem stops at a lower degree bound. It does not turn a
   macroscopic component into a contradiction.
