- The signed-edge atlas prints two positive parallel `DE` records followed
  by one negative `DE` record.
- `(KBP1B4-X0P0-1)` proves the first positive copy empty when it is missing
  under canonical matching zero.
- Exact comparison of product rows, squared-sum rows, residual ordering, and
  guards shows that swapping the two positive copies changes no equation.
