# Audit

The factor `W-y_i` is the third, unselected root of the cubic fiber. It is
not a root of `L`; otherwise that color would have multiplicity three.

The color-ratio equation uses an ordering of the three colors. Permuting the
colors permutes the `u_i`; implementations must either enumerate the six
orders or use a symmetric equivalent. Cyclic multiplication of every color
does not change the ratio.

The structured product is necessary only. It does not impose the
assignment-preserving Frobenius equation, full cyclotomic divisibility, or
the inner lift.
