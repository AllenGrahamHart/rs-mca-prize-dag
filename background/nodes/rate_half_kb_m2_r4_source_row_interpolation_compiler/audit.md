1. The row quartics are projective and nonzero; full support records the
   twelve independent row scales.
2. The parity-check dimension is `12-3=9`, producing `5*9=45` equations.
3. The interpolant is only of bidegree at most `(2,4)`; exact degree and
   irreducibility are retained side conditions.
4. The square product follows from the proved complete-source divisor
   identity and includes ramification multiplicity.
5. `B=A(X^2)` is used only in the geometric source-line coordinates where
   `psi=X^2`; the invariant statement is `Res_T(A,H) proportional B^2`.
