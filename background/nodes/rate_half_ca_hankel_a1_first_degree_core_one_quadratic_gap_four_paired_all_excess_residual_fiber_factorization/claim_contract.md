# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every off-line split-biform fiber factors as actual-inside
  locator times a degree-at-most-excess residual times all padding.
- **Inputs:** the two endpoint/three-center reductions, the contracted
  source and split-biform interpolation identities, standard RS zero
  factorization, and the full padded locator.
- **Outputs:** `(AEF2)--(AEF7)`, including the exact fiber degree-drop
  identity and transversality at every actual-support root.
- **Quantifiers:** every off-line supported slope in both paired profiles,
  with no zero-excess restriction.
- **Falsifier:** a missing padded factor, a fiber degree drop different from
  `a_delta-deg H_delta`, or a zero first-jet determinant at an actual-
  support root.
