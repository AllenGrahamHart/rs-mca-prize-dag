# Source evidence

Lassina Dembele, *An intriguing hyperelliptic Shimura curve quotient of
genus 16*, Algebra & Number Theory 14 (2020), no. 10, 2807-2827:

```text
https://doi.org/10.2140/ant.2020.14.2807
https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.06772
```

The load-bearing locations are:

- Section 5.2: `E=Q(i(zeta_64+zeta_64^(-1)))` and `h(E)=17`;
- Theorem 5.9 and the second proof of Theorem 6.4: the Hilbert class field
  and its normal closure are the Harbater field;
- Remark 6.2: the exact degree-17 Elkies polynomial whose splitting field is
  the Harbater field.

The exact modulo-257 irreducibility of that polynomial is not imported; it
is replayed by `verify.py`.
