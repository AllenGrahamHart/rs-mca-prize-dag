# Q(zeta_128) J_65 Harbater nonprincipality

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** one explicit ideal in `Q(zeta_128)`

Put `zeta=zeta_128` and

```text
q_1 =(257,zeta-9),
q_65=(257,zeta-248),
J_65=q_1 q_65.
```

Then `J_65` is nonprincipal.

The proof descends both primes to the class-number-17 CM subfield of
`Q(zeta_64)` and detects the descended prime by its nontrivial Frobenius in
the Harbater/Hilbert class field. The finite calculation is the exact
irreducibility of the published Elkies polynomial modulo 257.
