# Audit

- The CM field has conductor 64 and degree 16; it is not the maximal real
  field and not all of `Q(zeta_64)`.
- The polynomial is used through its splitting field, not through the
  degree-17 root field alone.
- Reduction modulo 257 is separable and irreducible, so Dedekind index
  ambiguity cannot alter the Frobenius cycle type used here.
- Since 257 splits completely in the CM base field, the rational Frobenius
  lies in the order-17 Hilbert-class subgroup.
- The two primes defining `J_65` contract to the same lower prime because
  `9^2=(-9)^2=81 mod 257`.
- Norming a principal `J_65` would make the square of that lower prime
  principal. Class number 17 makes squaring injective.
