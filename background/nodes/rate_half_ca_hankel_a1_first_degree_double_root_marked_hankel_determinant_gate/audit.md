# Audit

- The rectangular identity uses signed maximal minors. A single scalar is
  fixed once; signs are not silently changed from row to row.
- `D_0` has degree `rho-e`, not the core-one regular size `e-2`.
- The rank-one update has determinant degree `d=rho-1`: the degree-`rho`
  term `det M_1` vanishes identically.
- The factor `g_*` is the squarefree gcd with `H`; the remaining quadratic
  is cubed because the vertical divisor is `R_*+3B`.
- The scalar `c^2` need not be a sixth power in the base field.
- The formulas do not assume irreducibility of the apolar curve or
  noncancellation in a Cauchy--Binet expansion.
- The theorem gives a concrete next gate but no target promotion.
