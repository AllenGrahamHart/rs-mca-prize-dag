# Proof

For the core-free branch, the half-distance residual pencil has size
`rho x (rho+1)`, generic rank `rho`, one right singular block `L_e`, and a
regular Kronecker block of size `rho-e`. Let `m(U,V)` be its signed vector
of maximal minors. The cofactor identity gives

```text
M_0m=0.                                               (1)
```

The rational kernel is generated by the primitive vector `q`. Gauss's
lemma therefore gives `m=D_0q` for a polynomial `D_0`. Every maximal minor
has parameter degree `rho`, while every entry of `q` has degree `e`, so

```text
deg D_0=rho-e=2e-1.                                  (2)
```

Equivalently, `D_0` is the determinant of the regular Kronecker block, up
to a nonzero scalar. Expanding the determinant of the stacked matrix along
its last row gives

```text
det M_0[x]=nu(x)m=D_0 nu(x)q=D_0Q(U,V;x),             (3)
```

after fixing that scalar normalization. This proves `(MHD2)--(MHD3)`.

The polynomial `P_3` is monic in `X` and has the roots of `G_L` once,
`x_s` twice, and `x_d` once. The root-product definition of the resultant
therefore gives, up to its fixed ordering sign,

```text
Res_X(Q,P_3)
 =(product_(x in L)Q(U,V;x))Q(U,V;x_s)^2Q(U,V;x_d).  (4)
```

There are

```text
|L|+3=deg P_3=3rho+9=3(rho+3)                       (5)
```

factors in `(4)`, counted with multiplicity. Substituting `(3)` into `(4)`
proves `(MHD4)`. The cubic normal-form theorem gives row deficits
`(c_s,c_d)=(1,1)` in the three no-ordinary packets and `(2,1)` in the
ordinary packet. Since `deg K_x=c_x`, this proves `(MHD5)--(MHD6)`. The
low-degree resultant theorem supplies the asserted right-hand
factorizations.

For the core-one branch, the general middle-adjugate theorem gives

```text
adj M_1=D_1qq^T,       deg D_1=d-2e=e-2.             (6)
```

The rank-one determinant identity is

```text
det(M+tau vv^T)=det M+tau v^T adj(M)v.               (7)
```

Here `det M_1=0`, and `nu(x)^Tq=Q(U,V;x)`. Equations `(6),(7)` give
`(MHD8)`.

At the `u=4` double root, the proved vertical divisor is

```text
V_*=R_*+3B,       deg R_*=e-6,       deg B=2.        (8)
```

The squarefree supported form cutting out `R_*` is exactly
`g_*=gcd(Q(U,V;x_*),H)`. Pushing `(8)` to the parameter line and comparing
homogeneous forms gives

```text
Q(U,V;x_*)=c g_*S_B^3                               (9)
```

for one `c in F^x`. Substitution into `(MHD8)` proves `(MHD11)`. In
characteristic three the derivative of the cube in `(9)` is zero; division
by the nonzero form `g_*` in an affine parameter chart proves `(MHD12)`.
All arguments are over the base field and require no factorization of `Q`.
QED.
