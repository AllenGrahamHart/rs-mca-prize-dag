# Claim contract

- **Claim:** primitive Hankel cofactors turn the retained cubic and quadratic
  double-root packets into the exact marked determinant identities
  `(MHD3)--(MHD12)`.
- **Currency:** signed maximal minors, a symmetric adjugate, the rank-one
  determinant lemma, and the already classified vertical divisors.
- **Dependencies:** the half-distance `A=1` Kronecker ledger, the general
  core-one middle-adjugate factorization, and the low-degree double-root
  resultant factorization.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted; `(MHD12)` is the characteristic-three
  specialization.
- **Nonclaim:** the marked determinant factorizations are not shown to be
  impossible, and no signed Cauchy--Binet sum is treated as positive.
- **Falsifier:** a retained packet whose signed maximal-minor vector is not
  a scalar regular-block factor times the primitive kernel, or whose
  rank-one updated determinant differs from `(MHD8)`.
- **Next gate:** use the evaluation-source decomposition of the Hankel pencil
  to exclude the linear/quadratic residual factors in these determinants.
