# Audit

The compiler uses elementary symmetric functions of the three quadratic
fiber parameters; it does not assume that the `u_i` are distinct. Exact
fiber degree and distinct colors remain enforced by the dependency's
subresultant and color-ratio saturation.

Equations (TSC4)--(TSC6) are coefficient identities, not a dimension or
unit-ideal verdict. The phrase "square p-free core" counts four equations
in four retained variables and does not assert a zero-dimensional ideal.

Any implementation must clear and saturate the displayed HNF denominators
before interpreting components. Retained points still owe every color and
Frobenius condition listed in the statement.
