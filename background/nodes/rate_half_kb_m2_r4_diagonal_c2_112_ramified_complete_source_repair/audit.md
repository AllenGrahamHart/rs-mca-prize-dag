# Audit

1. The new condition on `V` uses complete-source row multiplicities; it
   does not follow from the ramified square fiber alone.
2. Each root row has order at most two because it divides `B/z_i`; total
   order four then forces the allocation `(2,2)`.
3. Projective roots, including a possible endpoint root at infinity, are
   handled by the binary-form row identity.
4. Deck distinction excludes `V=0` after evaluation at `W=0`.
5. The theorem restores the coefficient cut but does not remove geometric
   source ramification or prove packet nonexistence.
