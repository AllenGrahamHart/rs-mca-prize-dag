# Proof

The original `A=1` divided-power form has degree `2rho-1`. Contracting the
one fixed domain factor lowers its degree to

```text
2rho-2=2(rho-1)=2d.
```

The residual catalecticant has `rho=d+1` rows and columns. It is therefore
the square symmetric middle catalecticant in `(GMA2)`. The core-stratified
Kronecker ledger gives generic rank `d`, one right block `L_e`, one left
block `L_e^T`, and regular size

```text
d-2e=Delta.                                           (1)
```

Its rational kernel is one-dimensional and is generated by the primitive
vector `q`.

Since `M` has generic corank one, its adjugate is nonzero and

```text
M adj(M)=adj(M)M=0.                                   (2)
```

Every column of `adj M` lies in the rational line spanned by `q`.
Primitivity over the UFD `F[U,V]` and Gauss's lemma make each polynomial
column a polynomial multiple of `q`, so

```text
adj M=q w^T.                                          (3)
```

Symmetry of the adjugate and another use of primitivity give `w=Dq` for a
single polynomial `D`. Every adjugate entry has homogeneous degree `d`,
while every product `q_iq_j` has degree `2e`. Hence `D` is nonzero,
homogeneous, and has degree `d-2e=Delta`. The scalar normalization of `q`
is absorbed into `D`, proving `(GMA3)`.

The gcd of all products `q_iq_j` is one because the diagonal products are
included and `q` is primitive. Thus the common divisor of all nonzero
maximal minors is exactly `D`. Strict Kronecker equivalence changes maximal
minors only by nonzero scalar combinations. The singular blocks have
constant rank at every projective parameter, so this common divisor is the
determinant of the regular block, up to scalar.

Fix `gamma` and work over the DVR of the parameter line there. If the local
Smith exponents of the regular block are `a_1,...,a_Delta`, then

```text
ord_gamma(D)=sum_i a_i,
c_gamma=#{i:a_i>0}<=sum_i a_i.                       (4)
```

Summing `(4)` proves `(GMA4)`.

Finally decompose the pole scheme by parameter slope. The local
fibrewise-multiplication argument gives

```text
p_gamma<=o_gamma<=c_gamma,                            (5)
```

where `o_gamma` is the residual root omission and `p_gamma` is the local
pole colength. Combining `(4),(5)` shows
`p_gamma<=ord_gamma(D)` at every slope. Therefore the effective pushforward
divisor `P_p=sum p_gamma[gamma]` is a subdivisor of `div(D)`. Its residual
effective divisor has degree `Delta-p`, which proves `(GMA5)`. QED.
