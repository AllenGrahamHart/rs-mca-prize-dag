# Result

Every irreducible factor of the extremal paired split biform is itself fully
split on every classified domain row and on at least `e+6+d_A` clean
parameter fibers, and satisfies

```text
3e n_j>=(3p-3+d_A)m_j.
```

At least one factor has parameter degree at least `ceil(e/3)` for `d_A=0`
or `ceil(3e/7)` for `d_A=1`. Thus no bounded-degree factor construction can
realize the surviving two-directionally split biform.
