# Frontier

The generic pure-power escape is no longer a large degree spectrum. It has
two sharply separated obligations:

1. pay the almost perfectly saturated `e=2048`, `K'=2049` row, where at
   most 72 root slots are absent and every direction owns one full line;
2. pay `e=4096`, `4097<=K'<=4237`, where at least 211 directions occur.

The next theorem must either classify the endpoint rational map into this
or another quotient-periodic normal form, or use the received-line and
owner semantics to exclude these two pure-power branches directly.
