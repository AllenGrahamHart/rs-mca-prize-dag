# Claim contract

- **input:** the proved 218-plane direction bank and the additional
  hypothesis that its coprime residual direction pencil is projectively
  equivalent to `(X^e,1)` for a power-of-two divisor `e` of `N`;
- **degree output:** only `e=2048` and `e=4096` survive;
- **2048 output:** `K'=2049`, all 218 directions represented, one full line
  per direction, and at most 72 missing fiber slots;
- **4096 output:** `4097<=K'<=4237`, at least 211 represented directions,
  at most seven duplicate-direction lines, and at most 28744 missing slots;
- **not claimed:** that every quotient-periodic pencil is pure-power, that
  the endpoint pencil has this form, or that either surviving case is paid.
