# Pure-power router for the 218-plane endpoint

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** the pure-power subclass of the endpoint direction bank

Assume the residual projective direction pencil from the 218-plane bank is,
after a projective change of basis, generated by

```text
X^e, 1
```

on `mu_N`, where `N=2^21`, `e` is a power of two dividing `N`, and
`e<=K'-1`. Then the endpoint inequalities force

```text
e in {2048,4096}.                                  (PP-1)
```

More precisely:

```text
e=2048  ==>  K'=2049, r=218,
              exactly 218 full affine lines,
              one in each represented direction,
              and at most 72 missing fiber slots;  (PP-2)

e=4096  ==>  4097<=K'<=4237, 211<=r<=218,
              at most seven direction-duplicate full lines,
              and at most 28744 missing fiber slots. (PP-3)
```

Here `r` is the number of represented full directions, and a missing fiber
slot is a root of the pure-power direction polynomial that is not a full
coordinate. This does not prove that the residual pencil is pure-power and
does not exclude or pay either surviving degree.

## Falsifier

A feasible pure-power degree other than 2048 or 4096; a feasible `K'` outside
the displayed intervals; `r<218` in the degree-2048 case; or a missing-slot
count above the stated cap.
