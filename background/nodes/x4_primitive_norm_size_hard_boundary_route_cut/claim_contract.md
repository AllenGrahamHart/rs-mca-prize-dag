- **Proved cell:** rate half, `e=N/8`, `d=e-t_XR-1`.
- **Proved route cut:** every possible primitive active/zero pattern passes
  the complete generic norm-size comparison.
- **Scope:** all official fields with `log2(q)<256`, including generated
  extension fields.
- **Retained routes:** exact factor/common-ideal sieves, locator incidence,
  and first-owner payments.

  false; or norm arithmetic with additional shape information is useless.
