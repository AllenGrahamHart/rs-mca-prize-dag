   no record is reassigned or counted twice.

   agreement set has size at least `m`, so an exact support containing
   `H_p` exists.

   agreement sets exactly `H_p`; saturated exact supports therefore also
   intersect exactly there.

   Thus the packet intersection is exactly `J`, not merely contained in it.

   and the polynomial dimension by exactly `c`.
6. The core-union lower bound uses one nonzero pair-component difference,
   so the intersection cap is `K'-1`, not a generic incidence estimate.
7. The strict root surplus is `m'-K'-21=67451`; it is uniform over the
   complete shortening range.

   outputs remain live.
