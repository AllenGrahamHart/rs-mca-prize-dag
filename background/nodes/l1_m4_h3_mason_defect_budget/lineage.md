# Lineage

The maximal-value complement theorem retained the characteristic-`p`
exception and obtained only `ord_0(R)<=u-p`. Specializing to
`n=4(p+1),h=3` makes that numerical bound four. Re-running its exact reduced
abc triple without discarding radical slack identifies the full residual:
the depressed cubic cannot have zero linear term, and every repeated-root or
overlap defect is charged to the four-unit gap. Retaining the Wronskian also
produces an explicit nonzero polynomial of degree at most four, dropping by
one with each unit of inner valuation. This is the first finite analytic
classification target for the exact two-Schur branch.
