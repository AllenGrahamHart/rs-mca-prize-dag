# Claim contract

- **Input:** one exact FPC5 GRS syndrome shell supplied by
  `l1_fpc5_tpetal_hankel_grs_syndrome_shell`.
- **Output:** the exact support-shortening cap `(CW4)` and the fixed-cell
  incidence cap `(CW5)`.
- **Preserved:** core, source, touched set, defect degree, required
  background set, locator support, shell syndrome, and primitive injection.
- **Nonclaim:** a cap after summing source layouts, touched sets, defects, or
  scales; polynomiality when the shortening depth or background multiplicity
  grows.
