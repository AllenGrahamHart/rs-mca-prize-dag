# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | Every constrained common-`K` ledger has the printed missing mate, finite nonzero forced product, nonsingular product involution, and exhaustive 30-cell outside-product routing. |
| assumptions | Exact constrained ledger, antipodal source signature, unique complete-edge multiset, and deployed characteristic. |
| exact interface | Pair/singleton table `(KB43W-1)`, forced products `(KB43W-2)`, involutions `(KB43W-4)`, and outside multiset `(KB43W-5)`. |
| nonclaim | A cell deletion, complete matching/interpolation, skeleton or coordinate closure, a Prize row, or either Prize theorem. |
