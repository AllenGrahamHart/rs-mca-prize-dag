# Proof

Read the five labels and products in the order
`(A,C,AB+,AB-,BC)`.  Negation pairs the labels exactly as shown in
`(KB43W-1)`; the sixth label of the antipodal `I` set is therefore the
negative of the singleton.

Append a candidate Mobius row `[-p,-p xi,1,xi]` to the five common rows.
For `X2,N1`, reduce its ten four-row determinants by the corresponding
`P_8,F_-/F_+` ledger.  Their ideal has one linear row in `p`, yielding
`P_X/22` or `P_N/22`.  For `L1`, reduction by
`M^2+1,2c^4+3c^2+2,3L-4c^3-2c+M` gives
`8p-3c^2-10`.  Conversely all ten candidate determinants vanish after
substitution, so `(KB43W-2)` is exactly the forced Mobius value.

For a product pair `(y,z)`, use the row `(yz,-(y+z),-1)`.  The cross product
of the two rows in `(KB43W-1)` gives `(KB43W-4)`, after removing the unit
factor `c+1` in `X2` and `c-1` in `N1`.  The L1 cross product is already in
the displayed scale.

Direct deployed-field ideals show that `b,c`, each numerator in
`(KB43W-2)`, all three coefficients in `(KB43W-4)`, and the involution
determinant have no common zero with their exact base ledgers.  The constant
denominators are invertible.  Thus the forced values and involutions are
defined throughout every candidate ledger.

Finally apply the universal complete-edge theorem.  Its two colored and
five internal edge types give `(KB43W-5)` independently of the common-row
cell.  Sign gauge and `F -> -F` leave the five forced-type representatives,
proving the 30-cell census. QED.
