# Audit

- The capacity lower bound drops `O>=0`; positive omission only increases
  the required heavy-factor degree and strengthens the table.
- Heavy rows are charged the maximal possible deficit `e`, and light rows
  the maximal allowed deficit `j`. Thus `(1)` is conservative.
- The table bounds the residual degree after the product of actual heavy
  domain rows is removed. No assumption about additional coefficient-gcd
  roots is made.
- All floor evaluations use the official `e=183251937963`; the small
  remainders in `(5),(8)` are strictly below the denominators.
- The six profiles remain separate even when two residual total degrees are
  similar.
