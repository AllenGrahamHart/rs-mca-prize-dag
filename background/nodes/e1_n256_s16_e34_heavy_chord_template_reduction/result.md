# Result

The E34 `(6,7)` frontier is split into four explicit heavy-position
templates. Every singleton heavy-heavy distance class is welded to a
heavy-light chord. The quarter template is pinned to `D_64=16`, opposite
outer heavy signs, and no light at the missing quarter; its former `D_64=20`
case is excluded.

The universal targets remain open. The next route can attack four additive
templates rather than arbitrary seven-point supports.
