# Claim contract

- **input:** the proved pure-power router specialized to `e=2048`;
- **configuration output:** an exact symmetric `218_15` configuration with
  218 distinct directions in `mu_1024`;
- **leave output:** point and line leaves are 7-regular with 763 edges;
- **linear output:** the two exact Gram identities, real nonsingularity of
  the incidence matrix, and cospectral leaves;
- **fiber output:** aggregate defect at most 72, at least 146 saturated
  fibers, one point on at least 11 saturated lines, and one point with
  incident defect at most four;
- **not claimed:** existence or nonexistence of the abstract configuration,
  official-field exclusion, or quotient-owner payment.
