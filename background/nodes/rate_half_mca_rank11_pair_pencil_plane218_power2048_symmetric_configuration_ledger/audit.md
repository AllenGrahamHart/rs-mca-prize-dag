   ceiling; it is not assumed.
2. Distinct represented directions rule out parallel full lines.
3. Point pairs and line pairs occur at most once because the objects are
   actual affine points and lines.
4. `M` is proved nonsingular only over the reals. No modular rank or
   characteristic-zero lift of the official arrangement is claimed.
5. Saturated fibers and the low-defect point are separate pigeonhole
   conclusions; the same point need not satisfy both.

   complete size and not a chronology payment.
