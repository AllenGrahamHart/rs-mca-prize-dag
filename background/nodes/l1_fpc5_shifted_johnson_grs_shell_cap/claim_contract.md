# Claim contract

- **Input:** one fixed FPC5 source/touched/degree cell, its owner-free or
  fixed-background exact GRS shell, Haboeck's MCA numerator theorem, and the
  self-contained adjacent-dimension deep-point conversion.
- **Output:** the exact `W L_m(q)` fixed-cell cap and sufficient prize-budget
  test `(SJ6)`.
- **Preserved:** primitive locator, source, touched set, defect degree,
  required background incidence, all guards, exact radius, field, and core.
- **Nonclaim:** source/profile/chronology aggregation, existence of a
  contributor in any parameter cell, or coverage outside the shifted-Johnson
  strip.
