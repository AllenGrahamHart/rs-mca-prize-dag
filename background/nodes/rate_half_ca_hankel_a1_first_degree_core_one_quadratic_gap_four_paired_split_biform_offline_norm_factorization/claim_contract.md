# Claim contract

- **Proposition:** the product over all off-line parameter fibers contains
  the classified-row locator to the full row degree, with the printed exact
  residual-degree caps; selected roots outside the classified rows divide
  that residual. In the extremal profile the residual is reconstructed
  uniquely from the printed local tangent products.
- **Quantifier:** both proved quadratic pair-boundary split biforms on the
  official first-degree row.
- **Dependencies:** the extremal and strict dual-MDS split-biform reductions;
  the extremal minimum-word and source-partition ledgers; the zero-excess
  padded-fiber factorization for `(ONF8)`.
- **Falsifier:** a classified row with fewer than the asserted distinct
  off-line roots, an identically zero off-line fiber, a nondivisible norm,
  a quotient exceeding the printed degree, or a failure of the local value
  or interpolation formula. The exact slack identity is falsified by any
  mismatch between row-incidence and fiber-excess counting.
- **Nonclaim:** no exclusion of either pair boundary and no classification
  of the residual norm; padding and triple-union excess alone cannot exceed
  the cap because `(ONF11a)` is an equality.
