# Result

The dense row splitting now has an exact norm form. In the extremal profile,
the product of all `3e` off-line fibers is a classified-row locator power
times a residual of degree at most `3e-9` or `2e-7`. Every padded-heavy root
is forced into this residual. Since its degree is below the classified-row
count, the residual is uniquely reconstructed from explicit local tangent
products. Its cap is exactly the total triple-union, padding, and exceptional
row slack minus fiber degree drops, so those incidence quantities cannot by
themselves yield a contradiction. The strict profile has the analogous exact
factorization and printed degree ledger. Neither boundary is yet excluded.
