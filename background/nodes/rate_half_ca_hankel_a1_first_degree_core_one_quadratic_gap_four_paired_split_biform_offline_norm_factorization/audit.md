# Audit

1. The norm product uses all off-line supported slopes, not only the selected
   zero-excess fibers.
2. `G(delta,X)` is proved nonzero before taking the product; otherwise a
   global parameter factor would be hidden in the norm. The strict
   `r_A=0` guard uses the fixed core point in addition to `|M|=rho`.
3. The row divisor uses vanishing multiplicity. Distinct row roots provide
   one copy each; any tangency or repeated `X`-root remains in `S_G`.
4. The degree is an upper bound because individual parameter fibers may
   drop `X`-degree.
5. Padding is localized only after using the independently proved exact
   fiber factorization. Incidence alone does not identify it.
6. In the extremal `d_A=0` case, the exceptional row is outside `M` and its
   selected incidences also consume residual degree. The equality
   `J_delta=R_delta` is claimed only for `d_A=1`.
7. The strict residual cap is recorded without presenting it as small; it is
   quadratic in `e` for low `r_A`.
8. The tangent product uses `partial_X`, not the parameter derivative. A
   tangent incident fiber correctly makes both `D_x` and `S_G(x)` zero.
9. Residual interpolation is asserted only for the extremal profile, where
   the exact degree cap is strictly below the classified-row count.
10. The cap equals the complete extremal incidence slack. It is not valid to
    add triple-union excess and padding to the cap as though they were an
    independent lower bound; they are its two main summands.
11. Fiber degree drops are subtracted with their full degree loss. Counting
    only the number of dropped fibers would not prove `(ONF11b)`.
