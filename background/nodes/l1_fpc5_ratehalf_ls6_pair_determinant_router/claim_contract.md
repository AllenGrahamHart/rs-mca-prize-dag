# Claim contract

## Input

Two candidates from one fixed guarded LS6 atom.

## Output

An exact nonzero cross determinant of degree at most `ell-2a`, the resulting
locator-intersection cap, a fixed-base determinant injection, and the exact
identity of its Johnson denominator with the live `J<=0` parameter.

## Preserved

- one fixed source/triple/defect/cross-ratio atom;
- candidate multiplicity and monicity;
- the exact LS6 gcd guard;
- split squarefree core locators when translating gcd degree to roots.

## Nonclaims

- the field-sized determinant bound is a prize payment;
- pairwise distance controls the live Johnson-nonpositive tail;
- abstract constant-weight packings are realizable LS6 families;
- collective determinant compatibility or owner transport is solved.
