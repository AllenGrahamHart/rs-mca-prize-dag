# Audit

1. The support condition is `c+d<=11`, not `c+d<=12`.
2. The source stratum is nonempty, so an attaining carrier exists.
3. The outside budget is independent of `c` only after substituting the
   source carrier size into the common-root bound.
4. At `s=0`, annihilator-span containment uses `c+d<=11`.
5. A target circuit with `j` outside points is charged exactly `j` times.
6. The target-support multiplier is `C(m-d,11-d)`.
7. No zero circuit cap is assigned to targets when the source is empty.
