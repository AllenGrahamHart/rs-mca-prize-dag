- All 11 `S6` conjugacy classes are reconstructed from 720 permutations.
- Pair-action cycle types, class sizes, indices, and letter parity are
  derived rather than entered as unchecked labels.
- The residual multiset recursion is exhaustive at index 16.
- The final branch cycle is derived from product one, so no random tuple
  sampling occurs.
- Generated subgroup order is computed exactly for every product-one tuple.
- Both split `A6` 5-cycle classes are replayed.
- The primary certificate is checked canonically and hostile mutations are
  rejected; the audit verifier uses an independently written enumeration.
- Geometric existence is not promoted to challenge-field or source-star
  compatibility.
