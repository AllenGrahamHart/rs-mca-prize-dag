# Frontier

Attack the three three-point passports first:

```text
A6:  5.1, 2.2.1.1, 4.2
S6:  5.1, 2.1.1.1.1, 6
S6:  5.1, 2.2.2, 3.2.1
```

They are rigid finite branch-cycle problems after target normalization. For
each, construct or classify the rational map and test the imported conditions
that the pole points and one unramified 15-point zero fiber split over
`K=F_(p^6)`, then impose the quartic source-star correspondence.

Only after those rows are settled attack the four-point `S6` passport

```text
5.1, 2.1.1.1.1, 2.2.1.1, 2.2.2.
```

Its one-dimensional Hurwitz parameter must retain the same split-fiber and
source-star constraints; do not search arbitrary degree-15 maps.
