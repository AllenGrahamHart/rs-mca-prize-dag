# Proof

For a separable degree-15 rational map between projective lines,
Riemann--Hurwitz gives total branch-cycle index

```text
2*15-2=28.
```

The mandatory `5^3` pole cycle has index 12, leaving residual index 16.
Enumerating all 720 permutations of six letters gives the complete
nonidentity class table in the two-subset action:

```text
letter class   pair cycle type       index   parity
2.1.1.1.1      2^4 1^7                 4     odd
2.2.1.1        2^6 1^3                 6     even
2.2.2          2^6 1^3                 6     odd
3.1.1.1        3^4 1^3                 8     even
3.2.1          6 3^2 2 1              10     odd
3.3            3^5                    10     even
4.1.1          4^3 2 1                10     odd
4.2            4^3 2 1                10     even
5.1            5^3                    12     even
6              6^2 3                  12     odd
```

The product of the letter permutations is one, so the number of odd
residual classes is even. Exhausting unordered multisets from this table
whose indices sum to 16 leaves nine necessary passports: three all-even
`A6` candidates and six `S6` candidates.

Fix `g=(0 1 2 3 4)` for the mandatory pole. For each ordered version of a
necessary passport, enumerate every prefix from the printed conjugacy
classes and derive the last cycle uniquely from the product-one equation.
There are at most `15^3=3375` prefixes in any row. Compute the subgroup
generated by the resulting tuple. For the `A6` rows repeat with `g^2`, which
represents the other split 5-cycle class. The exact result is:

```text
ambient  residual classes                      product-one  generated order
S6       2.1^4,2.1^4,2.1^4,2.1^4                 125        120
S6       2.1^4,2.1^4,3.1^3                        25        120
S6       2.1^4,2.2.1^2,2.2.2                      25        720
S6       2.1^4,6                                    5        720
A6       2.2.1^2,3.3                                5         60
A6       2.2.1^2,4.2                               10        360
S6       2.2.2,3.2.1                                5        720
S6       2.2.2,4.1^2                                5        120
A6       3.1^3,3.1^3                                5         60
```

Both `A6` pole classes have the same counts and generated orders. Thus five
necessary passports generate only proper `A5` or `S5` subgroups and cannot
have the prescribed geometric monodromy. Every tuple in each of the other
four rows generates the required `A6` or `S6`.

By the Riemann existence theorem, a product-one generating tuple gives a
connected cover; the index sum makes its source genus zero, hence a rational
map over the algebraic closure. Therefore the four retained rows are exactly
the geometric genus-zero passport frontier. This proves the statement.
