# Proof

The canonical five-role matching atlas has fifteen cells.  Direct inspection
of singleton role and the two unordered antipodal pairs gives exactly the
six disjoint orbits in `(KB433C-1)`.

The aligned and crossed loop-singleton parents delete `[0]` and `[1,2]`.
The AB/AC finite classifier and complete-product exclusion delete `[3,6]`.
The mixed, BC-mixed, and opposite-pair parents delete `[4,5,7,8]`,
`[9,10,12,13]`, and `[11,14]`.  These sets are disjoint and their sizes
sum to `1+2+2+4+4+2=15`.  Every common matching therefore has a terminal
deletion, proving the sector empty. QED.
