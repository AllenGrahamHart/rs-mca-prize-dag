# Audit

1. Root signs are source square-root-lift signs, not the outside five-cycle
   sign.
2. Source projectivities pull back the complete component form; they do not
   assume pointwise invariance of the evaluation domain.
3. Independent sign flips use the loop as canonical root anchor and are
   asserted only for cells `3,...,14`.
4. Cell `0` needs the identical-`AB+` transposition; reciprocity alone flips
   both signs.
5. Orbit `[1,2]` has two sign classes, not one.  Their invariant is
   `epsilon_1 epsilon_2`.
6. Duplicate-role maps between other cells need no printed sign formula
   because source projectivities already join all four signs at each end.
7. Ten is a quotient count.  Only the `[5,8]` representative is proved
   empty.
