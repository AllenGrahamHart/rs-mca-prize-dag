# Lineage

The preceding affine router reconstructs `q` but intentionally leaves
`G_2`. This node returns to the original structural definitions. The
exceptional identity `X_*=0` fixes `Q_0=q^2/3`, after which the original
`Q_0` equation is linear in `G_2` with inherited unit coefficient `A`.

This is the structural analogue of the generic and `J_*!=0` exceptional
compilers, but it needs only two residual filters because the `Q_0` equation
is used as the `G_2` reconstruction.
