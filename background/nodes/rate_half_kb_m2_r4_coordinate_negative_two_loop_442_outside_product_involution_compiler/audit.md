# Audit

1. The involution is reconstructed independently in every row and sign as
   the cross product of its two known paired-product rows.
2. The forced product is checked homogeneously against all ten choices of
   three common rows, so no denominator chart is silently discarded.
3. Both protected factors are covered by exact iterated resultants; the
   exclusions `2,7,23` are stated rather than generalized away.
4. The singleton involution equation is reduced independently modulo all
   six exact row ideals.
5. Product injectivity, not a numerical root check, proves that the two
   known paired-product rows are independent and the involution is free.
6. The residual three equations compile only paired-product invariance.
   Full interpolation and quotient-label assignments remain open.
