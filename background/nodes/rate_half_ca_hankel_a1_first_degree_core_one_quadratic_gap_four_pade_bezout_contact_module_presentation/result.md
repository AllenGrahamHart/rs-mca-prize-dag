# Result

The Pade resultant has been upgraded from a determinant identity to an
exact local presentation. At every finite degree-preserving fiber, the
regular Hankel block is congruent to the Bezoutian of `(Q,P_F)`, and its
cokernel is the contact algebra `O_tau[X]/(Q,P_F)`. Complete Smith
classification can therefore be performed on the small local
intersection factor.
