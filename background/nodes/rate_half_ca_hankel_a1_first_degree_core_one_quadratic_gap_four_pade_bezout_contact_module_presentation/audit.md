# Audit

1. The leading coefficient is required to be a unit; otherwise `T_Q` is
   not a permitted local basis change.
2. The Bezout identity uses the same sign convention in the statement,
   proof, and verifier.
3. The kernel relation kills the `Q(Z)R` remainder because
   `deg_Z R<d`.
4. The result identifies a module, not only its determinant or length.
5. The verifier checks the Gram identity independently over two fields
   through degree six, the anti-triangular determinant formula, and 348
   independent comparisons of Bezout and multiplication Smith valuations.
