# Audit

1. A square whole-fiber quartic is enough here because both component
   factors are reduced quadratics on the same two-label support. There is no
   alternative reduced factorization of `P_(J_1)^2`.
2. The paired square follows from whole-fiber transport. No individual-star
   source-line lift is used.
3. The two square vertices are distinct because their supports lie in the
   disjoint sets `J` and `I`.
4. The eight `K_0` stars are reduced `J_0` edges and therefore use at most
   six, not sixteen, vertices.
5. Ramified source points count with multiplicity in the complete-source
   defect, so the proof does not require an unramified source orbit.
6. The result applies to both sides of the source-subfield dichotomy and
   does not inherit the preceding source-line-only scope.
