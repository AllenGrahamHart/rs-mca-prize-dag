# Audit

1. The unsafe cardinality is `B*+1`; this creates the exact residual mass in
   (2).
2. The low-span cap is applied once to the union of all nontransverse groups,
   after proving that every pair difference lies in the same `V_nt`. It is
   not summed once per rich container.
3. The interleaving theorem counts ordered pair types, while the MCA ledger
   counts slopes. The separate fixed-pair ownership factor `n-A` is retained.
4. The anchor is paid separately. Letting `M_6` also overcount it only makes
   the bound weaker and cannot invalidate the payment.
5. The `8406` count is taken after canonical merge and uses the larger
   dimension-three cap. It is a count of distinct promoted spaces.
6. The adjacent `h+1` calculation is a route wall, not an unsafe witness.
7. `verify.py` uses `math.comb`; `verify_audit.py` rebuilds binomials by
   numerator/denominator products and independently scans all cutoffs.
