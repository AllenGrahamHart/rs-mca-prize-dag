# Audit

- `eta` is the source-projection involution; `a,c` are the two endpoint
  coordinate involutions. They are not silently identified.
- The group `<eta,a>` is the deck group of the degree-four map to `W`, not
  the full-V4 quotient from `Gamma` to the outer component.
- Conjugation by `c` normalizes `<eta,a>` because it transports `W` by a
  target involution.
- The commuting-conjugation alternative is rejected only after invoking
  the proved line/conic coefficient-image exclusion.
- Fixed sets are disjoint because the action is tame and a point stabilizer
  on a smooth curve is cyclic.
- The proof obtains `g<=1`; it does not infer `g=0` or delete either row.
- No ledger quantity moves.
