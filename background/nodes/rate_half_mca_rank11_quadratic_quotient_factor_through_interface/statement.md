# Quadratic quotient factor-through interface

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** the cyclic and dihedral outputs of the synchronized quadratic
  exception pencil

In the scalar-locator normal form

```text
u+gamma v=(c_0+c_1 gamma)L_(E_gamma),
deg u,deg v<=2,       gcd(u,v)=1,
```

put `f=-u/v` as a projective rational map. There is a base-field Mobius map
`M in PGL_2(F)` such that exactly one of the quotient alternatives has the
following form:

```text
antipodal:        f(X)=M(X^2),
constant product: f(X)=M(X+kappa/X),       kappa in H.       (QF1)
```

Thus the bad-slope parameter map itself factors through the cyclic quotient
`X -> X^2` or the dihedral quotient `X -> X+kappa/X`. This is an exact
quadratic instance of the proposed quotient-periodic inverse theorem.

The theorem identifies the rational certificate seen by the retained owner
ledger. It does not bound or pay its first-owned record mass.

## Falsifier

Dependent `u,v` despite two disjoint fibers; a cyclic pencil whose span is not
`<X^2,1>`; a constant-product pencil whose span is not
`<X^2+kappa,X>`; failure of invariance under the corresponding involution; or
using factor-through as a payment.
