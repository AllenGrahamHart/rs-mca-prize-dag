# Result

collision chambers. Every chamber with at least two repeated colors is the
even two-antipodal-pair branch and carries the additional equation
`rho*c*(18+(c-1)-(c-1)^2)+192=0`.

The next closure attempt should intersect that equation with the proved
`h=7` conic before attacking the collision-free and one-repeat chambers.
