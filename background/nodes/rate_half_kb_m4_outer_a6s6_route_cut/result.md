# Result

PROVED. The `m=4` types with outer subdegree 1, 2, or 4 force a proper
degree-3 or degree-5 right factor of the outer map and hence impossible
inner degree 12 or 20. Only `(r,delta)=(8,2)` survives, with primitive
outer monodromy `A6` or `S6` on two-subsets. The independent transverse
frontier has nine types.
