# Audit

1. The certificate consumes the full degree-16 signed-pair resultant, not a
   selected factor or a sampled sufficient condition.
2. Every pseudo-reduction records its step count and only introduces a
   power of the printed plane-leading coefficient.
3. Proportionality is established by a zero quotient-ring cross identity.
4. The degree-2752 leading norm is factored exactly; all linear factors are
   replayed and all nonlinear factors are irreducible over the base field.
5. Zero specialized resultants are accepted only when the corresponding
   `b`, `b-1`, or `b+1` localization guard is printed.
6. Every deployed root at the sole proper leading fiber has an explicit
   product, denominator, or source-label guard.
7. All six compact-chart scales are factored exactly, and their five linear
   fibers independently replay to the unit ideal in the original system.
8. Mutation tests reject altered identities, norm roots, scale roots,
   finite guards, input custody, and DAG status.
