# Audit

The ordering `(alpha,beta,gamma)` records multiplicities `(3,2,1)`. A color
permutation is not harmless unless the role assignment and `lambda` are
updated with it.

The resultant remembers only the multiset of `F` values on roots of `G`.
It is a necessary consequence of the actual assignment, not a converse to
the rootwise Frobenius equation.

Implementations must retain `B!=0`, distinct colors, exact gcd degrees, and
squarefreeness. A retained factor packet still owes norm, pointwise
Frobenius, cyclotomic, and inner checks.
