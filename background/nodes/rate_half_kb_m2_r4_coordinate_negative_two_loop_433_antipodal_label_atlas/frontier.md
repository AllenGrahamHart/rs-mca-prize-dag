# Frontier

Normalize `A=1`, `B=b`, `C=c`, with products
`(-1,-c^2,b,-b,bc)` on `(A,C,+,-,BC)`.  For each of the nine label cells,
impose all five Mobius product minors and the remaining weld connecting
`BC` to one `AB` row.  Saturate by label and signed-pair distinctness; do not
return to the 240 labeled atlas.
