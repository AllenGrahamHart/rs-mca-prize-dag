| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | Every actual negative `(4,3,3)` two-loop packet obeys `k_C^2=k_+k_-`, and the antipodal five-set condition reduces the normalized labels to exactly the nine cells `(KB43-3)`. |
| deletion interface | Every role assignment outside the nine cells is deleted before product-map elimination. |
| assumptions | The coordinate source-facet five-set, the `(1,0,1;2,0,1)` skeleton, and the exact two-loop weld. |
| nonclaim | Any retained cell is a product/q packet; complement-fiber completion; another skeleton; positive parity; an owner/payment; a row; or either Prize theorem. |
