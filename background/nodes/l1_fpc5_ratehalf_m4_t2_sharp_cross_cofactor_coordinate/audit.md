# Audit

1. The anchor must be an exact contributor so that its cofactor pair is
   primitive and its defect locator is monic, squarefree, and core-split.
2. Division by `L_0` uses the guarded congruence for both pairs; it is not a
   generic cofactor determinant identity.
3. The affine bijection covers the guarded monic linear slice, not the exact
   contributor set.
4. The gcd equality is proved only against the fixed anchor locator `F_0`.
5. `E=0` is the anchor itself; all fixed-owner bounds concern distinct
   neighbors.
6. The theorem coalesces the coordinate body but does not count its split
   points or its realized gcd strata.
