# Claim contract

- **Inputs:** the exact sharp guarded cofactor slice and one exact primitive
  anchor; the specialized balanced determinant owner dictionary.
- **Output:** an affine bijection from the complete guarded monic slice to
  degree-at-most-`ell-3` polynomials `E`, with exact owner identity
  `gcd(E,F_0)=gcd(F,F_0)` for every distinct contributor.
- **Preserved filters:** splitness on the core, candidate-wise primitivity,
  untouched-petal nonagreement, and first ownership remain explicit tests on
  the reconstructed point.
- **Nonclaim:** a split-point count, aggregate gcd-owner bound, or FPC5
  payment.
