# Audit

The hypothesis is `t in F_p`, not merely `z in F_p`. After the `z=-1`
points are removed, the conic line formula makes this equivalent to
`z,w in F_p`; only then is `theta` known to be prime-field valued.

The non-prime-field invariant is load-bearing. Without it, the split
quadratics at `z=-1` or `z in {1,3}` would not be contradictions.

The two larger-row signs are candidates, not witnesses. Their existence,
first-three/full trace equations, cyclotomic divisibility, and inner lifts
are all unproved. No conclusion is made on `t notin F_p` or on `h=15`.
