# Frontier

The nonzero affine-reflection class can account for at most 67,348,594 of the
322,359,637 triple-owner records unless a high-complexity packet already
appears. The live rational frontier therefore contains one fixed pencil with
at least 4,370 disjoint fibers outside that class.

The downstream quadratic Mobius router now removes the degree-two
extension-field ambiguity and distinguishes:

1. antipodal and constant-product quotient pencils;
2. shifted inversions, which would need at least 8,740 nonfixed graph points;
3. degree one;
4. degree `3..11` decomposable or extension-normalized pullbacks; and
5. primitive pencils in those remaining degrees.

A 6,144-parameter official shifted-inversion probe found maximum 2,336, but
this is evidence rather than a uniform cap.

The published subgroup-curve bound used on the much larger Mersenne domain
does not close this KoalaBear threshold: its `32N^(2/3)` ceiling exceeds the
8,740 ordered graph points supplied here. A sharper official-field census,
retained factor ownership, or a different incidence argument is required.
