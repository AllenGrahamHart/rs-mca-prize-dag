# Attack notes

The main false shortcut was to treat a projective-plane intersection with a
secant variety as finite by Bezout.  Special planes can contain
positive-dimensional secant loci, so that route was rejected.

The replacement attack counts circuit completions of an independent set.
Generalized MDS gives the sharp coarse ceiling three.  Equality is useful:
the three labels span the entire quotient and force every sparse label into
one carrier of size at most seven.  Outside that equality case, the ceiling
drops to two and the deletion double-count is payable.

The theorem is falsified by a fourth completion, by failure of the carrier
conclusion after three completions, or by a full-rank eleven-set retaining
two completion labels.
