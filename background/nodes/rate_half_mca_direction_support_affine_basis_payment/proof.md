# Refutation

The numerator subtraction in the former proof is valid: a full basis of
normals must include a coordinate where the minimum-lift direction is
nonzero.  The denominator is not valid.  It was copied from the refuted
affine-span incidence compiler and assumes far more incident bases than
local pair noncontainment supplies.

In the `GF(1009)` counterexample, direct substitution gives

```text
floor((100*99-20*19)/(21*20))=22<31.
```

The same record satisfies minimum-lift support `e=80`, transformed affine
rank one, exact maximal supports, and pair noncontainment.  It therefore
refutes the printed theorem at its stated field-general scope.
