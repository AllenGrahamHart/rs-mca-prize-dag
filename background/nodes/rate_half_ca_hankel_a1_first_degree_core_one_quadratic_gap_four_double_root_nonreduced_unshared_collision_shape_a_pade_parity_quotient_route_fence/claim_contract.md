# Claim contract

- **Claim:** `J U_Q` is the mandatory `e+1` dimensional right-radical block
  inside `E_3`, and the remaining common-kernel freedom is measured exactly
  by `xi=dim(E_3 intersect J S_d)-(e+1)`.
- **Inputs:** the global Padé identity, the source-multiplier normal form,
  primitive locator coefficient independence, and standard split-RS residue
  duality.
- **Output:** `rank T=dim ker Phi-xi` and, at the boundary,
  `kappa=e-3+xi`.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaim:** no Shape-A exclusion and no assertion that `xi` vanishes or
  is positive.
- **Falsifier:** failure of monic cancellation in `A[z]`, a dependent
  primitive locator coefficient row, or a right radical larger or smaller
  than `E_3 intersect J S_d`.
