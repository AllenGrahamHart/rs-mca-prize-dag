# Claim contract

- **Claim:** `(FAS1)--(FAS3)` hold under the printed fixed-union hypotheses.
- **Quantifiers:** the union and vanishing subspace are fixed before the
  target circuit; `2<=d<=g-1`, `R>=0`, and `N>=R+d-1` are mandatory.
- **Composition:** only support-disjoint adjacent pair bounds may be added.
- **Nonclaim:** overlapping pairs such as `5/6` and `6/7` are not charged
  simultaneously.
