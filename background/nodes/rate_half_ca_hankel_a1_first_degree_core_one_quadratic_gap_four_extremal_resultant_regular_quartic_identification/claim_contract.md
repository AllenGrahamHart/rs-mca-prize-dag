# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the four-core eliminant is exactly the regular-block quartic.
- **Inputs:** the exact four-core, Pade regular-factor identity, all-excess
  fiber factorization, and extremal row-incidence ledger.
- **Outputs:** `(ERQ1)--(ERQ5)`.
- **Quantifiers:** both extremal `d_A` profiles and both quadratic root arms.
- **Falsifier:** a missing center cancellation, an off-line exponent other
  than `n-a_delta`, or a residual parameter root outside the correction
  forms.
- **Nonclaim:** no root arm is excluded solely by this eliminant.
