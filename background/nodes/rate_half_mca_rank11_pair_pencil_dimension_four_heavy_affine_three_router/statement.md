# Dimension-four heavy affine-three router

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** the scalar-dimension-four quotient pair-pencil branch

Let `J` be the common received-pair core arising from the gcd of the
four-dimensional scalar polynomial space. At least one of the following
route outputs holds.

1. **Large common core.** One has

   ```text
   |J|>=319539.                                      (D4-1)
   ```

   The reversible common-core shortening applies, preserving first
   ownership, quotient deficiency two, and `m-K=67472`.

2. **Heavy affine-three coordinate fiber.** Some coordinate outside the
   scalar gcd belongs to at least 234 quotient pair cores. Their scalar
   points lie in one affine three-space and do not lie in any affine plane.
   Thus they have affine span exactly three. Since every selected type owns
   at least 29 records, this fiber carries at least

   ```text
   234*29=6786                                        (D4-2)
   ```

   first-owned records, all whose exact supports contain the exhibited
   coordinate.

## Falsifier

A dimension-four family with common core below 319539 and every noncommon
coordinate in at most 233 pair cores; 234 fiber types lying in an affine
plane; or failure of the `6786` first-owned record floor.
