# Audit

1. Parallel full affine lines share a projective direction. Their combined
   coordinate count, not each line separately, is bounded by `deg T_eta`.
2. Every full coordinate has exactly one affine fiber and therefore exactly
   one projective direction.
3. The lower bound 210 applies to distinct directions, hence also to
   distinct full lines.
4. The saturation ratio uses 218 possible directions and is minimized at
   `K'=5025`.
5. A characteristic-zero Hirzebruch inequality would contradict the dual
   pair budget, but no such inequality is imported into the official finite
   field without a proved transfer theorem.
