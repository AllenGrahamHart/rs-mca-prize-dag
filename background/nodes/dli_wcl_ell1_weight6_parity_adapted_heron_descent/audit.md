# Audit

1. Product parity is exponent-sum parity, not the sign of a chosen lift.
2. Even total parity makes both parity-class cardinalities even; odd total
   parity makes both odd. This is what pays zero versus one mixed pair.
3. A same-parity pair product lies in `Q(zeta_256)` even when its internally
   signed pair sum does not.
4. In the odd sector the two conjugate Heron factors must be multiplied
   before descending to `K_0`; selecting one alone is not a base-field norm.
5. The deterministic pairing is applied after the affine-Galois canonical
   representative is fixed. No equivariance of a raw sorting convention is
   claimed.
6. The descent reduces field degree, not the number of unsigned support
   orbits. No compute authorization follows automatically.
