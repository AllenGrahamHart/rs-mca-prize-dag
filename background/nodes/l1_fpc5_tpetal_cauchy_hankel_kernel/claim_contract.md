# Claim contract

- **Input:** one owner-free weighted Cauchy divisor chart whose pair slice
  contains a saturated primitive monic anchor.
- **Output:** the exact full-row-rank Hankel kernel, its rational generating
  function and recurrence, the split-divisor predicate, and the punctured
  Hankel primitive guard.
- **Preserved:** actual petal labels and locators, source-core divisor,
  locator coefficients, numerator-degree condition, primitivity, and
  background Cauchy tests.
- **Nonclaim:** a split-divisor count, generic Hankel flatness, ordinary
  prefix flatness, base-field-normalized payment, first ownership, or a
  deployed row.
