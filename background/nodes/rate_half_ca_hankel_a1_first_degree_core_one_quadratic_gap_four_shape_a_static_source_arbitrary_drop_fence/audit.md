# Audit

1. The matrix has size `d+1`; rank `d` is exact corank one, not merely
   the existence of the displayed kernel vector.
2. The residue pairing is restricted to degree less than `d`, where
   evaluation at the `d` roots of `Q` is invertible.
3. All residue coefficients are nonzero: `Q` is squarefree and its roots
   avoid the roots of both `G` and `L`.
4. The infinity residue vanishes with degree gap at least four.
5. The Lagrange identity is used at degree `R-1` for the first nonzero
   defect; using the zero formula at that endpoint would be false.
6. For `q=0`, no initial defect is forced to vanish.
7. The construction is static. It does not satisfy the global
   parameter-linear or split-fiber constraints and therefore is not a
   counterexample to Shape A or to the Prize claim.
