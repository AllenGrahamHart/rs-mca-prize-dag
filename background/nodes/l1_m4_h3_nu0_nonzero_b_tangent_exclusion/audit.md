# Audit - L1 m=4, h=3, nu=0 nonzero-b tangent exclusion

1. `a!=0`, `b!=0`, and squarefreeness of the outer cubic make every
   denominator in `(NTE1)` nonzero.
2. The identity `g(y_0)=b Delta/(8a^3)` includes the sign convention
   `Delta=-4a^3-27b^2`.
3. The tangent evaluation uses `U=R` at `nu=0`; no power of `X` is omitted.
4. Every distinct root of `T`, including repeated tangent roots, is caught by
   the level-set equation.
5. `T'` is nonzero because the Euler node proves `XR'` nonzero.
6. The radical lower bound is exact multiplicity bookkeeping:
   `deg gcd(T,T')=p-deg rad(T)`.
7. For `h>0`, subtracting `kappa` does not cancel the leader of `H`.
8. The `h=0` conclusion is equality of constants, not exclusion.
9. The constant-case equation uses both zero-point identities and does not
   divide by `R(0)`.
10. The `b=0` arm is deliberately retained because then `y_0=0` and
   `g(y_0)=0`, so division in `(NTE1)` is invalid.
