# Claim contract

## Consumed content

- The prize field floor supplies the exact lower endpoint for `p`.
- Prime-field reduction supplies `p=1 mod 256` and the fixed reduction prime
  `P_r`.
- The full-unit theorem and the two height-collapse arguments apply after
  equal principal ideals have been established.
- The profile-(3,6) payment supplies the residual binding-row ledger and the
  exact profile-`(2,10)` dictionary weight.

## New proved content

1. The local valuation set `mu={1,...,10}` for ten singleton coefficients.
2. The exact thirteen-cofactor list after the residue-degree sieve.
3. The partition into ten pure and `3*128` split-prime ideal families.
4. At most one shift/sign orbit in each fixed ideal family.
5. The coarse profile bounds `T_210<=394`, `|D_210|<=100864`, and the exact
   associated edge charge.

## Guards

- Different primes `Q_s` above `257` or `769` are not unit associates and are
  not merged.
- The theorem bounds families; it proves neither collision existence nor
  split-prime occupancy.
- The edge charge is a coarse upper bound and does not close the residual
  weighted ledger.
- No claim is made for profiles of square mass greater than `18`.
