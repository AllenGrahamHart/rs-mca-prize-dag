This split uses the official congruence `p=7 mod 8`; it is not a
characteristic-zero factorization. The auxiliary square root `s` lies in
`F_p`, and changing `s` to `-s` only exchanges the two factors in every
signed pair.

The 21 systems form a disjunction. Do not impose their role equations
simultaneously. The last three conjugate products equal `2P_j`, not `P_j`;
two is a unit on the declared rows, so their zero loci agree.

Irreducibility uses the actual color assignment and Frobenius inversion. It
does not supply an assignment-preserving converse from a retained p-free
point. Every branch still owes the inherited saturations, exact gcd degree,
outer torsion/cyclotomic conditions, and inner split-pencil lift.
