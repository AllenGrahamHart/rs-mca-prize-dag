# Proof

For matchings `0,1,2`, the first residual pair is common.  Omitting the
positive `DE` record forces `P(m,-m)=0`; omitting `-DE` forces
`P(-m,-m)=0`, with `m=B(-t^2)/A(-t^2)`.

Reduce both cuts in the boundary-free row-6 tower of the elliptic common
kernel and take the four-dimensional multiplication norm to `F_p(r)`.  The
opposite-cut norm has numerator/denominator degrees `272/144` and 13 deployed
candidate roots per source-sign row.  The equal-negative norm has degrees
`284/148` and 15 candidate roots per source-sign row.

Direct replay against all seven common equations classifies every candidate.
The opposite cut has no ordinary zero.  Each equal-negative row has exactly
two ordinary zeros.  Across the eight rows, replay classifies 112 finite
points as 88 nonzeros, 16 impossible missing-value branches, and eight zeros;
it also records 72 route-boundary branches and 56 failed `b` lifts.  Nothing
remains unresolved. QED.
