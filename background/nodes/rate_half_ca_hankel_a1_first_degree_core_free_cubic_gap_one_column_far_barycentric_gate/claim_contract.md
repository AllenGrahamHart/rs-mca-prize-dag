# Claim contract

- **Claim:** column-farness strengthens every support difference to
  `c_alpha+1`, killing the prior minimum-cardinality branch. At union exactly
  `rho+1`, the first-jet radical forces the exact barycentric identities
  `(CBG5)--(CBG6)` with nonzero scalar.
- **Currency:** column-farness, exact unique-error supports, the two-slope
  source pairing, and the one-dimensional Vandermonde nullspace.
- **Dependencies:** split-pencil equivalence and the two-slope source
  Vandermonde gate.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted; all displayed roots are distinct domain
  points and the affine slope difference is nonzero.
- **Nonclaim:** no existence of a minimum-union pair, no uniqueness for a
  larger union, and no packet exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a supported pair with union at most `rho`, or a
  minimum-union transverse pair whose error values violate `(CBG6)`.
- **Next gate:** combine `(CBG6)` with the heavy-row cube/Forney values, and
  derive the second coefficient-jet equation for larger-union pairs.
