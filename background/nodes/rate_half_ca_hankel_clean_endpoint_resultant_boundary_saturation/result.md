# Result

The unit-resultant gate is completely saturated at parameter infinity:

```text
Res_z(Q,B)=constant*q_inf^(T+b)*(X-x_0),
Res_z(Q,W)=constant*(X-x_0)^(m-1),
deg_X B=N.
```

Thus the low parameter degree of `B` is paid by maximal domain degree and by
the entire infinity fibre. The next useful input cannot be a boundary-free
gonality argument; it must exploit the Hankel coefficient relations.
