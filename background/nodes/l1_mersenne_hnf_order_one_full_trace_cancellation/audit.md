# Audit

The cancellation uses the full order-one Frobenius data
`d_star=zeta/d` and `zeta^m=1`. It is not valid for an arbitrary formal star
parameter, and it must not be exported outside that gate.

The parity of `m` is load-bearing in removing the two minus signs. Both
official values, `m=8,16`, are even. The root is removed once, not once per
coefficient equation, and no extra orientation or multiplicity factor occurs.

The first three equations remain only necessary. Using all `H` trace
equalities recovers the complete reduced reciprocal identity through the
triangular Newton equivalence, but even that identity is only an outer
necessary condition until pointwise Frobenius, torsion, full cyclotomic
divisibility, and the inner lift are imposed.
