# Proof

The direct cell nodes prove `F04-R02` and `F07-R02` empty. The complete
fixed-literal inversion theorem is an isomorphism of named-open systems and
pairs `F04` with `F05` and `F06` with `F07` for every target. Transport the
`F04-R02` exclusion to `F05-R02`. Since inversion is involutive, transport
the `F07-R02` exclusion in the reverse direction to `F06-R02`. These are the
three previously open fixed `R02` cells. QED.
