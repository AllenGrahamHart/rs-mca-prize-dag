- **claim:** the exact saturation/cardinality axioms, pairwise union bound
  `(OV)`, and individual distance spend `(C2)` used by `(NEWCAP)` do not
  imply the exact max-intersection bound `|S_gamma intersect W|<=2m`;
- **scope:** an explicit incidence system at `m=64`, with the complete
  endpoint parameter tuple printed in `statement.md`;
- **load-bearing certificate:** the 1024-bit mask in
  `experiments/prize_resolution/rh_type2_fr_incidence_m64_result.json`;
- **consumer use:** retire an incidence-only proof of `(FR)` and require a
  Hankel/GRS-algebraic input for the type-2 residual;
- **not claimed:** realizability by a strict-`A=3` Hankel pencil, falsity of
  `(FR)` on such pencils, a bound on the official slope count, movement of
  either rate-half crossing budget, or a critical status change;
- **falsifier:** any failed block size, degree deficit, pairwise union,
  spend, mask digest, or intersection computation in either verifier.
