# Result

The numerical no-go profile is no longer structureless. At the next affine
dimensions, its exact-packed branch reduces to four fixed split members of
the primitive pencil and a tail of at most seven points. The resulting plane
census is below the local allowance for every possible tail on all three
rows at the next affine dimensions.

Mixed profiles still require a common weighted ledger, and higher affine
dimensions remain open; this theorem is not an additive payment for those
complements.
