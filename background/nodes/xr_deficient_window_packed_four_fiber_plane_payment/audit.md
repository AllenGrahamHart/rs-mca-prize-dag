# Audit

- A size-`ell` block part is the complete global `phi` fiber, not merely a
  large subset, because the proved fiber cap is also `ell`.
- The four full fibers are fixed across the stratum: five disjoint full
  fibers cannot fit in `D` at the official slack.
- The two singleton tail points may have the same `phi` value as each other.
  Each is nevertheless distinct from the two full-fiber values in its own
  block, so the common-ray triple remains valid.
- Counting individual blocks avoids choosing a complete pairing: there are
  exactly `binom(4,2)t=6t` block geometries, and every target contributes two
  distinct block occurrences. The factor two is not a symmetry quotient.
- `(P4F4)` bounds the exact-packed stratum only. A mixed family requires one
  shared weighted or first-match budget and cannot sum independent copies of
  the local allowance.
- The arithmetic closes no higher affine dimension and changes no critical
  status.
