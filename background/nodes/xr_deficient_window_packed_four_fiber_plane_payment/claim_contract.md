# Claim contract

| field | value |
|---|---|
| claim | The exact-packed four-fiber normal form, plane owner cap `(P4F3)`, aggregate cap `(P4F4)`, and printed next-dimension arithmetic. |
| inputs | Two-block kernel-slack router, `phi`-fiber cap, generalized-weight core cuts, and the plane common-ray eliminant. |
| output | A `6t` individual-block census, plane cap `9t`, and exact stratum payments for every official tail. |
| load-bearing hypotheses | Boundary `r=2ell+1`; both selected blocks have exact profile `(ell,ell,1)`; affine dimension is the printed `s` for the official payment. |
| falsifier | A fifth full fiber in `D`, a geometry or plane-owner excess, or an official arithmetic mismatch. |
| nonclaim | Caps for different profile strata are not additive without a shared budget ledger. |
