# Audit

1. The involution coefficients are reconstructed directly as a cross product
   of the two known paired-product rows.
2. Its determinant factor uses only original product and signed-pair guards.
3. The forced mate is evaluated from the homogeneous Mobius cofactor vector,
   so no affine normalization boundary is lost.
4. Both numerator and denominator are protected by exact resultant `2^32`;
   the odd-characteristic hypothesis is necessary.
5. The three residual bilinear rows are equivalent only to the paired-product
   rank gate.  Full twelve-label interpolation remains a separate condition.
6. The aligned/unaligned location split does not assert which edge type
   occurs at an unaligned `xi`.
