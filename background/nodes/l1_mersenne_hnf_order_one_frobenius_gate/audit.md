# Audit

The gate applies only to `ord_0(T)=1`; its nonzero penultimate coefficient
is essential and is included in the saturation factor.

The discrete variable `zeta` is not assumed to lie in the prime field by
descent. It lies in `mu_m`, which is contained in the official field because
`m|n` and the domain contains `mu_n`. Equation (OFG3) is an identity for an
actual survivor, not a parameterization of all bounded-system points.

The reciprocal identity remains valid with repeated `m`th-power roots. It is
necessary only: setwise Frobenius inversion does not imply the pointwise
cyclotomic equations.

The cancelled factor is exact: it comes from the unique zero split value,
and `zeta^m=1` proves that Frobenius sends its `m`th power to its reciprocal.
No unknown resultant factor is discarded.

Any unit saturation must clear and track the displayed denominators exactly.
A nonunit component, a bounded-system point, or a toy-field point is not an
outer split-pencil witness.
