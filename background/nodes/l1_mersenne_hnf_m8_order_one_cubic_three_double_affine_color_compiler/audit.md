# Audit

The safe equation is the full resultant (TAC2). The degree-seven statement
concerns its primitive characteristic-zero squarefree radical in the two
weighted variables `P^3,Q^2`. Reduce the primitive model first and then
squarefree it in an official characteristic; do not divide by a discriminant
or assume that the seven factors remain distinct modulo every official prime.

An earlier draft incorrectly merged the two scalene reflection pairs and
reported five factors. Euclidean reflection is not a complex affine map for
a scalene triple: it conjugates rather than preserves `Q^2/P^3`. The explicit
values (4) and `Theta_8` are the repaired statement.

The homogeneous form handles `P=0`; an implementation must not introduce
`Q^2/P^3` without splitting that locus. Pairwise-distinct colors and the
nonzero interpolant leader remain inherited saturations.

Equation (TAC3) is necessary, not sufficient, over the base field: equality
of the depressed-cubic invariant need not by itself produce the required
base-field affine scale. Retained points still owe the exact color, norm,
Frobenius, cyclotomic-converse, and inner-lift filters.

This compiler does not close the generic branch or either exceptional
branch. It only adds the previously omitted symmetric color equation without
restoring `u_1,u_2,u_3` or enumerating 42 ordered roles.
