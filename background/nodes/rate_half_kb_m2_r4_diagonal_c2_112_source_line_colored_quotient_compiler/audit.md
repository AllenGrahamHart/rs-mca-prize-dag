# Audit

1. The descent uses individual-star equivariance and is source-line-only.
2. The four transported stars exhaust the universal `I-J` category; this is
   what identifies `C_H`, not merely containment in two fibers.
3. Squarefreeness of `C_H` excludes ramification over `Omega`; it does not
   exclude ramification at the forced square fiber.
4. In the near-aligned case `Omega={xi,ell}` need not be a `tau` orbit.
5. The quotient-resultant identities are obtained by faithful pullback and
   do not identify individual factors beyond the printed divisors.
6. The exceptional `(KBDM-10)` and biquadratic branches remain outside scope.
