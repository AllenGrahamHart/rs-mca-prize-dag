# Audit

- The distinguished counts are `e-c_i`, not the deficits `c_i`.
- `Z_1` and `Z_2` are not assumed disjoint. Their overlap gives rank loss
  two and error weight `rho-2` at that slope.
- `C_tot=I_H` and simple-new excess roots exclude any additional rank loss.
- Full split locator blocks may contain one or two padded heavy roots; the
  actual error supports omit them.
- The line cap charges the sum of rank losses on the whole line, while the
  pairwise consequence uses only the deficits already present in the pair.
- No balanced-design or positivity assumption is used.
