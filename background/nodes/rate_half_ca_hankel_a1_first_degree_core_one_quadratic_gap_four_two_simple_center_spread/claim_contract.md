# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the two-simple `u=4` packet has the exact block degree sequence
  `(TSS3)`, exact error deficit `r_gamma`, center-line cap `(TSS5)`, and
  pairwise triple-expander count `(TSS6)`.
- **Currency:** zero omission, the `{3,9}` correction normal form, unique
  decoding, and column-farness.
- **Dependencies:** the quadratic gap-four root normal forms and the
  split-pencil equivalence.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** overlap of `Z_1,Z_2`, field-valued Forney data, and existence
  or exclusion of the design remain open.
- **Falsifier:** a wrong heavy row degree, an error weight other than
  `rho-r_gamma`, or a center line violating `(TSS5)`.
- **Next gate:** constrain the overlap and field-valued center assignment, or
  construct a support-only witness to fence those layers separately.
