# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the complete five-strip F-4 minimal-record ledger has strict
  size below `16n^3`, conditional on the wired h=3 and h>=4 inputs.
- **Rows:** official X4/LIST corridor rows covered by all four stratum inputs.
- **Object:** records satisfying equality through `e_(h-1)` in the fixed
  `u1` orientation/counting convention.
- **Nonclaim:** all general order-`t` records are minimal; every primitive
  list member enters this ledger; quotient rows automatically transport; or
  the final LIST numerator is below the prize threshold.
- **Consumer rule:** this is optional evidence for the direct primitive
  column target.  To use the minimal route, combine it with a strip-aware
  general-to-minimal injection; the exact-list consumer itself requires the
  resulting local column bound and final summed-budget theorem.
