# Audit

- The two simple outer poles are distinct, so their totally ramified
  preimages have distinct target values.
- Both ramification points are deployed-field source roots; this makes the
  domain normalization `phi` deployed-field rational even if `h` itself is
  only geometric.
- Equality of `h`-values is unchanged by the geometric target normalization.
- `p^6-1=3 mod 5`, so no nontrivial fifth root of unity is present.
- The contradiction uses five distinct rational active points in one fiber,
  not coefficient-field descent of `h`.
