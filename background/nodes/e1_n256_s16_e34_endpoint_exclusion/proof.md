# Proof

The three-profile theorem proves that every pair-feasible `V=68` collision has
one of `(6,7)`, `(9,4,1)`, `(12,1,2)`. The parity theorem excludes the latter
two, leaving `(6,7)` and its light-Sidon geometry.

The heavy-template theorem partitions every remaining vector into exactly one
of four exhaustive cases: quarter, nonquarter diameter, progression, or
generic. The quarter-template exclusion closes the first case. The
nonquarter-diameter exclusion closes the second, the progression exclusion
closes the third, and the generic exclusion closes the fourth. No case
remains, so `V=68` is impossible.

The earlier variance reductions leave only positive even variance. Removing
68 therefore sharpens the residual range to `V<=66`. QED.
