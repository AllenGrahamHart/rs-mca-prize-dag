# Audit

Equation (GLD6) replaces `P_5` only while `P_4` is retained. The linear
equation alone is not equivalent to the fourth and fifth coefficients.

The `C_1=0` locus is a required chart, not a denominator accident. It keeps
both `C_1=C_0=0`, all four residual equations, `Delta*W!=0`, and inherited
saturations.

On `C_1!=0`, reconstructing `d=-C_0/C_1` also requires the cleared
numerators of `d`, `q-d`, `Delta`, and `W` to remain nonzero. Multiplication
by powers of `C_1` is reversible only with this chart saturation.

The four equations in three variables are an exact necessary endpoint, not
a dimension proof or a unit certificate. Every official role packet is an
alternative branch and must be handled separately.
