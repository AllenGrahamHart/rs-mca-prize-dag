# Claim contract

## Imported facts

- Cofactor 64 forces multiplicity six in the six-singleton parity support.
- The energy-adaptive parent leaves `E=2,...,65`.
- Translation, odd Galois multiplication, and global sign preserve the
  collision question.
- The bounded-product extremum may be specialized at exact autocorrelation
  energy `E` and `L1` norm `L`.

## New theorem

An exact product certificate, separate complete atlases for primitive and
all-one-parity multiplicity-six supports, two complete radius engines, and
rigorously audited fixed-point root intervals exhaust every cofactor-64
vector. Every norm is strictly below or above the prize interval, so the
cofactor-64 collision class is empty.

## Exclusions

1. The primitive `{0,1}` atlas does not cover all-one-parity singleton
   supports. Those are divided by two and exhaustively classified at exact
   multiplicity three in `Z/64`.
2. The radius syndrome is only a necessary filter. Every hit is followed by
   exact integer autocorrelation replay.
3. Product-live filtering is conservative in `L`; it can retain extra vectors
   but cannot discard a possible collision.
4. Long-double norm values are diagnostic only. Closure uses integer interval
   products from an Arb-audited root table.
5. This removes one cofactor, not the full profile or aggregate pair budget.
