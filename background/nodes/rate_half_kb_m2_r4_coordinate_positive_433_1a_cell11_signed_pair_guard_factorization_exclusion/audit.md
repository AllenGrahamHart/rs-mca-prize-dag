# Audit

1. The certificate consumes the complete degree-16 resultant.
2. Every pseudo-reduction records its step count and only introduces a
   power of the printed plane-leading coefficient.
3. Proportionality is a zero quotient-ring cross identity.
4. The degree-2664 leading norm is factored exactly; all base-field roots
   are assigned to the main atlas or scale ledger.
5. Both non-scale `b=-1` fibers and all twelve roots at the proper norm
   fiber have explicit original guards.
6. Every compact-kernel scale root is replayed in the original localized
   common ideal, including the two nontrivial values.
7. Mutation tests reject changes to custody, identities, root ledgers,
   scale units, finite guards, and DAG status.
