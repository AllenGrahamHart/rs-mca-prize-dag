# Attack

This router does not improve the endpoint merely by expanding its numerator:
the descended degree-67 factor has 162,322 terms, only modestly below the
existing coefficient-one degree-70 factor with 182,336 terms.

A direct rank-two `c/d` quotient implementation reproduced the F04-R02
cubic/J11 base curve with a 36-element, dimension-one basis, then timed out
at 900 seconds before emitting any mapped logarithmic component. Do not
repeat the monolithic rational map.

The four degree-7/11 denominator branches are closed by the dedicated child
exclusion. Splitting the thirteen logarithmic contributions was also tested:
all seven expected contributions and the observed `b,1/b` pair completed,
but observed `2,1/2,c,d` still timed out at 900 seconds after reproducing the
route basis. Do not retry contribution-level quotient mapping.

The successful replacement evaluates the descended numerator directly. Its
degree in `w` is only 15, so homogeneous Horner reduction modulo the route
basis avoids both rational mapping and expanded substitution. The dedicated
child proves this guarded numerator branch empty for `R02` and `R20`; the
composition child therefore closes both `F04` cubic/full-`J` routes.
