# Statement

Let the F04 full-`J` identity compare an observed product `O(W)` with an
expected product `E(W)`, up to a nonzero scalar. On the chart where every
individual constant factor is nonzero, product equality implies

```text
O'(0)/O(0) = E'(0)/E(0).                         (KBLD-1)
```

For an observed factor

```text
g(W)=u(W)^2-W v(W)^2,
```

write `u=u0+u1 W+...`. Its contribution to the left side is

```text
g'(0)/g(0) = 2 u1/u0 - (v0/u0)^2.               (KBLD-2)
```

The 24 expected linear factors contribute their reciprocal-label sum with
multiplicities. Exact source simplification and symmetric `c,d` descent give
one irreducible numerator of degree 67 with 162,322 terms. The denominator
factors into named opens and four nonnamed factors: two of degree 7 and two
of degree 11, each with exponent two.

Hence every full-`J` solution lies either on one of those four denominator
branches or, when all four are nonzero, on the degree-67 numerator branch.
