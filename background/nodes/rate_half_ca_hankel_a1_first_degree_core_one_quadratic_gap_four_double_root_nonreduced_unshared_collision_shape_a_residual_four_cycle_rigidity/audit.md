# Audit

1. `B` and `Z_4` live on the normalized locator curve, not on the large
   shape-A factor `G=0`.
2. The local equality uses the Pade syzygy only after checking that both
   `Lambda(tau)` and `L_U0(x_*)` are units.
3. The degree-four conclusion is projective; no affine resultant degree or
   hidden point at infinity is substituted for it.
4. The section count uses a second elementary modification. It does not
   assume that the locator curve is smooth or has its generic gonality.
5. The residual divisor `R_*` is nonempty. This is what separates every
   second-modification direction from the constant fibre-algebra line.
6. After removing possible constant components by a bundle automorphism,
   the largest negative degree is `2-d<0`; hence no extra section is hidden
   in an extension.
7. Rigidity does not exclude shape A. It records exactly why the four-core
   alone cannot be treated as a moving quartic pencil.
