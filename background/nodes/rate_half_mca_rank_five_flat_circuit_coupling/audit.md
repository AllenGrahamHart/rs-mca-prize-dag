# Audit

- The proof counts each witness through an independent four-set at most four
  times; it does not assume distinct rich flats produce distinct four-sets.
- A fifth point in the rank-three flat is excluded by minimality of the
  five-circuit, not by representability.
- The rank-four ceiling is applied to the complete closure, including loops.
- The `N-B` factor uses `N-b>=N-B` and therefore requires `N>=B`.
- Primary and independent finite-matroid replays test uniform and nonuniform
  fixtures and reject coefficient mutations.
