an unrelated global atom.

## Arithmetic

The primary verifier derives every displayed value from `(n,K,m,B*)`, checks
the two-anchor guard, scans every integer `g` in the large-owner interval,
and checks all branch margins.  The independent audit uses direct endpoint
and affine-function identities and validates the source contract separately.

## Scope

conditional proof can be reissued with the separate owner.  It is not
evidence that the stricter maximum-fiber/large-owner theorem is true.
