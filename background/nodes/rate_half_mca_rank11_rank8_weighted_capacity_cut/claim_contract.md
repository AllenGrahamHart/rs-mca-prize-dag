# Claim contract

## Input

The weighted fixed nine-subset component demand and the rank-eight
owner-pair incidence cap. The proof is conditional only on being in the
rank-eight typed alternative; it does not use elimination of rank nine.

## Output

The rank-eight affine-owner fixed target is impossible for every
`37996<=K'<=1048576`.

## Nonclaim

No rank-eight payment below `K'=37996`, kernel payment above `K'=4598`,
complete rank-eleven payment, active-v4 ledger movement, or prize closure is
asserted.
