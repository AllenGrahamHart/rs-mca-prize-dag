# KoalaBear degree-12 R20 B0 generic-boundary exclusion

Let `p0=2130706433`. In each of the literal fixed cells

```text
F04-R20, F05-R20, F06-R20, F07-R20,
```

the selected degree-12 resultant branch is empty on the complete `B0` chart
with its nonnamed degree-10 leading factor `K10` nonzero.

The theorem is geometric: it holds over every extension of `F_p0`, not only
over `F_(p0^6)`. It does not cover the complementary `K10=0` leading-drop
branch and therefore does not close a whole R20 cell.
