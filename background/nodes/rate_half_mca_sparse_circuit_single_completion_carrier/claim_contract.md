# Claim contract

- `A` is an independent deletion of exactly `c-1` distinct domain points.
- `M_c` counts distinct exact minimal-circuit completion points outside `A`.
- Only dependence is needed to put each completion functional in `span(A)`.
- Minimality identifies the support stratum but is not needed for vanishing.
- The carrier is fixed after choosing one attaining deletion.
- No relation to another support carrier is assumed.
- The node supplies geometry, not a numerical row payment.
