# Audit

The common quadratic uses the exact gcd degree two, not merely the repeated
root of the value resultant. Implementations must retain the subresultant
conditions and `Q(y)!=0`; otherwise a `3+3` or singular packet may enter.

The role parameter `lambda` is fixed before applying (TQC7). Color
permutations change the multiplicity roles and generally change `lambda`.
The bound of 42 is an upper bound before coincident role values are merged.

The four-variable system is overdetermined but is not declared unit. A
retained point still owes the HNF and fiber saturations, norm color,
assignment-preserving Frobenius, cyclotomic tests, and the inner split-pencil
lift.
