# Audit

1. Roots of `S_B` are taken over the algebraic closure; no base-field split
   hypothesis is introduced.
2. The proof assumes only that the selected correction root is not a center.
   Other correction/support or center overlaps are left intact.
3. At an unsupported root the exact resultant order is two because neither
   an off-line supported factor nor a center factor contributes there.
4. At a supported root, the complete gcd `A_sigma R_sigma` forces the new
   row point into the actual/padding dichotomy.
5. Actual support contradicts the first jet; padding contradicts
   `gcd(g_*,S_B)=1` by the definition of `g_*`.
6. Squarefreeness is used only in passing from root containment to the form
   divisibility `S_B|Lambda`.
7. The conclusion localizes a zero row; it does not exclude the remaining
   full-overlap cases.
