# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every actual prize consumer of Conjecture F has one typed,
  multiplicity-preserving flat descriptor accepted by the packing/payment
  ledger.
- **Strict callers:** `spi_point_counting` only.
- **Route-retirement guard:** `imgfib` has direct FPC5 and mixed/partial
  targets and no Conjecture-F edge.
- **Required family:** the Hankel/split-pencil slope fibers.
- **Required data:** domain, `j`, `r`, root threshold, punctures, and first
  owner.
- **Nonclaim:** the full Pade section is linear; every proper flat is
  packable; either direct L1 residual is proved; the singleton caller
  inventory remains exhaustive after a new strict edge.
