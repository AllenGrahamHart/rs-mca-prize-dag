# Result

The clean `nu=3` cubic case now has a canonical antiderivative and only a
quadratic Frobenius kernel. Its two high-degree summands are
`(X^4-Q)^p+J` and `Q^p-J`, with `Q=q_2X^2+q_1X`, `q_2^p=a`, and
`J'=cX^4U^2`. This is a two-parameter wild normal form, but its interpolation
and domain-splitting conditions remain to be classified, so the case and L1
remain open at this node. The tangent-radical successor now excludes the
entire `nu=3` case; L1 remains open through other strata.
