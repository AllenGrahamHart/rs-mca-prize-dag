# Claim contract

- **claim:** the exact weighted collision identity, its pointwise rank
  floor, and the two-list full-agreement interpretation;
- **scope:** every finite prime field, every finite-dimensional linear map,
  and the full subset cube;
- **dependencies:** none;
- **nonclaims:** no mass upper bound, max-fiber bound, random-code theorem,
  or F2 prize close;
- **falsifier:** a finite matrix for which either exact integer identity or
  either lower bound fails.
