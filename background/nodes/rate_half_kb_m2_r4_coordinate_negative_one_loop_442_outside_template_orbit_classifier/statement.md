# KoalaBear m2 r4 coordinate negative one-loop 442 outside template-orbit classifier

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** every forced-value/residual-matching product template of the
  one-loop `(4,4,2)` outside skeletons, for each fixed common sign row
- **dependencies:**
  `rate_half_kb_m2_r4_coordinate_negative_one_loop_442_complete_edge_skeleton_classifier`
  and
  `rate_half_kb_m2_r4_coordinate_negative_one_loop_442_outside_sign_orbit_classifier`
- **consumer:** `rate_half_band_closure`

A template consists of one forced record among seven and one perfect
matching of the remaining six records.  Acting simultaneously by outside
representative sign changes and every unsigned skeleton automorphism gives
exactly

```text
S0:  64 template orbits from  8*105= 840 raw templates;
S1: 114 template orbits from 16*105=1680 raw templates;
S2:  23 template orbits from  1*105= 105 raw templates. (KB41TO-1)
```

Thus the canonical cap is `64+114+23=201` templates per common sign row and
`4*201=804` over the four sextic common rows.

The orbit-size distributions are

```text
S0: 6 orbits of size 4, 14 of size 8, 44 of size 16;
S1: 18 orbits of size 8, 96 of size 16;
S2: 1 orbit of size 1, 6 of size 2, 9 of size 4, 7 of size 8. (KB41TO-2)
```

This theorem does not evaluate any canonical template, impose outside sums
or interpolation, classify another common orbit, close the coordinate
orientation or a row, or prove either Prize result.

## Falsifier

A raw template omitted from the printed orbits, two templates identified by
the action but placed in different orbits, or failure of the orbit-size sums
in `(KB41TO-2)`.
