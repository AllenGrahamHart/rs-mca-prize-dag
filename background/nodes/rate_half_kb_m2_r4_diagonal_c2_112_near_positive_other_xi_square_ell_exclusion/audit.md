# Audit

1. The four product-branch pairs are separate primary and audit tasks.
2. The primary and audit reconstruct by direct and fraction-free solves.
3. Resultants and terminal subresultants are independent projections.
4. Every nonstandard projected factor has a saturated generic basis.
5. The full unsolved `q17` leading-zero ideal is checked for both quadratic
   branches; all remaining degeneration support is standard forbidden support.
6. Every support and basis assertion is repeated modulo the deployed prime.
7. Each task runs under `timeout 60s` and `ramguard tiny`.
