# Claim contract

- **Claim:** in the degree-`2..43` full-gcd branch, at least `5079`
  selected polynomial pairs lie on components individually defined over
  `F(X)`.
- **Field:** `F=F_((2^31-1)^4)`; the characteristic `2^31-1` exceeds
  every possible factor degree.
- **Geometric input:** a non-base-field component of degree `delta`
  contains at most `delta^2` `F(X)`-rational points, by conjugation and
  Bezout.
- **Aggregate loss:** all non-base-field components contain at most `d^2`
  selected rational pairs.
- **Consequences:** one base-field absolutely irreducible component carries
  at least `132` selected pairs; their union has at least `126263`
  received factor points and at most `3974` inside exceptions.
- **Nonclaims:** the full gcd or component union is irreducible; a component
  is linear, rational, or a split pencil; the 132-section component alone
  carries 126263 received points; or the branch is safe.
- **Falsifier:** a degree-`2..43` Mersenne full gcd with fewer than `5079`
  selected pairs on base-field components, or a valid field-of-definition
  obstruction not covered by the printed characteristic guard.
