# Claim contract

- **Claim:** all three classwise interpolation maps are restrictions of the
  explicit residue forms `(CRP2)`, each having radical exactly its center
  fiber; the combined rank deficit is exactly the common-kernel dimension
  `kappa`.
- **Inputs:** the three center-fiber formulas, center-fiber coprimality, and
  the locator-interpolation syzygy map into `ker Phi`.
- **Output:** the exact rank formula `(CRP7)` and the boundary necessity
  `kappa>=e-3`.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaim:** no upper bound on `kappa` and no Shape-A exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** an extra radical vector for one residue form, or a mismatch
  between the residue weights and the classwise interpolation matrix.
