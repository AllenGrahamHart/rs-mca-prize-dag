# Audit

1. The sign gauge leaves one invariant sign `tau=wuv`; it does not discard
   either signed matching of a singleton edge.
2. `F -> -F` identifies only the choice of sign for the `DF` and `EF` `xi`
   types; it does not identify the five edge types with one another.
3. Binary-form proportionality is division-free and includes every one of
   the 15 residual perfect matchings at once.
4. Product distinctness and the fixed-point coprimality are required for the
   converse; proportionality alone could contain repeated or fixed roots.
5. The 20 cells compile only the paired-product gate.  Full interpolation at
   the twelve specified quotient labels remains mandatory.
6. The independent `T`/`W` normalization fence prevents the false
   substitution `D,E,F={1,M,M^2}` and any resultant based on it.
