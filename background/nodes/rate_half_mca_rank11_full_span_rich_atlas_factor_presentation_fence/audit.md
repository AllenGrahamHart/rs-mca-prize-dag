# Audit

The countermodel retains the exact polynomial degree, actual split roots,
container dimensions, container count, collective span, and absence of a
global common factor. It intentionally does not realize records, slopes,
minimizing pairs, or ownership chronology.

The product count includes presentations of rank below ten and therefore
overcounts the forbidden locus. Degree cancellation cannot evade `a+b<=D`:
the product of one maximal-degree element from each factor space has nonzero
leading coefficient and belongs to the product span.

The conclusion is a scope fence. It must not be quoted against a theorem
that uses the omitted received-line data.
