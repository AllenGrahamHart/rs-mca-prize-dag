# Audit

1. Basepoints inside and outside `U_0`, including infinity, are treated
   separately through the homogeneous norm divisor.
2. Exact degree `n` follows from the proved pure split fibers.
3. An actual-support root is simple in the `X` direction by the exact
   all-excess factorization, so every `U_0` point is unramified. No claim is
   made that an outside point is unramified.
4. An outside point of ramification index `h` is charged `h` times for each
   of the `m` distinct incident slopes; no independence or genericity is
   assumed.
5. The residual norm degree is exactly `3en-Rm=2m-3`, including infinity.
6. One unit of outside fiber degree is retained, leading to the alternatives
   `d_X|R` and `d_X|(R+1)`.
7. Birationality does not prohibit repeated projective values at singular
   image points.
