# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every nontrivial minimal tensor presentation of an official
  Shape-A survivor has a birational domain coefficient map.
- **Dependencies:** exact all-excess fiber factorization, pure split fibers,
  and the concentrated norm identity.
- **Falsifier:** a coefficient-map basepoint not exceeding the residual norm
  budget, an outside fiber-degree unit not charged by every incident row,
  total outside fiber degree at least two within norm degree `2m-3`, or a
  nontrivial common divisor of `n` and one of `R,R+1`.
- **Status effect:** one PROVED all-rank structural node; no critical status
  change.
- **Nonclaims:** no singularity bound, tensor-rank bound, or Shape-A
  exclusion.
