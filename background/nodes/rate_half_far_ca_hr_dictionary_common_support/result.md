# Result

The column-far band is classified: `h_r` is not a coordinate but a degree
(`rho` plus `deg(e_1/e_0)`, capped at `2rho` by the row count), common
support is a theorem rather than a modelling choice, and LB1 is the unique
`h_r = rho+1` family with its type-1 count forced to `r+1` with zero
accidentals. The `p*(d)` law remains POSED with five named failures and
must not label strata; its converse is refuted.

Consequences of record: R-PSTAR-INTERMEDIATE is retired as a stratum; the
`h_r = rho+2` band is not moment-bound; and "LB1-limited" fails even as a
value at the crossing offset after the round-37 refutation (the count is
`r+1 + Theta(n/rho)`), while LB1 itself survives as the fibre floor of the
value ledger.
