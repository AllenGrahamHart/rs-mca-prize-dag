# Audit

- The extension uses only matchings `0,1,2`; all and only these canonical
  matchings retain `(0,1)` as the first pair.
- It transports a necessary first-pair equation, not a sufficient partial
  outside system. Emptiness of the necessary equation is enough for deletion.
- The negative-copy sign remains distinct: `xi=2` uses `P(-m,-m)`, not the
  opposite-record equation for `xi=0,1`.
- Target lanes need no multiplication assumption beyond the parent theorems:
  the first-pair cuts contain neither target sign.
- The count is `3*3*4*4=144`, corresponding to `9` of `105` slices; no case
  at `xi>=3` or `pairing>=3` is claimed.
