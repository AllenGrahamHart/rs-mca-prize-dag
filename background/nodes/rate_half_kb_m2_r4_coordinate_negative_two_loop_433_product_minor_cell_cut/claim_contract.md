# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | Three of the nine `(4,3,3)` antipodal cells are deleted by one maximal product minor, and three of the six survivors necessarily satisfy `b=-c^3`. |
| deletion interface | Delete `X1,N2,Z1`; delete any `X2,N1,L1` candidate off `b=-c^3`. |
| assumptions | The nine-cell atlas, the exact negative product matrix, odd characteristic, and actual label/signed-pair distinctness. |
| nonclaim | Remaining-minor or q classification, complete packet realization, another skeleton, positive parity, owner/payment movement, a row, or either Prize theorem. |
