# Audit

1. The product order is `(A,C,AB+,AB-,BC)` in every determinant.
2. `M` is a normalized `K` label; `b,c` are normalized `J` representatives.
3. `b!=+/-c` follows from distinct signed `J` pairs, not product injectivity
   alone.
4. In `Z1,L1`, reduction by `M^2+1` occurs before the printed factorization.
5. The relation `b=-c^3` is necessary only; no remaining cell is claimed
   nonempty or q-compatible.
6. The three cells `M1,M2,M3` are retained without a relation, not certified
   as product survivors.
