# Audit

1. `F00(b^-1)` selects exactly the two edges in `F01(b)`.
2. The proof is reparameterization, not endpoint-coordinate covariance.
3. The root targets depend on `c,d`, which are not moved.
4. Full quotient locators depend on the unchanged endpoint sets, not the
   orientation used to name `b`.
5. The nonzero chart is preserved bijectively by inversion.
6. The theorem does not transfer any of `F02`--`F07` or `M00`--`M03`.
