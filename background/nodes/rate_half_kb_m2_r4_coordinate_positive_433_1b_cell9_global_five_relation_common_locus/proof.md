# Proof

Fix one source-sign row.  The five product rows and the `AB` sum row form a
seven-column compact matrix.  The other three nonloop sum rows lie in its
span exactly when the three printed `7 x 7` determinants vanish, provided
the product block has rank five and the pivot raises its rank to six.

The global compiler removes only the printed route-guard factors, saturates
the determinant ideal by their product, and then saturates by the ideal `P`
generated by all six stripped product cofactors.  Algebraically this takes
the closure of the guarded determinant locus outside `V(P)`, exactly the
product-rank-five branch.  Singular returns dimension `1` and compact basis
size `17` in every source-sign row.  After the same localizations, adding the
primitive `AB` pivot scale gives the unit ideal, so the pivot loses no point.

Changing to lex order `(c,b,t,r)` gives seven elements.  The compiler reduces
the global basis by the independently saturated chart-`1` basis and the
chart basis by the global basis.  All `4*(7+7)=56` remainders vanish; the
bases are also byte-identical.  Hence chart `1` covers the global guarded
rank-five common locus, rather than merely one open subchart.

Finally, for every sign row the subset replay forms each ideal generated by
`K0,K1` and a nonempty proper subset of `K2,...,K6`, computes its lex basis,
and reduces all seven full generators explicitly.  All 120 jobs complete.
The three five-element sets stated above have seven zero remainders; direct
containment in the full ideal gives equality.  In particular all three omit
`K2`, proving its redundancy. QED.
