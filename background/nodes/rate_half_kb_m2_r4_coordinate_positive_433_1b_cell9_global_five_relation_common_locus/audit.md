# Audit

- Saturation by the six-generator cofactor ideal removes simultaneous
  product-rank drop; it is not saturation by one favored chart.
- The pivot scale is made primitive before its boundary is tested, and the
  boundary ideal is re-saturated by both route guards and the cofactor ideal.
- Ideal equality is checked by fourteen explicit reductions per sign row,
  not inferred from equal dimensions or equal basis sizes.
- The subset verdict is derived from seven individual remainders.  Singular's
  compacted ideal-level reduction is deliberately not used as an exactness
  flag.
- No common survivor is interpreted as an outside witness or exclusion.
