# Claim contract

## Inputs

- The PROVED rank-eleven line-global C/S/A/E anchor router.
- The PROVED common-core cancellation and certificate-lift adapter.
- The PROVED support-local transversality and rank-nine
  margin/interleaving payments.
- The PROVED order-32 support-collapsed extraction and collision-rigidity
  interfaces.

## Output

The nonempty line-global common-core branch is no longer an undifferentiated
shortened family. A descended deviation rank at most nine is paid. At rank
ten, one fixed residual 31-anchor star routes the whole family to exactly
one of:

```text
(H_C) relative high-complexity core,
(A)   coherent lifted rational atom,
(E)   pure-locator, denominator-root, or collision exception.
```

## Forbidden promotion

`(H_C)` is not the deployed first-match primitive spread terminal `(S)`.
The original supports share the nonempty factor `C_*`; a relative abundance
or direct common-core payment is still required.
