# Audit

Equation (QDR4) is obtained by squaring and is used only as a necessary
condition. A norm survivor must be checked against the unsquared sign in
(QDR3); no converse from `R_12(d)=0` is asserted.

The factor `d+6y` is removed by the inherited saturation `q-d!=0`, not by a
genericity assumption. The elimination polynomial (QDR7) also handles the
case `E=F=0`; no division by `E` is part of the claim.

The norm gcds are pending. Any retained root still owes reconstruction of
`y,x,b`, the quadratic and sixth coefficient equations, exact color ratio and
fiber-degree saturations, assignment-preserving Frobenius, cyclotomic tests,
and the inner split-pencil lift.
