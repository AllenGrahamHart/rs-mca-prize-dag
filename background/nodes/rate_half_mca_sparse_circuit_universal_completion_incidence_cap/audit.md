# Audit

1. `A` is independent because it is a proper subset of a circuit.
2. The common-zero count includes both `A` and all completion points.
3. The quotient polynomial space has dimension `K-c+1-b`, yielding exactly
   `b<=K-10`.
4. Two completion labels are independent by their private coordinates.
5. The selected eleven-set has a one-dimensional annihilator intersection,
   so it cannot retain both labels.
6. The remaining coordinates avoid every completion, not only the chosen
   one.
7. Every circuit is charged once for each of its `c` deletions.
8. The integer maximum is retained; monotonicity in `b` is not assumed.
