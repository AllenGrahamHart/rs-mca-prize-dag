# Audit

This node gives a necessary finite endpoint, not a unit-gcd verdict. It uses
only the residual conic and reduced fifth coefficient, so omission of `D_b`
and `M_6` cannot discard a genuine packet; those equations remain optional
filters on any norm survivor.

The divisions in (XQ5) occur only after the separate branches `d=-2` and
`d=-3/2` have been removed. The three base-field norm obstructions are
checked independently for every declared characteristic.

Do not infer that a root of `P_5` lifts to a packet. Such a root still owes
the determined `q`, a solution `b`, exact color ratio and fiber-degree
saturations, assignment-preserving Frobenius, cyclotomic tests, and the inner
split-pencil lift.
