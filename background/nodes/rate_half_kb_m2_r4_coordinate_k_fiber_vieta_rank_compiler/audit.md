# Audit

1. `q=r*s*(a+b)`, not `a+b`, is the deck-independent homogeneous sum
   coordinate.
2. The positive second equation is multiplied by `r*s`, producing
   `u*v B_1+q A_2=0`; no square-root choice remains.
3. The negative second equation is already `A_1+q B_2=0`.
4. A ramified `K` fiber is allowed in the positive branch. It is
   impossible in the negative branch because the residual binary quadratic
   has only the two fixed points of `tau`.
5. Rank alone is not sufficient: the kernel must have
   `A_2(kappa)!=0` or `r*s*B_2(kappa)!=0` at all five fibers.
6. The smaller determinant gates are necessary consequences, not
   replacements for the coupled `10`-row systems.
7. No determinant is asserted nonzero for every admissible endpoint
   record.
