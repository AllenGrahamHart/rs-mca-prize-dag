# Frontier

For each admissible coordinate `K`-star packet, compute only its five
quotient edge records `(p_kappa,q_kappa)`. Apply the small determinant
gates first, then the full Vieta kernel, and only then the colored
quotient-resultant identities. A survivor must still satisfy leading
support, nonzero odd part, exact bidegree, irreducibility, and the outer
factor identity. Do not enumerate generic source rows.
