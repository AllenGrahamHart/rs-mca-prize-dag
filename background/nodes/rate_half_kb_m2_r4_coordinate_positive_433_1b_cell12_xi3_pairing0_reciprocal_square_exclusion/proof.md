# Proof

After deleting `DF`, matching `0` pairs the residual products as

```text
(de,de), (-de,sigma_o ef), (bf,sigma_c cf).
```

For `L_i(q)=B_i-qA_i`, direct expansion gives

```text
paired(q,q)=4 L_0(q)L_1(q)^2L_2(q).              (RS12-1)
```

The characteristic is odd, so every finite solution lies on one of the
three branches `q=B_i/A_i`; zero denominators are included in replay.

Let `m=df`, `S=(d+f)^2`, and `y=1/d^2`.  The missing Vieta equation becomes

```text
1+(2m-S)y+m^2y^2=0,             ef=qmy.           (RS12-2)
```

On each branch, take the quadratic resultant in `y` of `(RS12-2)` and
`paired(-q,sigma_o qmy)=0`, then norm it through the exact four-basis common
algebra.  Twenty-four rows cover four source signs, three branches, and two
`sigma_o` signs; each row later checks both `sigma_c` lanes.

The exact candidate union contains every deployed root of each norm
numerator and denominator and every inverse numerator and denominator.  Its
340 case-labeled roots replay through the original tower.  The replay
accounts for 240 route or leading-boundary terminals, 84 no-lift terminals,
and 472 guarded source points.  The 24 cell-12 `b`-leading terminals are
precisely in the leading complement paid by the rational-boundary parent.

At each guarded point, direct scalar arithmetic checks `(RS12-1)`, intersects
the two exact `y` root sets, and lifts every deployed root of `d^2=1/y`.
There are 48 common `y` rows and 96 `(y,d)` candidates.  Recovering
`e=q/d,f=m/d` and evaluating the final pair in both `sigma_c` lanes gives
192 nonzero values.  There are no witnesses, free branches, or unresolved
rows.  Thus missing `DF`, matching `0`, is empty.  Exact `D/E` transport pays
the partner missing `EF` label, and the parent pays the rational boundary.
QED.
