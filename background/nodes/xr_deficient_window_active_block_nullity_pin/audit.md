# Audit

- `G_d` may be larger than `D`; the proof uses only `D subset G_d`, so this
  can strengthen rather than weaken the nullity bound.
- The two selected blocks are disjoint because two independent scalar error
  equations cannot vanish at an active-defect point.
- The pin concerns stacked-window kernel dimension `dim K_d`, not affine
  dimension of the target parameter set. Those dimensions are unrelated and
  must not be substituted.
- The full-rank node is the case `dim K_d=0`; `(ABN2)` does not import or
  prove its locator census.
