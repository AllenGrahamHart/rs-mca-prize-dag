# Claim contract

| field | value |
|---|---|
| claim | The active-block stacked-nullity bound `(ABN1)` and its official boundary pins `(ABN2)`. |
| inputs | Primitive multiplier-space dimension bound and two disjoint `D`-local selected blocks. |
| output | A general nullity ceiling and exact `6,6,2` extremal pins. |
| load-bearing hypotheses | Deficient primitive normal form, complete `D`-locality, two selected live slopes, and block disjointness. |
| falsifier | A target with `dim K_d>3d-2h-ell`, or an official boundary remainder different from the printed pins. |
| nonclaim | Bounded nullity is not itself a locator-count payment. |
