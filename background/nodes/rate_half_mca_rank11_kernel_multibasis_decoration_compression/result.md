# Result

The canonical-basis kernel cap loses a guaranteed multiplicity. Retaining
all bases of each rank-deficient eleven-set and then dividing by the sharp
loopless-matroid lower bound improves every corank-`d` term by a factor of
at least `d+2`.

The lemma is structural and uniform in `K'`; its exact finite consequence
is recorded in `rate_half_mca_rank11_kernel_multibasis_capacity_cut`.
