# Attack

Try to realize a loopless rank-`r` matroid on eleven points with fewer
than `12-r` bases. The sharp partition-matroid witness shows that no larger
uniform divisor follows from looplessness alone.

Audit the second step separately: for a fixed noncanonical basis `B`,
collect records arriving through different eleven-sets and verify that the
quotient solution on `B` is common before invoking `M_d`.

Any further improvement must use more than basis multiplicity, for example
restrictions on which sharp partition-matroid flats are realizable by the
residual polynomial evaluation space.
