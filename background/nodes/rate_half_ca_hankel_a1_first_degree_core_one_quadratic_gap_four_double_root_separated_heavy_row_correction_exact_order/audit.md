# Audit

1. The proof works on the normalized curve, where the divisor orders
   `3B` and `2B` are exact local valuations.
2. Squarefreeness of `S_B` makes the parameter a uniformizer at each point
   of `B`; no ramification factor is omitted.
3. `L_U0(x_*)` is a unit because `g_*` is nonempty and makes `x_*` a
   padded-heavy root, while the paired fiber theorem places all padding
   roots outside the contracted domain.
4. A center overlap contributes exactly one to `ord Lambda`; the center
   product is squarefree.
5. The fixed-row and moving-curve values differ by `X-x_*`, of order three.
   This cannot alter a leading order of one or two.
6. `gcd(g_*,S_B)=1` is used to compute the exact order of `H`.
7. Coprimality with `S_B` does not imply that `T_j` is constant or that the
   augmented remainder gate fails.
