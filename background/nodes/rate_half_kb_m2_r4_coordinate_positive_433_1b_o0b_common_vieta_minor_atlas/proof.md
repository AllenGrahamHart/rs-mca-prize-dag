# Proof

The signed-edge atlas gives stratum lane counts `2,4,2,2` for
`S0,SBC,SDE,SDF`. Only `SBC` repeats `BC`; hence six lanes are split and four
are repeated. Direct inspection of the atlas's first five records gives the
two product templates in the statement.

The source-facet theorem gives fifteen role cells and four root-sign rows for
each target lane. The split template is exactly the input of the proved
split compiler. In `SBC`, the common-triangle invariant is precisely
`sigma`; the other invariant changes only the outside `EF` record. Therefore
the repeated compiler's two sign blocks each serve two formal target lanes.

The formal census is `10*15*4=600`. The distinct algebra census is
`15*4 + 15*4*2=180`. Multiplication by six minors gives 3,600 formal and
1,080 compiled minors in each mode. The assembly script checks every lane
record and reconstructs these counts. QED.
