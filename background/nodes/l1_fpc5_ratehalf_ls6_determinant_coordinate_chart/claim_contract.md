# Claim contract

## Input

One fixed guarded LS6 atom and one primitive base candidate.

## Output

An exact affine determinant chart for the complete monic unguarded slice,
explicit inverse formulas, the induced pair-determinant operation, and an
exact root-local translation of the primitive guard.

## Preserved

- the fixed source, touched triple, defect, cross-ratio, `E`, and `M`;
- monicity and the exact remainder degree;
- the guarded base candidate;
- core splitness when translating the gcd guard root by root.

## Nonclaims

- the unguarded chart is itself a payment;
- formal Plucker compatibility bounds the guarded split subset;
- the root-local inequalities have been counted;
- quotient or dihedral first ownership has been transported.
