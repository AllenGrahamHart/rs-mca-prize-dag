# Audit

1. The external signs contribute four factors, not eight, because global
   negation has already been quotiented.
2. The three internal signs and two external signs give exactly five binary
   choices, matching all 32 classes.
3. `H` has six terms and no denominator; the argument is valid in every odd
   characteristic, including characteristics supporting non-ambient
   relations.
4. The quadratic extension is formal. Specialized pair products can already
   lie in the cyclotomic base field; this can lower the extension degree but
   does not change the product identity.
5. Prime-support equality is for the union of all eight factors. No one
   selected pairing factor is asserted to own every relation.
6. All 15 pairings are equivalent algebraically, but their specialized norm
   arithmetic may differ. Selecting a pairing for computational convenience
   requires an explicit deterministic owner rule.
