# Claim contract

## Proved

- Every active projective clone has one common four-dimensional residual
  kernel and one squarefree locator dividing that kernel.
- The clone size lies in `10001<=c<=K-5`.
- The clone bucket shortens reversibly to a `2 x 4` factor family with the
  same first-owned mass, slopes, and pair noncontainment.
- The three official row differences are invariant under shortening.

## Required semantics

- The input is the active-clone alternative of the proved clone/line node.
- `B` and `PB` are polynomial spaces in the exact factor presentation, with
  `span(PB)<=F[X]_{<K}`.
- Clone coordinates are distinct deployed field points in the anchor-good
  universe.

## Not claimed

- Payment of the shortened `2 x 4` bucket.
- A common locator across different clone buckets.
- Resolution of the genuine rank-two alternative or the full crossing.
