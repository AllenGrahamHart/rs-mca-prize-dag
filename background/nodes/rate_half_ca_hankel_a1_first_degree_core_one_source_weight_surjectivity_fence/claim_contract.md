# Claim contract

- **Claim:** core contraction maps RS word values diagonally and invertibly
  to residual source weights, and the official residual-domain Vandermonde
  map is onto all `2d+1` Hankel moments.
- **Currency:** nonzero dual RS multipliers, one linear apolar contraction,
  and a square Vandermonde minor.
- **Dependencies:** the split-pencil equivalence and the core-one marked
  source frame.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** arbitrary source weights need not be column-far, carry the
  supported split-locator census, or realize a retained double-root packet.
- **Falsifier:** a zero multiplier `(x-s_0)v_x` on `D_res`, or fewer than
  `2d+1` distinct residual evaluation points.
- **Route consequence:** do not seek a source-only positivity or
  noncancellation theorem; couple source weights to a non-source invariant.
