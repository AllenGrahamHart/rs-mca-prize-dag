# Audit

- Divided-power contraction by `X-s_0` gives
  `h_k=y_(k+1)-s_0y_k`; no characteristic-dependent binomial factor appears.
- The diagonal map is invertible only after deleting the fixed point
  `s_0`, exactly as in `D_res`.
- Moment surjectivity uses `2d+1` distinct columns, not a square matrix on
  the entire much larger residual domain.
- A source representation is automatic; column-farness and the supported
  split-locator packet are not.
- The fence refutes only source-only noncancellation strategies. It does not
  refute the marked determinant identity or construct a prize counterexample.
