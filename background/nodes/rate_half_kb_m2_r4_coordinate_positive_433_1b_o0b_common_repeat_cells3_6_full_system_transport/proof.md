# Proof

The target permutation fixes `A,D`, swaps `B,C`, and swaps `E,F`. It sends
each edge and signed product exactly as listed in the statement. Vertex sums
and products, hence every complete-fiber Vieta row, are equivariant under
the corresponding variable substitution. The common role-cell enumerator
then sends cell 3 to cell 6 and is involutive.

For an outside label, transport the missing record and every endpoint of the
residual perfect matching by the seven-record permutation, then reindex the
six residual records. Direct enumeration checks that this operation is an
involution and covers all 105 labels. The four independent sign choices are
fixed, so the Cartesian-product system map is a bijection on all 1,680
systems. QED.
