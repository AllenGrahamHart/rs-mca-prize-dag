# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every zero-excess off-line slope obeys the exact minimum-circuit
  identities `(FBG5)--(FBG7)`, and at least `e+6+d_A` such slopes are
  deficit-free.
- **Inputs:** the extremal source partition, Cycle-127 minimum words, exact
  packet deficits, standard RS dual multipliers, and the split-Hankel
  locator equation.
- **Outputs:** fixed sizes `(FBG2)`, reciprocal outside-locator interpolation
  `(FBG6)`, three-class specialization `(FBG7)`, and multiplicities
  `(FBG8)--(FBG9)`.
- **Falsifier:** an outside support of size other than `p+2`, a varying value
  of `omega_x B_delta(x)L_U0'(x)` on `X_delta`, or fewer than `e+6+d_A`
  clean zero-excess slopes.
- **Nonclaims:** no distinctness, pencil structure, or nonexistence of the
  outside locators is asserted.
