# Claim contract

- **Input:** one general `t`-petal saturated slice and one squarefree exact
  anchor locator.
- **Output:** an affine bijection from the complete monic pair chart to
  `K[X]_(<=e-1)` and exact recovery of every common-defect gcd owner.
- **Preserved:** locator and numerator, field, labels, splitness and primitive
  filters, contributor identity, and common-defect roots.
- **Nonclaim:** an aggregate split-point, gcd-owner, source-layout, or row
  bound.
