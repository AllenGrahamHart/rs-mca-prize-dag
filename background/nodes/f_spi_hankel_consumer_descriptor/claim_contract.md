# Claim contract

- **Input:** the Hankel pencil `M(Z)` and the first-owner component
  classification.
- **Output:** for every supported slope, the exact flat
  `P(ker M(z))`, its projective dimension, unchanged domain, degree/root
  threshold, and owner chronology.
- **Multiplicity:** slope-incidence multiplicity, not deduplicated locator
  multiplicity.
- **Nonclaim:** a bound on supported slopes; a bound on the split-locator
  intersection; uniqueness of the slope over a degenerate locator.
