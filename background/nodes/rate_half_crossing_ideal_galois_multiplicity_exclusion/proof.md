# Proof

Write `I=I_(S,w)` and suppose the odd prime `p` divides `N(I)`.  Some prime
ideal `P` above `p` contains `I`.  Since `p` is unramified in the dyadic
cyclotomic field and the extension is abelian, the decomposition group of
`P` is generated by `sigma_p:zeta_n -> zeta_n^p`; in particular
`sigma_p(P)=P`.

For every index `s`, `sigma_p(x_s)=x_(ps)`.  The initial containments
`x_1,...,x_(w-1) in P` therefore propagate around all `p`-orbits, so
`x_s in P` for every `s in Z_w(p)`.

If `s` is odd, `sigma_s` is a Galois automorphism and
`x_s=sigma_s(x_1)`.  Hence

```text
x_1 in P_s := sigma_s^(-1)(P)       for s in Z_w^odd(p).       (1)
```

Let `delta=ord_n(p)`.  Two primes in `(1)` agree exactly when their indices
differ by the decomposition group `<p>`.  The odd closure is a union of
free `<p>`-orbits, each of size `delta`; therefore `(1)` contains
`|Z_w^odd(p)|/delta` distinct primes, each with norm `p^delta`.  Their
product divides `(x_1)`, proving

```text
p^|Z_w^odd(p)| divides |N_(K/Q)(x_1)|.
```

This is `(IG1)`.

For `(IG2)`, the embeddings of `K` are indexed by odd `c mod n`, and
`sigma_c(x_1)=x_c`.  Orthogonality gives the exact second moment

```text
sum_(c odd mod n) |x_c|^2
 = (n/2)(r-a_(n/2)(S)).                                      (2)
```

Indeed, for `d=i-j`, the sum of `zeta_n^(cd)` over odd `c` is `n/2` when
`d=0`, `-n/2` when `d=n/2`, and zero otherwise.  Applying AM-GM to the
`h=n/2` nonnegative numbers `|x_c|^2` in `(2)` yields

```text
|N(x_1)|^(2/h) <= r-a_(n/2)(S),
```

which is `(IG2)`.  Since `x_1!=0`, its norm is a nonzero integer; combining
`(IG1)` and `(IG2)` gives `(IG3)`.  The seed interval contains exactly
`ceil((w-1)/2)` odd integers, proving `(IG4)`.

Now suppose `S` is invariant under addition by `n_a=n/2^a`.  Group each
orbit as `i_0+j*n_a`, `0<=j<2^a`.  The inner geometric sum is zero unless
`2^a` divides the moment index and equals `2^a` otherwise.  Thus

```text
x_s=0                                      if 2^a does not divide s,
x_(2^a t)=2^a iota(x'_t)                   otherwise,             (3)
```

where `iota:Q(zeta_(n_a))->K` is the natural inclusion and `x'_t` is the
reduced moment of `S_a`.  Formula `(3)` identifies the reduced window as
`w_a=floor((w-1)/2^a)+1`.  Extending an ideal from the smaller cyclotomic
field raises its odd-prime norm factors to `[K:K_a]`, while multiplication
by `2^a` adds only powers of two.  Hence the odd-prime supports agree
exactly and `(IG4)` at the reduced parameters is precisely `(IG5-a)`.

Finally let `w=2^v`.  For `a<v`,

```text
ceil(floor((w-1)/2^a)/2)=2^(v-a-1).
```

Multiplying `(IG5-a)` by `2^a` makes the left side independent of `a`,
while the right side becomes `(n/4)(log_2 r-a)` and strictly decreases.
Thus the top exclusion propagates through all nonstructural strata.  For
`a>=v`, no positive index below `w` is divisible by `2^a`, so all conditions
vanish identically.  This proves the periodic and power-of-two claims. QED.
