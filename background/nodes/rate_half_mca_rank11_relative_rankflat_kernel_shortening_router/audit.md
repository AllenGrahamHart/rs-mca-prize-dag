# Audit

1. Vertical and slope-dominating irreducible components are separated.
2. The complement solution is polynomial because the evaluation matrix is
   constant and the coordinate equations have slope degree at most 31.
3. High-core absorption is projected modulo the kernel before the affine
   owner is defined.
4. Every kernel word vanishes on all of `T`, not merely on the chosen basis.
5. Translation makes both received columns vanish on `T`; locator division
   is therefore typed and reversible.
6. The kernel rank falls strictly: `u=s-r<=s-1<=9`.
7. The rank-drop payment is invoked componentwise. The proof does not infer
   an aggregate bound from many componentwise bounds.

No numerical experiment or Modal computation is used.
