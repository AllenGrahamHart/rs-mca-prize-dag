# Rank-eleven rank-flat kernel-shortening router

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** one high-core-absorbing evaluation rank-flat component in the
  relative correction ten-flat
- **component dimension:** `2<=s<=10`

Let `T` be the `s+1` coordinate flat, let the evaluation map

```text
ev_T:W -> F^T
```

have rank `0<=r<s`, and put `U=ker(ev_T)`, `u=s-r`.

- A vertical irreducible component projects to one slope and costs one.
- Rank zero permits only vertical components. Every slope-dominating
  component has `1<=r<s` and is, after one affine codeword-pair
  translation, a family of `U`-valued corrections which agree with the
  translated received line on every coordinate of `T`.

Every word of `U` vanishes on `T`. Exact division by the locator of `T` and
deletion of `T` therefore produces a support-wise MCA-bad shortened family
whose explanation affine rank is at most

```text
u=s-r <= s-1 <=9.
```

The proved shortened rank-drop payments bound that component below the
official KoalaBear budget. Thus every individual high-core-absorbing
rank-flat component is either a one-slope vertical component or a paid
rank-at-most-nine shortened component.

Together with the absorbing clone-to-affine theorem, all individual
positive-dimensional `(H_C)` components are now routed. The remaining issue
is a chronology-correct aggregate bound across potentially many components
and affine owners; this theorem does not sum them.

## Falsifier

A nonzero kernel word not vanishing on `T`; a slope-dominating component with
no polynomial complement solution; a high coefficient retaining a
nonkernel part after projection; failure of the affine owner to agree with
the received line on `T`; loss of badness or support labels under exact
division; residual explanation rank above `u`; or summing component caps
without an owner selector.
