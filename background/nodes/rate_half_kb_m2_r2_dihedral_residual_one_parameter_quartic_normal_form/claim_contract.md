# Claim contract

- **Claim ID:** `rate_half_kb_m2_r2_dihedral_residual_one_parameter_quartic_normal_form`
- **Input:** an actual residual full-V4 profile with `n=3` or `n=6`.
- **Output:** a geometric coordinate system in which its coefficient image
  is `(KBMN-3)` with the explicit coefficients `(KBMN-2)`, `a=-1` or `1`,
  and one parameter `b notin {2,-2}`.
- **Dependencies used:** the dihedral reflection normal form, the exact
  source V4 branch passports, and the canonical coefficient-quartic pin.
- **Not claimed:** irreducibility or actual realization for arbitrary `b`,
  emptiness of either residual profile, an owner, a payment, or a row close.
