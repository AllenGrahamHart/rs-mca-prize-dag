# Frontier

For `a=-1` and `a=1`, factor the projective quartic `(KBMN-3)` over the
geometric closure as a function of `b`, and classify the exceptional
parameters where it leaves the irreducible birational-quartic branch. For
the irreducible locus, substitute the six order-five pole fibers into the
normal form and impose the exact source locators. This is now a one-variable
elimination problem per factor degree; do not return to arbitrary quartics
or unconstrained star orientations.
