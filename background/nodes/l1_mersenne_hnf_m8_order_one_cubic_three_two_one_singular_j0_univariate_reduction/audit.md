# Audit

The division by `A(q)` is justified only after combining `F_J=0` with the
inherited `q!=0` saturation. Do not apply the univariate representation on
the discarded `q=0` chart.

A unit gcd closes only the coefficient-matrix `Delta=J=0` arm. It does not
close the `Delta!=0` branch, the singular `J!=0` branch, any other cubic
profile, or L1. A nonunit gcd is not a counterexample or an HNF packet; its
roots still owe every equation and arithmetic lift listed in the claim
contract.

Any official gcd result must be accompanied by independently replayable
polynomial coefficients and Euclidean/Bezout data. A printed software gcd
alone is route evidence, not a proof artifact.
