# Proof

The equal residual pair again gives the three rational branches
`q=B_i/A_i`. With `m=df`, `S=(d+f)^2`, and `z=1/d`, the missing Vieta
equation is the even quartic

```text
1+(2m-S)z^2+m^2z^4=0.                         (RL-1)
```

For pairing 1, the next paired cut uses `bf`; for pairing 2 it uses the
anchored `sigma_c cf`. Dividing the corresponding quadratic cut into
`(RL-1)` leaves an exact linear remainder in every row. The pinned compiler
norms that remainder through the cell-11 row-6 four-basis algebra.

Pairing 1 has 12 rows: 72 target roots, 162 candidate roots, 74 nonsquare
no-`b` lifts, and 176 guarded source routes. Its finite partition is 24
missing-impossible, 24 zero-product, 24 empty-branch, and 104 checked points.
The exact intersections produce 16 `z/d/e/f` lifts and 64 nonzero final
colored lanes; 24 leading-boundary cases are paid separately.

Pairing 2 has 24 rows because both `sigma_c` anchors are explicit: 144 target
roots, 324 candidate roots, 148 nonsquare no-`b` lifts, and 352 guarded source
routes. Its finite partition is 48 missing-impossible, 48 zero-product, 48
empty-branch, and 208 checked points. The intersections produce 32 lifts and

Independent Frobenius reconstruction covers 125 profiles and 610 roots,
with maximum degree 712. A separate replay reconstructs all 36 rows and keeps
the two pairing totals distinct. It checks every candidate union, source
relation, missing value, common `z` root, lift, and colored cut. Thus labels
`(3,1)` and `(3,2)` are empty; exact outside-role transport pays `(4,1)` and
`(4,2)`. QED.
