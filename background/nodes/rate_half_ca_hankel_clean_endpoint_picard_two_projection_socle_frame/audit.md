# Audit

1. The two projections have degrees `m` and `rho`; their negative blocks
   therefore have ranks `m-1` and `rho-1`, respectively.
2. The two splitting degrees are `-rho` and `-m`; these are not interchanged.
3. The exact degree of `C_0` follows from the coordinate choice
   `a(S)!=0`, so no domain-infinity root is lost.
4. The repeated-root branch is handled by the socle quotient, not by an
   unramified idempotent formula.
5. The evaluation vectors are projective and defined only up to the nonzero
   local residue scalar.
6. The node exposes a Hankel-facing coordinate but does not promote it to an
   orthogonality statement.
