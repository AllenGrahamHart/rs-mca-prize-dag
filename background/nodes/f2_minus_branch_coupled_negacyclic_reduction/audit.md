# Audit

1. The order law uses `v_2(p+1)`, not `v_2(p-1)`. This is the exact defect
   in canonical Rounds 17 and 18.
2. Prime-field proportionality classes are singleton after the antipodal
   quotient, but their moment equations are Frobenius-coupled. They cannot
   be multiplied as independent masses.
3. The scalar rank is the cardinality of the Frobenius closure of the odd
   roots: `hR`, not the number of displayed extension-field rows `R` and
   not the number `2^40` of singleton proportionality classes.
4. The initial run `1,3,...,2R-1` and the official bound `2R<2^36` are
   load-bearing for the printed root-disjointness and DLI statements.
5. The theorem covers the four prize-facing top windows at orders `2^40`
   and `2^41`. Lower layers are not silently declared paid.
6. The mass identity and lower bound are exact, but neither is the needed
   mass upper bound. The final F2 target remains open.
7. The Mersenne witness `p=2^61-1`, `q=p^2` lies in this theorem with
   `b=61`, `k=h=2`; its bounded-class reduction was correctly refuted.
