# Claim contract

- **claim:** the minus-branch order law, exact top-window root-code model,
  Frobenius-closure rank, weighted collision floor, and ternary distance;
- **dependencies:** the DLI Newton cutoff for distance and the generic
  weighted collision theorem for `(MINUS-L2)`;
- **scope:** official maximal rate-half `p=3 mod 4` rows, top exact/nested
  order layers `a=40,41`, and the initial odd run in the deployed range;
- **nonclaims:** no direct-sum factorization, no mass upper bound, no lower
  layer payment, no PP5.0 normalization, and no F2 target close;
- **falsifier:** an official minus row violating the order law, a collision
  in the printed Frobenius root set, a rank other than `hR`, or a ternary
  kernel word of weight at most `2R`.
