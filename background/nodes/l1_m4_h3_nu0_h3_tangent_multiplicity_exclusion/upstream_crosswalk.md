# Upstream crosswalk

This is a finite first-checkpoint split-pencil result in the terminology of
the exact second-moment ledger. It removes the nonzero-constant-term,
zero-valuation, cubic-eliminant component of the official `m=4,h=3` pencil.
It can be vendored as a compact local lemma after the depressed-pencil and
Euler-factorization reductions; it does not claim the full split-pencil
census or L1.
