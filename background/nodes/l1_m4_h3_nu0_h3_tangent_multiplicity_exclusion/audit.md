# Audit

The argument is local and characteristic-sensitive only at one point. Since
the tangent fiber has at least two roots, every local multiplicity is less
than `p`, so differentiating lowers its order by exactly one. The derivative
of `phi(Y)=4alpha Y/g(Y)` at the tangent value is nonzero because
`alpha`, `b`, `Delta`, `a`, and `g(y_0)` are nonzero. A tangent root at zero
adds one to the correction order and therefore cannot weaken the proof.

The conclusion uses the proved upper bound `deg rad(T)<=3`; it does not infer
that bound from computation or from one cyclic-code constraint.
