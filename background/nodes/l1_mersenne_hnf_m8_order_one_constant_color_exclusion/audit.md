# Audit

The theorem concerns the six roots of the reduced factor `L`, not all seven
roots of `P`. The removed root may have a different color. No
assignment-free color multiset is substituted for the rootwise Frobenius
map.

The divisions in the proof are guarded. `alpha=1` and `alpha=-1` are handled
before division by `1-alpha` or `1+alpha`; `d`, `alpha`, `30`, and the HNF
saturation factors are nonzero on every official row.

The two final integers are checked against every official characteristic,
not merely over characteristic zero. The proof does not infer the converse
of the colored reciprocal identities and does not claim that a nonconstant
color assignment survives the original cyclotomic or inner equations.
