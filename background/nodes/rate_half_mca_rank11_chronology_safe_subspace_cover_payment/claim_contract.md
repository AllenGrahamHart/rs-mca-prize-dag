# Claim contract

## Proved

- An additive slope payment for any explicit partition into correction
  subspaces of dimensions at most nine.
- Exact single-dimension cover thresholds at the official row.
- Exclusion of every unsafe two-five-space cover.
- The `64502` used-slice lower bound inside an assumed `2 x 5` factor
  presentation.

## Required semantics

- The cover classes partition represented row spaces from the anchored
  first-match ledger.
- Each assigned row space is contained in its printed correction subspace.
- The transverse envelope is the `tau=1679`, `h=38384` envelope.

## Not claimed

- Existence or uniqueness of a cover.
- A `2 x 5` factor presentation for arbitrary unsafe survivors.
- Equality or synchronization of rich locators.
- Payment of a factor presentation using at least `64502` slices.
