# Audit

- The two points are smooth because the contact parameter cuts each with
  length one; vertical multiplicity two then gives `t=unit*s^2`.
- The elementary-modification direction is `t/s=s`, not an idempotent delta
  class. It is nilpotent in the double local factor.
- Both directions vanish on the other fibre factors. The existence of
  `e-4>0` such factors separates their span from the constant line.
- The argument uses the natural constant summand of `pi_*O_C`; it does not
  depend on a noncanonical choice of splitting of the negative block.
- Only the two-section branch is excluded. No generic multiplication-map
  injectivity is claimed.
