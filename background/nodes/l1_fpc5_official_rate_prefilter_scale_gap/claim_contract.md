# Claim contract

- **Input:** the official rows `n=2^s`, `13<=s<=44`, at rates `1/R` for
  `R in {4,8,16}`, the source equation, and the exact residual `(PF6)`.
- **Output:** emptiness of `(PF6)` on the three scale prefixes `(SG1)` and
  the narrowed unresolved ranges `(SG2)`.
- **Preserved:** every official row, source scale, touched-petal count,
  defect degree, and every inequality in `(PF6)`.
- **Finite closure:** `t>=4` is removed symbolically; the remaining 4,608
  exact integer cells are replayed by independent algorithms.
- **Nonclaim:** the boundary parameter survivors are actual contributors,
  or any aggregate payment on the retained scales.
