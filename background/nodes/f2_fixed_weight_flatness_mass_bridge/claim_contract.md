# Claim contract

- **Input:** a finite-field linear map on the binary cube and
  fixed-weight mean-plus-one bounds `(FW-1)` on a declared weight band.
- **Output:** exact bounds `(FW-2)` and `(FW-3)` for the full-cube weighted
  ternary mass, plus complement symmetry.
- **Closure:** proof; no assumptions are hidden in the implication.
- **Nonclaims:** no fixed-weight flatness theorem; no identification with
  upstream's pruned first-match `Q` atom; no PP5.0 normalization or F2
  extras-budget close.
- **Falsifier:** a finite linear map satisfying `(FW-1)` but violating
  `(FW-2)`, or a failure of the complement bijection.
