1. The terminal primitive degree-six groups are `A5,S5,A6,S6`, all with
   subdegrees `1,5`.
2. A kernel-free degree-ten outer action can only be one of four
   wreath-type groups or `A10,S10`.
3. The `A10,S10` point stabilizers have no primitive degree-six quotient.
4. Every admissible wreath-type chain has an intermediate subgroup of
   endpoint index five and therefore forces an inner-degree-five map.
5. In the nontrivial-kernel case, the four-point orbit forces a synchronized
   compatibility class of size five or ten.
6. Size five is excluded. Size ten gives a degree-ten same-fiber suborbit
   of size four, so the degree-ten map factors to inner degree two or five.
7. Every `m=6` producer is impossible or routes to `m=2`.
8. The independent transverse frontier has 12 types in degrees `2,3,4`.
