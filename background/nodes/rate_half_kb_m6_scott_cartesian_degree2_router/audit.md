# Audit

- The kernel-free argument uses all 45 transitive degree-ten groups, not
  only primitive groups.
- Divisibility by 600 is necessary because the degree-ten point stabilizer
  has a primitive degree-six quotient of order divisible by 60.
- The two order-14400 wreath forms have the same parity base but distinct
  lifts of the block swap; both are reconstructed independently.
- The unique remote `A5` composition factor and the realized outer
  automorphism distinguish the `A5` quotient in entry 40 from the `S5`
  quotients in entries 41--43; the audit then verifies quotient orders and
  subdegrees `1,5`.
- `A10,S10` are excluded by normal-subgroup arguments in `A9,S9`, not by
  an unchecked subgroup search.
- Scott twists are not assumed realizable. They are refined into
  permutation-compatible classes before synchronized columns are formed.
- Incompatible `A6` twists are transitive on six points; they do not
  contribute hidden fixed counterparts.
- The size-ten case is not routed through the earlier generic `m=10`
  theorem, which could obscure a cycle. Its four-point orbit is proved to
  lie in one column, forcing a proper right factor directly.
- No degree-two row, owner, or ledger quantity is closed.
