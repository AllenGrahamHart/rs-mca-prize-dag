# Audit

1. The argument uses the stronger identity `supp(b_0,b_1)=U`, not only the
   definition `U=S_alpha union S_beta`.
2. Since `c^L(gamma)=c_gamma` at all three assigned centers, every center
   error support is contained in `U`, including the third center `theta`.
3. `x_*` is outside `U` because it is a padding root for the nonempty
   supported factor `g_*`; padding lies outside `U_0`, and `x_*!=s_0`.
4. Therefore a center occurrence of `Q(gamma,x_*)=0` is necessarily padded,
   so a correction-center root would also be a `g_*` root.
5. The contradiction uses the separated coprimality exactly.
6. Once correction-center overlap is absent, the only contribution to `J`
   comes from center roots of `g_*`, whose exact count is at most one.
7. The two remaining scalar coefficients are not presumed independent and
   no rank conclusion is inferred from their count.
