# Audit

The three substitutions are exact:

```text
h>=d+ell-b
  iff d-(t-1)ell<=b;

(N-d)+(t ell-d)<ell+(ell-b)
  iff 2d>N+(t-2)ell+b;

r=2d-h=2d-t ell,
u=ell-(h-d)=d-(t-1)ell.
```

Strict inequalities are converted to integer endpoints only in `(PF7)`.
No sampled percentage, asymptotic estimate, or floating-point comparison is
used in the proof.
