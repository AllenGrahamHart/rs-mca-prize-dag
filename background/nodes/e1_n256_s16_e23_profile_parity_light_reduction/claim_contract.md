# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the proved `V=48` endpoint;
- the proved sparse-L1 recurrence and signed-chord gate;
- the complete proved one-diameter light atlas;
- the collision norm criterion.

## Output

Every `V=46` profile-`(3,4,0)` candidate lies in one of four profiles on one
of eight exact affine templates.

## Nonclaims

No surviving vector is excluded here.  No cubic cutoff, lower variance,
profile `(4,2,0)`, later band, route-uniform allowance, or prize terminal is
claimed.

## Falsifier

A candidate with `L>13`, an eighth energy profile, a surviving seven-odd
profile, or a light support outside the pinned three-odd atlas refutes this
router.
