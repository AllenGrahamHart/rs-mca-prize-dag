# Audit

1. Congruences are solved modulo `p^6-1`, not merely modulo `p-1`.
2. Base-field logarithms are embedded with multiplier `(p^6-1)/(p-1)`.
3. Smith solutions are replayed in every original exponent equation.
4. Both signed-target and all-twelve-product injectivity are checked.
5. Positive-dimensional `Z2` systems have polynomial collision certificates;
   finite sampling is not used to exclude them.
6. Guarded live-type certificates are necessary product packets only.
