# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the strict-boundary source and locator define the exact biform
  `(STB4)`, with at least `2p+r_A` fully split domain rows and
  `(e+15)/2+r_A` fully split parameter fibers.
- **Inputs:** the strict-boundary minimum-word reduction, the fixed-line
  Hankel locator equation, and standard dual-GRS interpolation on `U_0`.
- **Outputs:** exact bidegree `(e-1,p-2)`, row splitting `(STB5)`, and clean
  fiber factorization `(STB9)`.
- **Falsifier:** failure of termwise divisibility by `Lambda_2`, a moment in
  `(STB3)` that does not vanish, an `X`-degree above `p-2`, or a clean fiber
  not proportional to its inside-support locator.
- **Nonclaims:** the biform is not asserted reducible, rational, or
  impossible; the coordinates present at both endpoints are deliberately
  excluded from the row count.
