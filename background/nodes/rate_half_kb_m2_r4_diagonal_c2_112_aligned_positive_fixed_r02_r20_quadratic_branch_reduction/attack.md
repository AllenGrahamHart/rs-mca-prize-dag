# Attack surface

The generic frontier has two qualitatively different pieces:

1. The surviving cubic plus `J11` curve for each target. Derive a quotient
   ring normal form of another full-identity coefficient, reducing after
   every multiplication; do not expand the degree-70 coefficient globally.
2. The degree-12 resultant factor. Seek a low-degree eliminant, syzygy, or
   factor-specific full-identity reduction before another basis request.

Replay `F06/F07` only after one representative route closes. Rank drop is
already proved empty in a separate node.
