# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the exact supported rank-loss divisor consumes `e-6` degrees
  of the degree-`e-2` regular determinant, leaving one quartic in both
  `u=4` root patterns; the marked determinants sharpen to `(RQP3),(RQP5)`.
- **Currency:** local Smith valuation, exact excess degree, and the heavy-row
  parameter factorizations.
- **Dependencies:** the general middle-adjugate theorem, the quadratic
  gap-four normal forms, and the two-simple marked factorization.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** no squarefreeness, coprimality, correction-divisor identity,
  or packet exclusion for `E_4`.
- **Falsifier:** a supported rank-loss valuation missing from `D_1`, or a
  quotient degree different from four.
- **Next gate:** classify the quartic through the Hankel/source coefficients
  or relate it to the degree-four total correction divisor.
