# Audit

- Local Smith form gives a lower valuation for `D_1`; exact quotient degree
  then supplies the quartic. Equality of individual Smith exponents is not
  assumed.
- At a common root of `G_1,G_2`, rank loss two forces the product
  multiplicity two in `D_1`.
- The same symbol `E_4` denotes the branch-specific quartic; no equality
  between the double and two-simple branches is asserted.
- The quartic may absorb additional multiplicity at an already supported
  slope.
- No identification with `S_B^2` or `S_1S_2` is made.
