# Proof

For each of the eight exact common inputs, Singular computes the reduced
standard basis of the localized common ideal.  Reduction of
`t-(epsilon_1 epsilon_2)r^2` is zero in every row.

The selected degree-seven product cofactor reduces to a nonzero normal form.
Adding that cofactor to the common ideal gives dimension zero.  Singular's
vector-space dimension is four in every BC- row and six in every BC+ row.
Consequently the cofactor-open is a genuine dense branch and its omitted
boundary is finite with the stated scheme degree.

Only one cofactor is used.  Its zero fiber is a conservative finite boundary;
the proof does not identify it with the full rank-at-most-four locus.
