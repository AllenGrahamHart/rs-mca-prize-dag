# Audit

1. Both affine coefficients must be nonzero. At `t=0` or `t=1`, the affine
   map sends all of `H` to one point or preserves `H`, respectively.
2. The norm-one premise is load-bearing. A prime-subfield multiplicative
   group can meet a nondegenerate affine image in far more than two points.
3. The quadratic equation is only a necessary norm equation; this is enough
   for an upper bound and does not assert that both roots lie in `H`.
4. `I_aff` includes `(z,w)=(1,1)`, while `R` does not. The subtraction by one
   is exact for `t!=0`.
5. The Mersenne-characteristic multiplicative subgroup descends to
   `F_(p_M^2)`, not `F_(p_M)`. This route is separate from the prime-subfield
   Mattarei descent.
6. The deployed Mersenne-31 line round is the `chi`-projection of a twin
   coset. It is not this multiplicative-subgroup instance, and no adapter from
   that projected domain to the H3 ledger is supplied here.
7. The scoped C36' close does not compose any adjacent-row owners.
