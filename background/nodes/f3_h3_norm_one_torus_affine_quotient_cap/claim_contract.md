  norm-one torus in at most two points; the H3 identity point sharpens this to
  `R(t)<=1`
- **scope:** subgroups of the quadratic norm-one torus, including the
  order-`2^21` multiplicative subgroup inside `F_(p_M^4)` for
  `p_M=2^31-1`
- **dependency:** the exact H3 PGL2 identity `R=I_aff-1`
- **consumer effect:** proves `17X_18<=300n^2` on that scoped multiplicative
  extension instance
- **nonclaims:** no bound for the deployed Mersenne-31 Chebyshev line round,
  prime-field corridor subgroups, a Mattarei extension, the full DSP8
  inequality, or an adjacent-safe row certificate
- **falsifier:** a nonidentity target with three quotient representations on a
  subgroup satisfying the printed norm-one premise
