# Result

Every H3 multiplicative subgroup contained in a quadratic norm-one torus has
`R(t)<=1` at every nonidentity target. Consequently its cutoff-18 weighted
excess satisfies `17X_18<17n^2`. For `p=2^31-1`, this applies to the
order-`2^21` subgroup of `F_(p^4)^*`, but not to the deployed Chebyshev
line-round evaluation domain.
