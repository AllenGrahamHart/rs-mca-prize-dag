# Claim contract

This abstract ledger assumes one unique circuit per selected rank-ten
eleven-set, the exact omitted-pair census, one global capacity `G` for all
rank-nine marks, and recordwise caps for supports two through nine.

It strengthens the former baseline-45 ledger by pricing every missing
rank-nine shadow.  The premium weights are exactly the rank-eight shadow
counts; no separate rank-eight capacity is invoked.

The node does not construct `G` or the cap vectors.  It asserts no kernel
payment, finite-row comparison, chronology movement, or prize closure.
