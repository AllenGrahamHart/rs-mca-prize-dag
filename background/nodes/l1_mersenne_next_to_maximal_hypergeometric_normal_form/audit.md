# Audit

The recurrence is an exact coefficient form of the already-proved quadratic
outer identity. It does not assume a field descent. Divisions in the
top-down construction are only by integers at most `h<p` and by `2A`, which
is nonzero in the dependency.
The generating-function powers are truncated generalized-binomial series,
so they make sense for field-valued exponents without choosing integer lifts.

The hypergeometric form applies only to `ord_0(T)=0`. In that chamber the
dependency already gives the full differential identity modulo `P`; the
order-one chamber has one removed root and is not silently inserted into the
same formula.

The assertion `s notin F_p` uses the prior rational-derivative exclusion:
`theta=h` is in `F_p`, so a survivor cannot also have `c` in `F_p`.

The torsion condition in the order-one chamber uses only the known split
value zero and the already-proved subgroup gate. It is necessary, not a
claim that every point of `Phi_h(A,c)=0` lifts to an outer or inner passport.

The registered `p=31` analogue is evidence only. Its certificate independently
replays all symbolic remainders and the common gcd, but no analogue output is
used to prove the characteristic-uniform normal form or an official row.
