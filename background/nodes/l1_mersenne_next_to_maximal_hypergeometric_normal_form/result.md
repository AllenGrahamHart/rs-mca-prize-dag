# Result

The live next-to-maximal outer search is no longer a search over all
degree-`h` polynomials. Its order-zero chamber is one explicit
hypergeometric family with one cyclotomic remainder test, and its order-one
chamber is one explicit recurrence curve intersected with a printed torsion
condition. Both residues remain open.

The exact `(m,h,p,n)=(8,7,31,256)` analogue has common order-zero remainder
gcd `s-1`; after removing its prime-field part, the quotient is `1`. This
calibrates the univariate gcd route but is not an official exclusion.
