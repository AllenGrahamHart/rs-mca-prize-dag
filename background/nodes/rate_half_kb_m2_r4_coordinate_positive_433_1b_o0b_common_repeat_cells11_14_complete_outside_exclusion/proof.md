# Proof

The complete cell-11 theorem excludes all `16*105=1,680` raw systems. The
duplicate-role transport is a bijection from these systems to all cell-14
systems at identical sign and outside-label scope. Therefore cell 14 is also
empty on 1,680 labels. The two role cells are disjoint owner classes, so the
union contains `32*105=3,360` empty labels. QED.
