# Result

The extremal paired split biform is content-free. Every irreducible
factor has the exact domain degree

```text
n_j=ceil((3p-3+d_A)m_j/(3e)).
```

Its complete factor-degree multiset has exactly one of three forms: one
large odd factor; two large odd factors plus one small odd factor; or one
huge even factor plus one small odd factor. All remaining factors are
ordinary even. This replaces an unrestricted reducible escape by three
exact degree profiles with no residual degree slack.
