# Attack

1. Preserve the complete typed consumer descriptor through every descent.
2. Sum exact leaf ratios after first ownership; do not replace them by
   `n^r` independently at each state.
3. Seek a telescoping potential combining divisor depth, residual dimension,
   and quotient scale. A valid potential must have a row-independent terminal
   exponent.
4. Separate any branch where scale recursion pays the numerator from a branch
   requiring Face-4 payment; do not count the same point in both.
5. Stress-test candidate potentials on full-space and near-full-dimensional
   rational-normal families; reject any scope that excludes them only by
   assertion.
