# Claim contract

- **Scope:** every descriptor proved admissible by
  `f_prize_consumer_flat_scope`.
- **Currency:** distinct unpaid section points after tangent, quotient,
  dihedral, and descent first ownership.
- **Required uniformity:** one absolute exponent, not `B(r)`.
- **Inputs:** exact spread leaf bounds and polynomially many memoized descent
  states.
- **Nonclaim:** polynomial state count alone gives polynomial population;
  `r<j` alone is the required quantitative scope; a full-space or near-full
  case is harmless without an explicit owner or scope proof.
