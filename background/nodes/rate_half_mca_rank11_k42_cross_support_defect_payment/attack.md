capacity exceeded demand by
`2710771376158610722953158157862051010402433288229120154217278`.

The prior ledger maximized each sparse support stratum independently.  The
cross-support theorem prices their common annihilator span.  A support-five
deletion within four completions of saturation constrains lower-support
circuits globally; if no such deletion exists, the support-five deletion cap
itself improves.  This exhaustive coupling lowers the premium enough to
close by `4081031051590194485758587836050845115467905186032497191061176`.

At `K'=43`, the same coupling saves almost the same absolute premium but no
longer pays the rapidly changing endpoint.  The next attack needs a stronger
coupling, another global resource, or a different component ledger.
