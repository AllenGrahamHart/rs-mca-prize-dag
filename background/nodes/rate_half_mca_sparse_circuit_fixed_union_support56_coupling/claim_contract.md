# Claim contract

- `D` and `W` are fixed before any circuit is selected.
- The residual budget is `R=K-u-g` and must be nonnegative.
- The outside ground-set size satisfies `N>=R+4`.
- The original circuit's inside set is independent and is contracted exactly.
- Contracted-matroid circuits are an overcount; no converse lifting is used.
- Flat caps count all outside ground elements, including loops and parallels.
- The adjacent coupling is used only for `i<=3`, where both annihilators are nonzero.
- The weighted envelope uses nonnegative integer weights and exact floors.
- No distributional, generic-position, or field-size premise is assumed.
