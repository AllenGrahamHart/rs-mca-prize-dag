# Claim contract

## Inputs

- The PROVED relative `(H_C)` core and full-rank anchor construction.
- The PROVED relative core-interpolant and one-ray payment.
- Standard MDS generalized-weight growth for a punctured Reed-Solomon code.

## Output

The many-ray residual is reduced to:

```text
dimension at least 12,
or a positive-dimensional evaluation rank-flat / polynomial clone component.
```

At dimensions at most nine, every surviving component must also absorb all
high slope coefficients of the fixed core interpolant.

## Nonclaim

No positive-dimensional component classification, high-dimensional census,
or final `(H_C)` abundance bound is proved.
