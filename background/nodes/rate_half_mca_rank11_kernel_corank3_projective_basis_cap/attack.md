# Attack

A counterexample must exhibit one of the following:

- a zero normal despite `K=s=3`;
- two proportional incident normals despite the `m-1` outside-one-span
  bound;
- three collinear incident projective points despite the `m-2`
  outside-two-span bound;
- a projective set contained in one plane despite full incident rank;
- more than `C(m-1,4)` coplanar quadruples in a spanning no-three-collinear
  `m`-point set;
- two parameter points determined by one independent affine-hyperplane
  quadruple.

The cap may be sharpened only by improving the extremal coplanar-quadruple
bound using structure absent from the present hypotheses.
