For each literal cell, reconstruct the generic `V!=0` branch from the pinned
atlas and select the retained degree-12 factor. Its nonnamed `x`-leading
factor is irreducible of degree six. `F04/F07` share the 15-term factor with
hash `980f453a...`; `F05/F06` share the distinct 17-term factor with hash
`68d2b371...`. Both target labels are replayed literally.

Over the deployed prime, first compute the ideal generated by `L6` and the
retained factor. Its Gröbner basis has size eight and dimension one. Reduce
both essential reconstructed rows by that basis, adjoin the remainders, and
compute the full literal Gröbner basis. The result remains one-dimensional;
the basis has size 46 in the `F04/F07` cells and 43 in the `F05/F06` cells.

Finally reduce the cumulative product of all 24 transported named-unit
factors. No individual factor specializes to zero, and the product first
reduces to zero after factor 17 in every literal cell. Thus the complete
generic localizer belongs to each full ideal. Localization therefore gives
the unit ideal, so every named-open `L6=0` branch is empty over the algebraic
closure, hence over the deployed extension. QED.
