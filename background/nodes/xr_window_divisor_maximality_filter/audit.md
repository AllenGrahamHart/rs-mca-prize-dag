# Audit

- The predicate is evaluated on the reconstructed maximal pair, not on an
  isolated raw locator. This is what retains first-match and strip semantics.
- The inversion is finite because agreement depth is bounded by `n-k`.
- Bonferroni parity follows from the sign of every omitted maximal-depth
  stratum; no probabilistic or independence hypothesis is used.
- Negative coefficients forbid replacing each raw moment by an unrelated
  upper bound. The signed aggregate must be controlled coherently.
- The theorem gives an exact interface to maximality but no estimate for its
  inputs. Neither critical child is promoted by this result alone.
- The verifier exhausts arbitrary small nonnegative spectra, which also
  covers arbitrary fiber-constant predicate sub-spectra.
