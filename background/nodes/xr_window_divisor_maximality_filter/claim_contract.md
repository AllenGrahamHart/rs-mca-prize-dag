# Claim contract

| field | value |
|---|---|
| claim | The predicate-filtered fiber identity `(PF)`, exact inversion `(INV)`, and both Bonferroni inequalities `(BON)`. |
| inputs | Unique interpolation from every raw support and a predicate depending only on the reconstructed maximal pair. |
| output | An exact signed support-moment representation, and finite upper and lower truncations, for maximal exact-depth occupancy. |
| load-bearing hypotheses | `Pi` is constant on each maximal-pair fiber; all maximal-depth spectra are finite and nonnegative. |
| falsifier | A raw support in two fibers, a nonconstant fiber predicate, failed inversion, or either reversed parity inequality. |
| nonclaim | No signed moment estimate, row-sharp occupancy bound, full-rank anti-concentration theorem, or deficient mixed-profile census is proved here. |
