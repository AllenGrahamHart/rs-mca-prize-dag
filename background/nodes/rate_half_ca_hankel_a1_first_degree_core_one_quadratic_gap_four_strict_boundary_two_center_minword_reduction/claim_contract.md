# Claim contract

- **Claim:** a pair at union `rho+p` has exactly two line centers, total
  off-line triple-union excess `p`, and at least `p+2` exact minimum-word
  center differences.
- **Inputs:** the macroscopic pair dichotomy, exact light incidence and
  deficits, affine center geometry, and RS minimum distance.
- **Outputs:** source partition `(SBR4)`, excess identity `(SBR6)`, minimum
  words `(SBR8)--(SBR9)`, and clean count `(SBR10)`.
- **Falsifier:** a third line center, a total excess different from `p`, or a
  zero-excess center difference that is zero or not minimum weight.
- **Nonclaims:** no distinctness or exclusion of the strict boundary.
