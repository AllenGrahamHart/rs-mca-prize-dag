# Audit

1. The maps live in the generic squared quotient; they are not source-root
   lifts.
2. The primary calculation solves and checks three exact rational-function
   Krylov systems, not interpolated finite-fiber systems.
3. The independent checker proves the defining linear identity exactly with
   NTT polynomial products and separately replays all map actions at `t=2`.
4. Large coefficient degrees are an output-complexity fact, not a claim
   about an exceptional-fiber locus.
5. No guard is yet shown nonzero on any residue component.
6. No colored edge, cell, route, K3 row, or Prize status is claimed.
