# Audit

- The factor is the full gcd of `(Y,Z)` degree exactly one; higher-degree
  factors are not included in this branch.
- Coefficients may initially lie in the algebraic closure.  Johnson remains
  valid there, and two captured `F`-rational pairs make the surviving center
  `F`-rational.
- Primitivity is needed to remove `gcd(A,B)` after one section shows that it
  also divides `C`.
- The scalar parameter degree is `5-max(deg A,deg B)`, not always five.
- `A` and `B` have no common evaluation zero after normalization, so `tau`
  is defined at every inside coordinate.
- The Johnson denominator at `s=4` is positive but only 130301; its exact cap
  is 802, still far below 4,982.
- The `A=0` case is a projective center at infinity, not a finite MCA slope.
- The node identifies the primitive-star shape but does not pay its slope
  population.
