# Claim contract

- **Proved:** coefficient gaps, multiplicative periodicity, and quotient
  locator form are equivalent on a cyclic multiplicative coset.
- **Proved:** maximal common coefficient scale gives unique quotient
  extraction with exact depth `ceil((t+1)/c)-1`.
- **Proved:** locator-difference degree descends by the exact factor `c`.
- **Dyadic consequence:** quotient-borne nonconstant pairs have even side
  width and even difference degree.
- **Quantifier:** every cyclic coset and every characteristic coprime to its
  order; no official-row size estimate is used.
- **Nonclaims:** coefficient-primitivity bounds the number of pairs; every
  pair with `gcd(n,e,d)>1` is quotient-borne; the quotient compiler has paid
  its row numerator; or primitive local SP control is closed.
