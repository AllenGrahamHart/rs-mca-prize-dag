# Audit

1. The root-domain assumption is a multiplicative coset, not a Chebyshev
   projection or an arbitrary smooth set.
2. Characteristic coprime to `n` is needed for distinct quotient fibers and
   is automatic for multiplicative subgroup order `n`.
3. Coefficient indices are measured down from the leading coefficient;
   divisibility of exponents follows because `c|e`.
4. The common scale is the gcd of the two individual maximal scales, not a
   scale inferred only from `A-B`.
5. `c|gcd(n,e,d)` is necessary, not sufficient, for quotient ownership.
6. Quotient extraction preserves the shift-pair inequality but changes its
   depth by a ceiling; replacing the ceiling by a floor is rejected.
7. This sieve removes imprimitive pairs but supplies no local-degree bound on
   the coefficient-primitive residue.
