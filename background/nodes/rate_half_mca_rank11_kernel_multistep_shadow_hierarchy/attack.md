# Attack

The first genuinely new rows have `t=3`.  At the old `K'=18102` optimizer,
the `d=5,7,9` triple rows are violated by a factor about `3.083935`, while
the `d=4,6,8` rows are slack by the reciprocal factor.  This makes the family
a structural cut rather than an integer-rounding correction.

For later attacks, equality requires simultaneously:

- the dual independent-set lower bound to be sharp;
- every successive generalized-MDS closure cap to be sharp;
- every target to attain the maximum loopless coloop count.

Any incompatibility among these equality cases strengthens the hierarchy.
