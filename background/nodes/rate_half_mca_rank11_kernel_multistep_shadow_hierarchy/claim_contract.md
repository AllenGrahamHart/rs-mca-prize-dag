# Claim contract

## Quantifiers

The inequalities hold support-by-support, for every kernel-lane exact support
and every admissible pair `3<=d<=9`, `2<=t<=d-1`.

## Inputs

- the evaluation matroids are loopless;
- generalized-MDS bounds a rank-`(10-d+j)` closure by `K'-d+j`;
- `m'=K'+67472`.

## Output

Twenty-eight exact linear inequalities coupling `I_d` to `I_(d-t)`.

## Nonclaims

The theorem is a resource family, not by itself a kernel payment.  It does
not constrain `I_0`, pay rank eight, close error rank eleven, or resolve a
prize row.
