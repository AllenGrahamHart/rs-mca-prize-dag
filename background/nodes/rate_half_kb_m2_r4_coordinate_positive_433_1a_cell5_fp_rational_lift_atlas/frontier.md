# Frontier

Reduce the target-free `DE+/DE-/BE` equations chartwise to the reciprocal
`(b,t)` curve.  The `r` formula is global on the guards.  Use the four `c`
charts separately, saturating each signed system by its selected `L_j`, and
take the union of their survivor ledgers.  No extra deployed-field branch is
needed where every `L_j` vanishes.

The leading coefficient of the reciprocal quartic is also a deployed-field
unit on the guards, so finite-algebra reductions may use a rank-four `b`
basis without a separate leading-coefficient branch.

Append only the residual `BE` cubic and its unsquared sum row.  Keep any
extension-field denominator branch outside this `F_p` claim.
