# Audit

- Finite flatness uses the proved absence of vertical components, not
  irreducibility.
- The two points are distinct points of `C` but have the same image under
  `pi`; their quotient is `k_(x_*)^2`, annihilated by the base parameter.
- Smith exponents are therefore `(1,1)`, not `(2)`. This rules out an
  `O(2)` summand.
- The automorphism argument retains the rank of the projection to the
  negative block, which is the complete orbit invariant.
- The PENCIL branch is recorded as a low-degree map, not excluded by an
  unjustified smoothness, gonality, or multiplication-injectivity claim.
