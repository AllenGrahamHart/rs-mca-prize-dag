# Audit

- `u>=4` comes from the independent bound `v<=Delta`, not from a root
  multiplicity assumption.
- Zero omission makes every excess root new and simple. Ordinary incidences
  and excess degree away from residual-root rows are then killed by the
  unit-residual cube law.
- A single simple heavy root is excluded by vertical degree, but two simple
  heavy roots remain and must be retained.
- The parity of odd official `e` is needed to reduce the correction pairs
  from five formal multiples-of-three pairs to `{3,9}`.
- In the double branch, the coefficient two in the contact divisor follows
  from `3k=m+2n`; it is not copied from the simple-root calculation.
- The degree-two class has only its canonical reduced-root quotient section.
  This classification is not an exclusion.
