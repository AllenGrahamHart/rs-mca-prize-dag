# Audit

- The supported rank-loss sum may be below `Delta`; the variable `w`
  retains that gap throughout.
- The omission lower bound charges every non-distinguished omission to an
  available nonbaseline rank-loss degree. It does not assume all determinant
  zeros are supported.
- Effectivity is proved coefficientwise from local intersection lengths.
  No transversality or fibre separability is used.
- The length-two divisor is a proper subdivisor of the vertical fibre, so
  its modification subspace is fibre-nilpotent and misses constants even if
  its support is nonreduced.
- The theorem excludes only pencil splittings, not the canonical packets.
