# `A=1` core-one no-ordinary packets are effective canonical classes

- **status:** PROVED
- **closure:** vertical cube ledger and fibre-supported elementary modification
- **consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`

Retain any core-one scalar packet with `I_0=0`, namely

```text
(u,v,c) in {(0,2,2),(1,1,3),(2,0,4)}.                (NOE1)
```

In the bounded-divisor notation, every residual contact zero lies on the
distinguished vertical fibre and

```text
H_2:=Z_c-E_u                                             (NOE2)
```

is an effective degree-two subdivisor of that fibre. Consequently

```text
O_C(rho+2,-e-1)=O_C(H_2).                              (NOE3)
```

Moreover the positive length-two modification defined by `H_2` projects
with rank two to the negative block of `pi_*O_C`. Therefore

```text
pi_*O_C(H_2)
 =O direct_sum O(1-d)^2 direct_sum O(-d)^(e-3),
h^0(C,O_C(H_2))=1.                                    (NOE4)
```

Thus all three no-ordinary packets have only their canonical degree-two
section; none has a pencil branch.

## Scope

`I_0=1,2` retain signed degree-two divisor classes.
