# Proof

The complete cell-5 theorem excludes every principal label in cell 5. The duplicate-role theorem sends every cell-5 principal system bijectively to a cell-8 system while permuting source signs and the complete 105-label set. Therefore the principal branch is empty in cell 8.

The global product-rankdrop theorem excludes the complementary rank-drop branch independently in both role cells. Principal and rank-drop branches exhaust the deployed route. Hence role orbit `[5,8]` is completely empty.
