# Claim contract

- **Claim:** every nontrivial minimal tensor presentation of an official
  Shape-A survivor has a birational parameter coefficient map.
- **Dependencies:** exact split rows, nonzero parameter fibers, and the
  all-excess column-deficit ledger.
- **Falsifier:** two empty columns within deficit `2e-7`, an active
  normalization fiber not completed inside `Gamma`, or a nontrivial common
  divisor of `m` and one of `3e,3e-1`.
- **Status effect:** one PROVED all-rank structural node; no critical status
  change.
- **Nonclaims:** no rank bound, image-degree bound, or Shape-A exclusion.
