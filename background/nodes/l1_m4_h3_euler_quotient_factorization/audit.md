# Audit - L1 m=4, h=3 Euler quotient factorization

1. The derivative of `X^n-alpha` is `4X^(n-1)` because `n=4(p+1)` in
   characteristic `p`.
2. The `R`-form Wronskian uses `-nRD`; replacing `n` by four is valid only
   as a field coefficient.
3. The elimination identity is `3g-Yg'=2aY+3b`; signs and factors matter.
4. Division by `X^nu` is exact because `R=X^nu U` and
   `XR'/X^nu=nu U+XU'`.
5. For `nu>0`, `bD(0)=-alpha` makes `b`, `D(0)`, and `alpha` nonzero.
6. The coefficients `4-3nu` are nonzero for `nu=1,2,3` on every official
   prime.
7. For `nu=0`, the separate constant-term argument uses `V(0)=0`; formula
   (5) is not applied.
8. The right leader in `(EQF3)` is `H R^3`; the `U` correction cannot cancel
   its degree.
9. The left outer factor has degree `p` because the earlier theorem proves
   `a!=0`.
10. Degree comparison gives equality `deg V=p+h-4`, not merely an upper
    bound.
11. The theorem is a factorization and degree compression, not an exclusion.
