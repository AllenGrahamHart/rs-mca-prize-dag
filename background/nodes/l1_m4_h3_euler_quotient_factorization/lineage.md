# Lineage

The Mason and Cartier nodes produced a low-degree differential eliminant but
used only its derivative-image constraints. Eliminating `D'` between that
Wronskian equation and the derivative of the original domain factorization
reveals a companion quotient with factors `D`, `2aR+3b`, and the Euler
derivative of `U`. Degree comparison converts the remaining eliminant degree
directly into the number of top Euler-coefficient cancellations.
