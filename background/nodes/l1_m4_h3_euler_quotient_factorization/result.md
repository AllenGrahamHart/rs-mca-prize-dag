# Result

Every surviving `m=4,h=3` valuation satisfies a second exact factorization
linking the complement, the derivative of the depressed inner polynomial,
and the cubic Wronskian eliminant. The eliminant has nonzero constant term,
and `V=nu U+XU'` has exact degree `p+deg(H)-4`, between `p-4` and
`p-nu-1`. This exposes at most three top Euler-coefficient cancellations but
does not yet exclude a valuation or close L1.
