# Claim contract

- **Proved identity:** all dyadic fold energies consume one exact budget
  `2e`; they are not independently charged.
- **Proved product gate:** every selected set of nonzero prefix-supplied folds
  obeys the exact cross-multiplied inequality `(HP-3)`.
- **Primitive clause:** the complete active set contains scale zero.
- **Cell specialization:** exact difference degree `d` supplies depth
  `T=e-d-1`.
- **Nonclaims:** higher-scale nonvanishing; zero-fold divisibility transfer;
  a count of active patterns or vectors; first-owner compatibility; or an X4
  status change.
