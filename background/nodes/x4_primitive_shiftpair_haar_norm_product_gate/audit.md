# Audit

1. The full Haar identity includes the terminal order-two level, but norm
   factors are used only at orders at least four.
2. The set `S` contains only algebraically nonzero folds.  No modular
   nonvanishing inference is made.
3. The lower exponent retains residue degrees and distinct Frobenius orbits.
4. The rational upper bound is always checked in cross-multiplied integer
   form; no floating-point rounding is part of the theorem.
5. Structural zero folds may carry additional information, as in the HGE4
   lane, but none is imported here.
