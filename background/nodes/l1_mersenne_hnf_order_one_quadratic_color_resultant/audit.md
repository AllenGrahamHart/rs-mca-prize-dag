# Audit

The theorem uses the pointwise identity `E(x)=x^(p+1)` and the official
congruence `p=-1 mod m`. The weaker remainder `E^m=1 mod L` alone does not
produce the polynomial `g_X`.

At `h=15`, only the seven-distinct-color lower bound is used; no
multiplicity claim is needed. At `h=7`, repeated colors are counted with
Sylvester intersection multiplicity, which is essential for the double
factor in (QCRS5).

The resultant records a color multiset, not the assignment between colors
and roots. It is a necessary endpoint for subsequent coefficient comparison,
not a sufficient outer-survivor certificate.
