# Audit

1. Directions, rather than affine lines, are counted at each full owner
   coordinate; the endpoint bank makes the 210 directions projectively
   distinct inside that plane.
2. A direction occurrence is a genuine root of its residual direction
   polynomial, so the degree cap is `K'-1`.
3. Distinct projective directions use disjoint unordered selected-point pair
   currencies, even if one direction has several parallel saturated lines.
4. The individual root floor comes from an actual saturated-line
   received-pair core.
5. Aggregate saturation uses the largest possible direction population in
   the denominator, so it remains a valid lower bound when the actual
   population is smaller.
6. No statement that most individual directions are nearly split is made;
   the saturation claim is aggregate.
