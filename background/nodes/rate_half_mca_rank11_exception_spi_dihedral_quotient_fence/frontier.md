# Frontier

Do not attempt to prove that twenty disjoint split fibers force a power map.
The explicit inversion quotient disproves that route.

The next useful theorem is one of:

1. a bounded-degree classification separating cyclic, dihedral, and
   primitive pencils at the actual twenty-fiber scale; or
2. a heavy-ruling semantic lemma showing that cyclic and dihedral pencils
   are already owned and paid by an existing factor/chronology column.

The general many-fiber toral theorem in the DAG has an `N^(2/3)`-scale
trigger and does not apply to twenty fibers.
