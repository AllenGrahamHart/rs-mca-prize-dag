# Claim contract

- **input:** an official row `F_(p^e)`, its generated field
  `B=F_p(mu_n)=F_(p^k)`, a coset domain `g mu_n`, and the same moment window
  and exponent set on both rows
- **output:** an exact bijection of t-null blocks, moment-trade block
  families, syndrome fibers, kernels, and weighted masses
- **consumer consequence:** all seven non-generating degree/order types reduce
  to three of the five signed generating types
- **nonclaims:** the generated-field row need not itself be an official
  prize-max ambient row; `(O1)` is not restored; no upper mass, max-fiber,
  extras-budget, PP5.0, or Prize endpoint is proved
- **falsifier:** a scaled subset or block family whose moment-zero/equality
  status changes, or a kernel word present on exactly one side of `(AI-2)`
