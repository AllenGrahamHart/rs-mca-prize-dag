# Claim contract

- **Proved:** the sum of every exact nonconstant `(e,d)` stratum with
  `4e^2>N(e+d)` is at most `5N^3/16` at one fixed base support.
- **Residual geometry:** every unpaid nonconstant record satisfies
  `4e^2<=N(e+d)` and `e>=t_XR+d+1`.
- **Currency:** local incident records, matching the maximum-degree X4/SP
  consumer; no sum over base supports occurs.
- **Transport:** first-owner deletion only reduces the aggregate.
- **Nonclaims:** the Johnson-nonpositive wedge, `D_0`, quotient-row arithmetic,
  the complete primitive budget, or either Prize result.
