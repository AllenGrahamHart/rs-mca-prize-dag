# Audit

1. The Johnson theorem for degree at most `d` legitimately bounds its exact
   degree-`d` subfamily; exact strata remain disjoint in the final sum.
2. The proof deliberately overcounts degree-lower records across cell bounds.
   This weakens the upper bound but cannot invalidate it.
3. The boundary-cell argument uses the exact step
   `Delta_(e,d+1)=Delta_(e,d)-N`; there is at most one such cell per `e`.
4. The aggregate is local at one base.  Summing it over all supports would be
   the wrong second-moment currency.
5. The residual allowance is sufficient, not an assertion that the remaining
   wedge attains it.
