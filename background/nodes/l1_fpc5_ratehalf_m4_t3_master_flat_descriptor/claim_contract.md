# Claim contract

## Input

One nonempty exact guarded LS6 atom after fixing the first source, touched
triple, defect `a`, and its determined cross-ratio.

## Output

An injective enlargement to the unpunctured full-domain split-flat section
`P intersect D_j(H)` with exact `(j,r)`, trivial common gcd, strict
`2r<j`, and an exponentially sub-unit ambient mean.

## Preserved

- one fixed atom and one projective locator point per monic candidate;
- the official full smooth domain and generated field;
- exact degree `j` and full split-root threshold `j`.

## Deliberately forgotten

- the requirement that all roots lie in the source core rather than `H`;
- the candidate-wise LS6 gcd guard.

Both omissions enlarge the counted set and are valid for an upper bound.

## Nonclaims

- no primitive max-to-mean bound;
- no quotient or dihedral owner transport;
- no invocation of consumer-scoped Conjecture F;
- no FPC5 payment.
