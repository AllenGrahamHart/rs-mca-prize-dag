# Proof: rate-half FPC5 `M=4,t=3` master-flat descriptor

## 1. Full-domain split-flat embedding

The exact complement-slice theorem puts every LS6 locator in the vector
space

```text
W_(lambda,a)^<=j
 ={D:deg D<=j, deg rem_(L_2L_3)(D Etilde)<=ell-a}.
```

An LS6 member is monic of degree `j`, divides `L_C`, and satisfies the
candidate-wise coprimality guard. Since `C subset H`, it also divides the
full domain locator `L_H`. Projectivization therefore maps it injectively to
`P intersect D_j(H)`. Monic normalization makes the projective
representative unique. Forgetting `D|L_C` in favor of `D|L_H`, and forgetting
the guard, only add points.

## 2. Exact dimensions and properness

If the atom is nonempty, the mu-basis balance theorem applies to one of its
saturated members. In the live branch it gives

```text
dim W_(lambda,a)^<=j=ell-2a+2.
```

Thus `P` has projective dimension `r=ell-2a+1`. The ambient projective space
of degree-at-most-`j` polynomials has dimension `j`, so

```text
j-r=(2ell-a)-(ell-2a+1)=ell+a-1,
j-2r=3a-2>=1.
```

This proves the numerical parts of `(MF1)` and `(MF3)`.

## 3. The flat-wide common gcd is trivial

Let `(F_p,W_p),(F_q,W_q)` be the two reduced mu-basis pairs. Nonemptiness in
this branch puts both basis pairs in the truncated space, and their
determinant satisfies

```text
F_pW_q-F_qW_p=kappa L_1L_2L_3,       kappa!=0.          (1)
```

Suppose a nonconstant polynomial `G` divided every locator in the flat.
Then `G` divides an LS6 member `D_0`. The polynomial `D_0` is squarefree and
split on `C`, so every root of `G` lies in `C`. Choose one such root `x`.
The core and petals are disjoint, hence the right side of `(1)` is nonzero at
`x`. But flat-wide divisibility gives `F_p(x)=F_q(x)=0`, making the left side
zero. This contradiction proves `gcd(P)=1`.

## 4. Sub-balance

The fixed `M=4` rate-half layout has

```text
n=(4ell+b-2)+b+4ell=8ell+2b-2.
```

Since `b<ell`, one has `n<=10ell-4`. Let `B_0` be the field generated by the
domain and the flat coefficients. It contains the order-`n` group underlying
`H`, so `Q=|B_0|` satisfies `Q>=n+1`. Official rows have `n>=2^13`.
Therefore

```text
log_2(binom(n,j)/Q^(j-r))
 <=n-(ell+a-1)log_2 Q
 <=n-13ell
 <=-3ell-4.
```

This proves `(MF4)`.

## 5. Pure multiplicative pullbacks

If a degree-`j` locator is `g(X^M)` for a nontrivial scale `M|n`, then `M`
divides `j`. Thus only `M|gcd(n,j)` can occur. At official rows `n` is a
power of two. Since `j=2ell-a` has the same parity as `a`, odd `a` makes
`j` odd and excludes every `M>1`. QED.
