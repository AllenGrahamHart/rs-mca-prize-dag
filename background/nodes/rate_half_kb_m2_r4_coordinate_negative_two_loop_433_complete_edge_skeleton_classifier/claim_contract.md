| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | Every complete negative `(4,3,3)` two-loop packet has no outside loop and has the unique outside signed-pair skeleton `(KB43S-1)--(KB43S-3)`. |
| assumptions | The exact source-facet census, negative complete-fiber Vieta equations with leading support, common-`K` skeleton `(1,0,1;2,0,1)`, and product injectivity. |
| interface | Five possible signed internal edge types may occupy `eta`; the other four internal and two colored cross types occupy `L^c`. |
| nonclaim | Actual `I` labels, quotient-label placement, product/q interpolation, skeleton deletion, coordinate closure, a row, or either Prize theorem. |
