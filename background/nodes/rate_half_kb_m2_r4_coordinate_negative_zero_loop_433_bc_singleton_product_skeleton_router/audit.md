# Audit

1. All congruences are solved in the order-`p^6-1` multiplicative group.
2. Negation uses exponent differences; reciprocal involutions use sums.
3. Every isolated Smith solution is replayed before guard testing.
4. Every free family in a deleted type has a polynomial collision certificate.
5. Duplicate root-sign packets are represented once by their `(b,c)` row.
6. Live certificates are not promoted to complete q/interpolation packets.
