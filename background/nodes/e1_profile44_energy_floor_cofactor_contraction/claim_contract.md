# Claim contract

## Inputs

- the exact profile-`(4,4)` local cofactor list;
- the proved official collision floor `E>=5`;
- the coefficient `L1` cap and negacyclic Parseval identity.

## Output

Every official collision has `m<=932364`; exactly `657` locally admissible
cofactors remain.

## Nonclaims

- no assertion that any retained cofactor occurs;
- no orbit count within a retained cofactor;
- no payment of the profile or complete E1 pair budget.
