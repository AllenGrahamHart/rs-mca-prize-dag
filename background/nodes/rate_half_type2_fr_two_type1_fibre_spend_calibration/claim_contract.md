# Claim contract

- **claim:** the two-type-1 fibre argument gives every type-2 slope outside
  spend at least `2m+1`, yielding the exact total cap `9m/2`; the same
  argument reaches the calibrated closing spend exactly when the two named
  fibres have total size at least `25m/4`;
- **scope:** the strict endpoint at `a=7m-1`, `4|m`, with a two-dimensional
  representation pencil, minimum joint-support comparison, and exactly two
  type-1 slopes;
- **consumer use:** replace the provisional claim that two type-1 fibres
  close `(FR)` by the exact residual `m/4` in fibre mass, or equivalently
  `m/4` in the two type-1 support deficits;
- **not claimed:** existence of the two-type-1 stratum, forced
  `25m/4` concentration, tightness of the overlap inequality, or closure of
  the rate-half crossing;
- **falsifier:** failure of the projective fibre partition, a supported
  pair violating the minimum joint-support comparison, an arithmetic
  mismatch in `(TFC5)`, or a different threshold for the lower bound in
  `(TFC3)` to reach `p_req`.
