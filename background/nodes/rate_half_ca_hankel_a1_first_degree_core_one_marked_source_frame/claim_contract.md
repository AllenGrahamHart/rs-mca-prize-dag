# Claim contract

- **Claim:** the first-degree core-one kernel coefficients form a common
  totally isotropic plane, and the marked determinant is the exact source
  subset sum `(MSF7)` equal to `c^2D_1g_*^2S_B^6`.
- **Currency:** the symmetric minimal-vector coefficient chain, finite moment
  sources after core contraction, and Cauchy--Binet.
- **Dependencies:** the general core-one middle-adjugate theorem and the
  marked-Hankel determinant gate.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** the source sum is not positive and its terms are not proved
  noncancelling; no support-size lower bound is inserted here.
- **Falsifier:** dependent kernel coefficients, failure of either endpoint
  isotropy, or a direct Cauchy--Binet expansion differing from `(MSF7)`.
- **Next gate:** exploit the actual RS source weights or a characteristic-
  specific invariant to rule out the factor `g_*^2S_B^6`.
