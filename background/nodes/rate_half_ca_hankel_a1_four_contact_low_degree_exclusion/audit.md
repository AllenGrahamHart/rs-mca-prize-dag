# Audit

1. Four contact copies, rather than three, make the target's first degree
   `-s-1`; this is what permits interpolation degree three.
2. The restriction-kernel first degree simplifies to `-rho-1`, independent
   of the core size.
3. The parameter offset is `4-beta`, not the three-contact offset `3-beta`.
4. The component-avoidance hypothesis is strict: equality in `(A1Q2)` is
   not covered.
5. The official threshold endpoints are retained because the cohomology
   inequality first reaches equality there.
