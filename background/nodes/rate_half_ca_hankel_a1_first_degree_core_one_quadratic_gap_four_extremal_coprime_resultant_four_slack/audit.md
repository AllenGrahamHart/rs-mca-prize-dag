# Audit

1. The resultant is nonzero only because coprimality was proved first.
2. Classified-row roots contribute at least `m` to local resultant order;
   tangencies can only increase the residual factor and are not assumed
   absent outside selected zero-excess roots.
3. Padded roots lie outside `U_0`, so none is already removed by `L_M^m`.
4. The exceptional-row exponent is derived from the exact `a/r/b` ledger,
   not from an unsupported claim that all roots of its row polynomial are
   supported slopes.
5. Repeated padding coordinates across slopes are retained with
   multiplicity in the product and in the resultant order.
6. `W_QG` is bounded above; equality of its degree is not asserted because
   resultant leading coefficients may drop.
7. No numerical experiment or irreducibility assumption enters the proof.
