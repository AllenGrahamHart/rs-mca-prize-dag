# Audit

The primary verifier pins both exact certificates, checks the full
sign/chart and component/sign Cartesian products, validates all dimensions
and ten-row kernel reductions, and enumerates both restricted group actions.
The independent audit recounts the raw and Burnside totals and mutates a
mixed chart to nonunit, a component row check to nonzero, and the S0 D-sign
action to identity. All three mutations must be rejected.
