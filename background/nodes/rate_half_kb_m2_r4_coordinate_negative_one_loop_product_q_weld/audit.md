# Audit

1. `p_h-p_s` is nonzero because the product map and source labels are
   injective.
2. `D(h)` and every `D(s)` are nonzero by leading support.
3. The residual form `C` is allowed to be constant; rank at most two covers
   both constant and genuinely linear cases.
4. Two welds are sufficient only after fixing two distinct nonloop labels
   and retaining the product gate.
5. No claim is made about the seven fibers outside `K`.
