# Audit

1. The bijection concerns the explicit polynomial boundary words `U_z`; it is
   not presented as a classification of every received word.
2. Those words are admissible worst-word inputs, so any universal upper ledger
   must control their heaviest residual prefix fiber.
3. The characteristic statement distinguishes full elementary prefixes from
   the `p`-free information carried by power-sum nullity.
4. The `F_17` example refutes only raw mode-at-null. A strip-aware
   exchange-compression theorem remains possible and is exactly the missing
   bridge a null-fiber route would need.
5. The proof and verifier use exact finite arithmetic only.
