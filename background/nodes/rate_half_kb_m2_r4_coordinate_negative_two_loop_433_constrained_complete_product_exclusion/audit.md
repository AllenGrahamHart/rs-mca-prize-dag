# Audit

1. Geometric normalized parameters are retained in quotient algebras; no
   false base-field rationality cut is used.
2. The three constrained ledgers remain separate rank-eight algebras.
3. All five forced types, two signs, and fifteen matchings are covered.
4. Primary projection degeneracies are rerouted before evaluation.
5. Norm/gcd and multiplication-matrix rank are independent unit tests.
6. Alternate `DF/EF` shards partition, rather than sample, their matchings.
7. Full interpolation is bypassed only because paired products are a proved
   necessary gate.
8. The conclusion is complete 433 closure, not coordinate closure.
