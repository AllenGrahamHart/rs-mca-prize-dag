# Claim contract

## Input

The full-rank component-star owner with within-support deficiency at most
`22320`, on a residual RS row with `m'-K'=67472`.

## Output

The owner pair is unique among all owners of that record with the same
deficiency ceiling. It is therefore independent of the ten-subset used to
find it.

## Nonclaim

No global owner count, first-match order, component-lane payment, KoalaBear
closure, or prize closure is asserted.
