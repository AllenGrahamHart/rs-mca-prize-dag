# Audit

The 3.4 MB primary packet is split into two digest-pinned JSONL shards. The
verifier streams all 48 rows and checks every digest, byte count, record
count, Cartesian key, terminal status, and orbit transport.

Root ownership is independent: FLINT computes each field-root part by
Frobenius gcd. A separate six-container replay reconstructs the quartic
missing and colored root sets, every reciprocal lift, and both final
antipodal/outside `q` intersections directly from the compact kernel. It
also joins every paid free row to its exact tower-boundary or
regularized-base owner.
