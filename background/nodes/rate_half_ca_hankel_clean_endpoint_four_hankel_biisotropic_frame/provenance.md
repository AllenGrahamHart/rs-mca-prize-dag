# Provenance

The coefficient-chain argument is the rectangular `A=3` analogue of the
repository's proved `A=1` coefficient bi-isotropic theorem. It is rederived
here because the strict endpoint has two adjacent square Hankel blocks and a
different Kronecker profile.

No computation is load-bearing. The primary verifier checks an exact
canonical singular-block model; the independent audit replays all four
Hankel identities on the banked `m=1`, `F_17` witness after a generic
parameter-basis change.
