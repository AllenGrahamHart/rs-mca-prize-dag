# Proof

The proved logarithmic router supplies one descended numerator `N` of degree
67, with degree 15 in `w`. Its exact metric and hash are reproduced before
the route calculation.

Fix one target. The generic quadratic reconstruction gives `w=-U/V`, with
`V` among the transported open factors. If

```text
N(w)=a_15 w^15 + ... + a_0,
```

then `N(-U/V)=0` is equivalent on `V!=0` to

```text
V^15 N(-U/V)=0.                              (KBLN-1)
```

The replay computes `(KBLN-1)` by homogeneous Horner evaluation. Each
coefficient and each intermediate product is reduced modulo the exact ideal
generated by the surviving cubic factor, the essential `E2,E3` rows, and
the transformed full-`J` factor `J11`. This yields the printed nonzero
remainders without ever expanding the substituted degree-67 polynomial.

Adjoin the corresponding remainder to the route ideal. The resulting bases
have sizes 35 and 44 for `R02` and `R20`. In each quotient, sequential
reduction of the complete square-free transported named product reaches zero
at factor 15. Therefore every algebraic point of the numerator intersection
lies on a forbidden named boundary. The named-open intersections are empty
over the base field and every extension. QED.
