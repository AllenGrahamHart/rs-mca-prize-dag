# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | In loop stratum `ell=0,1,2`, the negative common-`K` q equations reduce exactly to the square determinant `(KBNQ-3)` of size `5-ell`. |
| deletion interface | A nonzero determinant, or absence of a kernel with `B_2` nonzero on all `K`, deletes the signed packet. |
| assumptions | The complete negative Vieta equations and the proved loop-budget/factor pin. |
| nonclaim | Universal determinant nonvanishing, exclusion of any of the five multiplicity skeletons, full packet realization, owner/payment movement, a row, or either Prize theorem. |
