# Claim contract

## Proved

- The uniform `T=12` high-margin cap and low-mass floor over every shortened
  heavy-plane row.
- At most 58,361 low pair types and at least 645,992,192 ruling records.
- A per-ruling low-record cap of 236,448,715 and a three-ruling floor.
- At least 644,952,717 nonzero ruling records.
- One canonical ruling orientation has mass at least 322,476,359 and uses
  at least two planes.
- Left rulings synchronize the original factor; right rulings synchronize a
  residual factor across original slices.

## Required semantics

- Low pairs are fixed deterministically after the reversible affine gauge.
- Mass is first-owned and is never charged to two pair types or two ruling
  orientations.
- The Segre product map is injective and both factors are two-dimensional.

## Not claimed

- Payment of either ruling orientation or any aggregate selected-support
  ledger.
- Descent of a ruling plane to the base field.
- Equality or synchronization between different ruling planes.
- Closure of the recursive common-base horn, heavy bucket, or MCA.
