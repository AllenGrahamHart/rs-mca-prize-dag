# Audit

The 9.2 MB primary packet is split into two digest-pinned JSONL shards.
The verifier streams all 48 rows and checks their digests, byte and record
counts, Cartesian cover, terminal status, and orbit transport.

Independent FLINT root custody reconstructs every norm/guard root. A separate
six-container replay reconstructs all 1,824 missing-quartic roots and
reciprocal lifts, both signed quadratic pair equations and all 240 common
`q` roots, and all 480 final target-lane cuts directly from the compact
kernel. It also joins every paid free row to its exact proved owner.
