The rank-eleven kernel component demand, all nine fixed-basis record caps,
and the proved `d+2` basis-decoration divisor.

`10<=K'<=11641`, eliminating the dominant kernel lane on that interval.

the affine-owner lane, rank eleven, or either prize.
