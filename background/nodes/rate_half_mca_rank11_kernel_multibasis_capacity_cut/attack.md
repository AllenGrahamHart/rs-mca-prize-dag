# Attack

Replay every integer row and mutate the endpoint, wall, lane density,
record floor, and one decoration divisor. The first failed row must be
reported rather than hidden by interpolation.

Above `K'=11641`, do not seek a uniform scalar improvement to `M_d` without
new structure. Near the wall the `d=1` and `d=2` strata dominate; much
later the dominant corank moves upward. A continuation should constrain
the realizability or overlap of sharp low-basis flats, or introduce a
second resource that couples different bases.
