# Published input

I. Vyugin and S. Makarychev, *Solutions of polynomial equation over F_p and
new bounds of additive energy*, arXiv:1504.01354, Theorem 2.

For an absolutely irreducible polynomial of bidegree `(m,n)` with
`P(0,0)!=0` and `deg P(x,0)>=1`, and a multiplicative subgroup `G`
satisfying

```text
100(mn)^(3/2)<|G|<p^(3/4)/3,
```

the number of points in a product of two cosets of `G` is at most

```text
16mn^2(m+n)|G|^(2/3).
```

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.01354
