# Rank-eleven factor-flag three-coordinate rank collision

- **status:** PROVED
- **scope:** the weighted residual-root bucket in the base-free `2 x 5`
  factor branch

Let `B` be the five-dimensional residual factor space and let the weighted
root-concentration theorem supply an anchor-good coordinate `x_1` and
first-owned residual classes of total slope mass at least

```text
M_1=303637675671716.
```

Every such class has a residual subspace `B_i<=B` with at least `37736`
common-zero coordinates, including `x_1`. Then there are distinct
anchor-good coordinates `x_2,x_3` and a subfamily of total slope mass at
least

```text
M_2=10266384562185,
M_3=347110921118,                                      (TR1)
```

whose residual spaces vanish on `x_1,x_2,x_3`. Moreover the three evaluation
functionals on `B` have rank at most two:

```text
rank(ev_{x_1},ev_{x_2},ev_{x_3}:B -> F^3)<=2.          (TR2)
```

Thus the factor branch emits a literal three-coordinate evaluation-column
collision supporting at least `347110921118` first-owned slopes.

## Nonclaim

This theorem does not classify the rank-one/rank-two collision, identify it
with a coordinate-clone component, or pay the concentrated slopes. It does
not assert that the three coordinates are common to the complete unsafe
line.
