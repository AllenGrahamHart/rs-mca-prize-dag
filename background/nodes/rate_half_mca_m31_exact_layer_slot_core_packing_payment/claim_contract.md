# Claim contract

- **claim:** exact-layer line slots force `(EL1)`, and the resulting capped
  recursion pays `130226<=e<=130236`.
- **dependencies:** exact-layer ownership of every bank slot, off-core line
  disjointness, the high-core absorption dichotomy, capped convex charging,
  and distinct-line inside-core intersection at most `K-1`.
- **quantifiers:** all official Mersenne full-lift instances covered by the
  parent route at the eleven printed supports.
- **not claimed:** safety or unsafety at `e>=130237`, a bound for arbitrary
  cross-layer lines, or closure of the global MCA target.
- **falsifier:** a selected exact-layer slot violating `(EL1)`, an illegal
  table cutoff, or an endpoint replay mismatch.
