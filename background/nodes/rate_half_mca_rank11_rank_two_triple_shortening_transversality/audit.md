# Audit

1. The triple has evaluation rank exactly two; rank one belongs to the clone
   horn and rank three was excluded by the parent theorem.
2. The bucket mass is first-owned and is capped once if its complete
   correction space has dimension at most four.
3. Locator division uses the three actual field coordinates, not an
   abstract projective-line parameter.
4. Correction rank is preserved because multiplication by `L_T` is
   injective.
5. The `37733` residual-root floor uses that `T` was counted inside each
   selected `37736`-set; it is not inferred from evaluation rank alone.
6. The support-local bounds are recomputed at `(n-3,K-3,m-3)` and are not
   copied from the unshortened row.
7. A cap strictly below retained integer mass forces the printed low-margin
   output; equality would not suffice.
