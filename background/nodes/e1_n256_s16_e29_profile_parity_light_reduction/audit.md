# Audit

The primary packet independently recomputes the slack dynamic program, all
17 energy profiles, the exact rational cubic boundary, all normalized light
supports, their affine canonical forms, and every diameter ledger.

The verifier rederives the profile list and cubic forms without importing the
Modal program. Its audit uses a separate recursive profile enumerator,
Gaussian elimination for the Hermite basis, and direct affine-orbit expansion.
The 111 representatives expand disjointly to all `264+14,400` supports needed
by the eight surviving profiles. The unused eight three-odd orbits are checked
separately so the complete one-diameter atlas totals 119 orbits.

All replay is verifier-scale; no census is rerun locally.
