# Attack

Test the dual independent-pair lemma against matroids with loops, parallel
classes, and disconnected components. The proof must use looplessness of
the primal as colooplessness of the dual; merely counting nonloops is not
enough.

The coefficient is sharp for `9-d` coloops plus one parallel class of size
`d+2`. Any stronger continuation must use a smaller shadow, more than one
shadow size at once, or extra algebraic structure beyond looplessness and
generalized MDS.
