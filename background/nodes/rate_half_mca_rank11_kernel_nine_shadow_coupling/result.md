# Result

The nine corank strata can no longer spend nine independent copies of the
record support. They share one exact `C(m',9)` shadow budget, with weight

```text
C(d+2,2)/C(K'-d-9,2)
```

for each corank-`d` incidence. The coefficient is sharp in the abstract
loopless-matroid category, so any further gain must introduce another
shared resource or additional evaluation-space structure.
