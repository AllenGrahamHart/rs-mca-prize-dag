# Audit

1. Primal loops, not primal parallel pairs, are excluded.
2. Dual colooplessness is used in the exactly-`c` parallel-class case.
3. Spanning nine-subsets are complements of independent dual pairs.
4. The closure has size at most `K'-d`, leaving `K'-d-9` new coordinates.
5. The `J_d` are disjoint rank classes and exhaust all support nine-subsets.
6. Zero extension capacity forces the corresponding incidence count to zero.
