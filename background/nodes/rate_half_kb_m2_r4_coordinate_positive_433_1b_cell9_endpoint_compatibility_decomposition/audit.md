# Audit

- Factorization is used only to enumerate deployed-field points; higher-degree
  factors cannot contain a deployed point.
- Every triangular lift is replayed in the original lex basis and all source
  guards are checked.
- The cleared endpoint equation alone is not treated as an exclusion.
- At the two exceptional points the whole stored kernel section vanishes;
  pointwise regularization is delegated to a child node.
