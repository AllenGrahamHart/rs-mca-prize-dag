# Audit

The lower bound concerns the numerical charge produced by the declared
method, not the true number of residual subspaces. It is therefore a method
wall only. Both intermediate terms are retained; dropping either would be an
unauthorized assertion that one residual dimension is absent.

The denominator maxima are taken over a larger domain than any legal cell,
which weakens the lower bound and is safe. Factor-heavy and transverse costs
are omitted, again weakening the obstruction.
