# Statement

There is an exact principal-system bijection from positive `433-1b` role
cell `12` to role cell `13`:

```text
B <-> C,
t -> -t,
(D,E,F) -> sigma_c (D,E,F),
BF <-> sigma_c CF.
```

It fixes both source signs and both target-lane signs, preserves all common
and outside rows and guards, and bijects every one of the 105 outside labels.
Thus it transports all `4*4*105=1680` signed principal systems. Since the
global rank-drop theorem already excludes the complementary branch,
complete cell-12 exclusion implies complete cell-13 exclusion.

The premise, complete cell-12 exclusion, remains open.
