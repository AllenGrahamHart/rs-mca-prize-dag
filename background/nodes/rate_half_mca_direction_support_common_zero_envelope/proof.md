# Refutation

The fixed-`z` maximization in the former proof is a correct optimization of
its assumed incidence ratio.  It cannot validate the ratio's denominator.
The exact `GF(1009)` counterexample satisfies every printed field-general
hypothesis and gives

```text
31 > floor((100*99-20*19)/(21*20)) = 22.
```

Thus the theorem is refuted independently of the official threshold scans.
