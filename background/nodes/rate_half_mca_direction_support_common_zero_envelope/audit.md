# Audit

The primary checker uses direct falling products and exhausts all
`1,048,577-r` integers in the uniform `x` interval at every nonempty
last-paid and every first-unpaid wall.  It compares rationals before taking
floors, pins both suppliers, and rejects four mutations.

The independent checker advances falling products by exact recurrence,
performs the same exhaustive rational maxima without the primary product
routine, and separately checks fixed-`z` denominator monotonicity in 140
small parameter cells.  Three contract controls are rejected.

The scans are constant-memory bounded integer arithmetic.  The primary
replay uses the local RAMguard profile and completes well below one minute;
no Modal compute is used.
