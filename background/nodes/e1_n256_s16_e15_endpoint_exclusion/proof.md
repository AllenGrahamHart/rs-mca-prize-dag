# Proof

The E15 router proves that every `V=30` candidate lies in one of two profiles
on eight templates. The two-profile exclusion proves that every
proper-conductor vector is removed by the conductor theorem and every
full-conductor norm has positive odd part below `2^250`. These branches
exhaust the router, so the entire `V=30` chamber is empty. Variance is even,
hence the next live positive endpoint is at most 28.
