# Attack

A counterexample must exhibit one of the following:

- a zero normal despite `K=s=1`;
- an exact-support incident normal family contained in one projective line
  despite same-support pair noncontainment;
- two distinct parameter points determined by the same independent affine
  line pair;
- a partition of `67473` into at least two positive parts whose square sum
  exceeds `67472^2+1`.

The cap may be sharpened only by forcing at least three projective classes
or controlling their maximum multiplicity; neither is claimed here.
