# Frontier

The remaining denominator-root task is now a row-sharp bound or absorption
theorem for the pole-defect records. Their data are:

```text
(P, gamma_i, p0_i, p1_i, u_i, v_i),
u_i=-gamma_i v_i on P,
v_i != 0,
i -> (p0_i,p1_i) injective.
```

Exploit the small pole degree `1<=|P|<=m-k`, first by stratifying on `|P|`
and the span of the vectors `v_i|_P`. No exhaustive large-field computation
is justified until that symbolic stratification produces a finite target.
