# Audit

The theorem does not claim that the defect is supported only on `P` across
the whole evaluation domain. It says only that, relative to the original
witness support, punctured agreement leaves a nonzero defect on `P`.

The ordered explaining pair may not be unique for one slope. Any deterministic
choice is allowed, and the proof shows that two distinct slopes cannot share
the same chosen pair. This does not itself bound the number of available
pairs, so the denominator-root exception is narrowed but not paid.
