# Claim contract

## Proved

- an exhaustive disjoint post-cancellation split;
- preservation of the reduced MCA witness on the nontrivial side;
- a global polynomial decomposition for every trivialized support;
- a nonzero rank-one defect on the deleted pole set retaining the same line
  and slope;
- slope recovery from that defect;
- injectivity of slope into the chosen ordered explaining pair.

## Not proved

- uniqueness of the explaining pair for one slope;
- injectivity into either `p0` or `p1` alone;
- any cardinality bound on the trivialized branch;
- absorption into spread or large-owner payment;
- complete `(E)` routing.
