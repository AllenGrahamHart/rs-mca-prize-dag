The proved ambient/record hybrid individual caps, the proved per-record
nine-shadow coupling, the exact residual-record lower bound, and the
dominant kernel-lane incidence demand.

## Output

Exact exclusion of the dominant kernel lane for every
`10<=K'<=15445`, with the first method wall at `K'=15446`.

No kernel exclusion above `15445`, rank-eight payment, dense-owner
chronology payment, rank-eleven payment, or prize closure is asserted.
