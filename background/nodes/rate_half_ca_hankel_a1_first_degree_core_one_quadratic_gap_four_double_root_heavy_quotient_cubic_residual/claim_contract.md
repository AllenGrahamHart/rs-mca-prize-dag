# Claim contract

- **Claim:** when `S_B` is squarefree and coprime to `g_*`, dividing the
  primitive locator by its fixed double-heavy row produces a vector whose
  Hankel image is exactly divisible by `D_1`, with a degree-at-most-three
  quotient; each correction point is one Smith chain of length two.
- **Currency:** the exact Pade contact divisor, the heavy-row cube
  factorization, the Hankel shift recurrence, and local Smith valuation.
- **Dependencies used:** the double-root normal form and the Pade regular-
  factor identity.
- **Status movement:** none; the separated double-root locus is reduced to
  a cubic vector recurrence and two type-`[2]` correction chains.
- **Falsifier:** under the separated-correction hypotheses, a root of `S_B`
  at which `P_F(t,x_*)` has order below two, a component of `M u` not
  divisible by `D_1`, or two positive local Smith invariants.
- **Nonclaims:** no assertion that the cubic residual vanishes, no exclusion
  of the `[2]` chain, and no claim on nonreduced/shared corrections.
