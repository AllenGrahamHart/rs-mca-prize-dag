# Audit

- `P_F(t,x_*)=Phi_t(U)` follows with the signs cancelling twice; this is
  checked explicitly in the proof.
- The correction order uses `div_C(s_F)=R_*+2B` and the higher vertical
  contact `R_*+3B`. The fixed infinity contact is a local unit at `x_*`.
- Squarefreeness and coprimality are load-bearing. Without them, two contact
  branches can project to one parameter or a supported and correction
  contact can share a parameter; the divisor data alone then need not give
  `D_1|P_F(t,x_*)`.
- The degree-three quotient is homogeneous after projective completion;
  affine degree drops only lower its degree.
- `(HQC7)` makes the class of `U` highly divisible, but the determinant
  valuation proves one exact type-`[2]` Smith chain at each separated root
  rather than a contradiction.
