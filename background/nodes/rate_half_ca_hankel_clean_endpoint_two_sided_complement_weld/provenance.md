# Provenance

The domain-side complement was banked in the endpoint norm theorem. The
slope-side complement and its elimination are new to this route. The
construction is analogous in form to the repository's `A=1` two-sided weld,
but is independently derived here at the `A=3`, `O=0` profile with different
degrees and a linear domain defect.

No computation is used in the proof. The verifiers check the exact degree
ledger and independently replay the elimination identity.
