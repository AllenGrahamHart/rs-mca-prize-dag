# Audit

1. The category census uses only source facets and row degree, not a
   stabilizer action.
2. `eta` is `L minus K`; no equality `L=I` is assumed.
3. The five degree profiles are ordinary integer profiles, not paired
   involution profiles.
4. Ramified coordinate fibers retain two divisor slots.
5. The coordinate theorem may narrow the list; that narrowing is not
   transferred to the diagonal or trivial-stabilizer type.
