# Audit

- The norm algebra has rank equal to the actual generic `X`-degree `d` of
  `Q`. No irreducibility assumption is made.
- The resultant formula includes `q_d^deg(P)`. It may be omitted only after
  a separately proved monic normalization whose scaling is tracked.
- The denominator norm is `H^d`, not `H^deg(P)`.
- Cubehood is asserted over the prize base field because the radical roots
  are heavy domain points and hence base-field valued.
- In characteristic three, derivative zero is sufficient only because the
  finite base field is perfect.
- The norm test is one-way. A cube norm need not lift to a cube in `K_C`.
