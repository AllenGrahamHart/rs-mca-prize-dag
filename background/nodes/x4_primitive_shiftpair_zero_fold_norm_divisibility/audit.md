# Audit

1. Active and zero levels are disjoint, so no zero evaluation appears in a
   norm product.
2. The dyadic norm formula uses distinct orders at least four.
3. The factor `X-1` is independent of every dyadic cyclotomic factor.
4. Characteristic is odd because a field containing `mu_N`, `N>=4`, cannot
   have characteristic two.
5. The theorem gives arithmetic divisibility only.  It does not assign the
   associated supports to an existing first-owner bucket.
