# Claim contract

- **Proved zero interpretation:** `beta_a=0` is exact integral divisibility
  of the signed support polynomial by `Phi_(n_a)`.
- **Proved extra divisor:** balance and all selected zero folds contribute the
  printed power of two to every active norm product.
- **Proved pattern gate:** `(ZF-2)` is a necessary exact integer inequality.
- **Nonclaims:** a zero fold is quotient-owned; every zero pattern occurs;
  surviving patterns are sparse; or the X4 population is bounded.
