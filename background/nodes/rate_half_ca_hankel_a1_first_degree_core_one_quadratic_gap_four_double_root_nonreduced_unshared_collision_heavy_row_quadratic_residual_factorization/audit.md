# Audit

1. The degree-`e-6` form `g_*` includes exactly `d_A` assigned-center
   factors. The all-excess theorem applies only after removing them.
2. No claim is made that a padded-heavy center divides `G(t,x_*)`; the
   center factor is cancelled in `g_off=g_*/J_*`.
3. Bihomogeneity and nonzeroness make `G(t,x_*)` a binary form of exact
   projective degree `e-2`; affine degree drop is a root at infinity.
4. Exact order two at `tau` is already proved by the Pade value-row gate.
5. Unsharedness and center-disjointness of `tau` are used before division.
6. The residual has exact projective degree `2+d_A`; it is not asserted to
   split over the base field.
7. The remainder matrix is a gate, not a rank conclusion.
