# Result

Order-3 sharing has a home: it is a Lüroth-lattice pullback with cubic
inner degree and zero budget waste, its demand arithmetic is calibrated
(supply meets demand only at `m = 3`), and its existence mechanism — lines
of constant-norm split cubics — is structural, at cost `1/N` rather than
`1/q`.

The `m = 4` question the template was built for remains open and has
sharpened since drafting: demand minimality (>= 10 merges, two placements)
is proved, Door A is closed budget-neutral, sporadic sharing is closed by
dichotomy, and DOOR B (deficit 2, existing 14-slope objects as candidates)
is the live route, with Conjecture C38 posing the 14-ceiling as intrinsic.
