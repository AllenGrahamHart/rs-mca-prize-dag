# Claim contract

- **Input:** one FPC5 rational Hankel chart on a distinct-point core, with
  its complete `d+c` moment segment and primitive Cramer amplitudes.
- **Output:** an exact bijection to one weight-`d` GRS syndrome shell, the
  injective `d+c>=N` branch, and the MDS support-overlap cap.
- **Preserved:** locator support, amplitudes, exact Hankel equations,
  primitivity, fixed required background set, Cauchy guards, first owner,
  and chronology.
- **Nonclaim:** a worst-case GRS list bound below the injective range,
  background-set coalescence, an aggregate payment, or a deployed row.
