# Claim contract

- **Claim:** primitive normalization of `B_src` gives a degree-`D` domain
  map whose three center fibers contain exactly the three source classes
  on `U_0`, with residual degrees and relation `(PSP7),(PSP11)`.
- **Inputs:** the three-center source partition, center Pade quotients, and
  padded-center Pade transversality.
- **Output:** one fixed-gcd integer `h`, three pairwise-coprime residual
  fibers, and one exact three-term polynomial relation.
- **Consumer:** `rate_half_band_crossing_location`.
- **Nonclaims:** no bound stronger than `h<=d-2` and no ramification bound
  is asserted.
- **Falsifier:** a basepoint on `U_0` or at `x_*`, a fourth classified
  point in a center fiber, or a common root of two primitive fibers.
