# Audit

1. Homogenization in `X` is essential: every primitive fiber has the same
   projective degree even when an affine leading coefficient vanishes.
2. Parameter rank two follows from the three distinct roots of the
   nonzero source forms on the classified rows.
3. The homogeneous gcd has no root on `U_0`; padded-center transversality
   separately excludes `x_*`.
4. Pairwise coprimality applies to complete primitive fibers. It therefore
   removes class-locator/residual cross factors as well as residual/
   residual factors.
5. `(PSP8)` is a set statement. A point of `M_gamma` may still be
   ramified in its own fiber.
6. The relation in `(PSP11)` has all coefficients nonzero because no two
   distinct evaluations span the same functional on `S_1`.
7. No critical status changes.
