# Audit

The structural verifier pins the adapter, compiler, result, dedicated root
certificate, dedicated direct-audit source, and Modal replay result. The
independent replay reconstructs the root union, replays all tower relations,
recomputes both quadratic cuts, all 192 common-`f` lifts, and their nonzero
colored cuts.

The audit verifier checks the printed quadratic resultant symbolically and
rejects a hostile count mutation. No pairing-11 or `xi=2` assertion is
imported into this leaf.
