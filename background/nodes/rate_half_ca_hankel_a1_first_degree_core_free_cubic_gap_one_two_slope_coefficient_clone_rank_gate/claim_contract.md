# Claim contract

- **Claim:** on `S_beta\S_alpha`, the coefficient evaluation rank of the
  primitive kernel biform is at most
  `|S_alpha union S_beta|-rho`; minimum-union pairs therefore force all row
  forms on the difference set to be proportional.
- **Currency:** the complete minimal-kernel coefficient recurrence, exact
  specialized left apolar kernel, two-slope syndrome sources, and
  Vandermonde rank.
- **Dependencies:** minimal-index budget, split-pencil equivalence, cubic
  gap-one normal forms, and the column-far support gate.
- **Characteristic:** unrestricted.
- **Nonclaim:** no domain-point separation theorem and no packet exclusion.
- **Falsifier:** a supported pair whose coefficient-row rank exceeds its
  union excess, or a minimum-union pair with two nonproportional row forms
  on the difference.
- **Next gate:** prove point separation or classify low-rank fibres of the
  coefficient map using its Hankel/apolar and heavy-row structure.
