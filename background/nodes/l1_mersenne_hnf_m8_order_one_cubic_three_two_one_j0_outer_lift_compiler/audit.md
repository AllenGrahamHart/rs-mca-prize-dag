# Audit

The 21 role packets are alternatives. A role factor has two roots, and a
role value can in principle have more than one normalized ordered color
representative; the replay retains every matching `(beta,gamma)` and every
global `tau`.

The factor identities alone are not a pointwise Frobenius converse. The
congruence in (OLC8) is mandatory. Likewise, `d^(p+1)` must be tested before
using the known split root.

Do not infer polynomial divisibility merely from distinct geometric roots.
The proof uses exact congruences modulo `L` and the inherited coprimality of
`L` with `W+1/d`. Every coefficient-chart and exact-fiber guard remains a
separate nonzero test.

The compiler pays only the outer HNF lift. The global inner lift mentioned
throughout the L1 route is not defined or discharged here.
