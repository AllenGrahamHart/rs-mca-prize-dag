   the `BE*CF` terminal equation.  Omitting it permits colored products whose
   ratio is incompatible with the `DE/DF` ratio.

   nonzero `b,c,X_BE,X_CF` stratum.  No unsupported denominator is divided
   away.

   `R`; it is not an independent target variable after substitution.

   unsquared `q` orientations required by the original Vieta rows.
5. The compiler covers all 267 formal target-gauge representatives by
   relabeling one universal system.  This is not a claim that any of those
   source systems survives the common equations and guards.

   projections, but their product chains required the cross-relation repair.
