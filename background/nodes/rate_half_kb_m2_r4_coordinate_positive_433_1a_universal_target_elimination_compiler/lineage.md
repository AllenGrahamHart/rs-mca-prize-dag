# Lineage

The first target-free compiler covered two `EF` matching templates and
listed three product relations.  Exact quotienting showed that those
templates covered only five of 39 formal `EF` orbits.  Exponent-lattice
inspection then exposed a stronger fact: every formal case is a relabeling
of one seven-record target monomial system.  Its complete kernel has four,
not three, binomials.  Adding the missing cross relation and explicit target
reconstruction yields the universal exact compiler in this node.
