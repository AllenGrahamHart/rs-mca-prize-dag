# Audit

1. The contradiction retains exactly 219 points, which must span the plane
   because the affine-line cap is 15.
2. Plane-specific shortening uses the actual common received-pair core, not
   the scalar gcd's entire root set.
3. Residual nonmatching gcd roots have multiplicity zero; all other residual
   fibers have multiplicity at most 15.
4. Distinct full 15-point lines through one point have disjoint sets of 14
   other points, giving the 219-line incidence bound.
5. A repeated full line is bounded by a nonzero scalar difference of degree
   at most `k'-1`.
6. The cap 218 is proved; no claim is made that 218 is attainable.
