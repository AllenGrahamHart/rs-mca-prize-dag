# Claim contract

- **Input:** an odd-characteristic cyclic odd-prefix syndrome map with
  root order twice its binary length and `p>2R`.
- **Output:** a lossless injection of every binary syndrome fiber into an
  ordinary half-size, depth-`2R` split-locator prefix fiber.
- **Closure:** proof.
- **Nonclaims:** no split-locator flatness bound; no normalized-band
  instantiation; no proof that quotient/common-divisor pieces match the F2
  structured owners; no pruned first-match Q-atom transport.
- **Falsifier:** two binary vectors with equal odd syndrome whose selector
  prefixes differ, a selector prefix not recovering its odd syndrome, or a
  branch map not related by invertible row scaling to the displayed form.
