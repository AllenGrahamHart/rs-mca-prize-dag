# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | Every actual negative coordinate packet pairs its twelve edge products into six free orbits of one projective involution and satisfies the rank-two gate `(KBNP-1)`. |
| deletion interface | Any nonzero `3 x 3` paired-product minor deletes the proposed negative packet. |
| symbolic deletion | Two distinct antisymmetric product pairs force every pair to be antisymmetric; this deletes the banked defect-zero fixture over every odd field. |
| exact regression | The deleted fixture has no gate survivor among 5,040 signed-square assignments over `F_29`. |
| assumptions | The proved complete-fiber negative product map and odd characteristic. |
| nonclaim | Sufficiency of the involution gate, deletion of packet graphs other than the printed fixture, universal negative or coordinate exclusion, owner/payment movement, a row, or either Prize theorem. |
