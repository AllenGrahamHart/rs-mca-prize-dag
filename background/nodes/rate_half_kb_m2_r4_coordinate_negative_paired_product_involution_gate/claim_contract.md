# Claim contract

| field | content |
|---|---|
| claim | Every actual negative coordinate packet, and every positive packet with reduced product degree at most one, pairs its twelve edge products into six free orbits of one projective involution and satisfies `(KBNP-1)`. |
| deletion interface | Any nonzero `3 x 3` paired-product minor deletes the proposed negative packet. |
| symbolic deletion | Two distinct antisymmetric product pairs force every pair to be antisymmetric; this deletes the banked fixture in negative parity and the positive linear-product locus over every odd field. |
| exact regression | The deleted fixture has no gate survivor among 5,040 signed-square assignments over `F_29`. |
| assumptions | The proved complete-fiber negative product map and odd characteristic. |
| nonclaim | Positive genuinely quadratic product maps, sufficiency of the involution gate, deletion of other packet graphs, universal parity/orientation exclusion, owner/payment movement, a row, or either Prize theorem. |
