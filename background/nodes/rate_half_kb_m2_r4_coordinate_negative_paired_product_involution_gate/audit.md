# Audit

1. The gate applies only to negative parity, where the product map is
   projective linear.  The positive ratio has degree at most two.
2. Rank at most two is necessary, not sufficient: one common product
   involution need not align all six source pairs under one conjugating map.
3. The determinant condition `a^2+bc!=0` excludes a singular trace-zero
   matrix.
4. Source square roots, oriented edge sums, and the `q` equations are not
   used.
5. The symbolic contradiction deletes one abstract packet graph over every
   odd field.  It does not prove that graph exhausts the seven negative
   skeletons or delete the negative orientation.
6. The 5,040-case `F_29` census is only a regression for the symbolic
   fixture deletion.
