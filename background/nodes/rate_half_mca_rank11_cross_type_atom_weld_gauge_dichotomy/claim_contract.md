# Claim contract

- **Input:** canonical pairwise atom welds on the complete graph of large
  types, with one fixed 18-record anchor set per vertex.
- **Normalization:** all edge scalar coefficient pairs are scaled to one
  common nonzero pair.
- **Triangle law:** edge differences are polynomial gauge multiples of
  `(1,a_p,b_p)` at their common vertex.
- **Outputs:** one globally identical canonical edge atom, or rational rank
  at most two for all pair-type vectors.
- **Not claimed:** extension to every owned record, payment of either output,
  high-complexity payment, or MCA closure.
